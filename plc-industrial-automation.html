PLC Industrial Automation and Control Panels

IBBS supports PLC-based automation upgrades, industrial control panel engineering and SCADA integration. Each project is scoped around the plant process, existing equipment, operator needs and the information available for the control system.

PLC upgrades, panels and SCADA

Industrial automation work can involve reviewing existing control logic, planning a PLC-based upgrade, designing or building a control panel, and connecting process information to a SCADA interface. IBBS discusses the intended process changes and available drawings before defining a project scope.

Useful information for an initial discussion includes the process description, existing PLC and HMI models, panel photographs, electrical drawings, required inputs and outputs, and the site location. This helps establish what can be assessed and whether a site visit or additional documentation is needed.

Our Story

15 YEARS OF INDUSTRIAL AUTOMATION & ENGINEERING EXCELLENCE

Industrial Boiler & Burner Services (IBBS) is a trusted provider of advanced industrial automation and engineering solutions based in Lahore. For over 15 years, we have specialized in delivering high-quality automation products, machinery components, and general order supplies to major manufacturing plants across Pakistan.

We deliver complete smart solutions, from boiler automation and burner modifications to PLC-based control systems and SCADA integration. By combining cutting-edge automation technologies with reliable nationwide parts supply, we ensure your plant operates with maximum efficiency, safety, and zero downtime.

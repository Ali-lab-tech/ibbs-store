Our Story

Industrial Automation & Engineering Since 2012

Industrial Boiler & Burner Services (IBBS) is a Lahore-based industrial automation and engineering company serving manufacturing businesses in Pakistan and international markets. Since 2012, we have supplied automation products, machinery components, and general-order industrial supplies.

Our work includes boiler automation, burner modifications, PLC-based control systems, and SCADA integration. We combine engineering support with parts supply for plants in Pakistan and international markets, including Saudi Arabia, Dubai in the UAE, and Bangladesh, to help improve efficiency, support safe operations, and reduce unplanned downtime.

SITE AUDIT

Flue gas analysis and boiler efficiency assessment to optimize operations.

PANEL DESIGN

Custom PLC-based automation control panel engineering and layout planning.

PARTS SUPPLY

Sourcing premium industrial components, valves, and burner spare parts.

SYSTEM BUILD

Burner modifications, structural erection, and smart system integration.

WATER TREATMENT

Setting up specialized industrial waste water treatment plant solutions.

COMMISSION

Final pumps installation, precise testing, calibration, and live startup.

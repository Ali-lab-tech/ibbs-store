Product details

Rd-01 radar module, which supports Wi-Fi&BLE. The radar part is equipped with S3KM111L chip of Sidian Micro, working in 24GHz k band, each frequency up to 1GHz modulation bandwidth. The working principle is to use FMCw FM continuous wave to detect human body targets in the set space combined with radar signal processing and accurate human body induction algorithm to realize highly sensitive human presence state induction, identify human body in motion and static state, and calculate auxiliary information such as the distance of the target. The Wi-Fi&BLE part uses the BL602 chip as the core processor and supports wi-Fi 802.11b/g/n and BLE5.0 protocols. This product is mainly used in indoor scenes, sensing whether there is a moving or twitching human body in the area, real-time transmission of detection results through Wi-Fi&BLE, providing visual configuration tools, which can easily configure the range of induction distance, induction sensitivity of different sections and unmanned delay time. It supports GPIO and UART output and wi-Fi&BLE wireless configuration of radar parameters, which can be flexibly applied to different intelligent scenarios and terminal products.

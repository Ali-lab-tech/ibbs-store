Product details

The YAXUN PS-1502DD Adjustable DC Power Supply is a compact and reliable bench power supply designed for electronics testing, circuit development, and repair work. It provides an adjustable DC output voltage of 0–15V and current up to 2A, making it suitable for electronics labs, technicians, students, and hobbyists. This power supply features dual digital LED displays for monitoring output voltage and current. Its voltage and current adjustment knobs allow users to set the required output according to their project needs. The compact design and clearly marked output terminals make it convenient for everyday electronics work.

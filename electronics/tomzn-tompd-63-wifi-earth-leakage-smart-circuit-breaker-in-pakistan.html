TOMZN TOMPD-63 WIFI Earth Leakage Smart Circuit Breaker The TOMZN TOMPD-63 WIFI Earth Leakage Smart Circuit Breaker is a modern and reliable solution for protecting your home or office electrical systems . First, it provides real-time monitoring to keep track of electrical conditions. Additionally, it offers enhanced protection against earth leakage, overcurrent , and overvoltage issues . Moreover, its intuitive WIFI connectivity allows you to remotely control and monitor the breaker through the TUYA Smart App. Finally, this smart circuit breaker ensures reliable safety with ease of use. Key Features First, Over Voltage Protection is adjustable between 110V-300V (default: 275V) to prevent damage from excessive voltage. Next, Under Voltage Protection can be set between 85V-220V (default: 170V) to protect against low voltage conditions. Then, Over Current Protection is adjustable from 1A to 63A (default: 63A) to avoid overloads. Additionally, Earth Leakage Protection offers adjustable sensitivity from 10mA to 300mA for safe operation. Moreover, Remote Monitoring and Control allows real-time control via the TUYA Smart App , and it is compatible with Alexa and Google Home for easy integration. After that, the Timer Function provides programmable settings, allowing you to customize the circuit breaker’s operation schedule. Finally, the Reconnect Delay is adjustable between 1s and 500s (default: 5s) to control the delay before reconnecting.

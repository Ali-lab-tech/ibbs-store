Product details

Description: This component is an N-Channel Power MOSFET (specifically, the MDF3752 made by MagnaChip ). Think of a MOSFET as an electronic switch . Unlike a mechanical switch that you flip with your finger, this switch turns on and off automatically using electrical voltage signals. How It Works Imagine a high-pressure water pipe: Drain & Source Pins: These act as the inlet and outlet for electric current to flow through. Gate Pin: This acts as an electric valve handle. When a small voltage signal is applied to the Gate pin, the valve opens wide, allowing a large electric current to flow between the Drain and Source pins instantly. Key Features & Specs Voltage Handling (250V): It can safely hold back up to 250 Volts across its switch pins when turned off. Current Capacity (43A): When fully turned on, it can switch high power levels carrying up to 43 Amperes of electric current. TO-220F Package: The fully plastic casing has a mounting hole at the top, allowing it to be screwed directly onto a metal heatsink to keep it cool during high-power operation without short-circuiting. Common Uses You will typically find this component in high-power electronics, such as: Switch-Mode Power Supplies (SMPS) (like inverter circuit boards or computer power supplies) DC-to-DC Voltage Converters Motor Speed Controllers & Drivers Power Amplifiers Package Includes: 1x MDF3752 N-Channel Power MOSFET

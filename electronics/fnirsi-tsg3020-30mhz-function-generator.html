Product details

The FNIRSI TSG3020 is a dual-channel DDS function and arbitrary waveform generator with output up to 30MHz, 200MSa/s sampling and 14-bit vertical resolution. A 4.3-inch touchscreen and physical keypad make setting frequency, amplitude and offset quick. It covers sine, square, ramp, pulse, noise and DC, plus user-defined arbitrary waveforms, AM/FM/PM/FSK/ASK/PSK modulation, linear and logarithmic sweep, and a 100MHz frequency counter. It suits R&D labs, university teaching and automated test setups.

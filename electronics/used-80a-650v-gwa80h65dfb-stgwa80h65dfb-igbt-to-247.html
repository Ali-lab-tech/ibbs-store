Product details

The GWA80H65DFB , also known by the ST part number STGWA80H65DFB , is a Trench Field‑Stop IGBT engineered for high-speed switching applications. Featuring an integrated ultra-fast soft recovery diode , this device offers superior efficiency in hard-switching environments such as solar inverters , high-frequency converters , motor drives , and UPS systems . The tight parameter distribution and positive saturation voltage temperature coefficient make it optimal for demanding, parallel-operated systems.

Ausbond Conductive Paint Pen - High-Precision Electrical Repair Tool The Ausbond Conductive Paint Pen is an essential tool for professionals working on electronics and electrical circuits. It provides a simple, effective way to repair broken connections or create conductive pathways with precision. The pen dispenses conductive silver-based ink, making it ideal for use on PCBs, wires, and other conductive surfaces. Benefits of Ausbond Conductive Paint Pen High Conductivity: Delivers reliable electrical connections with silver-based ink. Durable and Long-Lasting: Creates permanent conductive pathways resistant to wear and corrosion. Easy to Use: Suitable for both professionals and DIY enthusiasts.

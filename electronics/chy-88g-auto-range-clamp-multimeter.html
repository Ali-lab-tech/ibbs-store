Product details

The CHY 88G is an auto-ranging clamp multimeter: pick the function and it chooses the range itself. It reads AC current up to 600A through a 35 mm jaw, and voltage, resistance, capacitance, frequency, diode and temperature through the test leads. A built-in non-contact voltage (NCV) sensor warns you of a live wire before you touch it, and the 3999-count display gives a finer reading than basic 1999-count clamps.

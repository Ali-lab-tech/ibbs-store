Product details

The IR2110 is a high voltage, high speed power MOSFET and IGBT driver with independent high and low side referenced output channels. It is designed for use in a wide variety of applications, including motor control, power conversion, and power amplifiers. Features Of IR2110: High speed: Up to 1 MHz switching speed High voltage: Up to 500 V operation Independent channels: Separate high and low side output channels Rugged construction: Built using ruggedized monolithic construction Available in multiple packages: 14-pin through-hole PDIP and 16-pin surface mount SOIC 3.3 V logic compatible: Available in a version that is compatible with 3.3 V logic Benefits OF IR2110 MOSFET And IGBT Driver IC | : Fast switching speeds High voltage operation Independent channels Rugged construction Multiple package options 3.3 V logic compatibility Applications: Motor control Power conversion Power amplifiers Package Includes: 1x IR2110 IC In Dip Package

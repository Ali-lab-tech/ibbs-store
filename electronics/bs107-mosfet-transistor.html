BS107 MOSFET: The Versatile N-Channel Powerhouse The BS107 is a **versatile and reliable N-channel MOSFET** that can handle a wide range of switching and amplification tasks in your electronic projects. Its compact size and **low on-resistance** make it a popular choice for hobbyists and professionals alike. Here's what you can expect from the BS107: 1. Efficient Switching: 200V Drain-Source Voltage:** Suitable for handling moderate to high voltage applications. 1.9A Maximum Drain Current: Capable of switching moderate currents for various tasks. Enhancement Mode: Turns on only when a voltage is applied to the gate, making it ideal for digital circuits and low-power applications. 2. Easy to Use: TO-92 Package: Compact and easy to mount on PCBs. Low Gate Threshold Voltage (0.8V to 3V): Requires minimal gate voltage to turn on, simplifying circuit design. Fast Switching Speed: Responds quickly to changes in gate voltage, making it suitable for high-frequency applications. 3. Durable and Reliable: High Breakdown Voltage (200V): Can withstand voltage spikes without damage. Wide Operating Temperature Range (-55°C to +150°C): Performs well in various environmental conditions. Lead-Free Construction: RoHS compliant for environmentally conscious projects. 1-Piece Package Details: 1x BS107 N-Channel MOSFET Transistor

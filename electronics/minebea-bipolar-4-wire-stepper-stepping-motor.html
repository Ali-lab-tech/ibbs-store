Product details

The MINEBEA Bipolar 4 wire Stepper Stepping Motor is a high-quality, NEMA 17-sized stepper motor that is well-suited for a wide range of applications. It has a step angle of 1.8 degrees, a holding torque of 2.2 Nm, and a rated voltage of 24 V. The motor is also equipped with a high-quality ball bearing system for smooth and precise operation. Use cases: 3D printers CNC machines Robotics Medical devices Industrial automation equipment Cameras and other optical devices Automotive and aerospace applications Specifications: Motor type: Bipolar stepper motor Step angle: 1.8 degrees Phase current: 1.2 A Phase resistance: 2.1 ohms Rated voltage: 24 V Motor weight: 0.5 kg Package include: 1 x MINEBEA Bipolar 4 wire Stepper Stepping Motor Additional information: The MINEBEA Bipolar 4 wire Stepper Stepping Motor is compatible with a wide range of motor controllers. It is also available with a variety of mounting options and accessories.

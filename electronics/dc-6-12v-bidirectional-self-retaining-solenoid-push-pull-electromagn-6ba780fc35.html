Product details

The characteristics of the electromagnet: when through a positive voltage, push-pull rod inhalation, after power off the push-pull rod keep the suction state by the internal magnet; when reverse connecting the power supply, push-pull rod launched, after power off the push-pull rod keep the push out state by the internal magnet. The solenoid is energized only a short period, only need short energized, it can achieve the purpose of use.

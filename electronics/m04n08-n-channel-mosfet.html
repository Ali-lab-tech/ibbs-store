Product details

The M04N08 N-Channel Power MOSFET is a high-speed, energy-efficient transistor ideal for use in power management, switching, and control circuits. With an 80V drain-source voltage and 4A current handling , it delivers stable performance in inverter circuits, power supply units, and motor driver boards. Its TO-220 package ensures easy installation and excellent thermal dissipation.

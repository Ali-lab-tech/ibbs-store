Product details

The BENETECH GT5105A is a digital earth (ground) resistance tester for checking earthing pits of houses, factories, solar systems and transmission towers. It supports accurate three-wire measurement with test spikes and a quick two-wire method. It measures ground resistance from 0.01Ω to 2000Ω and ground voltage up to 200V, warns on screen if the C or E lead has poor contact, and has a backlight and data hold. Key Features 🌍 Ground resistance 20Ω / 200Ω / 2000Ω 📌 3-wire precise or 2-wire simple measurement ⚡ Ground voltage to 200V ⚠️ Bad lead-contact warning on the LCD 💡 Backlight, data hold , battery check Technical Specifications Model GT5105A Brand BENETECH Ground resistance 20Ω ±(2% + 10), 0.01Ω Ground resistance 200Ω ±(2% + 3), 0.1Ω Ground resistance 2000Ω ±(2% + 3), 1Ω 2000Ω (two-wire) ±(2% + 5), 1Ω Ground voltage 200V, ±(2% + 5), 0.1V Test signal about 820Hz; about 3.2mA on 20Ω range Safety IEC 61010-1 CAT III 600V; IP40 Overload protection Ground resistance 200V AC (10 s); ground voltage 400V AC (30 s) Power 9V (6 × 1.5V AA) Size 176 × 77 × 110 mm What's in the Box 1 × BENETECH GT5105A earth tester

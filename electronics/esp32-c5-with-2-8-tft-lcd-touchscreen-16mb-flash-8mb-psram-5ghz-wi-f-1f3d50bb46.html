Product details

Product Highlights This is ESP32 Bruce — an Open-Source firmware based on ESP32 hardware, specifically designed for Red Team operations in the field of network security, providing a range of offensive security testing capabilities. Its main uses include conducting penetration testing and security assessments for various wireless communication methods. Use the CYD Bruce to unlock Flipper-Zero-like features on the ESP32 2432S028 device. This board comes flashed with ESP32-Bruce 1.9.1 firmware on the Cheap Yellow Display, offering powerful WiFi and Bluetooth testing features on an affordable and accessible hardware platform. With the CYD Bruce, you can easily work with WiFi, BLE, Sub-GHz module (CC1101), NFC (PN532), GPS (ATGM336H / NEO-6M), IR, and more — a powerful platform for exploration. Hardware Compatibility The Bruce firmware is compatible with the Cheap Yellow Display ecosystem and development boards similar to CYD. USB-C and Micro-USB Ports , Micro-SD card slot, acrylic case. CYD (Cheap Yellow Display) with 2.8″, 240×380 touch screen , driver chip ST7789. Power via laptop, phone charger, or power bank. Functions with or without an SD card — SD card required to save your work. More Information Bruce Firmware GitHub: https://github.com/pr3y/Bruce Module Setup Guide (GPS, NFC, IR, RF, CC1101, NRF24 & Speaker): YouTube Tutorial Important Notice This listing is for ESP32 Bruce , not NMMiner. However, this device includes an NMMiner License — you can flash to NMMiner for free at flash.nmminer.com (choose device: esp32-2432s028r-st7789). Micro SD card not included — required for some capabilities. The use of this software for any illegal or unauthorized activities is strictly prohibited .

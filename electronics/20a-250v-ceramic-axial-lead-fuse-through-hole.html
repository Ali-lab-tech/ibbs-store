Product details

This 20A 250V Ceramic Axial Fuse is a robust circuit protection component designed to safeguard electronic devices against overcurrent and short circuits. Unlike standard glass fuses, this ceramic fuse is filled with a filler material (often sand) which provides a high breaking capacity, allowing it to safely interrupt higher currents without rupturing. The fuse features pre-attached axial leads (pigtails), making it ideal for through-hole mounting directly onto printed circuit boards (PCBs) without the need for a fuse holder. It is commonly used in power supplies, microwave ovens, inverters, and heavy-duty industrial electronics. Features: High Breaking Capacity: Ceramic body construction prevents explosion during severe short circuits. Axial Leads: Tinned wire leads allow for easy soldering directly to boards or wrapping around terminals. Opaque Body: Ceramic tube offers better thermal, arc, and shock protection than glass equivalents. Universal Application: Suitable for use in AC power lines and DC circuits up to 250V. Specifications: Current Rating: 20 Amps (20A) Voltage Rating: 250V AC Fuse Type: Ceramic Cartridge with Axial Leads Mounting Style: Through-Hole (THT) Body Material: Ceramic Tube with Nickel Plated Brass Caps Lead Material: Tinned Copper Package Includes: 1 x 20A 250V Ceramic Axial Lead Fuse

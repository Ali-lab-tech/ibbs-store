Product details

The 4 Axis 6 Axis CNC Breakout Board is a versatile and reliable solution for controlling your CNC machines. This powerful board provides precise control over your machine's movements, making it ideal for various applications, including CNC engraving, lathe work, plasma cutting, laser engraving, milling, and machining centers. Key Features: Compatibility with MACH3 Software: Works seamlessly with MACH3 CNC system software for easy configuration and control. 5 Input Signals: Supports 5 input signals for communication with the host computer, including high-speed inputs for encoder and hand wheel connections. 11 Output Signals: Provides 11 output signals for controlling your machine's movements, supporting up to 6 axes. High-Speed Processing: Utilizes a special high-speed chip for accurate and stable signal processing. Isolated Power Supply: Ensures secure and reliable operation by separating the computer's power supply from the machine's power supply. Spindle Speed Control: Supports spindle speed control using PWM signals. Versatile Output: Can be connected to stepper or servo drives for flexible control options. Wide Input Voltage Range: Accommodates input voltages from 15-32VDC. Order Now: Upgrade your CNC machines with our 4 Axis 6 Axis CNC Breakout Board.

Product details

The DF06M is a bridge rectifier IC designed to convert alternating current (AC) input into direct current (DC) output. It is commonly used in power supply circuits and other applications requiring rectification of AC signals. The device is capable of handling moderate current levels and is suitable for a variety of consumer electronics and industrial applications. High Voltage Handling: Maximum repetitive peak reverse voltage (V_RRM): 600V Maximum average forward rectified current (I_O): 1A Low forward voltage drop (V_F) ensures efficient power conversion and minimal power loss during operation. Capable of withstanding high surge currents, which makes it suitable for applications with transient load conditions. Operating temperature range suitable for various environments, ensuring reliable performance under different conditions. Maximum Ratings: Repetitive Peak Reverse Voltage (V_RRM): 600V Average Rectified Output Current (I_O): 1A Peak Forward Surge Current (I_FSM): Typically 30A Electrical Characteristics: Forward Voltage (V_F) per element: Typically 1.1V at 1A Reverse Current (I_R): Typically 10µA at 600V Thermal Characteristics: Junction Temperature Range (T_J): -55°C to +150°C Storage Temperature Range (T_STG): -55°C to +150°C Applications Power Supplies: Used in AC-DC converters to provide a stable DC output. Battery Chargers: Converts AC input to the required DC output for charging batteries. Consumer Electronics: Commonly found in TVs, radios, and other electronic appliances. Industrial Equipment: Used in various industrial power control and conversion circuits Package Includes: 1 x DF06M 600V 1A Bridge Rectifier Diode IC

Product details

STM32 Nucleo 64 board provides an affordable and flexible way for users to try out new concepts and build prototypes using STM32 Nucleo 64 or STM32 microcontroller, choosing from the various combinations of performance, power consumption, and features. For the compatible boards, the SMPS significantly reduces power consumption in Run mode. The Arduino™ Uno V3 connectivity support and the ST morpho headers allow the easy expansion of the functionality of the STM32 Nucleo open development platform with a wide choice of specialized shields. The STM32 Nucleo board does not require any separate probe as it integrates the ST-LINK/V2-1 debugger and programmer.

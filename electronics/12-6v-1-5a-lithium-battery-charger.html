Product details

The 12.6V 1.5A Lithium Battery Charger is a high-quality AC to DC charger designed for 3-cell (3S) 11.1V lithium-ion and lithium-polymer battery packs . Featuring a constant current/constant voltage (CC/CV) charging method, it delivers safe, stable, and efficient charging while helping to extend battery life. It also includes LED charging indicators and built-in protection against over-voltage, over-current, and short circuits. Ideal for DIY electronics, robotics, RC projects, LED lighting, portable devices, and rechargeable battery packs, this charger offers reliable performance with a compact, lightweight design. Key Features Output Voltage: 12.6V DC Output Current: 1.5A Designed for 3S (11.1V nominal / 12.6V full) lithium battery packs CC/CV charging technology for safe and efficient charging LED charging status indicator (Charging/Full) Built-in over-voltage, over-current, and short-circuit protection Compact, lightweight, and easy to carry Wide input voltage: 100–240V AC , suitable for worldwide use.

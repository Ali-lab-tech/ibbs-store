Product details

The JK-BD6A24S6PD is a smart active balance Battery Management System from JIKONG (JKBMS), designed to protect and optimize the performance of multi-cell lithium battery packs. Built with active balancing technology, this BMS actively transfers energy between cells rather than simply bleeding off excess charge — helping your pack reach true full balance, extend battery life, and maximize usable capacity. This model supports a flexible 8S to 24S configuration, making it suitable for a wide range of battery pack sizes, and works with Li-ion, LiFePO4, and LTO cell chemistries. It handles up to 60A continuous charge/discharge, and delivers active cell balancing at 0.6A, keeping cells tightly matched even under load. Built-in Bluetooth connectivity and display support let you monitor pack voltage, cell balance, temperature, and BMS status in real time via the JKBMS app — simply scan the QR code on the board to download the app and user manual. Key Features Smart Active Balancing — actively transfers charge between cells for true balance, not just passive dissipation Battery Strings: 8S–24S configurable Charge/Discharge Current: up to 60A Balancing Current: 0.6A Compatible Battery Types: Li-ion / LiFePO4 / LTO Connectivity: Bluetooth + Display support Function: Real-time monitoring via JKBMS mobile app Compact, robust PCB design with clearly labeled P-/B- terminals and balance lead connectors (B+ to B24) Ideal For Custom battery packs for solar energy storage, e-bikes, EVs, marine/RV battery banks, and other multi-cell lithium applications where precise balancing and long pack life matter. Package Includes 1 x JKBMS JK-BD6A24S6PD BMS Board

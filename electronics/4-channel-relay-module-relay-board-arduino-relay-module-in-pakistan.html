Product details

The 4 Channel Relay Module is a versatile and reliable component that allows you to control larger loads and devices using your microcontroller. This module is compatible with popular microcontrollers like Arduino, AVR, PIC, and ARM, providing a simple and effective way to interface with your digital outputs. Key Features: 4 Channels: Control up to four devices simultaneously. High-Current Relays: Handles loads of up to AC250V 10A or DC30V 10A. Easy-to-Use Interface: Compatible with various microcontrollers and provides clear indication LEDs. Versatile Applications: Control AC or DC motors, electromagnets, solenoids, and incandescent light bulbs. Active High Input: Easily control relays using logic high or 3.3V to 5V signals. Specifications: Input Voltage: 5V Relay Type: High-current relays Relay Rating: AC250V 10A or DC30V 10A Driver Current: 15-20mA per channel Order Now: Upgrade your microcontroller projects with our 4 Channel Relay Module. Experience the convenience and versatility of controlling various devices with ease.

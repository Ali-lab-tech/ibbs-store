600W High Power DC-DC Step Down Power Supply Module with Fan (12V-80V) Adjustable Voltage Converter for High-Performance Applications This 600W DC-DC step down power supply module with integrated fan provides robust, reliable voltage conversion with a wide input range from 12V to 80V and an adjustable output from 2.5V to 50V. Designed for high-current environments, it’s ideal for DIY projects, battery charging, LED systems, and more. Product Specifications Input Voltage: DC 12V-80V (Not suitable for unregulated 72V batteries) Output Voltage: 2.5V - 50V adjustable (Max output = input voltage × 0.8) Output Current: Up to 20A (Max), overcurrent protection at 25A Maximum Power: 600W Conversion Efficiency: ~93% Protections: Overcurrent protection, reverse polarity protection Working Temperature: -40°C to 85°C Storage Temperature: -20°C to +50°C Dimensions: 125mm × 70mm × 32mm Humidity: 65% ± 20% Cooling: Integrated fan for heat dissipation Key Features Wide adjustable output voltage range (2.5V to 50V) High current handling up to 20A continuous (25A peak) Rugged design for harsh environments with a wide temperature operating range Built-in protection against overcurrent and reverse polarity Efficient fan cooling ensures stable operation at high loads Application Areas Battery chargers and power supplies High-power LED systems DIY electronics and maker projects Automotive voltage regulation Off-grid and solar energy systems Interface Description IN+: Positive input IN-: Negative input OUT+: Positive output OUT-: Negative output Blue Light (B-Bulb): No constant voltage current Green Light (G): Current mode indicator Red Light (C-R): Constant voltage mode indicator IADJ: Current adjustment (Clockwise increases current) VADJ: Voltage adjustment (Clockwise increases voltage) FAQs Can this module handle a 72V battery? No, this module is not suitable for an unregulated 72V battery as the voltage can exceed the safe working range. How do I adjust the voltage and current? Use the VADJ knob to adjust output voltage and the IADJ knob to set the current limit—both turn clockwise to increase. Is the fan always running? The fan operates automatically based on load and temperature conditions to ensure efficient cooling. Package Includes 1 × 600W DC-DC Step Down Power Supply Module with Fan

Product details

The TOOLTOP ET72 is a handheld process calibrator and signal generator for industrial instrumentation. Use it to source and measure 4-20mA loop signals, DC voltage and thermocouple signals when commissioning or fault-finding PLC inputs, transmitters, valve positioners and panel meters. It can power a two-wire loop from its own 24V supply while reading the loop current, simulate thermocouples of eight types with automatic or manual cold-junction compensation, and step or ramp its output automatically. V, mA and percentage are shown together on one screen. Key Features 🔌 Measure and output 20mA DC current , including SIMULATE output for two-wire transmitters ⚡ Measure and output DC voltage in 100mV and 30V ranges 🔁 Loop check : supplies 24V loop power and measures the current at the same time 🌡️ Thermocouple measure and output , 8 types (R, S, B, K, E, J, T, N), ℃ and ℉, auto or manual cold-junction compensation 📈 Manual step and automatic waveform output ; V, mA and % shown together; switch-value measurement Technical Specifications Model ET72 Brand TOOLTOP DC voltage (measure / output) 100mV and 30V ranges DC current (measure / output) 20mA range (4-20mA loop) Loop power 24V, with simultaneous current measurement Thermocouple types R, S, B, K, E, J, T, N (measure and output) Cold-junction compensation Automatic and manual Output modes Manual step, automatic waveform, SIMULATE Display V, mA and percentage simultaneously What's in the Box 1 × ET72 calibrator 1 × tool bag 1 pair of test leads 1 pair of crocodile clips 1 × English user manual Packing box

Product details

The ESP32-C3 is a cost-effective, low-power Wi-Fi and Bluetooth dual-mode chip, ideal for IoT and wearable applications. Featuring a RISC-V single-core processor at 150MHz, it integrates Wi-Fi 4 and Bluetooth 5.0. Key Features: Dual-Mode Connectivity: 2.4GHz Wi-Fi and Bluetooth LE 5.0. Processor: RISC-V 32-bit single-core, 150MHz (up to 160MHz). Memory: 400KB SRAM, 384KB ROM. Low Power Consumption: Designed for efficient IoT applications. Development Support: ESP-IDF, MicroPython, Arduino, C/C++, VSCODE. Applications: IoT Devices Smart Home Sensor Networks Wearable Technology Monitoring, Video, and Photography Applications Wireless Communication Solutions Specifications: Processor: RISC-V 32-bit Single-Core Main Frequency: 150MHz (up to 160MHz) Wireless: Wi-Fi 4, Bluetooth 5.0 Memory: 400KB SRAM, 384KB ROM Power Supply: 3.3V - 6V (via 5V pin or USB) Dimensions: 27.178 x 38.227 x 1.524mm Power Supply Notes: For external power, connect the positive (+) to the 5V pin and ground (-) to GND. Do not connect USB and external power simultaneously. Package Includes: 1 x ESP32-C3 Development Board ESP32 SuperMini WiFi Bluetooth

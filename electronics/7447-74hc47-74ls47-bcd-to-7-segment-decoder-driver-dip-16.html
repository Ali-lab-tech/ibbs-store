Product details

74hc47 accepts four lines of BCD (8421) input data, generates their complements internally and decodes the data with seven AND/OR gates having open-collector outputs to drive indicator segments directly. Each segment output is guaranteed to sink 24 mA in the ON (LOW) state and withstand 15V in the OFF (HIGH) state with a maximum leakage current of 250 µA. Auxiliary inputs provided blanking, 74hc47 BCD to 7-Segment Decoder/Driver IC. Features Single BCD to 7-Segment Decoder with Open Collector Outputs Standard Pin Configuration Operating Temperature to 70°C Standard TTL Switching Voltages Superseded, Not Recommended for New Designs Note: Product Images are shown for illustrative purposes only and may differ from the actual product.

TM1638 Button Digital LED Display Module - Efficient and Versatile The TM1638 Button Digital LED Display Module is a powerful and efficient solution for adding a digital display and buttons to your microcontroller projects. With its compact design and versatile features, this module offers a convenient and cost-effective way to enhance your projects. Key Features : Efficient Design: Requires only three microcontroller IO ports for operation, saving valuable resources. Versatile Functionality: Combines LED display, buttons, and digital tube capabilities in a single module. Expandable: Supports cascading up to 6 modules for larger displays and more buttons. Easy Wiring: Simple connection to your microcontroller using VCC, GND, STB, CLK, and DIO pins. Customizable Brightness: Adjust the brightness of the LEDs and digital tubes to suit your needs. Ideal for a wide range of projects, including electronic displays, user interfaces, and interactive devices.

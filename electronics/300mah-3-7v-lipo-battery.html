Product details

A 3.7v 300mAh Lipo battery is a small, rechargeable lithium-ion polymer battery that is commonly used in drones, RC cars, and other small electronic devices. It has a nominal voltage of 3.7 volts and a capacity of 300mAh, which means that it can store 1.11 watt-hours of energy. Specifications: Voltage: 3.7v Capacity: 300mAh Energy: 1.11 Wh Weight: Varies depending on the manufacturer, but typically around 10-15 grams Dimensions: Varies depending on the manufacturer, but typically around 30x20x10mm Features: Lightweight and compact High energy density Rechargeable Long lifespan Low self-discharge rate Uses: Drones: 3.7v 300mAh Lipo batteries are commonly used in small drones, such as those used for racing or freestyle. They are a good choice for these applications because they are lightweight and have a high energy density, which means that they can provide a lot of power for their size. RC cars: 3.7v 300mAh Lipo batteries can also be used in RC cars. They are a good choice for small cars that do not require a lot of power. Other small electronic devices: 3.7v 300mAh Lipo batteries can be used in a variety of other small electronic devices, such as Bluetooth speakers, headphones, and portable game consoles. Safety: Lipo batteries are safe when used properly, but it is important to take precautions to avoid damage or fire. Here are some safety tips for using Lipo batteries: Always use a Lipo charger that is designed for Lipo batteries. Do not overcharge or over-discharge the battery. Do not store the battery in extreme temperatures. Do not puncture or damage the battery. Dispose of the battery properly. Package Include: 1 x 300mah 3.7v Lipo Battery

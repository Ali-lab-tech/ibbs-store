Product details

The Zero Delay Arcade USB Encoder allows for the connection of arcade-style joysticks and pushbuttons directly to a PC via USB. This interface board converts mechanical switch inputs into standard USB HID signals, enabling custom arcade controller builds or the conversion of legacy cabinets for use with modern emulation software. Enables plug-and-play USB connectivity for arcade controls. Compatible with standard arcade joysticks and buttons. Designed for zero-delay input response during gameplay. Functions as a standard game controller device on PC systems.

Product details

Description The SP20150R is a dual Schottky barrier rectifier diode , manufactured by SeCoS, rated for 20A forward current and 150V reverse voltage . Housed in the isolated TO-220F (fully-molded) through-hole package, it features a common-cathode dual-diode configuration, as shown by the schematic printed on the device. As a Schottky rectifier, the SP20150R offers a low forward voltage drop and fast switching characteristics compared to standard silicon diodes, translating into reduced conduction losses and higher efficiency in power conversion circuits. Its isolated TO-220F package simplifies mounting to a heatsink, since no separate insulating mica pad is typically required, while still providing solid thermal performance for demanding rectification applications. This device is well suited for high-current, low-to-medium voltage rectification tasks such as switch-mode power supply output rectification, freewheeling/flyback diode duties, and general power conversion where efficiency and reliable high-current handling are priorities. Key Specifications Type: Dual Schottky Barrier Rectifier (common cathode) Manufacturer: SeCoS Package Marking: SP20150R Package: TO-220F (isolated, 3-lead) Reverse Voltage (VRRM): 150V Forward Current (IF): 20A Low forward voltage drop, fast switching Suitable for heatsink mounting without separate insulator Common Applications Switch-mode power supply (SMPS) output rectification Freewheeling/flyback diode in switching converters General power rectification circuits DC-DC converter output rectification Package Includes 1 x SP20150R Schottky Barrier Rectifier Diode (TO-220F)

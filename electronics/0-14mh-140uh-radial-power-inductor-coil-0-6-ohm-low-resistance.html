Whether you are repairing electronics, building a DC-DC converter, or working on an Arduino/Raspberry Pi hobby project, this coil offers reliable filtering and energy storage capabilities. Its low DC resistance of 0.6 Ohm ensures minimal power loss during operation. Key Features: High-Quality Construction: Built with a ferrite drum core for high magnetic permeability. Low DCR: Features a low DC resistance of 0.6Ω for improved efficiency. Compact Size: Space-saving vertical design suitable for dense circuit boards.

Product details

The FNIRSI ERD-10 measures electromagnetic radiation around power lines, appliances, routers and phones. It reads electric field strength, magnetic field strength, RF power density and ambient temperature on a 2.4-inch colour screen. Alarm thresholds of 40V/m and 0.4µT warn you when a reading is high, so it is quick to check a bedroom, office or vehicle. A 1000mAh battery charges over Type-C.

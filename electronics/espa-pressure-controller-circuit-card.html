Product details

The ESPA Pressure Controller Circuit Card is an electronic device that is used to control the pressure of water in a pump system. The card is typically installed in a pump control panel and is designed to maintain a constant water pressure by adjusting the speed of the pump's motor. The ESPA Pressure Controller Circuit Card is equipped with a microcontroller that receives feedback from a pressure sensor installed in the system. The microcontroller processes this information and adjusts the speed of the motor to maintain the desired pressure level. The card is also equipped with a motor driver that controls the speed of the motor. The pressure sensor is installed in the water line and measures the pressure of the water as it flows through the system. The sensor sends this information to the microcontroller, which then determines whether the pressure needs to be increased or decreased. The microcontroller sends instructions to the motor driver to adjust the motor speed accordingly.

Product details

The UTC 20N60 is indeed an N-channel enhancement mode power MOSFET that utilizes UTC’s advanced technology. Let’s break down the key details: Description : The UTC 20N60 is designed to provide customers with planar stripe and DMOS technology. This technology allows for a minimum on-state resistance and superior switching performance. Applications : The 20N60 is widely used in various applications: Motor Control : It’s commonly employed in motor control circuits. Uninterruptible Power Supplies (UPS) : Used in UPS systems. DC Choppers : These are circuits that convert a fixed DC voltage to a variable DC voltage. Switch-Mode and Resonant-Mode Power Supplies : Suitable for both switch-mode and resonant-mode power supply designs. Here are some additional technical details: RDS(on) (On-State Resistance): At a gate-source voltage ((V_{GS})) of 10V, the on-state resistance is approximately 0.45 ohms. High Switching Speed : The 20N60 exhibits fast switching characteristics. If you’re working with this MOSFET, keep in mind its voltage rating (600V) and its suitability for the mentioned applications. As for the IRF 20N60, it seems like a related part, but I recommend checking the datasheet or product specifications to ensure compatibility.

Product details

The Kada 850VD+ is a hot air SMD rework station designed for precision soldering and desoldering tasks. This unit functions as a comprehensive SMD welding and BGA rework station, providing the necessary thermal control for working with surface-mount components on printed circuit boards. Key features of the Kada 850VD+ include: Integrated hot air rework capabilities for SMD components. Designed for BGA rework applications. Suitable for professional SMD welding and electronic repair tasks.

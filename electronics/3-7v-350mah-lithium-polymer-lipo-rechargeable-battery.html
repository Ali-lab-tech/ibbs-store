Product details

Description: This is a 3.7V, 350mAh Lithium Polymer (LiPo) rechargeable battery, designed to provide lightweight, high-density power for small electronic devices. LiPo cells like this one are widely favored in compact applications due to their excellent energy-to-weight ratio, stable discharge performance, and slim, flexible pouch-style construction, which allows them to fit easily into tight enclosures where a rigid cylindrical cell wouldn't work. The battery is housed in a soft, foil-wrapped pouch cell format, with a distinctive ribbed surface pattern that helps manage internal gas expansion and adds a degree of physical protection to the cell during normal handling. It outputs a nominal voltage of 3.7V, standard for a single-cell (1S) LiPo battery, and offers a capacity of 350mAh — suitable for powering small, low-current devices for a reasonable runtime between charges. Wiring is terminated in a small 2-pin JST-style connector (white housing), a common standard connector used across many hobbyist and small-device applications, allowing for simple plug-and-play connection to a compatible charger or device without needing to solder wires directly. Key Specifications: Battery Type: Lithium Polymer (LiPo), Rechargeable Nominal Voltage: 3.7V (1S configuration) Capacity: 350mAh Connector Type: 2-Pin JST Connector Cell Format: Soft Pouch Cell Applications: Mini RC drones and quadcopters, small RC toys and vehicles, wearable electronics, Bluetooth devices, small robotics projects, and other compact, lightweight electronic devices requiring a small rechargeable power source. Important Safety Note: LiPo batteries require careful handling — always charge using a charger specifically designed for LiPo cells, avoid puncturing or physically damaging the pouch, avoid over-discharging or overcharging, and store at a safe partial charge level when not in use for extended periods. Package Includes: 1 x 3.7V 350mAh LiPo Battery with 2-Pin JST Connector

Product details

UNI-T UT219P — professional AC digital power clamp meter combining a digital ammeter and a power meter in one handheld tool. Measures single-phase and 3-phase power up to 3000 kW , harmonics to the 32nd order with THD, power factor, phase angle and phase sequence, plus 3-phase unbalanced voltage. True RMS , Bluetooth 5.0 app logging, a large 75 mm jaw , IP54 sealing and a CAT III 1000V / CAT IV 600V safety rating make it suited to switchgear, distribution boards and energy audits. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with user manual and warranty.

XR2206CP – Monolithic Function Generator IC The XR2206CP is a high-quality monolithic function generator integrated circuit capable of producing high-quality sine, square, triangle, ramp, and pulse waveforms. Ideal for electronics hobbyists, engineers, and students working on signal generation, testing, and communication circuits. Key Features Generates Sine, Square, Triangle, Ramp & Pulse waveforms Wide frequency range: 0.01 Hz to 1 MHz Excellent temperature stability Low sine wave distortion: 0.5% typical Wide supply voltage range: 10V to 26V (single supply) TTL compatible FSK (Frequency Shift Keying) capability Standard DIP-16 package – easy to use on breadboards and PCBs Specifications IC Number: XR2206CP Package: DIP-16 (Dual In-line Package, 16 pins) Frequency Range: 0.01 Hz – 1 MHz Supply Voltage: 10V – 26V (Single Supply) Output Waveforms: Sine, Square, Triangle, Ramp, Pulse Sine Wave Distortion: 0.5% (typical) Operating Temperature: 0°C to +70°C Manufacturer: EXAR Corporation Applications Signal generators and function generators Frequency modulation (FM) and amplitude modulation (AM) Voltage-controlled oscillators (VCO) Tone generation and audio testing Educational and lab experiments Note: This is a genuine XR2206CP IC. Compatible with standard DIP-16 sockets.

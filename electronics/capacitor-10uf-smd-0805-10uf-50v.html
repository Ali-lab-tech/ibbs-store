Product details

A 10µF (microfarad) SMD (Surface Mount Device) capacitor with a 50V voltage rating and an 0805 package is a compact electronic component used in circuits for filtering, smoothing, and decoupling applications. Here's a breakdown of its features: Capacitance : 10µF (microfarads), which determines the amount of electrical charge the capacitor can store. Voltage Rating : 50V, the maximum voltage the capacitor can safely handle without breaking down. Package Type : 0805, a standard surface-mount package size. The dimensions of an 0805 package are approximately 0.08 inches (2.0 mm) in length and 0.05 inches (1.25 mm) in width. Construction : Typically, these capacitors are made of ceramic or tantalum, with ceramic being more common in this size and capacitance range. Applications : Used in power supply circuits, signal processing, and various other electronic devices where space is limited. Package Included: 1x Capacitor 10uf Smd 0805 10uf 50v

Description: This is a triple-lens, multi-view WiFi PTZ (Pan-Tilt-Zoom) surveillance camera, designed to deliver comprehensive, wide-area monitoring from a single mounted unit. Unlike traditional single-lens security cameras, this model integrates three separate camera lenses — two fixed wide-angle lenses positioned on the upper housing and one motorized pan-tilt lens below — allowing it to simultaneously capture multiple angles or fields of view, significantly reducing blind spots compared to conventional setups. The lower lens is mounted on a rotating PTZ mechanism, enabling remote pan-and-tilt control (typically via a companion mobile app) to actively track movement or manually adjust the viewing angle in real time, making it well-suited for monitoring larger open areas such as driveways, yards, parking lots, or warehouse exteriors. Each lens is surrounded by an array of infrared/white LED illuminators, providing supplemental night-time lighting for clear color or infrared night vision in low-light or fully dark conditions. Connectivity is provided via built-in WiFi (with dual external antennas visible on top of the unit for extended wireless range and signal stability), allowing the camera to stream and store footage over a home or business wireless network, with most cameras of this style also supporting smartphone app viewing, motion detection alerts, and cloud or microSD card storage options. The camera's housing is built with a weather-resistant metal and reinforced plastic construction, along with a wall/ceiling mounting bracket, making it suitable for both indoor and outdoor installation in varying weather conditions.

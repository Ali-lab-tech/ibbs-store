TU-941B-60W External Heating Soldering Gun Handheld external heating soldering gun with automatic solder feed, removable rack, and EU plug for 220V power. Specifications Item Type: Send Tin Model: TU-941B Power: 60W Frequency: 50/60Hz Voltage: 220V Plug: EU External heating Automatic solder feed Removable rack How To Use Press down the tin inlet, insert the solder wire from the tin inlet, pull the trigger until the tin wire is sent to the tin guide nozzle Insert the solder into the rated voltage power supply, turn on the power switch to preheat When the solder is heated enough, pull the trigger to start the welding operation When you want to withdraw the tin wire, just press the tin inlet and pull out the tin wire at the same time

Product details

UNI-T UT673A is an automotive battery tester / analyzer for 12V batteries and 12V/24V cranking and charging systems. It reports battery status, life, capacity and internal resistance across CCA, BCI, CA, MCA, JIS, DIN, IEC, EN and SAE standards (40–2000). Genuine UNI-T supplied by with manual and warranty. Key Features 12V battery test (7–16V) and 12V/24V cranking & charging system test (7–30V) Supports lead-acid, AGM (flat/spiral), GEL and EFB batteries, 3–250 Ah Standards: CCA/BCI/CA/MCA/JIS/DIN/IEC/EN/SAE 40–2000 Shows battery status, life, capacity and internal resistance Overvoltage & reverse-connection protection; poor-contact prompt Compact 264 g handheld; powered by the battery under test Technical Specifications Battery Test 12V (7–16V DC) System Test 12V/24V cranking & charging (7–30V) Battery Types Lead-acid / AGM / GEL / EFB Capacity 3–250 Ah Standards CCA/BCI/CA/MCA/JIS/DIN/IEC/EN/SAE 40–2000 Display 128×64 LCD Power From battery under test (no internal battery) What's in the Box UT673A battery tester English user manual Gift box Downloads UNI-T UT673A/UT675A Product Page & Manual Certifications: CE, FCC, RoHS | HS Code: 9030.39

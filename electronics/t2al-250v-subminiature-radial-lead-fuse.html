Product details

Description: This is a subminiature (chip-style) radial lead fuse, commonly used inside switch-mode power supplies, chargers, and small appliances for overcurrent protection. The "T" rating indicates a time-lag (slow-blow) fuse, designed to tolerate brief inrush current spikes without tripping, while still protecting the circuit against sustained overcurrent faults. Key Specifications: Parameter Value Rated Current 2A Fuse Type T (Time-lag / Slow-blow) Rated Voltage 250V AC Package Style Subminiature radial lead (through-hole) Lead Type Tin-plated wire leads Certifications/Markings: VDE, CCC, PSE, KC — indicating compliance with European, Chinese, Japanese, and Korean safety standards respectively, confirming suitability for use in globally distributed electronics and power supplies. Typical Applications: SMPS (switch-mode power supplies) Battery chargers and adapters Small household appliances Circuit boards requiring compact overcurrent protection Package Includes: 1x T2AL 250V Subminiature Fuse

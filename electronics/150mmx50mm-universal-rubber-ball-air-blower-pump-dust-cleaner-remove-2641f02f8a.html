Product details

The 150mm x 50mm Universal Rubber Ball Air Blower Pump is a professional-grade, non-contact cleaning tool designed to safely remove dust, lint, and microscopic debris from sensitive surfaces. Featuring an extra-long nozzle for precision aiming, this compact blower delivers a concentrated, powerful blast of clean air without the risks associated with chemical sprays or physical wiping. Molded from premium, high-elasticity rubber, it offers a comfortable, fatigue-free grip and instantaneous rebound, making it an essential maintenance tool for photographers, technicians, and hobbyists alike. Key Features Safe Non-Contact Cleaning: Removes abrasive dust particles without touching delicate components, eliminating the risk of scratches or static damage. Instantaneous Air Rebound: High-density rubber body compresses easily and springs back instantly to deliver continuous, high-volume air blasts. Precision Long Nozzle: The extended nozzle reaches deep into tight spaces, camera bodies, and hardware crevices with pinpoint accuracy One-Way Air Intake Valve: Built-in bottom valve prevents air from entering back through the nozzle, ensuring it only blows clean, dust-free air out. Chemical-Free & Eco-Friendly: Utilizes natural air instead of pressurized aerosol gases, making it completely non-toxic, moisture-free, and safe for internal electronics. Technical Details Total Length: 150 mm Bulb Diameter: 50 mm Material: High-elasticity natural rubber bulb with a durable ABS plastic nozzle Color: Classic Black (or custom styling depending on stock batch) Valve System: One-way directional inlet valve at the base Nozzle Type: Extended, slim-profile long nozzle Why Choose This Product? Protects Optical Coatings: Unlike microfiber cloths that can rub grit into delicate anti-reflective coatings, this blower blasts away abrasive debris entirely touch-free. Ergonomic Pocket Size: At 150mm x 50mm, it fits perfectly in the palm of your hand for maximum squeeze control and slips easily into any standard camera bag or tech pouch. Zero Moisture Residue: Pressurized air cans often spit liquid propellants that stain sensors and lenses. This manual pump guarantees a completely dry air blast every single time.

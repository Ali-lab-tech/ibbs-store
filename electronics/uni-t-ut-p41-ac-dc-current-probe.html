Product details

The UNI-T UT-P41 is a clamp-style AC/DC current probe for oscilloscopes measuring DC to 100 kHz, up to 100 A , at 10/100 mV/A, rated CAT III 600 V — for higher-current power and motor measurements on any scope. Clamp the split-core jaw around a conductor and read current as a voltage over BNC. Genuine UNI-T accessory supplied by Digilog.pk with warranty.

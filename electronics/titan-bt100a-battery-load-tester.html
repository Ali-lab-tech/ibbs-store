Product details

The TITAN BT-100A is a classic analog battery load tester for 6V and 12V lead-acid batteries in cars, motorcycles, generators and UPS banks. It puts a real load on the battery (100A on 12V, 50A on 6V) and shows on the meter whether the battery holds up, giving a good/bad verdict in about 10 seconds. It also checks state of charge, cranking voltage and charging voltage, and works with the battery in or out of the vehicle. Heavy-duty colour-coded clamps, a top-mounted rocker switch and a ventilated steel case.

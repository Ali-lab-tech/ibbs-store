Product details

The TOOLTOP ET829 puts three instruments in one handheld unit: a 2-channel 80MHz digital storage oscilloscope (250MSa/s real-time sampling), a 6000-count true RMS multimeter and a 1Hz to 5MHz signal generator . It suits field repair of inverters, SMPS, car electronics and control boards where a bench scope is not practical. It has a 2.8-inch 320×240 colour TFT screen, one-key auto measurement, waveform storage, and runs about 10 hours on built-in rechargeable 18650 cells with 15W fast charging.

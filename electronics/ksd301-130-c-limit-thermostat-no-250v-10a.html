KSD301 130°C Limit Thermostat NO 250V 10A The KSD301 130°C Limit Thermostat is a high-quality, normally open (NO) temperature switch designed for accurate thermal control. It is rated for 250V 10A , making it suitable for a variety of heating and cooling applications. Additionally, this thermostat automatically resets once the temperature drops below the set threshold, ensuring reliable temperature management. Key Features Operating temperature: 130°C (±5°C). Normally Open (NO) configuration – closes when the temperature reaches 130°C. Rated for 250V, 10A – suitable for high-power applications. Automatic reset for consistent performance. Durable metal casing for long-lasting operation. Applications The KSD301 130°C thermostat is widely used in: Electric water heaters and boilers. Ovens, dryers, and kitchen appliances. Industrial heating and cooling systems. Temperature-sensitive electronic circuits. A normally open (NO) thermostat remains open at normal temperatures and closes when it reaches the set temperature (130°C in this case), allowing current to flow. Can this thermostat be used for heating and cooling applications? Yes, it is suitable for both heating and cooling systems, depending on the circuit design and control requirements.

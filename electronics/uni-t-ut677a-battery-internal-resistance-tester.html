Product details

UNI-T UT677A is a battery internal-resistance tester using a 1 kHz AC 4-terminal method to measure internal resistance (0–3 Ω) and voltage (±7 V) simultaneously — for evaluating lead-acid, lithium and UPS battery health. Bluetooth app and USB, with PASS/WARNING/FAIL judgement. Genuine UNI-T supplied by with manual and warranty. Key Features 1 kHz AC 4-terminal method; internal resistance 0–3 Ω, resolution to 1 µΩ Simultaneous DC voltage measurement ±7 V; NTC temperature measurement 3.5" 320×240 true-colour screen; ~2 s measurement, 5 readings/second PASS / WARNING / FAIL preset judgement thresholds Bluetooth app and USB; up to 5000-set data storage 3.7V Li-ion (rechargeable); IEC 61010, AC 3700V puncture test Technical Specifications Resistance Range 0–3 Ω Resolution 1 µΩ (lowest range) Accuracy ±0.5% fs (typ.)

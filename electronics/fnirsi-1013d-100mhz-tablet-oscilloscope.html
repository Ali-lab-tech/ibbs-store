Product details

The FNIRSI 1013D is a two-channel tablet oscilloscope with 100MHz bandwidth per channel and a 1GSa/s real-time sampling rate, driven from a 7-inch 800 × 480 touchscreen. One-key AUTO and adaptive triggering bring up a usable waveform in seconds. Its 1GB storage holds 1000 screenshots and 1000 waveforms that you can export over USB. The 6000mAh battery runs about 4 hours, and the scope can be used while charging. This is the 1013D Standard kit. Key Features ⚡ 2 × 100MHz, 1GSa/s real-time sampling 🖐️ 7" 800 × 480 touchscreen with one-key AUTO 🎯 Single, normal and auto trigger with adaptive 25/50/75% levels 📐 Cursor measurements, FFT and Lissajous plus 12 automatic parameters 💾 1000 screenshots + 1000 waveforms with USB export 🔋 6000mAh battery about 4 hours per charge, Type-C 5V 2A/3A/4A Technical Specifications Channels 2 Bandwidth 100MHz per channel Sampling rate 1GSa/s Rise time <3ns Memory depth 240Kbit Time base 10ns – 50s Vertical sensitivity 50mV – 500V Input impedance 1MΩ Max test voltage 1X: 40V, 10X: 400V Voltage accuracy ±2% Frequency accuracy ±0.01% Display 7" TFT touchscreen, 800 × 480 Battery 6000mAh, about 4 h Charging Type-C 5V 2A/3A/4A What's in the Box 1 × FNIRSI 1013D tablet oscilloscope (Standard kit) Probes, charger and cable as supplied by FNIRSI

Description Protect your solar setup with the TOMZN TOB7Z-63 DC circuit breaker. It is rated for 63A and up to 1000V DC, so it suits solar PV strings, battery banks and other DC systems. The 2-pole design switches both the positive and negative lines, and the clear ON/OFF handle makes it easy to isolate the circuit for maintenance. Key Features Rated current: 63A 2 pole (2P) design Rated voltage: up to 1000V DC Breaking capacity (Icu): 6kA Standard: IEC/EN 60947-2, CE marked Green indicator windows and clear ON/OFF marking Screw terminals with marked polarity (+ / -) [Confirm: DIN rail mounting] Suitable For Solar PV panel strings and inverters Battery storage and off-grid DC systems DC distribution boxes and combiner boxes Important Safety Note This is a DC-only breaker and must not be used on AC mains. Connect the wires according to the +/- markings on the terminals, and have a qualified electrician install it. State this clearly in your listing, since wrong use is dangerous and causes returns.

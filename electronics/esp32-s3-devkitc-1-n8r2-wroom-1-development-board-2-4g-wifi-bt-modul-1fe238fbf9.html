Product details

The ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 is an entry-level development board equipped with ESP32-S3-WROOM-1, ESP32-S3-WROOM-1U, or ESP32-S3-WROOM-2, a general-purpose Wi-Fi + Bluetooth® Low Energy MCU module that integrates complete Wi-Fi and Bluetooth Low Energy functions. Most of the I/O pins on the module are broken out to the pin headers on both sides of this board for easy interfacing. Developers can either connect peripherals with jumper wires or mount ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 on a breadboard. Key Features: Model: ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1-N8R2 Connectivity: Wi-Fi 2. 4 GHz (IEEE 802. 11 b/g/n) Bluetooth 5 (LE) Processor: Dual-core Xtensa LX7 CPU, up to 240 MHz Single-precision FPU support Instructions to accelerate machine learning Ultra-low power RISC-V (RV32IMC) coprocessor Ultra-low power FSM coprocessor Memory: 512 KiB SRAM 384 KiB ROM 16 KiB RTC SRAM Connectable to external PSRAM and Flash via Quad SPI or Octal SPI Other Features: 45 programmable GPIOs 2 × 12-bit SAR ADCs, up to 20 channels USB OTG Form Factor: 44-pin, Type-C connector Additional Information: The ESP32-S3 is a low-power system-on-chip (SoC) integrating 2. 4GHz Wi-Fi and Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE 5. 0) dual-mode wireless communication. It has a complete Wi-Fi and Bluetooth LE subsystem with industry-leading low power and RF performance. Supports various low-power working states. Provides rich peripheral interfaces and unique hardware security mechanisms. Made of high-quality PCB material for durability and reliability. Intended Audience: Tech enthusiasts Developers Hobbyists Professionals in computer engineering and programming

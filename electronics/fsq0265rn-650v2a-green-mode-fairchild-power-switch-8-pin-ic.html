Product details

Description: The FSQ0265RN, marked "Q0265R" on the package with the ON Semiconductor logo, is an integrated PWM controller and high-voltage power MOSFET (SenseFET) combined in a single 8-pin DIP package, designed for offline switch-mode power supplies. It's part of the FSQ-series, an integrated Pulse-Width Modulation controller and SenseFET specifically designed for valley switching operation with minimal external components, which generally shows lower EMI and higher power conversion efficiency than a conventional hard-switched converter with a fixed switching frequency. The PWM controller includes an integrated fixed-frequency oscillator, under-voltage lockout, and Leading-Edge Blanking (LEB). Combining the controller and power switch into one die reduces total component count, board space, and BOM cost compared to a discrete MOSFET + PWM controller solution — a common approach in compact chargers, adapters, and small SMPS boards. Key Specifications Type: Integrated PWM Controller + Power MOSFET (Fairchild Power Switch) Manufacturer: onsemi (formerly Fairchild Semiconductor) Package Marking: Q0265R Package: 8-Pin DIP (PDIP-8) Drain-Source Voltage: 650V Rated Current: 2A Switching Topology: Quasi-resonant / valley switching (Green Mode) Built-in protections: Under-voltage lockout, Leading-Edge Blanking, self-protection circuitry RoHS compliant (typical for this series) Common Applications AC-DC switch-mode power adapters/chargers Offline flyback converters (typically for supplies around 16W) LCD/LED TV and monitor power boards Auxiliary/standby power supplies Package Includes 1 x onsemi FSQ0265RN Power Switch IC (8-Pin DIP)

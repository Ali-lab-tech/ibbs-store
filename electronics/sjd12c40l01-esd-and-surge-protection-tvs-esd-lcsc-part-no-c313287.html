Product details

In today’s fast-paced electronics market, protecting your circuitry from harmful voltage spikes is more important than ever. The SJD12C40L01 is engineered to safeguard sensitive components against electrostatic discharge (ESD) and surge events. With its bidirectional design and compact SOD-123S package, it meets the needs of consumer electronics, industrial equipment, and automotive systems. Key Features High Surge Capability: Handles peak pulse power dissipation up to 200W, ensuring resilience against high-energy transients. Low Clamping Voltage: With a clamping voltage of 64.5V, it effectively limits voltage overshoots, protecting downstream components. Bidirectional Protection: Shields circuits from both positive and negative ESD events, providing comprehensive circuit safety. Compact Design: Its SOD-123S package allows easy integration into tight PCB layouts. Applications The SJD12C40L01 is ideal for multiple applications, including: Consumer Electronics: Protect devices such as smartphones, tablets, and wearables. Industrial Equipment: Enhance reliability in control systems and sensor arrays. Automotive Electronics: Secure sensitive automotive circuits from unpredictable surges.

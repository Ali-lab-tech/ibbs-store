Description The BR-2/3AGCT4A is a non-rechargeable (disposable) 6V primary lithium battery pack engineered for industrial backup power applications. Featuring a stable operating voltage, high energy density, and an extremely low self-discharge rate, this battery ensures uninterrupted memory retention for PLC systems, CNC machinery, and FANUC servo drives during power outages. Key Features High Capacity & Stability: Offers a high nominal capacity of 4400mAh with steady voltage output across a wide temperature range. Low Self-Discharge: Retains charge over long periods with up to a 10-year shelf life. Plug-and-Play Design: Pre-wired with a standard black 2-pin keyed connector for direct replacement in compatible systems. Non-Rechargeable Primary Cell: Built strictly for long-term standby power—do not attempt to recharge. Technical Specifications Property Value Model / Part Number BR-2/3AGCT4A Chemistry Lithium Poly-carbonmonofluoride (BR) Nominal Voltage 6.0V Nominal Capacity 4400mAh Rechargeability Non-rechargeable (Disposable) Dimensions Approx. 33mm × 34mm × 34mm Connector Type Black 2-pin Dedicated Plug Shelf Life 10 Years Compatible Models & Replacements FANUC Part Numbers: A98L-0031-025, A06B-6114-K504 Battery Code: BR-2/3AGCT4A (4 x 2/3A cell arrangement) Applications FANUC CNC controllers, servo drives, and industrial robotics Programmable Logic Controllers (PLC) memory backup Real-Time Clock (RTC) and parameter backup in industrial automation systems Package Includes: 1x BR-23AGCT4A 6V 4400mah Disposable Lithium PLC Battery

Product details

The LM4881 Headphone Amplifier Board is a compact, ready-to-use audio amplifier module built around the LM4881 dual headphone amplifier chip. Just connect a DC power supply (3.6V to 12V), feed in an audio signal, and drive your headphones directly — no extra circuitry needed. It can also be used as a pre-amplifier stage in front of a larger power amplifier. An onboard volume knob lets you adjust the output level, and turning it fully counter-clockwise switches the board off. Audio input and output are connected through simple JST connectors, and a standard 3.5mm jack is provided for plugging in headphones directly. Key Features LM4881 dual headphone amplifier chip — up to 160mW output power Wide supply voltage: DC 3.6V–12V (onboard 5V regulator) Standard 3.5mm headphone jack output plus 2.54mm pin header output Volume adjustment potentiometer with built-in power switch JST connectors for audio input, audio output, and power Can be used as a headphone driver or as a pre-amplifier Compact board: approx. 50mm × 25mm, weight ~20g Ideal For DIY headphone amplifier and portable audio projects Adding a headphone output to DIY audio builds Pre-amplifier stage before a power amplifier board Arduino / Raspberry Pi audio output projects Students, hobbyists, and audio electronics labs Specifications Amplifier chip: LM4881 Operating voltage: DC 3.6V–12V Maximum output power: 160mW Output: 3.5mm headphone jack + 2.54mm pin header Control: volume knob with on/off switch Board size: 50mm × 25mm Weight: approx. 20g Package Includes 1 x LM4881 Headphone Amplifier Board

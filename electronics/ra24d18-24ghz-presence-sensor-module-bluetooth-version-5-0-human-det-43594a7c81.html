📝 Product Description The RA24D18 24GHz radar module (Bluetooth version) is a smart presence detection sensor that combines mmWave radar technology with Bluetooth connectivity . It is designed to detect human presence, motion, and even small movements like breathing, making it much more accurate than traditional PIR sensors. With its built-in Bluetooth module , the RA24D18 allows wireless configuration and data transmission , which makes it easier to integrate into IoT applications, smart lighting, home automation, and occupancy monitoring systems. The sensor can reliably detect through materials like glass, wood, and plastic , and is unaffected by lighting conditions, temperature changes, or weather interference.

Product details

The FNIRSI MC-40 is a budget True RMS clamp meter for electricians. Close the jaw around one conductor to read AC current up to 600A without breaking the circuit, then use the test leads for voltage, resistance, diode and continuity checks. Auto ranging, NCV, data hold, a backlit display and a flashlight cover everyday DB-box and panel work. It runs on two AAA cells and switches itself off after inactivity. Key Features ⚡ AC current to 600A through the clamp jaw 📏 AC/DC voltage to 600V True RMS, auto ranging 🔧 Resistance 400Ω – 40MΩ plus diode and continuity ⚠️ NCV detection for live wires 🔦 Backlight, flashlight and data hold Technical Specifications AC current 4A / 40A / 600A AC / DC voltage 4V / 40V / 600V Resistance 400Ω – 40MΩ Other True RMS, NCV, diode, continuity, data hold Sampling 3 readings/s Power 2 × AAA (not included) Auto power-off after inactivity Size 185 × 71 × 35 mm What's in the Box 1 × FNIRSI MC-40 clamp meter 1 × pair of test leads

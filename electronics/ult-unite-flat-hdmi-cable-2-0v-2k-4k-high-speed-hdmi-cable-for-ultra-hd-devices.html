Product details

The HDTV Ultra HD HDMI Cable 2.0V is a high-speed HDMI cable designed to support ultra-high-definition devices with exceptional video and audio quality. This cable is compatible with resolutions up to 4K (3840 x 2160 pixels) and 2K , providing vivid, sharp images and deep, dynamic colors. With HDMI 2.0V technology, it supports higher bandwidth of up to 18 Gbps , enabling smooth, lag-free connections for gaming, streaming, and other HD content. The cable features sturdy connectors and durable materials that minimize interference and ensure a stable signal transmission. It supports HDR (High Dynamic Range) , 3D , Ethernet , ARC (Audio Return Channel) , and CEC (Consumer Electronics Control) functions, making it a versatile choice for modern devices such as TVs, projectors, monitors, and gaming consoles. Whether you're watching ultra-HD movies, playing high-resolution games, or using it in a professional AV setup, this HDMI cable is engineered to deliver clear and reliable performance across all devices.

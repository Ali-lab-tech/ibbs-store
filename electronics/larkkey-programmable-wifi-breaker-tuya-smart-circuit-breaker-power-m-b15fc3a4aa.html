LARKKEY Programmable WiFi Breaker – TUYA Smart Circuit Breaker. The LARKKEY Programmable WiFi Breaker is an advanced TUYA Smart Circuit Breaker designed for intelligent power management, energy monitoring, and electrical safety. It combines reliable protection with smart automation, allowing you to control and monitor your electrical system directly from your smartphone. Ideal for smart homes, solar setups, and industrial applications, this breaker offers overcurrent, overvoltage, and undervoltage protection along with real-time power metering. With its Smart Life / Tuya App integration , you can remotely operate the breaker, schedule operations, set countdowns, and even share control access with family members. It’s compatible with Alexa, Google Home, and Yandex Alice , enabling seamless voice control for a complete smart home experience. The device supports adjustable parameters and is built to last with over 100,000 ON/OFF operations . Designed to fit standard 35mm DIN rails , the LARKKEY WiFi Circuit Breaker ensures quick installation and integration into your distribution board. Whether you’re in Lahore, Karachi, Multan, Quetta, or Islamabad , this smart breaker provides a safe, energy-efficient, and convenient solution to control your electrical systems anytime, anywhere.

Product details

18650 Lithium-Ion Cells are round high capacity cells have been mainly used in flashlight type applications but with its capability to be used as a drop-in rechargeable cell at 3.7V with a capacity of 1500mAh/2000mah. This is a great battery option for those of you who need a simple to install and replace 18650 cell. Specification of 18650 Lithium Ion Cell : Model: TR 18650 Standard voltage: 3.7V Rated Capacity:1500 to 2000mAh Diameter: approx. 18mm Height: approx. 65mm Applications: Arduino power supply, Robotics, Toys, etc. These 18650 Cells have a standard discharge current of 0.2C to a maximum of 1C and can handle about 300 charge cycles. Package includes: 1 x Lithium Ion Cell Used condition Noteio These cells are not suitable for High discharge applications like electric vehicles, motorcycles and electric cycles.

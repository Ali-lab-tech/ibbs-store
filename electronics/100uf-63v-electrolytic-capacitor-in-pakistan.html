This is a 100uf 63v Electrolytic Capacitor radial polarised good quality Electrolytic capacitor. USES of 100uf 63v Electrolytic Capacitor : Power Supplies: Improves voltage regulation and filtering in power supply circuits. Audio Circuits: Filters out unwanted noise and ensures smooth audio playback. Microcontroller Projects (e.g., Arduino, Raspberry Pi): Provides stable power for reliable microcontroller operation. LED Projects: Maintains consistent brightness and extends LED lifespan. DIY Robotics & Automation Projects (if applicable): Provides stable power for sensors and actuators in your creations. Specification of 100uf 63v Capacitor: Capacitance: 100 micro farad Maximum Voltage: 63 Volts Tolerance: ±15% Max Temperature: +85°C Capacitor Type: Radial Through Hole Electrolytic Package includes : 1 x 1pc 100uf 63v Capacitor

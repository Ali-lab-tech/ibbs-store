10cc UV Curable Solder Mask PCB Fixing Anti Corrosion Paint White 100% Brand New High-Quality 10ml UV white paint. Features with quick curing. Insulates printed circuit board against shorting and arcing. This paint is used to protect PCB traces from corrosion. It is also used for repairing PCB after soldering. How To Usage? Apply paint on your PCB. Spread it on the PCB with covering the transparent mask film. Expose it under UV light ( or sun ) for a few minutes. Wash away the extra paint by gasoline or oil base solvent.

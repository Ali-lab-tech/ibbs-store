Product details

The UNI-T LM100D is a long-range laser distance meter that measures up to 100 m with millimetre accuracy — ideal for larger sites, warehouses, facades, land plots and any job where a 50 m tool falls short. Point, press, and read the exact distance instantly on its bright 2.4-inch colour backlit (EBTN) display, single-handed. It handles far more than plain lengths: area and volume calculation, Pythagorean indirect heights, triangle area, curvature, displacement and trapezoid modes, plus add/subtract and 20-reading memory. On-screen electronic levelling and audio/voice feedback speed up angled and awkward measurements. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty and English manual.

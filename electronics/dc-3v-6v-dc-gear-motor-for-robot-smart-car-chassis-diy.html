Product details

This gear motor is perfect for your next robotics project. This gear motor is ideal for robotic car or line-tracing robot. With plastic construction and colored in bright yellow, the DC gear motor measures approx. 2.5 inch long, 0.85 inch wide and 0.7 inch thick. The wheel can be mounted on either side and the Gearmotor works well between 4V to 7V (recommended 6 Volts). At a ratio of 1:48 you can get some really good torque at 5 Volts.

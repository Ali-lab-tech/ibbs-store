Product details

Description The M22N10 is an N-channel power MOSFET housed in the industry-standard TO-220 through-hole package with a G-D-S pinout. Based on its part number, which follows the common "22N10" naming convention used across the industry (e.g., STMicroelectronics' STP22NE10L, a 100V, 22A, 0.07Ω N-Channel TO-220 STripFET Power MOSFET), this device is rated for approximately 100V drain-source voltage and 22A continuous drain current , with a low on-resistance suited for efficient switching and load control. Its TO-220 package makes it easy to mount to a heatsink for improved thermal performance in higher-power applications, and its low RDS(on) allows it to switch significant current with minimal conduction losses — useful in power supplies, DC motor control, battery management, and general-purpose switching circuits. Key Specifications Type: N-Channel Power MOSFET Package: TO-220 (3-lead, G-D-S) Drain-Source Voltage (VDS): ~100V Continuous Drain Current (ID): ~22A RDS(on): Low on-resistance for efficient switching Suitable for heatsink mounting for higher power dissipation Common Applications DC-DC converters and switch-mode power supplies DC motor drivers and speed control Battery/load switching circuits General-purpose power switching in DIY and industrial electronics Package Includes 1 x M22N10 N-Channel Power MOSFET (TO-220)

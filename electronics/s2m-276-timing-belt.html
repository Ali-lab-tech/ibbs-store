The S2M 276 timing belt likely refers to a belt following a specific profile and size configuration. Here's a breakdown of the designation: S2M : This indicates the pitch and type of the belt. S refers to the synchronous type, which means it's a toothed timing belt designed for precise movement between pulleys. 2M indicates the pitch of the belt, where the teeth are spaced 2 millimeters apart. 276 : This refers to the belt’s total length, usually in millimeters. In this case, the belt would be 276 mm long. Key Characteristics: Pitch : 2 mm (distance between the teeth). Length : 276 mm (circumference). Application : These types of belts are commonly used in small mechanical systems requiring precise, synchronized movement, such as printers, robotics, and small drives. Materials for S2M belts typically include rubber or polyurethane, with internal reinforcement (e.g., fiberglass or steel cords) for strength and longevity.

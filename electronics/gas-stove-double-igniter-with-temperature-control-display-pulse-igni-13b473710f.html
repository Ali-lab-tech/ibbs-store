The Gas Stove Double Igniter With Temperature Control Display pulse igniter is a versatile and user-friendly ignition system that can be used to light a variety of gas appliances, including gas stoves, ovens, and fireplaces. It features a double igniter design for added reliability, and it has a built-in temperature control display that allows you to monitor the temperature of your appliance while it is cooking.

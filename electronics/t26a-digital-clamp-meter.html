Product details

The T26A clamp meter reads AC current up to 400A and AC/DC voltage up to 600V, with a live-wire test that lights an LED when you touch the phase wire. Useful for electricians who want to confirm the phase before working. It also covers resistance in six ranges, diode, continuity and temperature, and runs on two AAA cells with auto power-off.

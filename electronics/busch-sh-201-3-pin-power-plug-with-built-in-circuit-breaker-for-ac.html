Product details

Description: This is a special power plug that has a safety switch (circuit breaker) built right inside it . You plug it into your wall socket like a normal plug, and then plug your AC unit or appliance into it. If there's ever an electrical fault — like a short circuit, overload, or power surge — this plug automatically cuts off the power instantly , protecting your appliance and your home wiring from damage. Think of it as a mini safety guard that sits between your wall socket and your air conditioner (or any electrical appliance), keeping everything safe. Key Features: ✅ Built-in Neon Light Indicator – Glows red when power is connected, so you know it's live ✅ Automatic Protection – Trips (turns off) instantly during a short circuit or overload ✅ 6KA Breaking Capacity – Can safely handle and cut off high fault currents ✅ Compact & Sleek Design – White and blue durable plastic body, fits comfortably in hand ✅ German Engineering – Made by Busch-Jaeger Elektro GmbH, Germany (a trusted, reputable electrical brand) ✅ ISO Certified – Meets international quality and safety standards Best Used For: Air conditioners (AC units) Refrigerators Water heaters / geysers Any high-power appliance that needs extra protection from electrical faults Package Includes: 1× Busch SH-201 Circuit Breaker Plug

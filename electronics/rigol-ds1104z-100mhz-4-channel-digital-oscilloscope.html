Product details

Rigol DS1104Z is a 4-channel, 100 MHz digital oscilloscope built on Rigol's UltraVision technology. It samples at up to 1 GSa/s, stores 12 million points per acquisition and captures up to 30,000 waveforms per second, so short glitches and rare events that slower scopes miss actually show up on screen. With four full analog channels you can watch a clock, data line, enable pin and power rail at the same time — ideal for debugging microcontroller boards, SMPS and inverter circuits, motor drives and communication buses. The DS1104Z is one of the most widely used bench oscilloscopes in university labs and repair workshops worldwide. ⏳ Pre-order item: this oscilloscope is available on pre-order. Place your order and our team will confirm the delivery timeline with you. Key Features ⚡ 4 channels, 100 MHz bandwidth — compare four signals side by side for timing and phase work. 📏 1 GSa/s real-time sampling — 1 GSa/s on one channel, 500 MSa/s on two, 250 MSa/s with three or four channels active. 🔧 12 Mpts deep memory (24 Mpts optional) — keep full sample rate over long time spans and zoom in on detail. ✅ Up to 30,000 wfms/s capture rate — catch intermittent glitches and noise quickly. 🔋 Multi-level intensity grading — see how often a waveform occurs, like an analog scope display. 🔧 Waveform record and replay — up to 60,000 frames (optional feature) for reviewing captured events.

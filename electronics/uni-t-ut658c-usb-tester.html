Product details

The UNI-T UT658C is a dual Type-A and Type-C USB tester for checking modern chargers, PD/QC power banks and USB-C cables — reads voltage, current, capacity and energy on either port. It measures 4–24 V with up to 3 A on Type-A and 5 A on Type-C , logs mAh/Wh, tests cable resistance and data-line voltage, and detects QC 2.0/4.0 fast charging. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by Digilog.pk.

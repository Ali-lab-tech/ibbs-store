Product details

The S2M timing belt (474 mm) is a type of synchronous belt designed for precise motion control in mechanical systems. Here's a detailed description of this belt: Pitch Type (S2M): The "S2M" designation refers to the tooth pitch of the belt. In this case, the pitch is 2 mm , meaning the distance from the center of one tooth to the center of the next is 2 millimeters. Belt Length (474 mm): The total belt length is 474 mm , which is the outer circumference of the belt. Tooth Profile: The S2M timing belt has a trapezoidal tooth profile , designed to mesh precisely with matching pulleys to avoid slipping. Package Included: 1x

The module is equipped with the BL602 WiFi BLE combo chip as the core processor and supports WiFi 802.11b/ g/n protocol and BLE 5.0 protocol. The BL602 chip has a low-power 32-bit RISC CPU, 276KB RAM, and a wealth of peripheral interfaces, including SDIO, SPI, UART, I2C, IR Remote, PWM, ADC, DAC, PIR and GPIO etc. It can be widely used in the Internet of Things (IoT), mobile devices, wearable electronic devices, smart homes and other fields.

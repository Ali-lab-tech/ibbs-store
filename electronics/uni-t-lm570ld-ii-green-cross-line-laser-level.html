Product details

The UNI-T LM570LD-II is a compact green cross line laser level that projects one horizontal and one vertical line for fast, accurate alignment — tiling, shelving, cabinets, dado rails, wiring and picture hanging. The bright 520 nm green beam is far easier to see indoors than a red laser. It self-levels automatically within 3°±1° and locks the pendulum for transport, holds ±3 mm at 10 m , and runs on ordinary AA batteries. A magnetic bracket and tripod thread let you mount it on brackets, tripods or steel surfaces. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty and English manual.

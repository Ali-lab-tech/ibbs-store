Product details

The 1000mW High Power Violet Light Laser heatsink is a high-performance device designed specifically for engraver printer machines. It’s developed by NEJE, who have incorporated a professional power IC and radiator to ensure long-lasting use. This design makes the carving time significantly longer and more stable compared to other lasers. The laser head emits a blue-purple light with a power of 1000mW, which allows it to operate at higher speeds and support a wider range of materials. It’s capable of engraving various items including hardwood, plastic, bamboo, rubber, leather, and even cut paper.

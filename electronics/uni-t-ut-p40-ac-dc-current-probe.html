Product details

The UNI-T UT-P40 is a clamp-style AC/DC current probe for oscilloscopes that turns current into a voltage your scope can display — DC to 100 kHz, up to 60 A , at 5/50 mV/A, rated CAT III 600 V. The split-core jaw clamps around a conductor without breaking the circuit; a BNC on a 2 m lead connects to any scope. Genuine UNI-T accessory supplied by with warranty.

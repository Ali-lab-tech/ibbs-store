Key Features: Voltage Range: Operates within a 12V to 24V DC voltage range, providing flexibility in different power systems. Speed: Capable of reaching 19500 RPM (revolutions per minute) at its optimal operating voltage, making it suitable for high-speed applications. Motor Type: 030 form factor —a small, flat, and compact motor design, typically used in space-constrained applications. Dual Axle Shaft: Features a shaft extending from both sides of the motor body, allowing for versatile configurations in mechanical assemblies. This dual shaft setup can be used for connecting gears, pulleys, or other mechanical components. Applications: Commonly used in hobby electronics, small robots, drones, model cars, and other small machinery that require fast rotational speed with minimal size. Characteristics: Body Size: Compact and lightweight for use in tight spaces. Torque: Provides low torque at high speed, suited for tasks where rapid movement is more important than heavy lifting. Durability: Designed for continuous operation at high speeds, often with metal or carbon brushes for reliable contact inside the motor. This motor is typically chosen for its compact form factor and high rotational speed, useful in applications requiring quick rotational action, like small-scale fans, propellers, or precision-driven mechanisms.

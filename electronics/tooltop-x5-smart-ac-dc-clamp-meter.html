Product details

The TOOLTOP X5 is a compact smart clamp meter with a three-line colour screen. In automatic mode it identifies what you are measuring, so electricians can check voltage and current without turning the dial for every test. It reads AC and DC current up to 400A , AC/DC voltage to 600V, resistance, capacitance up to 60mF, frequency and temperature, with NCV, live-wire detection and a flashlight. It weighs about 157 g.

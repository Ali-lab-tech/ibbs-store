Product details

The UNI-T UT262A is a non-contact phase sequence detector that checks three-phase rotation, missing phase, live power, circuit breaks and voltage presence — without exposed metal probes , so it's safer and faster for motors, drives, pumps and panel work where wrong rotation can damage equipment. Four phase lights show positive (clockwise) or negative (anti-clockwise) rotation with distinct beeping/solid tones, and it detects missing phase, live power and circuit breaks across AC 70–600 V, 40–70 Hz . Compact, light (180 g) and battery-powered. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty and English manual.

Product details

The VICTOR 6050 is a high-current clamp meter that measures AC and DC current up to 2000A through a 55 mm jaw, for large motors, generators, welding plants and main incoming cables. With test leads it also measures DC and AC voltage, resistance, capacitance and frequency, with diode test, continuity buzzer, data hold and relative-value mode on a 3999-count backlit display.

Product details

The LM2574 is a highly versatile and efficient switching voltage regulator that simplifies power supply design. With its wide input voltage range, adjustable output, and robust protection features, the LM2574 is ideal for a variety of applications. Key Features: Adjustable Output Voltage: Choose from fixed 3.3V, 5V, 12V options or adjust the output between 1.23V and 37V. High Efficiency: Minimizes power loss and improves overall system efficiency. Wide Input Voltage Range: Operates with input voltages up to 40V. Protection Features: Includes thermal shutdown and current limit protection for safety. Low Power Standby: Consumes minimal power in standby mode for energy efficiency. Easy to Use: Requires only four external components for a simple design. Upgrade your power supply designs with the LM2574 Switching Voltage Regulator and experience reliable and efficient performance.

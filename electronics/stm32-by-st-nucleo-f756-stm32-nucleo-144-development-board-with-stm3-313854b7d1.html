Product details

The STM32 Nucleo-144 Development Board is a powerful and versatile platform for exploring the STM32F7 series of microcontrollers. With its high-performance Cortex-M7 core, extensive peripherals, and user-friendly design, this board is ideal for a wide range of embedded applications. Key Features: High-Performance Core: Experience exceptional processing power with the ARM Cortex-M7 core at 216 MHz. Versatile Peripherals: Connect to a wide range of devices with Ethernet, USB, and on-board sensors. Arduino Uno Compatibility: Easily expand functionality with a vast library of Arduino shields. Integrated Debugger: Streamline development with the on-board ST-LINK/V2-1 debugger. Affordable and Flexible: Explore various STM32F7 options to suit your project needs. Upgrade your embedded development projects with the STM32 Nucleo-144 Development Board and unlock the full potential of the STM32F7 series.

3-in-1 120W Super Charger Cable — USB to Type-C / Micro USB / iOS, 1.2m Charge all your devices with just one cable. This 3-in-1 fast charging cable combines Type-C, Micro USB, and iOS (Lightning) connectors in a single USB-A cable, so you can power your phone, tablet, earbuds, and other gadgets without switching cables or carrying multiple chargers. Supporting up to 120W fast charging output, this cable dramatically cuts down charging time on compatible devices, while still being safe for lower-wattage devices thanks to smart, device-adaptive charging. Beyond charging, it also supports data transfer , letting you sync files, photos, and media between your device and computer. Built with a durable braided-style jacket and reinforced, zinc-alloy connector heads, this cable is designed to resist bending, fraying, and daily wear-and-tear far better than standard cables — making it a solid choice for travel, desks, cars, and everyday use. At 1.2 meters (about 4 feet) , it offers a comfortable length for charging from wall adapters, power banks, laptops, or car chargers. Key Features 3-in-1 connector design: Type-C + Micro USB + iOS (Lightning) in one cable 120W max fast charging output (actual speed depends on device/charger support) Supports data sync in addition to charging Length: 1.2m / ~4ft Reinforced metal connector housings for extra durability Tangle-resistant, sturdy cable jacket Compatible with a wide range of smartphones, tablets, and USB-powered accessories Compatible With Type-C devices: Android phones, tablets, laptops (charging only, not USB-PD laptop charging) Micro USB devices: older Android phones, power banks, speakers, etc. iOS devices: iPhone and iPad models with Lightning port Package Includes 1 x 3-in-1 120W Super Charger Cable (1.2m)

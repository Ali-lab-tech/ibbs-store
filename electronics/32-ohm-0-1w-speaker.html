Product details

A 32-ohm, 0.1W speaker is a small, low-power audio transducer often used in compact electronic devices for producing sound. Here’s a breakdown of its characteristics: Key Features: Impedance (32 ohms) : The 32-ohm impedance means the speaker requires less current, making it suitable for small, low-power amplifiers in devices like headphones, alarms, or mobile gadgets. Power Rating (0.1W) : Rated at 0.1 watts, this speaker is designed for low-power audio output, producing moderate volume levels, ideal for close-range listening or sound notifications. Compact Size : Typically very small (often around 15-30mm in diameter), making it perfect for integration in compact spaces such as mobile devices, toys, or embedded systems. Frequency Range : Limited frequency response, generally optimized for speech or alert sounds rather than full-range audio, as it’s intended for applications where high fidelity isn’t required. Magnetic Construction : Uses a small permanent magnet, making it lightweight and simple in design. Applications: Portable electronics (e.g., handheld games, small radios) Voice-based applications (e.g., talking toys, annunciators) Audio output for embedded systems and development boards Package Included: 1x 32 ohm 0.1w speaker

Product details

The UNI-T LM320D is a digital angle meter (inclinometer) that measures angles across a full 4×90° (360°) range at ±0.2° accuracy — for tiling, carpentry, staircases, bevels, roofing, machine setup and installation work. It is the angle-only model of the LM320 series: it has no laser . If you need laser lines, see the LM320E (2 lines) or LM320F (3 lines). It reads in degrees, %, mm/m and in/ft, offers relative/absolute measurement and angle hold, and its LED-backlit screen rotates automatically so it stays readable upside-down. A two-sided magnetic base (bottom & rear) holds it on steel hands-free, and the rechargeable 1000 mAh battery runs up to 14 hours . Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty and English manual.

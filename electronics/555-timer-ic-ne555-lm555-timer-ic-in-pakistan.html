Product details

The 555 Timer IC is a highly versatile and reliable component for creating precise timing intervals and oscillations in your electronic projects. This versatile device offers a wide range of features and applications, making it an essential tool for various designs. Key Features: Accurate Timing: Generates precise time delays and oscillations. Versatile Operation: Functions in both astable and monostable modes. Adjustable Duty Cycle: Controls the on/off ratio of the output signal. High Current Output: Can drive TTL circuits and source/sink up to 200mA. Temperature Stability: Maintains accurate timing over a wide temperature range. Easy to Use: Simple and straightforward integration into your circuits. Specifications: Supply Voltage: 4.5V to 16V Output Current: ±200mA Operating Temperature: -55°C to +125°C Package: 8-pin MSOP Applications: Timing, pulse generation, sequential timing, PWM, PPM, linear ramp generation Order Now: Upgrade your electronic projects with the 555 Timer IC.

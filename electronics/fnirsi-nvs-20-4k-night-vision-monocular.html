Product details

The FNIRSI NVS-20 is a single-lens digital night vision monocular. Its 850nm infrared illuminator lets you see up to 300m in complete darkness, and it works as a normal colour viewer in daylight. It records 4K video and up to 36MP photos to a TF card, has 6x digital zoom and a 1.54-inch IPS screen, and is IPX6 water resistant. It runs from an internal 18650 cell charged over Type-C.

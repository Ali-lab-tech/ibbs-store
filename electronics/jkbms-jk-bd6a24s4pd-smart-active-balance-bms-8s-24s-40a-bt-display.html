Product Description The JK-BD6A24S4PD is a smart lithium battery protection board (BMS) from JKBMS, designed for multi-cell lithium battery packs ranging from 8S to 24S configurations. Built for reliability in solar storage systems, e-bikes, e-scooters, and DIY battery packs, this board combines active balancing technology with smart monitoring for safer, longer-lasting battery performance. Key Specifications Feature Detail Product Type Smart BMS (Battery Management System) Battery Configuration 8S–24S Continuous Discharge Current 40A Balance Current 0.6A (Active Balancing) Communication Bluetooth / Display Brand JKBMS (JIKONG) Key Features Active Balancing Technology – Continuously redistributes energy between cells during charge and discharge (not just at full charge like passive BMS boards), improving overall pack capacity utilization and lifespan Bluetooth Connectivity – Pairs with the JKBMS mobile app for real-time monitoring of voltage, temperature, current, and cell balancing status Wide Voltage Compatibility – Supports 8S to 24S lithium-ion, LiFePO4, or similar chemistries, making it versatile for various pack sizes 40A Continuous Discharge – Suitable for mid-power applications like e-bikes, solar energy storage, and portable power stations Temperature Monitoring Port – Built-in temperature sensor connector for added thermal protection QR Code Setup – Scan directly on the board to download the companion app and user manual Compact, Robust Design – Reinforced mounting brackets and clearly labeled terminals (P-, B-, B+, B-) for straightforward installation Typical Applications E-bike and e-scooter battery packs Solar energy storage systems DIY power banks and battery packs Golf carts and light electric vehicles Backup power systems Package Includes 1x BMS Board 1x Sampling Line 1x NTC Temperature Sensor Cable 1x Switch Wire

Product details

The UNI-T LM570LD-I is a green cross line laser level that projects one horizontal and one vertical line (1V1H) for quick, accurate layout — tiling, partitions, cabinets, wiring and levelling. The 510 nm green beam is bright and easy to follow indoors. It self-levels automatically within 3°±1° and locks for transport, holds ±3 mm at 10 m , and reaches 20 m (up to ~40 m with a detector). Wide 110° fan lines and a tripod thread make it easy to set up on brackets, walls or a tripod. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty and English manual.

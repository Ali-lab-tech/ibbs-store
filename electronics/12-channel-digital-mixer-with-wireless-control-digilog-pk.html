This digital mixer offers unmatched flexibility and control, allowing you to connect up to 10 control devices simultaneously. Furthermore, its integrated Wi-Fi and full cross-platform compatibility across iOS, Android, Windows, Mac OS, and Linux provide seamless connectivity. Experience the power of Harman’s legendary signal processing from dbx®, DigiTech®, and Lexicon® with fully recallable mic preamps and complete wireless control over sound dynamics. Key Features: 4-band Parametric EQ, High-Pass Filter, Compressor, De-esser, and Noise Gate on inputs 31-band Graphic EQ, Noise Gate, and Compressor on all outputs Real-Time Frequency Analyzer (RTA) on inputs and outputs 3 Lexicon FX Processors: Reverb, Delay, and Chorus Subgroups, Mute Groups, View Groups, and Show/Snapshot Recall Password-protected access and security lockout With its compact dimensions (Height - 85mm, Width - 177mm, Length - 320mm) and lightweight design (2.29kg), this digital mixer is the perfect solution for live sound engineers and musicians seeking professional-grade audio performance and wireless control.

Mini Router UPS Board 52W V3 – New Version The Mini Router UPS Board 52W V3 is a reliable backup power solution designed for routers, ONU devices, and small network equipment. With support for 18650 or Lithium Polymer batteries, it ensures uninterrupted connectivity during power outages. This upgraded version comes with a built-in 10A BMS, multiple voltage outputs, and advanced safety features to protect your devices and batteries. Key Features Supports 3.7V 18650 or Lithium Polymer battery Input: 12V 3A (requires high-quality adapter) Output: 12V 1A + 12V 1A + 9V 1A + 5V 2A (max 52W) Operating temperature: 40°C – 55°C Built-in 10A Battery Management System (BMS) Ultra-fast power switching – no router restart 4-step battery level indicator Constant current charging: 1A to 0.5A Auto full-charge cut-off function Safety & Protection Overload protection Overvoltage protection Low voltage protection Short-circuit protection Package Includes: 1x power bank kit only without switch and box

Product details

The UNI-T LM120PRO is a professional-grade laser distance meter that measures up to 120 m with a tight ±2 mm accuracy — a longer reach and better precision than entry models, made for construction, surveying, real-estate and facilities work. Point, press, and read the exact distance instantly on its bright 2.4-inch backlit (EBTN) display. It handles far more than plain lengths: area and volume calculation, Pythagorean indirect heights, triangle area, plus add/subtract and 20-reading memory. Auto horizontal/vertical levelling with a built-in electronic leveler speeds up angled measurements, a tripod mount allows fixed setups, and it recharges over USB . Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty and English manual.

Product details

This is IRF540N MOSFET N Channel Transistor Hexfet Power Mosfet type. This is a general purpose and widely used product among hobbyist and professionals. The IRF540N is an N-Channel Power MOSFET transistor that can handle high currents and voltages. It is a popular choice for use in a variety of applications, including motor drivers, LED drivers, inverters, and converters. The IRF540N has a maximum voltage rating of 100V. It also has a low threshold voltage of 4V, which means it can be easily driven by low voltages like 5V. This makes it a popular choice for use with Arduino and other microcontrollers for logic switching. Specifications : Drain-Source Volt (Vds): 100V Drain-Gate Volt (Vdg): 100V Gate-Source Volt (Vgs): 20V Drain Current (Id): 30A Power Dissipation (Ptot): 150W Type: N-Channel Applications High Current, High Speed Switching Solenoid and Relay Drivers DC-DC & DC-AC Converter Automotive Environment (Injection, ABS, AIR-BAG, Lamp Drivers etc.) Switching high power devices Controlling the speed of motors Dimming or flashing LEDs High speed switching applications Converters or inverter circuits Package Includes: 1 x IRF540N - IRF540 N-Channel MOSFET Transistor TO220 package

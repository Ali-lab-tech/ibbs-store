Product details

The UNI-T UT58C digital multimeter adds frequency measurement to the UT58 platform, making it the pick of the series for anyone working on drives, switching circuits and signal-carrying lines. It measures AC/DC voltage to 1000V, current to 20A, resistance to 20MΩ, capacitance, frequency to 20kHz and contact temperature to 1000°C with the supplied K-type probe. With a 2000-count display, transistor hFE socket and audible continuity, the UT58C suits electricians, electronics repair benches and technical institutes that need frequency alongside the standard measurement set. Key Features Frequency to 20kHz ⚡ — 2kHz and 20kHz ranges at ±(1.5%+5), the key upgrade over the UT58B. 2000-count display ✅ — 3.5-digit reading refreshed 2–3 times per second. AC/DC voltage to 1000V 🔧 — DC ±(0.5%+1), AC ±(0.8%+3). 20A current measurement ⚡ — AC and DC ranges to 20A for load and motor work. K-type temperature to 1000°C 📏 — point-contact probe included, ±(1%+3). Capacitance and resistance 🔧 — to 100µF and 20MΩ for component-level testing. Transistor (hFE) measurement ✅ — multi-purpose socket for transistor gain checks. Diode, continuity buzzer and data hold 🔋 — with auto power-off and overload protection.

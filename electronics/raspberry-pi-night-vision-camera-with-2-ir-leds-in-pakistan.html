The noir camera night vision was designed by British Raspberry Pi Foundation and manufactured panel with a 5MP (2592 × 1944 pixels) OmniVision 5647 fixed-focus sensor module which serial interface (CSI) through a dedicated 15-pin MIPI camera and 15-pin ribbon cable to connect to the Raspberry Pi. CSI bus can be extremely high data transfer rates, and specifically for transferring pixel data to the BCM2835 processor.

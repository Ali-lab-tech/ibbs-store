Product details

UNI-T UTS3036B — The UNI-T UTS3036B Performance-Series Spectrum Analyzer covers 9 kHz – 3.6 GHz with a 10.1-inch capacitive multi-touch display, a 1 Hz resolution-bandwidth floor, and up to 40,001 sweep points. It targets the sub-3.6 GHz bench measurement workload — communications module verification, IoT and wireless development, harmonic and spurious testing — with the option to add a tracking generator in the field when your work demands scalar transmission measurements. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty.

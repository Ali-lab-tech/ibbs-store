Product details

The UNI-T UT658LOAD is a USB tester with a built-in electronic load — it not only reads voltage, current, capacity and energy, but can also draw a selectable load (0.5 / 1 / 2 / 3 A, up to 60 W) to properly test the real output of chargers and power banks. It measures 4–24 V and 0.05–3 A on Type-A, logs mAh/Wh, tests cable resistance and data-line voltage, and records up to 99 hours. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by .

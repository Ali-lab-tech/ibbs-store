Product details

Description: The OB2365 is a highly integrated current mode PWM control IC designed for offline flyback converter applications. It's engineered for high efficiency, low standby power consumption, and cost-effective power supply design — commonly used in chargers, adapters, and small switch-mode power supplies (SMPS). Specifications: Parameter Detail Part Number OB2365PCP Manufacturer On-Bright Electronics Package SOIC-8 (8-pin SMD) Control Topology Current Mode PWM, Flyback Max Switching Frequency (QR Mode) 77 kHz Fixed Frequency (CCM Mode, Low Line/Heavy Load) 65 kHz Operating Modes QR (Quasi-Resonant), PFM, CCM, Extended Burst Mode Typical Application Offline flyback power adapters, USB chargers, SMPS Key Features: Adaptive multi-mode operation for high efficiency across the full load range QR mode with valley switching at normal/high-line loads to reduce switching losses PFM mode with valley switching at light loads for improved conversion efficiency Extended Burst Mode at very light loads to minimize standby power consumption Fixed-frequency CCM mode at heavy loads under low-line input voltage Low VCC startup and operating current for reliable power-on behavior and low standby power design Technical Notes: Mode switching (QR → PFM → Burst) is handled internally based on load conditions, requiring minimal external control circuitry. Well-suited for cost-sensitive designs needing good no-load/standby efficiency (e.g. energy-star compliant chargers). As a current mode controller, it requires an external current-sense element (typically a sense resistor) and works in conjunction with an external high-voltage MOSFET (unlike integrated "power switch" variants such as OB2365T, which include the MOSFET on-chip). Common Applications: Phone/tablet USB wall chargers Small AC-DC power adapters Standby/auxiliary power supplies in appliances Charger repair and replacement (as a diagnostic/replacement part when a charger fails) Package Includes: 1x OB2365PCP On-Bright PWM Power Switch IC SOIC-8

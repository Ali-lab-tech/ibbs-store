Product details

The UNI-T UT387S wall scanner is the top of the UT387 line: a behind-wall detector that finds ferrous metal to 100 mm, non-ferrous metal to 80 mm, wood to 38 mm and live AC wiring to 40 mm — then adds a 40 m laser distance meter and a wood-moisture reading from 6% to 40% in the same tool. Results appear on a 2.4-inch colour screen with voice broadcast, so you get a spoken confirmation while your eyes stay on the wall. For renovation work, carpentry and site surveys, it replaces a stud finder, a laser measure and a moisture meter in one rechargeable 161 g unit. Key Features Detects metal to 100 mm 🔧 — ferrous to 100 mm and non-ferrous to 80 mm behind the surface. Live AC wire detection to 40 mm ⚡ — find cables before you drill. Wood detection to 38 mm 📏 — locate studs and joists behind plasterboard. 40 m laser distance meter 📏 — built-in laser measurement, no second tool needed. Wood moisture 6%~40% ✅ — check timber moisture on the same device. 2.4-inch colour screen ⚡ — clear graphical readout of what is behind the wall. Voice broadcast ✅ — spoken results so you can keep your eyes on the scan. Li-ion rechargeable 🔋 — with auto power-off and multiple measurement modes.

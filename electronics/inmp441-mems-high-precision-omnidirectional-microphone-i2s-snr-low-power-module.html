INMP441 MEMS High Precision Omnidirectional Microphone I2S SNR Low Power Module The INMP441 is a high-performance, low-power, digital-output, omnidirectional MEMS microphone module designed for advanced audio and voice recognition applications. It integrates a MEMS sensor, signal conditioning, an analog-to-digital converter (ADC), anti-aliasing filters, power management, and a standard 24-bit I²S interface, allowing seamless connection to digital processors without the need for an audio codec. Key Features: Digital I²S interface with high-precision 24-bit data High Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR) of 61 dBA Sensitivity: -26 dBFS Flat frequency response from 60 Hz to 15 kHz Low current consumption of 1.4 mA High Power Supply Rejection (PSR) of -75 dBFS Compact surface-mount package: 4.72 × 3.76 × 1 mm RoHS/WEEE compliant Related Products Czn-15e Omnidirectional Microphone Wi-fi Antenna Learn More about MEMS Microphones For a deeper understanding of MEMS microphone technology and its applications, explore resources like Wikipedia on MEMS Microphones . Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) What is the operating voltage range for the INMP441 module? The INMP441 operates within a voltage range of 1.8 V to 3.3 V. Can this module be used with microcontrollers that have an I²S interface? Yes, the INMP441 is designed to interface directly with microcontrollers and digital signal processors (DSPs) that support the I²S protocol. Is the INMP441 suitable for far-field voice recognition applications? While the INMP441 has a high SNR and flat frequency response, it is optimized for near-field applications.

The SD4938 is a high-performance current mode PWM controller IC with integrated 150V MOSFET designed for switch-mode power supplies (SMPS) in Pakistan. Featuring a built-in high-voltage start-up circuit and burst mode operation at light loads, this power management IC delivers efficient AC-DC conversion for chargers, adapters, and low-power switching power supplies. The SD4938 provides excellent line and load regulation with minimal external components for cost-effective SMPS designs.

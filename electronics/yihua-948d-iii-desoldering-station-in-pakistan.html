Product details

The YIHUA 948D III is a 2-in-1 desoldering station that combines a vacuum desoldering gun and a 110W soldering iron in one PID-controlled unit. It removes through-hole components by melting the solder joint and sucking the solder out of the hole with a built-in vacuum pump (0.05 MPa), so parts lift out cleanly without damaging pads. In Pakistan it is often called a desoldering machine or solder sucker machine. It is built for repair benches that pull DIP ICs, connectors, relays, transformers and power components from UPS, inverter, TV and industrial control boards, then solder the replacement part with the same station. Key Features 🔧 Two tools, one station – desoldering gun and soldering iron work side by side, so you remove and re-solder without changing equipment. ⚡ Built-in vacuum pump – 0.05 MPa suction clears through-holes in one pull, no hand solder sucker or wick needed. 🌡️ PID temperature control – the station corrects temperature every 20 ms for fast heat-up and quick recovery on heavy joints. 🔥 110W EVO soldering iron – 200–480 °C range for everything from fine work to large ground planes. 📏 Three desoldering nozzles – 0.8 mm, 1.2 mm and 2.0 mm to match lead and pad size. 🔋 10-minute sleep and auto shutdown – drops temperature when idle to extend element and nozzle life; sleep mode can be turned off. ✅ Digital temperature calibration – correct the reading against a tip thermometer. 🧹 Easy cleaning – removable solder residue chamber with replaceable ceramic filters and filter springs.

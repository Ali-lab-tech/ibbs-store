Product details

The FNIRSI SWM-20 is a handheld dual-pulse spot welder for building 18650 and lithium battery packs. It delivers up to 1200A, with a first pulse to break through oxide and a second pulse for a clean weld. It welds pure nickel strip up to 0.25mm and nickel-plated steel up to 0.4mm, with fine control of preheat, pulse and interval on a 2.4-inch screen. Its 5000mAh cells give about 1500 welds per charge, and it doubles as a 5V 2.1A power bank. Key Features ⚡ 1200A peak dual-pulse welding 🔩 Nickel strip to 0.25mm nickel-plated steel to 0.4mm 🎚️ 10,000+ adjustment levels and 4 presets 🔋 5000mAh, ~1500 welds per charge plus 5V 2.1A power-bank output 🛡️ 8 safety protections and detachable copper pens Technical Specifications Max welding current 1200A Welding thickness 0.1 – 0.5mm Pure nickel max 0.25mm Nickel-plated steel max 0.4mm Steel max 0.5mm Battery 5000mAh Charging / output 5V 2.1A Type-C / USB Display 2.4" TFT What's in the Box 1 × SWM-20 host 1 × welding pen set Replacement tips 1 × USB-C cable 1 × nickel strip 1 × user manual

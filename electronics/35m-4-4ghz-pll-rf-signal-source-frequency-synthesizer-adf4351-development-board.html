Product details

The Synthesizer ADF4351 Development Board is a versatile tool for precise frequency control in various electronic applications. Equipped with the ADF4351 frequency synthesizer chip, this board offers exceptional performance and flexibility. Key Features: Wide Frequency Range: Generate frequencies from 35 MHz to 4400 MHz. Versatile Output: Choose between fundamental wave (sine wave) and fundamental frequency division (square wave). Programmable Output Power: Adjust output power in 3 dB steps from -4 dBm to +5 dBm. External Reference: Connect an external reference source for added flexibility. Internal Reference: Development Board Utilize the built-in 25 MHz active oscillator. SPI Serial Interface: Easily control and configure the board. LED Indicators: Monitor lock status and power supply. Applications: Wireless Communication: Transmit and receive data at various frequencies. Test Equipment: Generate precise frequencies for testing and calibration. Radio Frequency (RF) Systems: Design and implement RF-based applications. Choose the Synthesizer ADF4351 Development Board for precise frequency control and versatile applications.

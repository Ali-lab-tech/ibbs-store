Product details

Description The module allows to control the actuators consuming the current up to 10 A with microcontroller's ports or with any implementation set (including Arduino ). For proper operation, it is sufficient to connect the power of the relay and the digital control signal. Leads are the standard strip goldpin with pitch 2.54 mm which enable to connect via connecting cables. Specification The supply voltage VCC: 5 V It is enabled by the high state: min 3.3 V Relay SRD-05VDC-SL-C Coil voltage: 5 V The maximum voltage of contacts: 250 VAC at 10 A / 125 VAC at 10 A Maximum current: 10 A / 15 A Tile sizes 34 x 26 mm Diameter of mounting holes: 3 mm

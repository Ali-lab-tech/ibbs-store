Product details

The Arduino Uno R3 with Cable is a microcontroller board based on the ATmega328 ( datasheet ). It has 14 digital input/output pins (of which 6 can be used as PWM outputs); 6 analog inputs, a 16 MHz ceramic resonator, a USB connection, a power jack, an ICSP header, and a reset button. It contains everything needed to support the microcontroller; simply connect it to a computer with a USB cable or power it with an AC-to-DC adapter or battery to get started. Power Specifications: Operating Voltage (VDC): 5 Input Voltage (Recommended): 7-12V Input Voltage (Limit): 6-20V Analog I/O Pins: 6 Digital I/O Pins: 14 (of which 6 provide PWM output) PWM Digital I/O Pins: 6 DC Current per I/O Pin (mA): 40 DC Current for 3.3V Pin (mA): 50 Clock Speed: 16 MHz SRAM: 2 KB EEPROM: 1 KB (ATmega328) Flash Memory: 32 KB On Board LEDs: On/Off, L (PIN 13), TX, RX Dimensions (mm): 75 x 54 x 12 Weight (gm): 28 (without cable), 54 (with cable) Shipping Weight: 0.08 kg Shipping Dimensions: 9 × 6 × 4 cm EAGLE FILES: DOWNLOAD SCHEMATIC: DOWNLOAD Package Includes : 1 x Arduino Uno R3 1 x USB Cable

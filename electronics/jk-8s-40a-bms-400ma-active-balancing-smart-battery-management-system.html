Product details

40A JK 4S–8S Smart BMS with 400mA Active Balancing & Bluetooth The JK-BD4A8S4P is a smart battery management system with 40A continuous current , 0.4A (400mA) active balancing and Bluetooth connectivity. The supplied unit's label specifies 4–8 cells in series and 60A instantaneous/peak current . The 60A figure is not the continuous current rating. Key Features Supports 4S–8S configurations as printed on the supplied unit. 40A continuous current; manufacturer's catalogue lists 40A charge/discharge. 400mA active cell balancing. Bluetooth monitoring and settings through the JK/Jikong mobile app. Bluetooth module marked ZX-D30 on the photographed unit. Specifications Brand JK / Jikong Model JK-BD4A8S4P Series configuration 4S–8S, as labelled Continuous current 40A Peak current 60A, labelled instantaneous current; duration not specified Balancing method Active balancing Balancing current 0.4A / 400mA Connectivity Bluetooth LiFePO4 Pack Voltages With 3.2V nominal LiFePO4 cells, nominal pack voltages are 12.8V (4S), 16V (5S), 19.2V (6S), 22.4V (7S) and 25.6V (8S).

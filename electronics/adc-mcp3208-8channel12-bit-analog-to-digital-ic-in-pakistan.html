Product details

Introducing the MCP3208, your solution to the limitations of a 10-bit ADC. With its 12-bit analog to digital converter, this IC revolutionizes the way input values are sampled, ensuring a more precise and comprehensive representation. Operating on the fundamental principle of sampling inputs at discrete intervals, the MCP3208 surpasses the Nyquist rate, capturing signals with exceptional accuracy. This means that all frequencies within the signal can be reconstructed efficiently, offering enhanced and valuable parameters for your projects. Designed for seamless integration, this 12-bit serial ADC is compatible with a range of platforms including Atmel, Arduino, Pic, and Raspberry Pi. Its specifications speak volumes: Resolution: 12-bit Number of ADCs: 1 Digital Interface Type: Serial Mounting Type: Through Hole Sampling Rate: 100ksps Architecture: SAR Typical Single Supply Voltage: 5V Package Type: PDIP Pin Count: 16 Input Signal Type: Pseudo Differential, Single Ended Input Type: Voltage Integral Nonlinearity Error: ±2LSB Maximum Operating Temperature: +125°C Minimum Operating Temperature: -40°C Dimensions: 19.05 x 6.35 x 3.3mm (Length x Width x Height) Experience the precision and versatility of the MCP3208, unlocking a new level of accuracy and performance for your projects across a spectrum of applications.

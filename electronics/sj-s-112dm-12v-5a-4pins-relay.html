Product details

The SJ-S-112DM 12V 5A 4-Pin Relay is a compact, high-performance PCB-mounted electromagnetic relay designed for reliable switching in electronic circuits. Operating with a 12V DC coil , this relay features a SPST (1 Form A) Normally Open (NO) contact configuration and supports switching loads up to 5A , making it ideal for automation, control systems, power supplies, home appliances, and DIY electronics projects. Its through-hole design allows for easy PCB installation, while the durable construction ensures long service life and dependable performance in a wide range of switching applications.

Product details

The SMD SN74HC47N is a BCD-to-seven-segment decoder/driver IC in DIP-16 through-hole package, designed for driving common-anode seven-segment LED displays in Pakistan. This CMOS logic integrated circuit converts 4-bit binary-coded decimal (BCD) input into seven-segment display outputs with active-low open-collector outputs, providing reliable display driving for digital counters, clocks, and instrumentation. Perfect for educational projects and commercial display applications.

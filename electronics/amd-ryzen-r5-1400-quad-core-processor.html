AMD Ryzen R5 1400 Quad-Core Processor Overview The AMD Ryzen R5 1400 is a budget-friendly quad-core processor designed for mainstream users looking for decent performance in gaming and multitasking. With a base clock of 3.2 GHz and turbo boost up to 3.4 GHz, this CPU fits well with AM4 motherboards and delivers multi-threaded capabilities at an accessible price point. Key Benefits: Multi-threaded performance capable of competing with higher-priced Intel Core i5-7400 CPUs in specific workloads. Unlocked multiplier for overclocking enthusiasts, with stable overclocks up to 3.8 GHz achievable with adequate cooling. Bundled with AMD's Wraith Stealth cooler, optimized for low noise and sufficient cooling under a 65W TDP. Support for AM4 socket ensures compatibility with a wide range of motherboards, enabling future upgrades within the Ryzen family.

Product details

Description: The MOC3063 is an optically isolated triac driver optocoupler. It combines an LED optically coupled to a phototriac to provide isolation between input and output circuits while allowing control of AC loads through a triac. The MOC3063 is commonly used in applications requiring isolated control of AC loads such as motors, lamps, and heaters, providing robust isolation and reliable switching performance. Key Features: Optically isolated triac driver High isolation voltage: 5000Vrms (min) Low trigger current (I_FT): 5mA (max) Peak off-state voltage (VDRM): 600V Zero-crossing circuitry for low EMI (Electromagnetic Interference) Low input control current requirement Compact DIP-6 package Technical Specifications: Isolation Voltage: 5000Vrms (min) Peak Repetitive Off-State Voltage (VDRM): 600V Trigger Current (I_FT): 5mA (max) Operating Temperature Range: -40°C to +85°C Package Type: 6-pin DIP Applications: AC motor controls Lighting controls Solid-state relays (SSRs) HVAC (Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning) systems Home appliances Industrial automation

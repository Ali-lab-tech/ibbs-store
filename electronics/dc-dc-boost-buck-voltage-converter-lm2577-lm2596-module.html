Key Features: Voltage Conversion: Supports both step-up (boost) and step-down (buck) voltage conversion, offering flexibility in voltage regulation. High Efficiency: Utilizes the LM2577 and LM2596 voltage regulator ICs, known for their high efficiency and reliable performance. Adjustable Output: Provides adjustable output voltage through onboard potentiometers, allowing you to fine-tune the desired voltage level. Wide Input Range: Works with a wide input voltage range, making it suitable for various power sources. Protection Features: Incorporates protection features such as overcurrent and over-temperature protection for added reliability. Specifications: Input Voltage Range: Typically supports input voltages from 3V to 40V. Output Voltage Range: Adjustable output voltage typically ranges from 1.25V to 25V. Output Current: Depending on the specific model, it can handle output currents from a few hundred milliamperes to several amperes. Efficiency: The LM2577 and LM2596 regulators offer high efficiency, reducing heat generation. Dimensions: Compact form factor for easy integration into electronic projects. Applications: Suitable for various electronic applications, including power supply design, battery charging, and voltage regulation. Protections: Includes overcurrent protection and over-temperature protection to safeguard the module and connected devices.

Product details

The KSD301 is a snap-action, bimetallic thermostat switch designed for temperature control and thermal protection in electronic and industrial equipment. This normally closed (NC) switch maintains a closed circuit until the temperature reaches 175°C, at which point it opens to interrupt the current, automatically resetting once the temperature drops to 160°C. Action temperature: 175°C Reset temperature: 160°C Contact configuration: Normally Closed (NC) Rating: 10A 250VAC Terminals: 6.3mm quick connect Function: Auto reset

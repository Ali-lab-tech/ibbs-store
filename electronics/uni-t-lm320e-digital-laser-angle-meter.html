Product details

The UNI-T LM320E is a digital laser angle meter (inclinometer) measuring angles across 4×90° (360°) at ±0.2° and projecting 2 laser lines to transfer the angle onto a surface — it adds laser marking to the angle-only LM320D for faster layout in tiling, carpentry, staircases and installation. It reads in degrees, %, mm/m and in/ft, has relative/absolute measurement, angle hold and screen rotation, and a two-sided magnetic base . A rechargeable 1000 mAh battery gives up to 8 hours. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty and English manual.

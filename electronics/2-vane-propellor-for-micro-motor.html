2 vane propeller for micro motor Vane Count: It has two vanes or blades. These vanes are the flat, wing-like structures that rotate and generate thrust when the motor spins the propeller . Material: The propeller is typically made from lightweight materials such as plastic or lightweight metals like aluminum. This choice of materials ensures it doesn't add excessive weight to the toy. Application : It’s designed for use with small toy motors. These motors are usually low-power and are commonly used in various small-scale, battery-operated toys, including miniature boats, cars, and aircraft. Function: The main function of this propeller is to convert the rotational motion generated by the small toy motor into thrust,which propels the toy forward(in the case of boats or aircraft) or provides movement (in the case of toy cars). Design: The two-vane design simplifies the propeller and is suitable for low-power applications. The shape and pitch of the vanes are carefully designed to optimize thrust while minimizing air resistance and power consumption. Safety: Since this propeller is designed for use in toys, safety considerations are essential. It's typically designed to be safe for handling by children and includes features to prevent injury, such as rounded edges and safety guards. Mounting : The propeller is usually designed to be easily attached to the motor shaft. It may include a hub or adapter that fits the motor shaft snugly and securely.

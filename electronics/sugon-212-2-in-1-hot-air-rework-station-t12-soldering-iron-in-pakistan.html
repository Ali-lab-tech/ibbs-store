Product details

Sugon 212 2 in 1 Hot Air Rework Station with T12 Soldering Iron The Sugon 212 pairs a hot air rework gun with a genuine T12 soldering iron in a single 760W unit that runs straight off 220VAC 50Hz mains, so it plugs into a Pakistani socket with no converter. The T12 side is the reason to pick this over a generic 2 in 1 station: the heating element and the tip are one cartridge sitting millimetres from the joint, so the iron reaches working temperature in seconds and recovers almost instantly on a large ground pad instead of stalling the way a long-barrel iron does. Both tools run from the same panel, each with its own power switch, and the display carries four readouts at once - hot air temperature and airflow on the left, iron set temperature and actual temperature on the right - so you can see the tip catching up rather than guessing. Three memory channels, marked CH1, CH2 and CH3 for each tool, hold the profiles you actually use. A brushless fan drives the hot air, which is noticeably quieter than the brushed diaphragm pumps in budget stations and lasts considerably longer. Automatic sleep, adjustable from 0 to 30 minutes, drops the iron when you set it in the stand so tips are not cooked through a tea break.

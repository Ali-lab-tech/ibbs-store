Description: This 50W 2.5Ω Aluminum Housed Wirewound Power Resistor is designed for applications that require reliable resistance and effective heat dissipation. It features a durable aluminum housing with a finned design that helps transfer heat away from the resistor during operation. The resistor is suitable for use in power supplies, LED circuits, motor controllers, audio equipment, battery systems, electronic loads, voltage regulation circuits, and other high-power electronic applications . Its wirewound construction provides stable performance, while the aluminum body offers improved thermal management compared with standard resistors. The built-in mounting tabs make it easy to securely install the resistor onto a metal chassis, panel, or other suitable heat-dissipating surface .

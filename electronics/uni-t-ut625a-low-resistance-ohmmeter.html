Product details

UNI-T UT625A is a 100A low-resistance ohmmeter (micro-ohmmeter) for measuring the contact resistance of high-current connections — circuit-breaker contacts, bus-bar joints and switchgear — using a 4-wire Kelvin method with resolution to 0.1 µΩ. Genuine UNI-T supplied by Digilog.pk with manual and warranty.

What It Is The 2SES01FA is cross-referenced to the TX4056C, a linear Li-ion/Li-polymer battery charge management IC. This chip is a single-cell lithium battery charger controller , commonly used in USB-powered charging circuits. Key Specifications Calibrated for standard Li-ion/Li-polymer cells with a 4.2V float (full-charge) voltage Operating temperature range: -40°C to +85°C Not AEC-Q100 automotive qualified — intended for consumer electronics, not automotive use Package: SOP-8 surface-mount What It's Used For This IC manages the charging process for a single-cell lithium battery — regulating charge current and voltage safely from a USB or DC input, commonly found in: Power banks Bluetooth speakers/earbuds Small rechargeable gadgets Battery-powered consumer electronics Package Includes 1 × 2SES01FA Li-Ion Charger IC (SOP-8)

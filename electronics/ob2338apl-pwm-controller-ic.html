The OB2338APL PWM Controller IC is a highly efficient current-mode PWM controller designed for off-line power supply applications. It integrates advanced protection features and optimized performance to deliver stable and energy-efficient operation. This IC is widely used in Switch Mode Power Supplies (SMPS) , LED drivers, and power adapter circuits.

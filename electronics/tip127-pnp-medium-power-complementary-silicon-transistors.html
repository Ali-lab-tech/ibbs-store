TIP127 PNP Power Darlington Transistor - High Current Amplification The TIP127 is a high-performance PNP Darlington pair transistor designed for driving high current loads and achieving high amplification. With its robust construction, exceptional power handling capabilities, and easy-to-use TO-220 package, the TIP127 is a versatile choice for various electronic projects. Key Features: High Current Handling: Capable of handling up to 5A of continuous current. High Voltage Capability: Withstands voltages up to 100V. High Gain: Delivers high current amplification (hFE of 1000) for efficient switching and signal amplification. Compact TO-220 Package: Convenient and space-saving design. Ideal for applications such as: Driving High Current Devices: Control motors, LEDs, and other high current loads. Audio Amplifiers: Achieve high gain for powerful audio amplification. General-Purpose Switching: Control circuits efficiently with high current capacity. Inverter and Rectifier Circuits: Manage power conversion tasks effectively.

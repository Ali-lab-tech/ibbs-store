Product details

Description: The MC7805ABT is a 3-terminal positive fixed voltage regulator manufactured by ON Semiconductor, designed to deliver a stable, regulated 5V output. It's a monolithic integrated circuit built for local, on-card power regulation, making it a staple component in countless electronic circuits and power supply designs. This regulator features built-in protection circuitry including internal current limiting, thermal shutdown, and safe-operating-area compensation, which makes it essentially indestructible under most overload conditions. It requires only a couple of external capacitors to function, making it extremely simple to implement in a design. The device comes in the TO-220 package , a widely-used through-hole format with a metal tab for heatsink mounting, ideal for applications where moderate heat dissipation is expected during voltage regulation. Key Specifications: Manufacturer: ON Semiconductor Part Number: MC7805ABT Type: Fixed Positive Voltage Regulator Output Voltage: 5V (±2% typical) Output Current: Up to 1.0A Package Type: TO-220 (3-Pin, Through Hole) Protection Features: Internal current limiting, thermal shutdown, safe-area compensation Max Operating Junction Temp: 125°C–150°C (grade dependent) Applications: Local on-board voltage regulation, power supplies, embedded systems, microcontroller/logic circuit power stages, and general-purpose 5V DC power regulation. Package Includes: 1 x ON Semiconductor MC7805ABT Voltage Regulator (TO-220)

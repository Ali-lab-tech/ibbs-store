Product details

The UNI-T LM585LD is a professional 8-line green laser level that projects multiple level and plumb lines for alignment across floors, walls and ceilings — ideal for tiling, partitions, drop ceilings and installation work. Its bright Osram 520 nm green diodes self-level automatically within ±3° and reach up to 30 m. It ships with a dedicated tripod mount and lifting platform for precise height setting, and a rechargeable 5200 mAh Li-ion battery. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by Digilog.pk with warranty and English manual.

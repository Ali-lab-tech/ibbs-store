Product details

The UNI-T LM576LD is a top-tier 4D green laser level projecting 16 lines across four 360° planes — the most complete level-and-plumb coverage in the range, built for tiling, flooring, ceilings, framing and full fit-outs. Its bright Osram 520 nm green diodes self-level automatically within ±3° and reach up to 30 m, with an outdoor pulse mode for receiver use and a bundled remote control . A rechargeable 5200 mAh Li-ion battery powers a full working day. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by Digilog.pk with warranty and English manual.

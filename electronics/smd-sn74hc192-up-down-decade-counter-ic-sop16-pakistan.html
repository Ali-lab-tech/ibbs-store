Product details

The SMD SN74HC192 is a synchronous presettable up/down decade (BCD) counter IC in SOP-16 (SOIC-16) surface-mount package, designed for bidirectional counting and timing applications in Pakistan. This CMOS logic integrated circuit features synchronous counting operation with separate up and down clock inputs, parallel load capability, and terminal count outputs, providing reliable counting from 0 to 9 in either direction for digital clocks, frequency dividers, and control systems. The SMD package enables compact PCB layouts and automated assembly for professional electronics manufacturing and prototyping.

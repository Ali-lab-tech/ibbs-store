AMD Ryzen 5 5600 CPU - 6 Cores, 4.4 GHz Boost | AM4 Socket Build your ultimate gaming rig or powerful productivity workstation with the AMD Ryzen 5 5600 processor. Engineered with the groundbreaking Zen 3 architecture, this CPU offers a perfect balance of high-speed performance and exceptional value. With 6 cores and 12 processing threads, it effortlessly handles everything from competitive gaming and content creation to everyday multitasking. Key Features & Benefits: Zen 3 Architecture: Get a massive leap in instructions per cycle (IPC) performance for faster, smoother, and more responsive computing. 6 Cores & 12 Threads: Ideal for modern games and demanding applications, allowing you to game, stream, and run background tasks without slowdown. High Clock Speeds: A base clock of 3.5 GHz can boost up to 4.4 GHz to provide the speed needed for intense gaming sessions and heavy workloads. Unlocked for Overclocking: Maximize your performance potential by manually overclocking the processor to push your system even further. PCIe 4.0 Support: Enjoy the fastest speeds available for your NVMe SSDs and modern graphics cards, reducing load times and improving in-game texture streaming. AM4 Platform Compatibility: Offers a wide range of compatible motherboard options (B550, X570, etc.), protecting your investment and simplifying your build. Package Includes: 1 x AMD Ryzen 5 5600 Processor (Tray)

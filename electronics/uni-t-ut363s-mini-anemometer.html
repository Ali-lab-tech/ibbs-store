Product details

The UNI-T UT363S is a pocket anemometer (wind speed meter) that measures air velocity from 0 to 30 m/s along with ambient temperature and Beaufort wind scale — great for HVAC, sailing, drone flying, shooting, outdoor sports and weather monitoring. Its built-in vane reads wind speed at ±(5% + 0.5) accuracy with 0.1 m/s resolution, shows the Beaufort scale (0–12) and temperature, and adds MAX/AVG and overload indication in a light, one-hand body. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty and English manual.

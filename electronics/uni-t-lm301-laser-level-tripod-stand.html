Product details

The UNI-T LM301 tripod stand is an aluminium-alloy support for laser levels and cross-line lasers, with adjustable-height legs extending to 1.5 m. Getting the laser off the floor and onto a stable platform at working height is what makes line and layout work repeatable. It suits UNI-T cross-line and multi-line laser levels as well as other measuring instruments that use a standard tripod mount. Please contact us on WhatsApp or call to confirm mounting-thread compatibility with your specific laser before ordering. Key Features Aluminium alloy construction 🔧 — light to carry between rooms but stable in use. Height adjustable to 1.5 m 📏 — set the laser at working height rather than floor level.

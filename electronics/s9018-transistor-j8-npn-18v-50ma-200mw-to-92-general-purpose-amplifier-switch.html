S9018 Transistor J8 NPN 18V 50mA 200mW TO-92 The S9018 is a general-purpose NPN bipolar junction transistor designed for low-power amplification and switching circuits. With a maximum collector current of 50mA and power dissipation up to 200mW, it is ideal for audio frequency signal amplification, RF signal processing, oscillators, timers, and small load control in DIY electronics and consumer projects. Packaged in a compact TO-92 through-hole case, it fits standard breadboards and PCBs. Basic wiring: connect the base through a current-limiting resistor, the collector to the load, and the emitter to ground.

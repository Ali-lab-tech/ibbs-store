Product details

The TL494 PWM Controller IC is a highly versatile and powerful component designed for precision power supply control. With its comprehensive features and flexible operation, the TL494 is ideal for a wide range of applications. Key Features: Fixed Frequency PWM Control: Ensures accurate and stable output signals. Versatile Functionality: Offers multiple functions, including error amplifiers, an oscillator, and a dead-time comparator. Wide Operating Voltage Range: Compatible with various power supply voltages. Flexible Output Options: Supports both push-pull and single-ended output configurations. High Efficiency: Optimizes power usage for efficient operation. Applications: Power Supplies: Control DC-to-DC converters for various electronic devices. Solar Inverters: Efficiently manage power flow in solar systems. Washing Machines: Provide precise control for motor and heating elements. And more! The TL494's versatility makes it suitable for numerous applications. Upgrade your power supply designs with the TL494 PWM Controller IC.

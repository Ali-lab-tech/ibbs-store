Product details

The NI-MH 1000mAh battery is a rechargeable Nickel-Metal Hydride (NiMH) battery with a capacity of milliampere-hours (mAh). It is designed for use in a wide range of electronic devices and offers the advantages of rechargeability and a high capacity for extended use. Features: Rechargeable: NiMH batteries can be recharged hundreds of times, reducing waste and saving money in the long run. High Capacity: With a capacity of 1000mAh, these batteries provide ample power for devices, allowing them to run for extended periods between charges. Versatility: Suitable for various devices such as digital cameras, cordless phones, remote controls, flashlights, and more. Environmental Friendliness: Rechargeable batteries are more eco-friendly compared to single-use disposable batteries. No Memory Effect: NiMH batteries do not suffer from memory effect, so they can be recharged at any time without needing to be fully depleted first. Stable Voltage: They deliver a consistent voltage throughout their discharge cycle, ensuring reliable performance in your devices. Specifications: Chemistry: Nickel-Metal Hydride (NiMH) Capacity: 1000 milliampere-hours (mAh) Voltage: Typically 1.2 volts per cell (1.2V) Size: Available in standard sizes like AA and AAA, as well as other sizes depending on the manufacturer. Cycle Life: Can be recharged hundreds to over a thousand times, depending on usage and quality. Self-Discharge Rate: NiMH batteries have a moderate self-discharge rate, meaning they hold their charge well over time compared to some other battery types. Compatibility: Designed for a wide range of consumer electronics and devices that accept NiMH batteries. These batteries are a practical choice for individuals looking for a reliable and eco-friendly power source for their portable electronic devices, providing both high capacity and the convenience of reusability. Package include : 1x NI-MH 1000mAh battery

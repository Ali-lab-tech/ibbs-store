Product details

The UNI-T UT206 is a full-size 1000A digital clamp meter for electricians and maintenance technicians who need to measure heavy AC load current without breaking the circuit. Its wide jaw clamps around cables up to 40 mm and reads current, voltage, resistance, frequency and temperature on a 3999-count auto-ranging display. Beyond current, it doubles as a capable meter — AC/DC voltage, resistance, diode and continuity, plus a temperature probe input — making it a one-tool solution for panel work, motor and HVAC servicing. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty.

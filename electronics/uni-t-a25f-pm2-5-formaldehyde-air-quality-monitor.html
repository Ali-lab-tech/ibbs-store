Product details

The UNI-T A25F is an indoor air quality monitor that measures both PM2.5 fine dust (0–500 µg/m³) and formaldehyde (HCHO) (0–2 mg/m³), alongside temperature and humidity — for homes, offices, new furniture/renovations, schools and clinics. A clear display with a red-backlight alarm warns when pollution rises, so you know when to ventilate or run an air purifier. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty and English manual. Key Features 🌫️ PM2.5 0 ~ 500 µg/m³ measurement 🧪 Formaldehyde (HCHO) 0 ~ 2 mg/m³ measurement 🌡️ Ambient temperature & humidity (5~99% RH) 🚨 Red-backlight alarm on high pollution 🔋 Auto power-off; low-battery indication Technical Specifications PM2.5 range 0 ~ 500 µg/m³ PM2.5 accuracy ±(10 µg/m³+10 dgt) low, ±(10%+30/50 dgt) high Formaldehyde range 0 ~ 2 mg/m³ Formaldehyde accuracy ±0.06 mg/m³ (0~0.6), ±10% (0.6~2) Temperature accuracy ±1 °C / ±1.8 °F Humidity range 5% ~ 99% RH Alarm Red backlight on high level Power Rechargeable / battery, auto power-off What's in the Box A25F air quality monitor USB charging cable English user manual Downloads UNI-T A25F Product Page Certifications: CE | HS Code: 9027.89

Product details

The FNIRSI FD10 is a plug-and-play OBD2 adapter that pairs with an Android or iOS phone. Plug it into the car's OBD port to read diagnostic trouble codes, see what the check-engine light means and clear the code after a repair. It supports CAN-BUS and K-line vehicles, works from 8 – 28V and draws under 1W.

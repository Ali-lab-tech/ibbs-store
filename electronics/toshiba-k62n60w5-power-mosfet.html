Description This is another high-power electronic switch component (MOSFET), similar to the other Toshiba parts shown earlier — used to control and switch high voltage/current in power electronics circuits. Key Details Brand: Toshiba (genuine) Part Number: K62N60W5 Type: N-Channel Power MOSFET Package: TO-247 (3-pin, with metal mounting tab for heatsink) Voltage Rating: 600V (indicated by "60" in the part number) Current Rating: Around 62A (indicated by "62" in the part number) Use: Switching power supplies, inverters, welding machines, induction cooktops, motor drives, UPS systems Why It's Useful This MOSFET is built to handle high power efficiently while staying reliable and running cooler than older-style switches. It's a common replacement/upgrade part used by repair technicians for power boards in inverter ACs, induction stoves, welding inverters, and industrial power supplies.

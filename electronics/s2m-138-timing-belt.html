Product details

The S2M 138 timing belt refers to a specific type of synchronous (toothed) belt with the following features: S2M : Indicates the pitch and type of belt. S stands for synchronous, meaning it is a toothed belt used for precise, non-slip motion between pulleys. 2M specifies the pitch, meaning the distance between the teeth is 2 millimeters . 138 : Refers to the total length of the belt in millimeters. In this case, the belt has a 138 mm circumference. Key Characteristics: Pitch : 2 mm (distance between the teeth). Length : 138 mm (total circumference). Applications : These belts are used in precise, small mechanical systems like 3D printers, small robotics, medical devices, and other compact equipment that requires synchronized movement.

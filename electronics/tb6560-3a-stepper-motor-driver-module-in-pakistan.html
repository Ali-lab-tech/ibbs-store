Product details

The TB6560 3A Stepper Motor Driver Board is a high-performance solution for controlling stepper motors in various applications, including CNC routers, 3D printers, and automation systems. With its powerful output, adjustable settings, and robust features, the TB6560 offers precise and reliable motor control. Key Features: Wide Voltage Range: Operates from 10V to 35V DC for compatibility with different power supplies. Powerful Output: Delivers a rated output of ±3A, suitable for a wide range of stepper motors. Micro-Stepping Support: Provides various micro-stepping modes for smoother and quieter operation. Advanced Protection Circuits: Features low-voltage shutdown, over-heating protection, and over-current protection for safe and reliable performance. High-Speed Optocoupler Isolation: Minimizes signal noise and ensures reliable performance. Adjustable Current: Customize the current output to match your specific motor requirements. Large Heat Sink: Efficiently dissipates heat for long-lasting operation. Applications: CNC Routers: Control stepper motors for precise and accurate cutting and engraving. 3D Printers: Drive stepper motors for smooth and reliable filament extrusion. Industrial Automation: Automate tasks requiring precise motor control. Upgrade your projects with the TB6560 3A Stepper Motor Driver and experience powerful, reliable, and user-friendly control.

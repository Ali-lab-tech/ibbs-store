Product details

The FNIRSI LPM-10A is a cable tracker and network tester for LAN installers and IT technicians. It traces network and telephone cables, with an anti-interference mode for busy patch panels, and finds a cable's port by flashing its link light. It measures cable length to 100m (±3.2m), checks wiremap and RJ45 crimping, detects PoE type and voltage, and includes NCV and a flashlight. A 1500mAh battery runs up to 10 hours. Key Features 🔎 Cable tracing normal and anti-interference modes 📏 Length measurement ±3.2m over 100m 🔌 Wiremap and crimp test open, short and crossover ⚡ PoE detection mode, standard and voltage 💡 Port flashing, NCV and flashlight Technical Specifications Functions Tracing, length, wiremap, crimp, PoE, port flash, NCV Display 2.4" colour Battery 1500mAh, up to 10 h Charging Type-C 5V 1A Size ≈152 × 68 × 31 mm What's in the Box 1 × LPM-10A unit 1 × Type-C cable 1 × alligator clip lead 1 × headphone cable 1 × RJ45 to RJ11 cable 1 × RJ45 cable 1 × storage bag

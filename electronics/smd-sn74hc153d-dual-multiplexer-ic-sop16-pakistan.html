Product details

The SMD SN74HC153D is a dual 4-to-1 multiplexer (data selector) IC in SOP-16 (SOIC-16) surface-mount package, designed for data routing and selection applications in Pakistan. This CMOS logic integrated circuit contains two independent 4-input multiplexers with common select inputs, providing reliable data selection for digital systems, signal routing, and control logic. The SMD package enables compact PCB layouts and automated assembly for professional electronics manufacturing and prototyping.

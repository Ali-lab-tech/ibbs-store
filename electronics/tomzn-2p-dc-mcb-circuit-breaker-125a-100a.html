Product details

The Tomzn 2P DC MCB is designed to protect your solar systems, batteries, and inverters. It effectively guards against overloads and short circuits. This ensures your DC circuits operate safely and efficiently. Moreover, it is ideal for off-grid and hybrid setups, offering long-term protection for your systems. Key Features: High Amp Rating (125A/100A): Perfect for large battery banks and energy storage systems. DC Circuit Ready: Specifically designed for solar and energy storage setups. 2-Pole Protection: Provides full circuit isolation, enhancing safety. Fast Tripping: Quickly disconnects power to prevent damage from faults. Durable Construction: Made from fire-resistant materials for added protection. Easy Installation: Fits standard DIN rails, making setup quick and easy. Versatile Use: Ideal for off-grid, RVs, and energy backup systems.

Product details

Description: This is a low-loss RF (radio frequency) coaxial extension cable fitted with N-Type male connectors on both ends, designed to extend the connection between an antenna and a wireless device (such as a router, repeater, signal booster, or base station) with minimal signal degradation over long cable runs. At 40 feet (12 meters) in length, it's built for installations where the antenna needs to be mounted a significant distance from the receiver/transmitter equipment — such as on a rooftop, tower, mast, or building exterior — while the connected equipment remains indoors. The cable uses a low-loss coaxial construction, meaning it's specifically engineered with a design (typically thicker center conductor and dielectric, along with quality shielding) that minimizes signal attenuation (loss) as RF energy travels along its length — an important factor since standard thin coaxial cable can lose substantial signal strength over longer distances, potentially degrading wireless performance significantly. This makes it well suited for applications like cellular signal boosters, WiFi antenna extensions, GPS antenna runs, and other RF systems where preserving signal integrity over distance is critical. Both ends terminate in N-Type male connectors, a widely used, weatherproof-capable RF connector standard known for its reliable, low-loss, threaded coupling — commonly found on outdoor antennas, signal boosters, and professional-grade wireless equipment. The cable's black outer jacket provides durability and UV/weather resistance suitable for both indoor routing and semi-exposed outdoor installation (verify full outdoor/UV rating with the specific cable type used, e.g., LMR-400 equivalent, if permanent outdoor exposure is intended). Key Specifications: Cable Length: 40 feet (12 meters) Connector Type (Both Ends): N-Type Male Cable Type: Low-Loss Coaxial (RF) Impedance: 50 Ohm (standard for N-Type low-loss RF cable) Jacket Color: Black Typical Use Case: Long-distance antenna-to-device RF signal extension Applications: Cellular signal boosters/repeaters, WiFi antenna extensions, GPS/GNSS antenna installations, ham/amateur radio antenna runs, IoT and M2M wireless deployments, and any RF system requiring a low-loss connection between an antenna and equipment over an extended distance. Package Includes: 1 x N-Type Male to Male Low Loss RF Coaxial Cable, 40ft (12m)

Product details

An 8-Cell Dual USB Power Bank Case with LCD Display is a portable power bank casing designed to house up to 8 individual rechargeable battery cells (usually 18650 lithium-ion batteries). Here's a detailed description: Exterior Design : The case is typically made from durable plastic or aluminum, providing a robust and lightweight shell. It may come in various colors, such as black, white, or metallic shades, and has a sleek, rectangular shape with rounded edges for easy handling. Dual USB Ports : The case features two USB output ports, allowing it to charge two devices simultaneously. These ports are usually located at the top or side of the case and are marked to indicate different output currents (e.g., 1A and 2.1A) to support various charging speeds. LCD Display : The front of the case includes a small LCD screen, usually positioned near the USB ports. This display provides real-time information such as the remaining battery capacity (in percentage), charging status, and output current. The display might also show indicators for each battery cell's status. Battery Compartment : The interior of the case is designed to hold up to 8 individual battery cells. It includes clearly marked slots for each cell, with metal contacts to ensure proper connection. The case often includes a secure snap-on or screw-on cover to keep the batteries in place. Additional Features : Some models may include a built-in LED flashlight, power button, and protective features such as overcharge protection, short circuit protection, and temperature control to ensure safety and longevity of both the power bank and the devices being charged. Input Port : The case also includes a micro-USB or USB-C input port for recharging the power bank itself, usually located next to the USB output ports.

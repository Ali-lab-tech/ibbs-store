13.56mhz Rfid Tag Key Ring is used for sensing and identifying tagged people and objects for access control, automation, and a whole range of different applications. This blank, smooth, and the mildly flexible RFID tag is ready for your logo (or hand-drawn scribble). Specifications and Features of 13.56mhz Rfid Tag Key Ring : The Response frequency: 13.56 Mhz Size: 40.5 mm * 32 mm * 4.2 mm Material: ABS.

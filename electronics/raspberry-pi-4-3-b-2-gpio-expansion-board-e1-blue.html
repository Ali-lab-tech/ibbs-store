Product details

The board provides the connection of the three modules to each of the GPIO pins of the Raspberry Pi. It has 3 rows of goldpin connectors , 40 pieces in each (pitch of 2.54 mm). Each row consisting of double straps, it's the same parallel connected connectors GPIO of minicomputer. Module is compatible with Raspberry Pi, version 3, 2 and B+ . The subject of the sale is a shield, minicomputer can be purchased separately. Pins Board has 120 outputs in the form of goldpin strips, 3 for each of the GPIO on the Raspberry Pi .

Product details

The UNI-T LM571R-I is a professional red cross line laser level (1V1H) with wide 130° horizontal / 150° vertical fan lines and a tight ±2 mm at 10 m accuracy — a dependable, great-value tool for tiling, drywall, ceilings, cabinetry and general layout. The 635 nm red beam self-levels automatically within 3°±1°, reaches 20–30 m (over 30 m with a detector), and runs for more than 12 hours on ordinary AA batteries. A tripod mounting hole allows fixed setups. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty and English manual.

AU75N65HP IGBT - High-Performance Transistor for Power Applications The AU75N65HP IGBT is a high-efficiency insulated-gate bipolar transistor designed for demanding power applications. It offers a voltage rating of 650V and continuous current handling of 75A, making it ideal for motor drives, industrial inverters, and renewable energy systems. Benefits of AU75N65HP IGBT High Voltage Capacity: Supports up to 650V, ensuring reliable operation in power systems. Fast Switching Speed: Reduces power loss, improving system efficiency. High Current Handling: Capable of continuous 75A operation for demanding applications. Thermal Stability: Maintains optimal performance across extreme temperatures. Compact TO-247 Package: Easy integration into various designs.

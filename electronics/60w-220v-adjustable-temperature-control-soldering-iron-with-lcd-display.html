Product details

Description: This is a 60W electric soldering iron featuring a built-in digital LCD display, allowing precise, real-time monitoring and adjustment of the tip temperature. Unlike basic fixed-temperature irons, this model lets users dial in the exact heat setting needed for the job, using the two control buttons positioned just below the display — making it easy to fine-tune temperature for different soldering tasks, from delicate electronic components to larger, heat-demanding joints. The iron uses an internal ceramic heating element to deliver fast heat-up and stable, consistent temperature at the tip, helping produce clean and reliable solder joints while minimizing the risk of overheating sensitive components. A white ergonomic handle houses the electronics and display, while a soft, textured black grip section provides a comfortable, non-slip, heat-insulated hold during use. The flexible coiled cable at the top reduces strain on the wiring and helps prevent cable fatigue over repeated use. Operating on a standard 220V AC input, this soldering iron is designed for home, hobbyist, and light professional use, offering an affordable way to get adjustable-temperature soldering without stepping up to a full bench soldering station. Key Specifications: Power Rating: 60W Input Voltage: 220V AC Display: Digital LCD (temperature readout) Temperature Control: Adjustable, via 2 push-buttons Heating Element: Ceramic Heater Handle Material: Heat-insulating silicone/rubber grip with plastic display housing Cable Type: Flexible coiled cord Features: Digital LCD temperature display Adjustable temperature control via onboard buttons Fast heat-up ceramic heating element Comfortable, heat-resistant ergonomic grip Compact, lightweight design for everyday use Applications: Electronics assembly and repair, PCB soldering, hobbyist and DIY electronics projects, small appliance repair, jewelry and craft soldering, and general precision soldering tasks.

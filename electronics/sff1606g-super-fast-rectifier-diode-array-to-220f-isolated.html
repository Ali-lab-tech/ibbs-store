Product details

Description: The SFF1606G is a high-current, glass-passivated super-fast rectifier diode, manufactured by Taiwan Semiconductor Corporation (TSC). Internally, the device is built as a diode array with a common-cathode configuration — two fast-recovery diode elements sharing a common cathode connection, useful for center-tap rectification in power supply designs. Rated for 400V and capable of handling 16A, this rectifier is built for high-efficiency, low-loss power conversion. It offers a low forward voltage drop, fast switching (recovery times as low as ~35ns), high surge current capability, and high overall reliability — making it well suited for demanding industrial, automotive, and power supply applications. The part is UL Recognized, RoHS compliant, and halogen-free, with AEC-Q101 automotive-qualified variants also available. The device comes in a TO-220F (isolated tab) package , allowing it to be mounted directly to a heatsink without a separate insulating pad — helpful for compact designs with multiple high-current rectifiers mounted close together. Key Specifications: Manufacturer: Taiwan Semiconductor Corporation (TSC) Part Number: SFF1606G Type: Super Fast Rectifier Diode Array (Common Cathode) Reverse Voltage (VRRM): 400V Forward Current (IF): 16A Forward Voltage Drop (VF): ~1.3V @ 16A Reverse Recovery Time (trr): ~35ns Package Type: TO-220F / ITO-220AB (3-Pin, Isolated, Through Hole) Certifications: UL Recognized, RoHS Compliant, Halogen-Free Applications: Switch-mode power supplies (SMPS), freewheeling and flyback rectification, industrial power conversion, automotive electronics, and general high-current, high-speed rectification circuits. Package Includes: 1 x SFF1606G Super Fast Rectifier Diode (TO-220F)

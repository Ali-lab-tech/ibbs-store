Product details

It has a magnetic base , so it easily sticks to any metal surface (like inside a metal enclosure, a car body, or a metal cabinet) — no drilling or mounting hardware needed. A 3-meter (about 10 ft) cable gives you flexibility to place the antenna somewhere with better signal (like near a window or on top of an enclosure) while your device sits elsewhere. It connects using a standard SMA male plug , which fits most GSM/GPRS modules, routers, and radio modules that use an SMA female port.

Product details

The UNI-T LM100G is a long-range green-laser distance meter that measures up to 100 m with millimetre accuracy. Its 515 nm green beam is far brighter to the eye than a standard red laser, so the dot stays visible in bright daylight and over long outdoor distances — ideal for construction, surveying, real-estate and large-site work. One press reads the distance on the large 2.4-inch reverse (EBTN) display; from there it calculates area, volume, Pythagorean and second-Pythagorean indirect heights and triangle area , adds and subtracts results, and uses a built-in angle sensor with auto horizontal/vertical levelling. It runs on 3 rechargeable AAA cells and is rated for a 1 m drop. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by Digilog.pk with warranty and English manual.

Product details

The Daly S Series Smart BMS is a cutting-edge battery management system designed to handle the demands of high-current lithium batteries. This powerful solution is ideal for applications requiring large discharge currents, such as electric vehicles, energy storage systems, and industrial equipment. Key Features: Wide Voltage Range: Supports 3S to 24S configurations, accommodating various battery pack voltages. High Current Capacity: Handles up to 250A, 300A, 400A, or 500A continuous discharge current, ensuring efficient power delivery. Advanced Protection: Safeguards against overcharge, over-discharge, short circuit, and temperature extremes. Smart Communication: Features CAN, RS485, and dual UART communication interfaces for seamless integration. User-Friendly Interface: Comes with a self-developed app and PC software for easy monitoring and control. Cloud Connectivity: Integrates with the DALY Cloud platform for remote monitoring and data analysis. Applications: Electric Vehicles: Electric cars, trucks, buses, and motorcycles Energy Storage Systems: Home energy storage, grid-scale energy storage Industrial Equipment: Heavy machinery, robotics, and automation systems Marine Applications: Electric boats and yachts Benefits: Enhanced Battery Life: Protects batteries from harmful conditions, extending their lifespan. Improved Safety: Prevents potential hazards such as fires and explosions. Optimized Performance: Ensures efficient charging and discharging, maximizing battery capacity. Remote Monitoring: Track battery health and usage data from anywhere. Scalability: Easily adapts to different battery configurations and applications. Choose the Daly S Series Smart BMS for reliable, efficient, and safe battery management in high-current applications.

Product details

This HX-4S-F100A based 100A BMS board also called ( battery management system ). HX-4S-F100A is a protection board for li-ion 18650 cells. There are 10 MOSFET'S, strong heat-dissipating aluminum sheet installed on it, and the continuous current limit is 100A. It is suitable for 4 series lithium battery pack. This Battery Protection board have overcharge protection, over-discharge protection, over current protection, short circuit protection, temperature protection. Features of HX-4S-B100A: Operating voltage 14.8v to 16.8v Overcharge protection Over-discharge protection Overheat protection Overload protection charging current 10A Package Contents Of HX-4S-B100A: 1 x4S 100A Balance 3.7V 18650 Battery Cell PCB BMS Protection Board 1 x cable

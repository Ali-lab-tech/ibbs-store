Product details

Description The SN74LS11N is a Triple 3-Input Positive-AND Gate IC, manufactured by Texas Instruments and housed in a standard 14-pin DIP through-hole package. It belongs to the TTL Low-Power Schottky (LS) logic family and contains three independent 3-input AND gates in a single chip, making it a compact solution when a design needs several AND logic functions without dedicating a separate IC to each one. Each of the three gates operates independently, accepting three logic inputs and producing a HIGH output only when all three inputs are HIGH — standard AND gate behavior. This makes the SN74LS11N a fundamental building block for combinational logic design, control circuits, and any digital system requiring multi-input AND conditions, such as enable/interlock logic, address decoding conditions, and general Boolean logic implementation. Being part of the well-established 7400 series, it's widely used in educational electronics, hobbyist digital logic projects, and legacy TTL-based circuit designs. Key Specifications Function: Triple 3-input AND gate (3 independent gates per chip) Logic Family: TTL, Low Power Schottky (LS) Package: 14-pin PDIP (through-hole) Output Current: 400µA (HIGH) / 8mA (LOW) Max Propagation Delay: 20ns @ 5V, 15pF load Quiescent Current (Max): 3.6mA Logic Levels: 0.8V (LOW) / 2V (HIGH) Supply Voltage: 4.75V–5.25V Operating Temperature: 0°C to 70°C Manufacturer: Texas Instruments Common Applications Combinational logic and Boolean AND functions Enable/interlock and control logic circuits Address decoding conditions in digital systems Educational and hobbyist digital electronics projects Package Includes 1 x SN74LS11N IC (14-pin DIP)

Product details

The MD7821 is a digital grain moisture meter for checking rice, wheat and corn during storage and processing. Push the 368 mm two-pin probe into the grain and read moisture and temperature on a 4-digit LCD. It runs on four AA cells and comes with a protective carrying case for field use. Key Features 🌾 For rice, wheat, corn and similar grains 💧 Moisture and grain temperature together 📏 368 mm two-pin probe on a cable 🖥️ 4-digit 10 mm LCD , 0.1 resolution 🧳 Protective carrying case included Technical Specifications Model MD7821 Grains Rice, corn, wheat, etc. Temperature range −10 to 55℃ Resolution 0.1 Display 4 digits, 10 mm LCD Power 4 × 1.5V AA Size 182 × 68 × 38 mm Probe 368 × 44 × 25 mm Weight 420 g without batteries What's in the Box 1 × MD7821 moisture meter 1 × probe 1 × protective carrying case 1 × manual

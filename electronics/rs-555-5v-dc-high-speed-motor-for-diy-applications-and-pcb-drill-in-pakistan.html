Product details

The RS-555 Motor is a Multipurpose Brushed DC Motor that is commonly used in PCB Drill machines and other drilling and cutting purposes. Electric brushed Motor RS-555 DC Motor 5V offers a high starting torque, which makes it perfect for use in applications that are designed to move heavier loads and industrial automation applications. Advantages of a brushed DC motor include low initial cost, high reliability, and simple control of motor speed. The brushed DC electric motor generates torque directly from the DC power supplied to the motor by using internal commutation, stationary magnets, and rotating electromagnets.

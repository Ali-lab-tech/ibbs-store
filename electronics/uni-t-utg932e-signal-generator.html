Product details

UNI-T UTG932E — The UNI-T UTG932E is a palm-sized two-channel arbitrary waveform generator producing 30 MHz sine output, 15 MHz square and pulse, with 200 MSa/s sampling and 14-bit vertical resolution. The Classic-Series sits at the entry of UNI-T's waveform-generator lineup, built around portability: 172 × 90 × 68 mm and 0.33 kg, USB-powered from a 5 V / 2 A adapter, with no separate AC cord to carry. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty. Key Features 30 MHz sine / 15 MHz square & pulse — dual-channel output with 1 µHz frequency resolution across the full range. 200 MSa/s sampling, 14-bit vertical resolution — 16,384 amplitude levels for clean output at common test frequencies. Palm-sized 172 × 90 × 68 mm, 0.33 kg — the most compact dual-channel AWG in the UNI-T lineup. Travels easily between bench, field, and classroom. 4 modulation types — AM, FM, PM, FSK — with internal source, covering the basics for analog and digital signal simulation. Linear and logarithmic frequency sweep — 1 ms to 500 s sweep time for component characterization and frequency-response work. 24 built-in arbitrary waveforms — non-volatile storage of standard and engineering shapes; custom waveforms uploadable via USB. Built-in 7-digit frequency counter — 100 mHz to 100 MHz input range (TTL compatible).

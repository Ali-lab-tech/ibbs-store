Product details

This is a standard IEC C14 male power connector, typically used as an input socket on many electronic devices or for creating custom power extension cables. Featuring three robust pins, it is designed to mate with a female IEC C13 connector, providing a secure and reliable electrical connection for equipment such as computers, servers, power supplies, and network devices. The re-wireable design allows for easy attachment to an existing cable, making it a versatile solution for custom power cable lengths, repairs, or DIY projects. Constructed from durable black material, this connector ensures long-lasting performance and compliance with IEC 60320 standards.

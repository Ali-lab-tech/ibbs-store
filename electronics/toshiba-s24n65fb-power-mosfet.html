Description This is another high-power electronic switch component (MOSFET), very similar in purpose to the K065N65Z shown earlier — used to control and switch high voltage/current in power electronics circuits. Key Details Brand: Toshiba (genuine) Part Number: S24N65FB Type: N-Channel Power MOSFET Package: TO-247 (3-pin, with metal mounting tab for heatsink) Voltage Rating: 650V (indicated by "65" in the part number) Current Rating: Around 24A (indicated by "24" in the part number) Use: Switching power supplies, inverters, welding machines, induction cooktops, motor drives, UPS systems Why It's Useful This MOSFET is built to handle high power efficiently while staying reliable and running cooler than older-style switches. It's a common replacement/upgrade part used by repair technicians for power boards in inverter ACs, induction stoves, welding inverters, and industrial power supplies. Physical Features ✅ 3 metal legs (Gate, Drain, Source) for board or wire connection ✅ Metal tab with mounting hole for heatsink attachment (helps release heat) ✅ Genuine Toshiba branding and batch code printed on the body Package Includes 1× Toshiba S24N65FB MOSFET

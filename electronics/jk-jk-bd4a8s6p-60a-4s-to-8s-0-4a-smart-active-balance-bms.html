JK JK-BD4A8S6P 60A 4S-8S Smart Active Balance BMS (with Bluetooth) Take full control of your custom battery pack with the JK JK-BD4A8S6P Smart Active Balance BMS . Engineered for maximum battery health, efficiency, and longevity, this high-performance Battery Management System supports 4S to 8S battery configurations with a 60A continuous charge and discharge current capability. Unlike conventional passive balancers that waste energy as heat, the JK Smart BMS features 0.4A active balancing , transferring energy seamlessly between cells to ensure uniform voltage levels, extended cycle life, and maximum usable capacity. Key Features Real-Time Active Balancing (0.4A): Actively transfers energy from high-voltage cells to low-voltage cells to keep all individual battery cells balanced without heat loss. Built-in Bluetooth Monitoring: Easily monitor total voltage, cell voltages, charge/discharge current, temperature, and SOC (State of Charge) via the mobile app on iOS and Android. Flexible 4S to 8S Compatibility: Ideal for 12V and 24V nominal battery systems using LiFePO4, Li-ion, or LTO chemistries. Continuous 60A Current: Designed to reliably handle up to 60A continuous charge and discharge loads. Comprehensive Multi-Protection: Advanced safety safeguards against over-charge, over-discharge, over-current, short-circuit, and high/low temperature events. Display Output Support: Connect an optional external display screen for convenient real-time status visibility.

Product details

The BENETECH GM1350 is a long-distance, high-temperature infrared thermometer reading from −18 to 1350℃ . Its 50:1 distance-to-spot ratio measures a small hot spot from far away, ideal for steel, glass, furnaces, boilers and power-plant equipment you cannot get close to. Emissivity is adjustable from 0.10 to 1.00 (0.95 preset), resolution is 0.1℃, and it responds in 500 ms.

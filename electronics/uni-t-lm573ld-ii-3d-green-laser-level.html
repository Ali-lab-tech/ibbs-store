Product details

The UNI-T LM573LD-II is a 3D green laser level that projects 12 lines across three 360° planes — wrapping level and plumb lines right around the room for floor tiling, dropped ceilings, partitions and fit-outs, with no repositioning. The bright 520 nm Osram-class green beams self-level automatically within ±3° and reach up to 40 m, with an outdoor pulse mode for use with a receiver and a bundled remote control . A rechargeable 5200 mAh Li-ion battery gives 6+ hours per charge. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty and English manual.

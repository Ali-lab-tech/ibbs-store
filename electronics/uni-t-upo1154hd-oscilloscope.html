Product details

UNI-T UPO1154HD — The UPO1154HD is a 150 MHz, 4-channel high-resolution oscilloscope built around UNI-T's R12™ 12-bit ADC architecture, with 1.25 GSa/s real-time sample rate, 100 Mpts memory depth, and a 7-inch capacitive touchscreen. Native 12-bit resolution gives 4,096 vertical levels — 16× the detail of an 8-bit instrument — enough to resolve power-supply ripple, sensor outputs, and small AC signals riding on DC offsets where 8-bit scopes lose them in the quantization floor. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by Digilog.pk with warranty. Key Features R12™ — Real 12-Bit Resolution — Native 12-bit ADC with 4,096 vertical levels at full bandwidth and full sample rate, no ERES averaging required. 150 MHz Bandwidth, 4 Channels — 1.25 GSa/s real-time sample rate (single channel), ≤ 2.2 ns calculated rise time. 100 Mpts Memory Depth — Capture full switching cycles and long protocol sequences without trading sample rate for memory. Ultra Phosphor 3.0 Display — Up to 500,000 wfms/s waveform capture rate with 256-level intensity grading, fast enough to catch the intermittent events that slower oscilloscopes spend hours missing. 5 Protocol Decoders Standard — RS-232/UART, I²C, SPI, CAN, LIN included; no optional licenses. Built-In Power Analysis — 12 measurement functions including switching loss, SOA, harmonics, ripple, and startup time. 4-Digit DVM and 6-Digit Frequency Counter — Hardware-accelerated, parallel to scope acquisition.

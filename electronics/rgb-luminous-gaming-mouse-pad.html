Description Illuminate your battle station with this RGB Luminous Mouse Pad. With an anti-slip rubber base and waterproof surface, this mouse pad is perfect for gaming or the office. The smooth surface can be easily wiped free of spilled liquid, whilst allowing your mouse to travel without friction. Stitching along the mousepad edges prevent any fraying that might result from travel, it can also be rolled up for easy transport. USB powered and no installation required – just plug and play! Product details: Single button design: press one time to change lighting mode, press twice to change brightness, press and hold approx. 3 seconds to turn ON/OFF. Large dimensions: 30 x 80cm Non-slip rubber base Water resistant 14 different light modes Package Included : 1x RGB Luminous Gaming Mouse Pad

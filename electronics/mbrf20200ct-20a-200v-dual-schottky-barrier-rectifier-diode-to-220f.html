Product details

The MBRF20200CT is a dual Schottky barrier rectifier diode, rated for 20A total forward current and 200V reverse voltage, packaged in the isolated TO-220F (ITO-220AB) through-hole package with a common-cathode, center-tap configuration (2 x 10A per leg). Built using guard-ring die construction for enhanced transient protection, this SWITCHMODE-style Schottky rectifier is designed for demanding power supply and switching applications where efficiency and reliability matter. As a Schottky diode, the MBRF20200CT offers a low forward voltage drop and fast switching characteristics compared to standard silicon rectifiers, resulting in reduced power loss and higher overall efficiency. It features high surge current capability, a lead-free, RoHS-compliant, halogen-free "Green" construction, and is well suited for use as a polarity protection diode, re-circulating (freewheeling) diode, or general switching diode in power circuits. Its isolated TO-220F package simplifies mounting to a heatsink, since no separate insulating mica pad is typically required, making it convenient to integrate into power supply designs, motor drives, and other high-current rectification circuits. Key Specifications Type: Dual Schottky Barrier Rectifier (common cathode, center tap) Package: TO-220F / ITO-220AB (isolated, 3-lead) Reverse Voltage (VRRM): 200V Forward Current (IO): 20A total (2 x 10A per leg) Forward Voltage Drop (VF): ~0.85–0.89V max @ 10A, 25°C Leakage Current (IR): ≤0.1mA @ 200V, 25°C Guard-ring construction for transient/stress protection High surge current capability Lead-free, RoHS compliant, halogen-free Common Applications Polarity protection diode Re-circulating (freewheeling) diode in motor/inductive circuits General switching diode in power supplies Rectification in switch-mode power supplies (SMPS) Package Includes 1 x MBRF20200CT Schottky Barrier Rectifier (TO-220F)

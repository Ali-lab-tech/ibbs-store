Product details

Description: The JK BMS LCD 2.0 steering wheel information screen is a device designed to monitor in detail the status of a battery made up of individual cells. It integrates seamlessly with the JK BMS battery management system. It is important to note that the device uses its own protocol, which is not compatible with BMS from other manufacturers. At the end of the screen case there is a multifunction button that provides the ability to control the display backlight and activate/deactivate the BMS from JiKong. This innovative device allows you to conveniently and efficiently control the parameters of the battery during its operation, providing you with full control over energy resources. Thanks to its high-quality mounting, the JK BMS LCD 2.0 screen is easily and conveniently mounted on the handlebars of your electric vehicle, such as an electric scooter or e-bike. This provides easy installation and a secure attachment to the handlebars, providing ease of use while monitoring and monitoring battery health and other parameters in real time. The JK BMS LCD 2.0 screen displays the following parameters for full control: Battery percentage. Total voltage level. Real-time amperage. BMS errors detected. This device provides a user-friendly and intuitive interface for efficient real-time monitoring and management of your battery system. Application of JK BMS LCD 2.0: E-bikes Electric scooters Waterproof O-ring switch for rugged use Real-time monitoring of battery parameters Handlebar and desktop mounting styles available Multiple display sizes for different system needs Universal compatibility with JK BMS and inverter BMS JK BMS LCD 2.0 LCD Display Instruction Manual: To turn on the BMS, press the touch button on the left side of the LCD screen once. If no output current from the BMS is detected within 10 seconds, the display will turn off the LCD backlight to reduce power consumption. If the display does not show any content, press the left button of the LCD screen once and the LCD screen will turn on the backlight. When the monitor displays content, press the left LCD screen button once to turn off the backlight. To turn off the BMS, hold the touch button on the left side of the LCD screen for 2 seconds and the BMS will turn off.

Product details

The IR2101 is a high voltage, high speed MOSFET and IGBT driver IC . It features independent high and low side referenced output channels, making it suitable for various power applications like motor drives and inverters. The device has a floating channel designed for bootstrap operation and can handle voltages up to 600V. It also offers features like undervoltage lockout and compatibility with 3.3V, 5V, and 15V logic inputs

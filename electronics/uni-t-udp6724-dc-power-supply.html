Product details

UNI-T UDP6724 — the flagship of the UDP6720 series: a 1500 W wide-span programmable switching DC power supply with a single 0–80 V / 0–40 A output. The wide-span design lets one supply cover work that would otherwise need several. An LCD shows voltage, current, power and operating status directly, while list mode and delay mode (up to 200 steps each) automate power sequences for burn-in, battery and reliability testing. Full remote control is available over RS-232, RS-485, USB and LAN. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with manual and warranty.

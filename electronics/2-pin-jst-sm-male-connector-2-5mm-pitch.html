Product details

Description This is a 2-pin male connector housing paired with two matching crimp terminal pins, commonly used for battery packs, LED strips, and small electronic wiring harnesses. Molded from durable black plastic, the housing features a keyed, latch-style design that ensures a secure, polarized mating connection with its corresponding female housing — preventing reversed or accidental disconnection during use. The included crimp-style metal terminals are designed to be crimped (or soldered) onto stripped wire ends and then inserted into the housing, allowing you to build custom cable assemblies and wiring harnesses to your exact length and wire-gauge requirements. This connector style is widely compatible with common JST-SM / VH-style 2.5mm pitch connector systems used throughout DIY electronics, RC hobby gear, and battery pack wiring.

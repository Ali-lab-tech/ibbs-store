XH-M299 AC-DC Switching Power Supply Module The XH-M299 is a compact power supply module that converts high-voltage AC (110–220V) into low-voltage DC output (5V and 12V) . It is commonly used to safely power electronic circuits and microcontroller projects directly from mains electricity. ⚡ Key Features AC to DC Conversion: Converts 110V–220V AC into stable DC power Dual Output Voltages: 5V DC (for microcontrollers, sensors) 12V DC (for relays, motors, etc.) Output Current: 5V: up to 100mA 12V: up to 500mA Isolated Design: Provides electrical isolation for safer operation and reduced interference Compact Size: Approx. 35 × 25 × 15 mm – small and easy to install 🔌 How It Works 👉 You connect AC mains (L & N) to the input side. 👉 The module converts it into low-voltage DC outputs (5V / 12V) . 👉 You can power your circuits directly from these outputs. 🧰 Applications Arduino & microcontroller projects DIY electronics Smart home devices Powering sensors, relays, and small modules ⚠️ Important Notes Works with high voltage (AC mains) — handle carefully Not suitable for high-power devices (limited current output) Ensure proper insulation when installing 💡 Simple Explanation 👉 It works like a mini power adapter on a circuit board — you give it wall electricity, and it gives you safe low-voltage power for electronics. Package Includes: 1x XH-M299 AC-DC Switching Power Supply Module

Product details

The 91023 iPhone Mobile Clock Repair Tool Set is a versatile and compact kit designed specifically for disassembling and repairing iPhones, clocks, and other small electronic devices. The set includes six essential tools, each tailored for precision and ease of use. Here’s an overview of its features: · Precision Screwdrivers : The set includes a variety of precision screwdrivers with different tips (such as Phillips, flathead, and Pentalobe) that are essential for removing the tiny screws commonly found in iPhones and other small devices. · Plastic Pry Tools : These non-abrasive tools are designed to gently pry open devices without damaging the delicate internal components. They are ideal for opening the iPhone casing and accessing internal parts like the battery or screen. · Suction Cup : A small suction cup is included to help lift and remove the screen or glass panel without scratching or damaging the surface. · Tweezers : The set contains fine-pointed tweezers for handling small components, screws, or cables with precision. These are particularly useful when working on delicate parts inside the device. · SIM Card Ejector Tool : A specialized tool is provided to easily eject the SIM card tray, which is often one of the first steps in iPhone disassembly. · Spudger : The set includes a spudger, a versatile tool with a flat tip and a pointed end, used for various tasks such as disconnecting cables, prying components, and lifting connectors. Additional Features: Durable Materials : The tools are made from high-quality, durable materials that resist wear and tear, ensuring long-term use. Ergonomic Design : Each tool is ergonomically designed for comfort, making it easier to work on delicate repairs without causing hand fatigue. Compact Storage : The tool set is compact and lightweight, making it easy to store and transport, whether for professional repair work or home use. Use Cases: Ideal for iPhone repairs, such as screen replacement, battery swaps, and internal component fixes. Suitable for clock repairs, where precision and delicate handling are required.

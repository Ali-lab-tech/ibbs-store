Product details

Description: This is an engaging, hands-on science experiment kit designed to introduce children to the fundamentals of magnetism and basic physical science through fun, interactive play. Marketed as a "科普实验工具" (popular science experiment tool), the set combines several classic magnetism-based demonstrations into one compact, blister-packed kit, making it an ideal educational toy for curious young learners, classroom science activities, or STEM-focused gift sets. The kit includes a small magnetic toy car built with alternating red and blue magnetic segments, designed to demonstrate magnetic attraction and repulsion in a simple, visual way as children experiment with how the magnets interact. It also includes a functional compass with a rotating dial marked in degrees, allowing kids to learn the basics of magnetic north-south orientation and how compasses work — a simple but effective introduction to Earth's magnetism. What's included, and what each piece does: Magnetic Toy Car — A small wheeled car with red and blue magnetic blocks attached to its sides. The color-coding (red = one pole, blue = the opposite pole) helps kids visually understand magnetic polarity — how like poles repel and opposite poles attract. Compass — A real, functional compass with directional markings (N, S, E, W, and degree markings from 0–360). This teaches how magnetism is used in real-world navigation, since a compass needle aligns with the Earth's natural magnetic field. Two Steel Ball Bearings — Silver metal balls that are attracted to magnets. Kids can use these with the magnetic car or compass area to observe and test magnetic pull firsthand. Bottle of Iron Filings — Fine iron powder/shavings sealed in a small bottle with a dropper-style red cap. When a magnet is placed near or under the bottle, the filings shift and arrange themselves — making invisible magnetic field lines visible. Star-Shaped Magnetic Pad — A flat, star-shaped rubber or plastic mat that likely serves as a base or holder for the magnets and experiments, possibly with magnetic properties itself for testing polarity. Purpose: This kit is meant to let children see and touch magnetism in action rather than just reading about it — reinforcing concepts like attraction, repulsion, magnetic fields, and compass navigation through simple, safe experiments.

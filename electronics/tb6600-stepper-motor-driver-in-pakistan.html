Product details

The TB6600 Stepper Motor Driver is a high-performance and easy-to-use component designed to control two-phase stepper motors. With its wide voltage range, adjustable current, and versatile features, the TB6600 is ideal for various applications requiring precise motor control. Key Features: Wide Voltage Range: Operates from 9V to 42VDC for compatibility with different power sources. Adjustable Current: Set the output current from 0.5A to 4.0A to match your motor's requirements. Micro-Stepping Support: Choose from 7 micro-step settings for smoother and quieter operation. Strong Anti-Interference Ability: Ensures reliable performance in noisy environments. Overcurrent, Overvoltage, and Under-Voltage Protection: Safeguards your driver and motor. Versatile Compatibility: Drives 4-wire, 6-wire, and 8-wire stepper motors. Applications: Robotics and Automation: Control precise movements in robotic systems. 3D Printing: Drive stepper motors for accurate and precise printing. CNC Machining: Control machine tools for precise cutting and engraving. Industrial Automation: Automate tasks with precise motor control. Upgrade your projects with the TB6600 Stepper Motor Driver and experience reliable and versatile control.

Product details

The 7426 is a quad two-input NAND gate integrated circuit designed for high-voltage applications. As a member of the 74-series logic family, this device performs the logical NAND function, where the output is low only if both inputs are high, with the added capability of handling higher voltage levels than standard TTL logic gates.

Product details

Description: The is an 80W electric soldering iron featuring a built-in digital LCD display, giving the user precise, real-time visibility into the soldering tip's temperature. Unlike basic fixed-temperature irons, the allows the temperature to be adjusted with degree-level precision, letting users tailor the heat output to the specific requirements of the task at hand — whether that's fine, sensitive electronic components or larger, heat-hungry solder joints. The iron uses an internal ceramic heating element, which delivers fast preheating (reaching working temperature in just seconds) along with stable, consistent heat delivery to the tip during use. This helps ensure clean, reliable, and uniform solder joints, while temperature stabilization circuitry prevents overheating that could otherwise damage delicate PCB components. Ergonomically, the features a soft, heat-insulating silicone grip handle designed to stay cool during extended use, improving comfort and safety while reducing hand fatigue during longer soldering sessions. The unit uses a coiled strain-relief section near the plug entry to reduce cable stress, connected to a standard 2-pin AC mains plug. Key Specifications: Power Rating: 80W Display: Digital LCD (temperature readout) Temperature Range: Approx. 180°C – 500°C (adjustable) Heating Element: Internal Ceramic Heater Handle Material: Heat-insulating silicone grip Tip Compatibility: 900M-series interchangeable soldering tips Power Input: AC Mains (region-dependent plug type) Features: Digital temperature display and adjustment Fast heat-up time (seconds to reach working temperature) Temperature stabilization for consistent results Comfortable, heat-resistant silicone handle Compatible with widely available 900M replacement tips Applications: Electronics assembly and repair, PCB soldering, hobbyist and DIY electronics projects, small appliance repair, and general precision soldering tasks. Package Includes: 1 x 80W LCD Digital Soldering Iron

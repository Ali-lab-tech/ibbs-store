SKU: FNIRSI-DSO-510

Product details

The FNIRSI DSO-510 is the fastest of FNIRSI's pocket scopes, with 48MSa/s sampling and 10MHz bandwidth in a 99 mm body. It handles both periodic analog signals and one-shot digital signals. A 50kHz signal generator with 13 waveforms is built in. It has a 2.8-inch 320 × 240 display, measures up to ±400V with a 10X probe, and runs about 4 hours on its 1000mAh battery. Key Features ⚡ 10MHz bandwidth, 48MSa/s single channel 🎛️ 50kHz signal generator with 13 waveforms, 0.1 – 3.0V 🎯 Auto, normal and single trigger with one-click AUTO 🛡️ Measures up to ±400V with a 10X probe 🔋 1000mAh battery about 4 hours, Type-C charging Technical Specifications Channels 1 Bandwidth 10MHz Sampling rate 48MSa/s Vertical sensitivity 10mV – 20V/div Time base 50ns – 20s/div Voltage range X1: ±40V, X10: ±400V Coupling AC / DC Signal generator 0 – 50kHz, 0.1 – 3.0V, 13 waveforms Display 2.8", 320 × 240 Battery 1000mAh, 5V 1A charging Size 99 × 68.3 × 19.5 mm Weight 104 g What's in the Box 1 × FNIRSI DSO-510 oscilloscope Probe and USB cable as supplied by FNIRSI

Product details

The ET612 is a two-piece cable tracker and tester for network, telephone and CCTV installers. Connect the transmitter to one end of a cable and use the receiver probe to find that exact cable in a crowded rack or bundle. It also checks continuity, battery voltage and polarity, and tests PoE (Power over Ethernet) voltage, polarity and mode on standard and non-standard devices. Analog and digital tracing modes, adjustable sensitivity and a built-in torch. Key Features 🔎 Cable tracing in analog and digital modes 🔌 PoE test : voltage, polarity and power mode 🔋 Battery voltage and polarity check ✅ Continuity test for wire pairs 🔦 Adjustable sensitivity , LCD display and LED torch Technical Specifications Model ET612 Modes PoE, digital, analog Tests Cable tracing, continuity, battery voltage and polarity, PoE Display LCD Other Adjustable sensitivity, LED flashlight Material ABS Applications Telephone, computer network, video network and other metal cable lines What's in the Box 1 × ET612 transmitter 1 × ET612 receiver

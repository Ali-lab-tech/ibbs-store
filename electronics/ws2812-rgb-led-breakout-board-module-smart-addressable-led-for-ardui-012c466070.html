WS2812 RGB LED Breakout Board Module The WS2812 RGB LED Breakout Board Module is a smart and compact lighting solution, because it includes an integrated driver IC . Since it is fully addressable, you can control each LED individually using just a single data line. With full compatibility with Arduino , Raspberry Pi , ESP32 , and other microcontrollers , you connect it easily to your favorite projects. Whether you're building DIY lighting effects , wearables, robotics , or art installations, you create vibrant and dynamic visuals with ease. Key Features With RGB color output, you get a full-color spectrum through precise 24-bit control . Since it includes a built-in WS2812 integrated driver IC , you enjoy reliable and intelligent performance. Because of its chainable design, you can easily connect multiple modules for extended lighting setups. Thanks to the single data pin control , you minimize wiring complexity in your projects. As it runs on 5V DC, you power it directly from most microcontrollers . With compatibility for Arduino libraries like FastLED and Adafruit NeoPixel , you program colorful effects quickly and efficiently. Technical Specifications Since it uses WS2812 RGB LED Breakout Board , you get vibrant and addressable lighting . With a voltage requirement of 5V DC, you can power it easily using standard microcontrollers. Because each LED draws around 60mA at full brightness , you manage power usage effectively. Thanks to its digital input/output interface , you control the LEDs with simple code . With compact dimensions of approximately 11mm x 11mm, you fit it into tight project spaces. As it comes on a mini breakout board , you enjoy easy integration and reliable performance.

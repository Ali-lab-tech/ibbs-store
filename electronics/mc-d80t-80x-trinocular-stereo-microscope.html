MECHANIC MC D80T 8X–80X Trinocular Stereo Microscope The MECHANIC MC D80T is a professional trinocular stereo zoom microscope built for mobile phone motherboard repair, BGA/IC micro-soldering and precision electronics work . Its 1:10 continuous zoom takes you from a wide 8X overview of the board down to 80X detail on tiny components and solder joints — without swapping lenses. Supplied by as genuine MECHANIC stock. In stock and ready to ship across Pakistan. Key Features 8X–80X continuous zoom – 1:10 zoom ratio covers everything from full-board inspection to fine pad and ball-level work. Galilean optical system – sharp, high-resolution images with good depth of field, a wide view and accurate colour. Coated optical glass – improves brightness and clarity for long repair sessions. Wide-field PL 10X/27 eyepieces – a larger viewing area means less moving the board around. 100 mm working distance – plenty of room for soldering iron, hot air nozzle and tweezers under the lens. Trinocular port – connect a camera or display through the standard 0.5X CTV adapter to record work, stream to a monitor or train staff. Comfortable viewing – 360° rotatable head, 55–75 mm interpupillary range and diopter adjustment on both eyepieces.

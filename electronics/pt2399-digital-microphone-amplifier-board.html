PT2399 Digital Microphone Amplifier Board Upgrade your DIY audio projects with the PT2399 Digital Microphone Amplifier Board , a compact and reliable audio processing module designed for microphone amplification, echo effects, and sound mixing. Powered by the popular PT2399 digital echo IC , this board delivers clear, low-noise audio with adjustable controls for microphone volume, music volume, echo level, and master output. Ideal for karaoke systems, DIY amplifiers, public address (PA) systems, audio mixers, Bluetooth speakers, and home entertainment projects , this amplifier board provides excellent sound quality with simple installation and user-friendly operation. Its dual 3.5mm audio ports, microphone input, and multiple rotary controls make it easy to customize your audio experience while maintaining stable performance and minimal distortion.

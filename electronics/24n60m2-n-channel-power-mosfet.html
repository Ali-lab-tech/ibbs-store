Product details

The 24N60M2 N-Channel Power MOSFET is a high-performance, fast-switching transistor designed for power electronics applications. With a 600V drain-source voltage and 24A continuous current , it delivers exceptional efficiency and reliability in demanding environments. Housed in a TO-247 package , it’s ideal for applications requiring high power handling and low switching losses—such as inverters, UPS systems, power supplies, and industrial control circuits.

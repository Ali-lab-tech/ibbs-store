Product details

The WT9050B is a pin-type moisture meter for checking timber and building materials. Choose one of 8 wood groups or 2 material settings , press the pins in, and read the moisture content, useful for furniture makers, carpenters and anyone checking damp walls. It also shows the ambient temperature, has data hold and a large backlit LCD, and switches off after 10 minutes of no use.

Product details

The UNI-T UT-M15 is a 16-channel logic analyzer probe pod that turns a compatible UPO/MSO-series oscilloscope into a mixed-signal instrument. It adds digital bus visibility for debugging embedded, automotive and industrial communication protocols alongside your analog channels. It connects through the scope's dedicated logic-analyzer port and ships with the full set of channel leads and grabbers. Genuine UNI-T accessory supplied by .

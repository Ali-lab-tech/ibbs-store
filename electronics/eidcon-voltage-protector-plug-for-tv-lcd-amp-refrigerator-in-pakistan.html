EIDCON Voltage Protector Plug – For TV, LCD & Refrigerator Product Overview The EIDCON Voltage Protector Plug is a reliable safety device designed to protect your valuable home appliances such as TVs, LCDs, and refrigerators from voltage fluctuations, surges, and power spikes. It automatically cuts off power when voltage is too high or too low and restores it when conditions are safe.

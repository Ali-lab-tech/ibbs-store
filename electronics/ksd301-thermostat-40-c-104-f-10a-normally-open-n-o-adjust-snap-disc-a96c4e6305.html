Product details

KSD301 bimetallic thermostat is a thermal fuse to protect electrical components from overheating. Used as temperature protection in electrical equipment: coffee makers, automatic toasters, laminators, electric pots, steam irons, air heaters, microwave ovens, water dispensers, etc. Thermal protection is an additional independent protection against overheating of equipment.

Product details

The CD4073 is a CMOS-based Triple 3-input AND gate integrated circuit. This logic gate performs the logical AND function on three independent inputs for each of the three gates contained within the package. This component is commonly used in digital logic circuits to implement Boolean functions where a high output is required only when all three corresponding inputs are at a high logic level.

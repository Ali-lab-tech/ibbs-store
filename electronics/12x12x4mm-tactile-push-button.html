Product details

12x12x4mm Tactile Push Button is Normally Open switch. The tactile button connects the circuit for the time it is kept pressed and as soon as it is released the circuit is disconnected. The Tactile Push-button has 4 pins. Two pins are connected internally. These Tactile push-buttons feature a through-hole design. Its high-precision mechanism offers acute operation and long life. Specification Switch Type Momentary Colour Black Mounting Type Through Hole Contact Resistance 30mΩ Insulation Resistance 100MΩ Actuation Force 1.6 to 2.5N Actuation Travel 0.3mm Operating Temperature -35°C to 85°C Size 12x12x4mm Country of Origin China

The MOSFET Trigger Switch Drive Module is of high power MOS trigger switch driver module and control board with a field-effect tube to adjust the electronic switch. This MOSFET voltage regulator adopts imported double MOS parallel active output to show lower internal resistance, greater electric current,and power. Hence besides it works at 15A,400W under common temperature. Which satisfies most devices usage and realizes the control of great power devices in a relaxed way. What’s more, it can be applied to powerful devices which could be controlled by output terminals like electric motors and light bulbs . To control the speed of the motor and the light of bulbs. Certainly, you can apply it to devices that could input PWM for control like an LED light belt, DC motor , Micropump, and solenoid valves. Features Of MOS Transistor Driving Module FET Trigger Switch Board: High power MOS field effect tube trigger switch driver module PWM regulating electronic switch panel. Imported dual-mos transistor parallels active output, lower resistance, higher current, and strong power. 15A, 400W in normal temperature condition, compatible with majority devices and equipment. Although the PWM controller can access the PWM signal, which can perfectly support a frequency of 0- 20khz. Hence this motor control module can tolerate wide input Voltage: DC 5V- 36V. This mos transistor board can be trigger by digital High-Low: DC 3.3V-20V. This MOS FET Trigger Switch Driver Module can be widely use in dc motor, lamp, bulb, LED lamp strip, micro water pump, also solenoid valve, etc. You can input PWM to control motor speed, lamp brightness, etc. Specifications Of MOS FET Trigger Switch Driver Module: Operating Voltage: DC 5V - 36V; The trigger source: digital high-low (DC3.3V - 20V), can be connected microcontroller IO port, PLC interfaces, DC power. Lower auxiliary cooling conditions, the maximum current up to 30A.

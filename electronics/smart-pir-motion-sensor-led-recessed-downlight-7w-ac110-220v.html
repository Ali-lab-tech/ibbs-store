Product details

Description: This is a smart human-body infrared (PIR) sensing LED downlight, designed for recessed ceiling installation. It automatically detects motion within its sensing range and switches on the integrated LED light source, making it well suited for stairwells, hallways, closets, garages, and other transitional spaces where hands-free, automatic lighting is desired. The unit features a built-in Passive Infrared (PIR) sensor module (visible as the central dome on the fixture face) that detects body heat and movement, triggering the light automatically without requiring a manual switch. It includes an adjustable delay timer that keeps the light on for a set duration after motion is last detected, and operates on universal AC mains input, making it compatible with both 110V and 220V electrical systems worldwide. The housing is a slim, round, flush-mount downlight design with a cutout-style trim for ceiling installation, pre-wired with a two-conductor lead for direct connection to mains wiring. Key Specifications: Product Type: Smart PIR Motion Sensor LED Downlight Input Voltage: AC 110V/220V, 50Hz/60Hz (Universal) Sensor Mode: PIR (Human Body Infrared) Motion Detection Power Rating: 7W Light Color: Cool White (白光) Sensing Distance: 3–8 meters Sensing Angle: 120° Delay Time (Hold Time): 30 seconds Light Sensitivity Threshold: 15 Lux (activates in low-light conditions) Mounting Type: Recessed/Flush Ceiling Mount Certification: CCC Certified Applications: Stairwells, hallways, corridors, closets, storage rooms, garages, basements, and any indoor space requiring automatic, hands-free, motion-activated lighting. Package Includes: 1 x Smart PIR Sensor LED Downlight 7W

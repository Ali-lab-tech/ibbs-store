Product details

The FNIRSI MRT-1050 is a digital insulation resistance tester (megger) for checking motor windings, cables and wiring installations. Select a 100V, 250V, 500V or 1000V test voltage to suit the equipment, and read insulation resistance on the digital display. It also measures AC and DC voltage and automatically discharges the circuit after each test. Contact us for the full specification sheet before ordering.

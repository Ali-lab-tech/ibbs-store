Product details

Description: The SN74HC75N contains four independent bistable (transparent) latches, commonly used for temporarily storing binary data between processing stages, such as between a microcontroller/CPU and an input/output or display device. Two latches are controlled together by one of two active-HIGH enable inputs (LE12 and LE34). While the enable (LE) input is HIGH, data present at the D input passes straight through to the Q output — the latch is "transparent." When the enable input goes LOW, the last data value present just before the transition is captured and held stable at the output, regardless of further changes on the D input, until the enable goes HIGH again. Center-pin power arrangement: Unlike many standard logic ICs with power at the corner pins, this device has VCC and GND on the center pins — a common wiring mistake when swapping this part into a board designed for a different pin arrangement. Voltage compatibility: The HC-series logic family operates from 2V to 6V and is generally NOT directly TTL-output compatible at the input threshold level without care — if this is being used in a mixed TTL/CMOS system, verify voltage threshold compatibility (HCT-series is typically used instead for that purpose).

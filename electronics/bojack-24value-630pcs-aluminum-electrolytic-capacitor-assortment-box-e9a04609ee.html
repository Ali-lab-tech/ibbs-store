Product details

Stock your workbench with everything you need for prototyping, repairs, and circuit design in one convenient kit. This BOJACK Electrolytic Capacitor Assortment Box includes 630 pieces across 24 different capacitance values, ranging from 0.1µF up to 1000µF, with voltage ratings spanning 10V to 50V — covering the vast majority of common capacitance needs for hobbyist and professional electronics work alike. Each capacitor is a standard radial-lead aluminum electrolytic type, ideal for power supply filtering, decoupling, coupling, timing circuits, audio applications, and general-purpose DC smoothing. The assortment spans multiple physical case sizes (from compact 4×7mm up to larger 10×16mm), so you'll have the right size on hand whether you're working on a compact PCB or a higher-capacity power circuit. Everything is neatly organized in a clear, labeled compartment box with a lid chart showing each value, voltage, size, and quantity at a glance — making it easy to find the exact capacitor you need without digging through loose parts. This keeps your components sorted, protected, and ready to grab for quick repairs or new builds.

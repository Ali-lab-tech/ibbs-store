Product details

The FNIRSI LCR-ST1 is a tweezer-style LCR meter for testing SMD resistors, capacitors and inductors right on the board or straight off the reel. It identifies the part type automatically and shows ESR, D, Q, Z and X. Test frequency is selectable at 100Hz, 1kHz or 10kHz with 0.3V or 0.6V RMS levels. The 1.14-inch colour screen, replaceable tips and magnetic back make it handy at a rework bench. Key Features 🔍 Automatic R / C / L recognition plus diode and continuity 📏 Capacitance 1pF – 22mF inductance 1µH – 10H, resistance 10mΩ – 10MΩ 🎚️ 100Hz, 1kHz, 10kHz test frequency 0.3V / 0.6V RMS 📊 ESR, D, Q, Z and X readout 🧲 Magnetic back and replaceable tips Technical Specifications Capacitance 1pF – 22mF Inductance 1µH – 10H Resistance 10mΩ – 10MΩ Test frequency 100Hz, 1kHz, 10kHz Test level 0.3V, 0.6V RMS Parameters ESR, D, Q, Z, X Display 1.14" colour Battery 250mAh, Type-C charging Size 28 × 19 × 150 mm Weight 41 g What's in the Box 1 × FNIRSI LCR-ST1 tweezer meter USB cable as supplied by FNIRSI

Product details

The UNI-T LM320F is the top digital laser angle meter (inclinometer) in the range — 4×90° (360°) at ±0.2° , 3 laser lines , and a four-sided magnetic base (bottom, rear, left & right) so it mounts on steelwork in any orientation for tiling, fabrication, staircases and installation. It reads in degrees, %, mm/m and in/ft, with relative/absolute measurement, angle hold and screen rotation, powered by a rechargeable 1000 mAh battery.

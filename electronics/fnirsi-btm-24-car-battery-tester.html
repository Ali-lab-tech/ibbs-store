Product details

The FNIRSI BTM-24 checks 12V and 24V lead-acid, AGM, GEL, EFB and lithium starter batteries in seconds. It measures voltage, internal resistance, CCA, state of charge and state of health, then gives a clear good/attention/bad verdict. It also runs a cranking test and a charging-system test so you can tell a weak battery from a bad alternator. It supports CCA, IEC, EN, DIN, JIS and GB ratings up to 2000 CCA and shows results on a 2.4-inch colour screen.

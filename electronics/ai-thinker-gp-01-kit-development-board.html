Product details

GP-01-Kit is a highly integrated development board with ceramic antenna and high performance multi-mode BDS/GNSS satellite navigation receiver. The AT6558R satellite positioning microcircuit serves as the main board. The GP-01 supports a variety of satellite navigation systems, including the Chinese BeiDou NavigationSatellite System (BDS), the American GPS and the Russian GLONASS. The GP-01-Kit follows the NMEA protocol. Features: Power supply: 5V Chip: AT6558R Operating temperature: from -40℃ to 85 ℃ UART: 256,000 bps (maximum) Useful links: Documentation (English)

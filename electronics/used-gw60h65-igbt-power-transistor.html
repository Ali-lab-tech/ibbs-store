With a 650V voltage rating and 60A continuous current capacity, it offers fast switching and minimal power losses, making it ideal for industrial motor drives, power inverters, and renewable energy systems. Benefits of GW60H65 IGBT High Voltage Capacity: Supports up to 650V for industrial-grade applications. Fast Switching Speed: Reduces power loss and increases system efficiency. High Current Handling: Operates continuously at 60A without performance degradation. Thermal Stability: Performs reliably across wide temperature ranges. Compact TO-247 Package: Easy integration into power system designs.

Product details

2pcs 6mm Brass Stud Remote Battery Power Junction Post Connector Terminal Set Make secure, high-current battery connections with this brass stud terminal set — built for tough jobs like welding machines, charging generators, marine setups, trucks, and automobiles. Each set includes two stud connectors so positive and negative terminals are easy to tell apart at a glance. Machined from high-quality brass, these terminals deliver excellent electrical conductivity along with strong resistance to heat and corrosion, so connections stay solid even under heavy load. The 6mm stud diameter supports high current flow while keeping the overall footprint compact and easy to fit into tight spaces. Installation is simple thanks to a two-nut system: just tighten down the nuts to lock the connection in place, with no risk of bolts working loose over time. Whether you're wiring up a battery bank, connecting a charger, or setting up a remote power post, this terminal set gives you a dependable, reusable connection point.

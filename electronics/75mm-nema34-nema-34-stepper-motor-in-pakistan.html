NEMA 34 75mm Stepper Motor is a high torque hybrid bipolar stepping motor with a 3.4×3.4 inch faceplate. NEMA 34 75mm Stepper Motor, Hybrid stepper has the combination of the features of the Variable Reluctance Stepper Motor and Permanent Magnet Stepper Motor. This motor has a step angle of 1.8 deg., which means that it has 200 steps per revolution and for every step, it will cover 1.8°. This NEMA 34 is a 2-Phase motor with 4 wires. Compatible controllers for this motor are CW230, CW250, and CW860.

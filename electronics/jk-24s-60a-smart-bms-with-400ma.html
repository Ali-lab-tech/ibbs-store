JK 24S 60A Smart BMS with 400mA Active Balancing Reliable and Intelligent Battery Management System for Li-ion Applications The JK 24S 60A Smart BMS by JIKONG is a CE, FCC, and RoHS certified battery management system that supports active balancing with up to 400mA balancing current. It’s engineered for lithium-ion battery systems and ideal for power BMS, energy storage, and 3C digital electronics. Efficient Active Balancing Technology Unlike traditional passive balancing that burns excess energy as heat, JK's active balancing system redistributes energy between cells with minimal power loss. This results in better energy efficiency, less heat, and longer battery life — even when charging, discharging, or idle. Smart Monitoring and Full Protection This smart BMS offers comprehensive protection features including overcharge, over-discharge, overcurrent, and short-circuit protection.

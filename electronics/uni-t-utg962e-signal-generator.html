Product details

UNI-T UTG962E — The UNI-T UTG962E is a palm-sized two-channel arbitrary waveform generator producing 60 MHz sine output, 20 MHz square and pulse, with 200 MSa/s sampling and 14-bit vertical resolution. It is the bandwidth-extended member of the UTG900E Classic-Series — identical chassis, sample rate, modulation set, and DAC resolution as the UTG932E, with double the top-end sine output frequency. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty. Key Features 60 MHz sine / 20 MHz square & pulse — dual-channel output with 1 µHz frequency resolution. Double the bandwidth of the UTG932E in the same chassis. 200 MSa/s sampling, 14-bit vertical resolution — 16,384 amplitude levels. Palm-sized 172 × 90 × 68 mm, 0.33 kg — same compact chassis as the UTG932E; the bandwidth upgrade carries no size penalty. 4 modulation types — AM, FM, PM, FSK — with internal source. Linear and logarithmic frequency sweep — 1 ms to 500 s sweep time. 24 built-in arbitrary waveforms — non-volatile storage; custom waveforms uploadable via USB. Built-in 7-digit frequency counter — 100 mHz to 100 MHz input range (TTL compatible).

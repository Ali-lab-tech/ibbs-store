Product details

The FNIRSI C1 is a compact Type-C power meter and fast-charge trigger. It reads voltage, current and power up to 24V, 6.5A and 130W, and logs capacity, energy and cable resistance. It detects and triggers QC2.0/QC3.0, Huawei FCP/SCP, Samsung AFC and USB PD, and reads cable E-marker data. This is the Bluetooth version, so you can monitor readings away from the device.

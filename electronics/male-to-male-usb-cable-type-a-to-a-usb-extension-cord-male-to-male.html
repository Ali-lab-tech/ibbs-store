USB male to male cables are primarily used for synchronizing data between devices. Double-sided USB cables are convenient as they eliminate the need to find the right side for data transfer. Male to male USB cables are compatible with keyboards, mice, modems, printers, and other USB peripherals. They offer a transfer rate of up to 480 Mbps, varying with the peripheral's USB version, and easily connect to any USB device. Foil and braid shielding reduce EMI/RFI interference, while twisted pair construction minimizes crosstalk, ensuring high-speed, error-free data transfer. These cables are highly useful for resolving connection issues. They allow flexible cable connections, unlike traditional USB cables. A double-sided USB cord simplifies device connections without worrying about orientation. It works regardless of how it's plugged in and has various other applications.

Product details

Heat Shrink Sleeve 70mm Industrial Grade Woer (HST) are excellent for insulation. They have exceptional insulation characteristics, excellent stress control properties Simply a great way to create a Flexible, Heat Resistant and Waterproof tight joint. Just slide a slightly larger tube at the desired location and apply heat through a cigarette lighter, or a heat gun; for about 10 seconds and the tube will shrink down and will hold the joint tightly.

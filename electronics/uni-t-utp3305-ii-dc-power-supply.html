Product details

UNI-T UTP3305-II is a precision-grade three-channel linear DC bench power supply — 335 W total, with two adjustable 0–32 V / 0–5 A channels plus a fixed 5 V / 3 A logic rail. Four-digit LED readouts at 10 mV / 1 mA, series/parallel tracking and ultra-low ripple make it ideal for analog and RF development. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by Digilog.pk with warranty.

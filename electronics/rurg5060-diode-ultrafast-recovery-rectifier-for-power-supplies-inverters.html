RURG5060 Diode - Ultrafast Recovery Rectifier for High-Frequency Applications The RURG5060 Diode is a powerful ultrafast recovery rectifier, optimized for use in high-frequency switching applications. With a reverse voltage rating of 600V and a forward current rating of 50A, it provides both efficiency and reliability, ideal for use in switching power supplies, inverters, and freewheeling diode circuits. Benefits of the RURG5060 Diode Ultrafast Recovery Time: Minimizes switching losses and reduces heat generation, improving circuit efficiency. Low Forward Voltage Drop: Enhances energy efficiency by reducing conduction losses. High Surge Capability: Withstands substantial current surges, ensuring durability and reliability under stress. Avalanche Energy Rated: Offers robustness for industrial applications. Environmentally Friendly: Pb-free and RoHS compliant.

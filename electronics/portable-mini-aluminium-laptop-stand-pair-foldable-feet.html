Description This is a portable mini aluminum laptop stand , designed to instantly lift the back of your laptop for a more ergonomic typing angle and improved underside airflow. Each stand attaches directly to the underside of your laptop and folds flat when not in use, making it virtually invisible and adding no bulk to your laptop bag when traveling. Made from durable, lightweight aluminum , these mini stands are built to withstand repeated folding and daily use without losing their structural integrity or grip. To use, simply flip up the small built-in riser with a light push of your finger — it locks into position to elevate the rear of your laptop. When you're done, fold it back flat against the laptop's underside for a slim, travel-ready profile. By tilting your laptop slightly upward, this stand helps reduce wrist strain, improves typing posture and screen viewing angle, and promotes better airflow underneath the device — helping keep your laptop running cooler during extended use.

Product details

REX-C700FK07 RKC Temperature Controller A dual-display PID temperature controller built for precise process control in industrial and DIY applications alike. The REX-C700 features a 96×96mm panel-mount housing with two bright 4-digit LED displays — the upper PV (Process Value) display shows the current measured temperature, while the lower SV (Set Value) display shows your target temperature, so you can monitor and adjust at a glance. Key Features: Dual LED Display – Simultaneous readout of actual and target temperature PID Control with Auto-Tuning (AT) – Automatically calculates optimal PID parameters for stable, accurate temperature regulation Dual Output Indicators (OUT1/OUT2) – Supports heating/cooling or primary/secondary output control Dual Alarm Outputs (ALM1/ALM2) – Configurable high/low alarm thresholds for process safety Simple Front-Panel Programming – SET button plus up/down/shift buttons for quick parameter configuration Standard 96×96mm Panel Cutout – Fits industry-standard control panel openings Included Mounting Bracket – Spring-clip bracket with mounting screw for secure panel installation Typical Applications: Ideal for oven and kiln temperature control, plastic extrusion and injection molding, incubators, food processing equipment, industrial furnaces, 3D printer hot ends, homebrewing/fermentation setups, and general process heating or cooling systems where accurate, repeatable temperature control is required. Package Include: 1× REX-C700FK07 Temperature Controller 1× Panel mounting bracket with screw

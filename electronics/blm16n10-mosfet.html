Product details

The BLM16N10 is not a rectifier . It is actually a power MOSFET (Metal–Oxide–Semiconductor Field-Effect Transistor) — specifically an N-Channel power MOSFET . MOSFETs are used for switching and amplification in power electronics, not for directly rectifying AC to DC like diodes or bridge rectifiers do. alltransistors.com 📌 Key Characteristics Type: N-Channel Power MOSFET transistor Voltage Rating (V<sub>DS</sub>): Typically 100 V maximum drain-to-source voltage Current Capability: Up to around 60 A continuous in some versions such as BLM16N10-P Low On-Resistance: Designed for efficient switching with low conduction losses Package Options: Commonly in TO-220, TO-252, etc. Used in: Power switching applications like DC-DC converters, motor drivers, load switches, and other power control circuits. A MOSFET like the BLM16N10 is a switch device . It controls current flow between drain and source depending on gate voltage, and is used in power management and switching circuits — not as a standalone AC-to-DC converter. Typical Applications of BLM16N10 Switch-mode power supplies (SMPS) DC-DC converters Motor control circuits Battery management systems General power switching in electronics Package Included: 1x BLM16N10 MOSFET

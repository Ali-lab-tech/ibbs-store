The SUOER SG-N601 is a 60W corded soldering iron with a pistol-style handle 🌟 Product Overview The SUOER SG-N601 is a pistol-grip style automatic soldering gun that operates at 60W power. Its biggest advantage is the internal solder wire feed mechanism . You do not need to hold the solder wire in one hand and the iron in the other; you can solder with just one hand simply by pulling the trigger. ⚙️ Key Features One-Handed Operation: The solder wire spool mounts directly onto the back of the gun. When you pull the trigger, the wire automatically feeds forward toward the nozzle tip. 60W Heating Element: Equipped with a 60W external heating element that heats up quickly and has a long operational lifespan. It is excellent for both heavy-duty appliance repair and standard PCB circuit boards. Detachable Wire Reel Holder: A removable steel stand is attached to the back of the gun to hold the solder wire roll. It can be easily detached when not needed. Ergonomic Design: The pistol-like design provides a very firm grip, preventing hand fatigue even during long hours of continuous work. 📋 Technical Specifications Feature Details Brand SUOER Model SG-N601 (Also known as TU-941B in some markets) Power 60 Watts Voltage AC 220V - 240V (50Hz/60Hz) Material Alloy Tip + Premium ABS Plastic Body Wire Diameter Supported Perfectly supports soldering wire from 0.8mm to 1.2mm Would you like help finding an English user manual or safety instructions on how to load the wire spool correctly into this gun?

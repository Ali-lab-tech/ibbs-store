The MPU6500 GY-6500 6DOF 6 Axis Accelerometer Gyro Sensor is a compact and versatile sensor module designed to provide precise motion sensing capabilities for various electronic applications. This module integrates both accelerometer and gyroscope sensors, offering a comprehensive 6-degree-of-freedom (6DOF) measurement solution in a single package. Features: Typically includes programmable full-scale range (e.g., ±2g, ±4g, ±8g, ±16g). Offers high resolution for precise measurement of acceleration. Usually supports a wide range of angular rates (e.g., ±250°/s, ±500°/s, ±1000°/s, ±2000°/s). Measures angular velocity around three axes (X, Y, Z).

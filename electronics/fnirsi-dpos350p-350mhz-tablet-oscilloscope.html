Product details

The FNIRSI DPOS350P puts four instruments in one 7-inch touchscreen tablet: a 350MHz, 1GSa/s two-channel oscilloscope, a 50MHz signal generator, a 200kHz–350MHz FFT spectrum analyzer and a 50MHz frequency response (Bode) analyzer. It suits circuit debugging, EMI checks and filter tuning on the bench or in the field. The 8000mAh battery gives about 3 hours of use, charges over USB-C at 18W, and the inputs are protected to 400V. Key Features ⚡ 350MHz, 1GSa/s oscilloscope with 50,000 wfm/s refresh and 1ns rise time 🎛️ 50MHz signal generator with 14 standard waveforms plus captured waveform output 📊 Spectrum analyzer 200kHz – 350MHz with waterfall and 3D waterfall views 📉 Frequency response analyzer 10Hz – 50MHz with gain and phase cursors 🖐️ 7" 1024 × 600 IPS touchscreen with grayscale and colour-temperature modes 💾 500 waveform files and 90 screenshots with USB export Technical Specifications Channels 2 Bandwidth 350MHz (single channel) Sampling rate 1GSa/s Memory depth 47Kpts Rise time 1ns Vertical sensitivity 2mV – 20V/div (1X) Time base 5ns – 50s Input impedance 1MΩ, 14pF Bandwidth limit 150MHz / 20MHz Max input 400V (1X probe) Signal generator 0 – 50MHz sine; 0 – 5Vpp; 14 waveforms Spectrum analyzer 200kHz – 350MHz, FFT 4K – 32K Frequency response analyzer 10Hz – 50MHz Display 7" IPS capacitive touch, 1024 × 600 Battery 3.7V 8000mAh, ~3 h Charging QC 18W, 12V 1.5A, USB-C Dimensions 190 × 128 × 37 mm What's in the Box 1 × FNIRSI DPOS350P tablet oscilloscope (EU plug charger) Probes and accessories as supplied by FNIRSI

This Wemos D1 mini pro external antenna WI-FI Antenna/U.FL To WI-FI Antenna is compact 2.4GHz omnidirectional "rubber-duck" WiFi antenna provides broad coverage and 3dBi gain. It is a coaxial sleeve design with an omnidirectional pattern. It is ideally suited for IEEE 802.11b and 802.11g wireless LANs.It has sma connector and communication antenna. This flexible antenna features a tilt-and-swivel RP-SMA connector, allowing them to be used vertically, at a right angle, or any angle in-between. It is suitable as a replacement RF antenna for many access points and radios that are equipped with RP-SMA connectors including D-Link, Linksys and others.

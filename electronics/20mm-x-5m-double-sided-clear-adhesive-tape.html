Product details

Description This is a high-strength, double-sided transparent adhesive tape , measuring 20mm wide by 5 meters long , backed with a red release liner for easy application. Built with a strong acrylic adhesive on both sides, it bonds securely to a wide range of surfaces — including glass, metal, plastic, and painted surfaces — making it a reliable choice for mounting, assembly, and repair projects where screws or visible fasteners aren't practical. Once applied, the tape itself is clear/transparent, leaving a clean, nearly invisible bond line — ideal for mounting phone screens, LED strips, nameplates, mirrors, trim panels, and other display or decorative items where appearance matters. Its high initial tack and strong holding power make it suitable for both light-duty crafts and more demanding mounting applications, while remaining resistant to temperature changes and general wear. The tape's slim 20mm width allows for precise, clean application along edges and narrow surfaces, and the extended 9-meter length provides plenty of tape for multiple projects or larger mounting jobs.

Product details

The Wireless Temperature and Humidity Meter is a compact and easy-to-use device designed to accurately measure and monitor temperature and humidity levels in various environments. With its built-in sensor and user-friendly LCD display, this meter is ideal for tracking conditions in smaller spaces like terrariums and feeding areas. Key Features: Built-in Sensor: Provides accurate readings without external probes. Compact and Lightweight: Easily fits into small spaces. LCD Display: Clear and easy-to-read display for quick reference. Wireless Connectivity: Monitor conditions from a distance. Wide Temperature and Humidity Range: Measures temperatures from -50°C to 60°C and humidity from 10% to 99%. Low Power Consumption: Powered by two LR44 button batteries for long-lasting use. Applications: Terrariums and Vivariums: Monitor conditions for reptiles, amphibians, and insects. Home and Office Environments: Track indoor climate for comfort and efficiency. Storage Facilities: Monitor temperature and humidity in storage areas. Upgrade your environmental monitoring with the Wireless Temperature and Humidity Meter.

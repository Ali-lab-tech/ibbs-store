75A 650V ON Semiconductor 75T65SQDT IGBT with Soft Fast Recovery Diode- 📄 Product Description: The 75T65SQDT is a 650V, 75A N-channel Field-Stop Trench IGBT from ON Semiconductor. It features an integrated soft, fast-recovery diode for efficient reverse current handling, making it ideal for hard-switching applications such as welding inverters , motor drives , UPS systems , and solar inverters . Designed for high performance, this IGBT delivers low conduction loss , fast switching times , and excellent ruggedness.

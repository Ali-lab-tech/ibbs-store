The K1 Universal Dual Mode Power On Switch Clip is a professional mobile repair tool designed to help technicians power on smartphones and electronic devices directly during diagnostic and repair procedures. This specialised clip provides a convenient solution when the original power button is damaged, missing, or inaccessible during motherboard testing. Built with durable materials and precision-engineered contacts, the K1 switch clip delivers stable electrical connectivity for reliable power activation. Its universal compatibility makes it suitable for a wide range of Android smartphones and repair applications. The dual-mode functionality allows technicians to perform various testing operations efficiently, reducing repair time and improving workflow. Its compact and lightweight construction makes it an essential addition to any mobile repair toolkit.

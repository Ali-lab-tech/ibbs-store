Product details

Description Marked "T EL 3063 104V" on the package, this is Everlight Electronics' EL3063, a random-phase, zero-cross Triac driver optocoupler. It's a phototriac-output optoisolator that provides electrical isolation between a low-voltage control (LED) input and a high-voltage AC output stage, using an internal zero-cross detection circuit to switch the output only near the AC waveform's zero-crossing point — reducing electrical noise and inrush current stress when driving a triac. The device consists of a GaAs infrared LED optically coupled to a phototriac zero-voltage crossing detector, allowing it to directly or indirectly drive an external triac for controlling resistive and inductive AC loads such as solenoids, relays, and lamps, while providing high-voltage isolation between the control circuit and the mains-connected load. Key Specifications Type: Triac/SCR Output Optocoupler (Zero-Cross Triac Driver Photocoupler) Manufacturer: Everlight Electronics Co., Ltd. Package Marking: EL3063 / 104V (date/lot code) Package: 6-Pin SMD (SOP-6 / DIP-6 surface-mount variant) Isolation Voltage: 5000Vrms Peak Blocking Voltage: 600V (VDRM) Forward Current (input LED): 60mA max Power Dissipation: 330mW max Operating Temperature Range: -55°C to +100°C Channels: 1 (single-channel) Common Applications Triac/SCR trigger circuits for AC power control Solid-state relays Solenoid, relay, and lamp switching with mains isolation Motor control and industrial AC switching circuits requiring zero-cross switching to reduce EMI Package Includes 1 x Everlight EL3063 Zero-Cross Triac Driver Photocoupler (6-Pin SMD)

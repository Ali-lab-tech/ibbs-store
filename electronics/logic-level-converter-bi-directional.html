The 4 Channel Bi-Directional Logic Level Converter is a valuable tool for connecting devices with different voltage levels. This compact module allows you to safely step down 5V signals to 3.3V and step up 3.3V signals to 5V, ensuring compatibility and seamless communication. Key Features: Four Channels: Convert four signals simultaneously between 3.3V and 5V. Bi-Directional Conversion: Allows for bidirectional signal transmission. Wide Voltage Range: Compatible with 2.8V, 3.3V, and 5V devices. Flexible Pin Configuration: Use any pin as input or output. Easy to Use: Simple setup and operation. Breadboard Friendly: Ideal for prototyping and experimentation. Specifications: Number of Channels: 4 Voltage on Lx Side: 1.8V-5V Voltage on Hx Side: 2.8V-6V Power: 2.8V - 6V Conversion Method: MOSFET Based (without signal inversion) Dimensions: 0.5" x 0.6" Order Now: Bridge the voltage gap and connect your devices with ease using our 4 Channel Logic Level Converter.

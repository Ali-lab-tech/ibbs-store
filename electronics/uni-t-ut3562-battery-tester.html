Product details

The UNI-T UT3562 is a high-precision battery tester (battery internal-resistance & voltage analyzer) from the UT3560 series, for battery analysis, quality control and production testing of lithium, lead-acid and button-cell batteries. It measures internal resistance from micro-ohms to kilo-ohms and DC voltage up to 100 V, with a 4.3-inch TFT display, comparator sorting and RS-232/USB/Handler interfaces. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by Digilog.pk. Key Features High-precision measurement of battery internal resistance and DC voltage simultaneously Internal resistance from micro-ohms to kilo-ohms ; automatic & manual range selection 4.3-inch TFT LCD showing voltage and resistance together Built-in comparator (PASS / FAIL / IN) with upper & lower limits for sorting Interfaces: RS-232, USB, Handler (PLC) for remote control and automated production lines Suitable for lithium, lead-acid and button-cell batteries Technical Specifications Measurement Battery internal resistance & DC voltage Voltage Range Up to 100 V Resistance Range Micro-ohms to kilo-ohms (µΩ–kΩ) Range Selection Automatic / manual Display 4.3-inch TFT LCD (simultaneous V & R) Comparator PASS / FAIL / IN, upper & lower limits Terminals 4-terminal Kelvin (SOURCE/SENSE ×2) Interfaces RS-232, USB, Handler (PLC) Power AC 100–240 V, 50/60 Hz, 10 W max What's in the Box UT3562 battery tester Kelvin test leads / clips Power cord English user manual Downloads UNI-T UT3562 Product Page & Specifications Certifications: CE, RoHS | HS Code: 9030.39

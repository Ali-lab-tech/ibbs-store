Product details

AA Cell Spring Contact Set This AA battery contact set provides the positive spring terminal and negative flat terminal needed to build or repair a single-cell AA battery holder. It is suitable for DIY electronics, battery-powered projects, prototypes and custom PCB enclosures. Key Features Designed for standard AA (R6) battery cells Includes spring and flat battery-contact terminals Nickel-plated steel construction for good electrical contact and corrosion resistance Spring terminal maintains firm pressure on the battery cell Solder tabs for convenient wiring and mounting Suitable for new builds, battery-holder repairs and prototype projects Specifications Product type AA battery contact terminal set Compatible battery AA / R6 cylindrical cell Material Nickel-plated steel Positive contact Spring terminal Negative contact Flat plate terminal Connection Solder tab SKU B421, KRT78 Suitable Applications Custom AA battery holders DIY electronics and Arduino projects Battery-operated toys and small devices Repair or replacement of worn battery contacts PCB and enclosure prototypes Package Includes 1 × AA Cell Spring Contact Set: 1 × Spring contact terminal 1 × Flat contact terminal Battery cell and battery holder casing are not included. Important Notes Confirm battery polarity before wiring your circuit. Use suitable wire and soldering technique to avoid overheating the contact. For one AA cell, install the spring and flat contact at opposite ends of the battery holder.

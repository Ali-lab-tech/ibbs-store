AWM 20624 80C 60V VW-1 10pin FFC Flexible Flat Cables 295mm AWM 20624 : This indicates the wire classification, as per the UL (Underwriters Laboratories) standards. AWM stands for Appliance Wiring Material. 80C : The maximum temperature rating is 80 degrees Celsius. 60V : The voltage rating is 60 volts. VW-1 : This denotes that the cable has passed the VW-1 flame test, indicating it is flame retardant. 10-pin : The cable has 10 conductive paths or pins. FFC : Stands for Flexible Flat Cable, which means it is flat and flexible, often used for high-density electronic applications. 295mm : The length of the cable is 295 millimeters. Key Features: Flat and Flexible : Suitable for tight spaces and can bend easily without breaking. High-Density Applications : Ideal for connecting PCB boards, LCD displays, and other internal connections in electronic devices. Flame Retardant : Meets VW-1 standards, ensuring safety in case of fire. Durability : Can withstand a temperature of up to 80°C and a voltage of 60V, making it reliable for various electronic applications. Typical Uses: Internal wiring for electronic devices like laptops, printers, and other compact electronic gadgets. Connection between moving parts in devices due to its flexibility. Any application requiring a high number of connections in a small space. Physical Characteristics: Conductors : Typically made of tinned copper for good conductivity and resistance to oxidation. Insulation : Often made from polyester or polyimide to provide flexibility and durability. Pitch : The distance between the centers of two adjacent conductors, which is a crucial aspect for fitting into connectors and PCB layouts.

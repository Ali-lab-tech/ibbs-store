Product details

This is the battery clip accessory for the FNIRSI HRM-10 battery internal resistance tester. It holds a cylindrical cell such as an 18650 or 26650 between its contacts, so you can test cell after cell without holding probes. It connects to the HRM-10 through its round connector and is useful when sorting large batches of cells for pack building.

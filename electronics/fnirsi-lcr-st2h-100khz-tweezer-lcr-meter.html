Product details

The FNIRSI LCR-ST2H is the higher LCR-ST2 kit option. It measures with five test frequencies up to 100kHz, four test levels and a full-band sweep mode for seeing how a part behaves across frequency. It shows series and parallel equivalents side by side and can grade parts against a nominal value and tolerance. Contact us to confirm the exact accessories in this kit before ordering. Key Features 📏 R 10mΩ – 10MΩ, C 1pF – 22mF, L 1µH – 10H plus diode and ±30V 🎚️ Five test frequencies to 100kHz and full-band sweep 📊 D, Q, Z, X, θ and ESR with series/parallel equivalents shown together ✅ Tolerance sorting for fast OK/NG batch checks 🔄 Auto-rotating screen for left- or right-handed use Technical Specifications Resistance 10mΩ – 10MΩ Capacitance 1pF – 22mF Inductance 1µH – 10H Diode / voltage ≤0.7V; DC voltage ±30V Test frequency 100Hz, 120Hz, 1kHz, 10kHz, 100kHz Test level 0.1V / 0.3V / 0.6V / 1.0V RMS Parameters D, Q, Z, X, θ, ESR Basic accuracy 0.5% ± 3 digits (1kHz, mid ranges) Display 1.47" HD colour Battery 300mAh, ~3 h, Type-C Size 31 × 170.7 × 20.3 mm Weight ≈60.8 g What's in the Box 1 × LCR-ST2 tweezer meter Accessories as supplied in the ST2H kit (contact us to confirm)

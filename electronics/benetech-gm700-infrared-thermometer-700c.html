Product details

The BENETECH GM700 is a non-contact infrared thermometer for checking hot surfaces safely: motors, bearings, transformers, breakers, furnaces and cooking surfaces. It reads from −50 to 700℃ with a 12:1 distance-to-spot ratio and a laser pointer to show where you are aiming. Adjustable emissivity (0.10 to 1.00) gives correct readings on different materials, and MAX, MIN, AVG and DIF readings, high/low alarms and data store/recall help during inspections.

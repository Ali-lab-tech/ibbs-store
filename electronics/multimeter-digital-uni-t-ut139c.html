Product details

The UNI-T UT139C is a digital multimeter that offers a wide range of measurement capabilities for both professionals and hobbyists. It is designed to provide accurate and reliable readings for various electrical parameters. Here is a description of the features and specifications of the UNI-T UT139C: Display: The multimeter features a large, easy-to-read digital LCD display that shows the measured values clearly. The display is backlit, allowing for easy reading in low-light conditions. Measurement Functions: The UT139C offers a comprehensive set of measurement functions, including voltage (both AC and DC), current (AC and DC), resistance, capacitance, frequency, duty cycle, temperature, diode testing, and continuity testing. Auto and Manual Ranging: The multimeter supports both auto-ranging and manual-ranging modes. In auto-ranging mode, the device automatically selects the appropriate range for the measured quantity, simplifying the measurement process. Manual ranging allows the user to manually select the desired range for more control. True RMS: The UT139C is equipped with True RMS (Root Mean Square) technology, which ensures accurate measurements of AC voltage and current, even for non-sinusoidal waveforms. Data Hold and Relative Mode: The multimeter features a data hold function that allows the user to freeze the displayed value for easy reading and recording. The relative mode enables the user to measure the difference between two values, useful for zeroing out test leads or comparing readings. Input Protection: The multimeter has built-in overload protection for all ranges, safeguarding it from accidental damage due to excessive voltage or current. Connectivity: The UT139C comes with a standard input jack for connecting the test leads. It also has a built-in low battery indicator to alert the user when the battery needs replacement. Design and Build: The multimeter is designed with ergonomics in mind, featuring a rugged construction with a protective rubber holster that provides durability and impact resistance. The device is compact and lightweight, making it portable and easy to handle. Power Supply: The UT139C is powered by standard AA batteries, providing convenience and flexibility in terms of power source. Overall, the UNI-T UT139C is a versatile and reliable digital multimeter that offers a wide range of measurement functions and features....

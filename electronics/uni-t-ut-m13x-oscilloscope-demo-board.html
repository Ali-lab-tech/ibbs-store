The UNI-T UT-M13X is an oscilloscope demo board — a USB-powered signal source that outputs 40 types of standard test signals for demonstrating and learning basic oscilloscope functions (waveform display, triggering and measurement). Designed for use with UNI-T oscilloscopes in training, classroom and benchtop settings. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by Digilog.pk.

Product details

The BTA41800 is a high-power thyristor designed for robust switching and control in AC circuits. As a triac, it functions as a bidirectional electronic switch that allows current to flow in both directions after being triggered, making it ideal for phase-control applications. This component is suitable for a variety of power regulation tasks, including: AC motor speed control AC fan speed control AC dimmer phase angle control Heater temperature control Specifications include a maximum voltage (Vmax) of 800V and a maximum current (Imax) rating of 40A.

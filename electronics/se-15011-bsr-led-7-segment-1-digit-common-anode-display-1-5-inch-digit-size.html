In a common anode display, the anodes of all LEDs are connected together, and each cathode is controlled individually. In contrast, a common cathode display has all cathodes connected together, with each anode controlled separately. Q2: Can I use this display for outdoor applications? The SE-15011-BSR is designed for indoor use. For outdoor applications, consider displays with higher brightness and weather-resistant features. Q3: Is the display compatible with microcontrollers?

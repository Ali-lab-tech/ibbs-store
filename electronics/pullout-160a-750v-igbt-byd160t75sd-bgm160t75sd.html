Product details

The BYD160T75SD / BGM160T75SD is a 750V, 160A Insulated Gate Bipolar Transistor (IGBT) developed by BYD Semiconductor. It features advanced Trench Field Stop (TrenchFS) technology and a soft, fast-recovery anti-parallel diode, making it suitable for high-speed switching applications. Key Specifications: Collector-Emitter Voltage (V<sub>CES</sub>): 750V Collector Current (I<sub>C</sub>): 160A Package Type: TO-247 Plus For detailed information, refer to the BGM160T75SD datasheet .

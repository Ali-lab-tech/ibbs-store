Product details

The CA-508 Boost Board Module is a versatile 4-channel adjustable step-up converter designed to provide precise voltage regulation for LCD TCON (Timing Controller) boards. It efficiently manages the critical voltages required for optimal LCD performance, including VGL, VGH, VCOM, and AVDD. Key Features: Adjustable Output Channels: VCOM: 3V to 5V AVDD: 7.5V to 12V VGH: 17V to 23V VGL: -6V to -8V Input Voltage Range: 3.3V to 5V Compact Dimensions: 42mm x 25mm Applications: LCD Panel Repairs: Ideal for restoring functionality to malfunctioning LCD screens by providing stable voltage outputs. DIY Electronics Projects: Suitable for hobbyists working on custom display solutions or integrating LCDs into various devices. Educational Purposes: Useful in academic settings for teaching and experimenting with voltage regulation and display technologies.

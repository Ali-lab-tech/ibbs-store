Product details

The HX-4S-F100A is a robust Battery Management System (BMS) engineered specifically for 4-cell (4S) 3.2V LiFePO4 battery packs. Operating safely at 12.8V to 14.4V, this board features integrated balance protection to ensure each individual cell charges evenly. This maximizes battery lifespan and maintains optimal performance for your custom power banks, solar energy storage systems, and DIY electronics projects. The module includes a clearly labeled, on-board wiring diagram showing exact connections, making installation straightforward.

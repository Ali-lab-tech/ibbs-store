RA24A18 24GHz Presence Sensor Module – Human Motion Presence Sensor Module mmWave Radar Technology: 📝 Product Description The RA24A18 24GHz radar module is a compact presence sensor designed to detect whether people are in a room, moving around, or even staying still. Using advanced 24GHz FMCW radar technology , it can pick up everything from obvious movements like walking or turning, to small gestures like using a phone or flipping a book. Unlike traditional motion sensors, this module works even when people are sitting quietly, making it ideal for smart lighting, home automation, offices, and hotel energy-saving systems . It is also reliable in different weather and lighting conditions, and can sense through materials like glass, plastic, thin wood, or clothing .

Product details

The 0.75A 5-phase Vexta PMM33AH2 Stepper Motor offers precise positioning through step angle increments hence determined by the rotor and stator's pole structure,ensuring accuracy in movement. However, the motor's rotation in step angles can lead to vibration and changes in speed. Moreover to address this issue, microstep driving technology regulates the current to the motor coils. Allowing for smoother operation at slow speeds with minimal vibration and noise, additionally making it an ideal choice for applications requiring precision and quiet performance.

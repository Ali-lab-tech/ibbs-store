Product details

The UNI-T LM571LD-I is a professional green cross line laser level (1V1H) with wide 130° horizontal / 150° vertical fan lines and a tight ±2 mm at 10 m accuracy — ideal for tiling, drywall, ceilings, cabinetry and general layout where precision matters. The bright 510 nm green beam self-levels automatically within 3°±1°, reaches 20 m (over 30 m with a detector), and runs for more than 12 hours with all lasers on. A tripod mounting hole allows fixed setups. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by Digilog.pk with warranty and English manual.

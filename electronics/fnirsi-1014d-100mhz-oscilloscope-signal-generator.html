Product details

The FNIRSI 1014D is a compact benchtop oscilloscope with two 100MHz channels, 1GSa/s sampling and a 7-inch 800 × 480 colour display. Physical knobs and buttons make it feel like a full-size scope at a much lower price. A built-in DDS generator outputs 14 standard waveforms (sine up to 10MHz) plus a captured custom waveform, so you can drive and measure a circuit with one instrument. It stores up to 1000 screenshots and 1000 waveforms.

Product details

The RA24K45 is a 24GHz presence sensor switch designed for motion and occupancy detection applications. This sensor utilizes microwave radar technology to detect the presence of individuals within its sensing range, making it suitable for automated lighting control, security systems, and energy-saving electronics. Operating frequency: 24GHz Function: Presence and motion detection switching Application: Automated control systems and occupancy sensing

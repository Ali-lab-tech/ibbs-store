8 Channel 5V USB Relay Board Module Controller Upgrade your automation, smart home, or DIY electronics projects with the 8 Channel 5V USB Relay Board Module . Designed for seamless computer control, this USB-controlled relay module allows you to switch high-voltage and high-current devices directly from your PC, making it an essential tool for developers, hobbyists, and automation engineers. Equipped with high-quality Songle SRD-05VDC-SL-C relays and driven by a robust microcontroller setup, this board ensures reliable performance and safety for your electrical projects. Key Features & Specifications 8 Independent Channels: Control up to 8 separate devices or circuits simultaneously . USB Interface: Connects directly to your computer via a standard USB port for effortless programming and real-time control. Operating Voltage: 5V DC power supply, compatible with standard USB power. High-Capacity Relays: Each channel is capable of switching up to 10A at 250VAC or 10A at 30VDC , supporting a wide range of household appliances, lights, and heavy-duty electronics. Onboard Components: Features clear status indicator LEDs for each relay, a stable crystal oscillator, and reliable driver circuitry for precise switching. Easy Wiring: Equipped with secure screw terminals for straightforward and robust cable connections. Ideal Applications Home Automation & Smart Home Systems: Control lights, fans, and electronic gadgets from your computer. Industrial & DIY Automation: Perfect for custom machinery control, testing rigs, and robotics. IoT & Prototyping: Great for software-hardware integration projects requiring PC-based switching.

Product Parameters: The power supply voltage is 6.5V-27V. The power supply must not be connected or exceed 27V. Otherwise, the module may be burned out. The control signal voltage is 3-6.5V, which is the enable signal and the forward and reverse control signals, respectively. Enable signal terminal (ENA) input PWM adjustable speed, PWM frequency range 0-10KHZ, PWM minimum pulse width 10us. Working temperature -25 ° C -80 ° C. Mounting hole diameter: 3mm, pay attention to the prevent short circuit of the back circuit during installation, you can add insulation pad or copper column to raise the circuit board. Suitable for motor parameters : For motors with rated voltage of 24V, it is suitable to identify the rated power of 115W and below or the rated current of 7A or less. For motors with rated voltage of 12V, it is suitable to identify the rated power of 40W and below or the rated current of 7A or less. Precautions: The driver power supply must not be reversed.

Product details

The UNI-T UT338A is a high-precision indoor formaldehyde (HCHO) meter and air-quality detector using an imported electrochemical sensor. It continuously monitors formaldehyde concentration and also displays ambient temperature and humidity, with an audible alarm and blue backlight — ideal for homes, offices, newly furnished rooms and cars. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by Digilog.pk. Key Features Electrochemical formaldehyde (HCHO) sensor, range 0–3 mg/m³ Also measures temperature and humidity (°C/°F) Audible alarm for high formaldehyde; blue LCD backlight Rechargeable 3.7 V / 1500 mAh lithium battery Compact desktop design for home, office and automotive use Technical Specifications Formaldehyde Range 0–3 mg/m³ Formaldehyde Resolution 0.01 mg/m³ Formaldehyde Accuracy ±10% / ±0.02 Temperature Range 0–40 °C Humidity Range 25–90 %RH Battery 3.7 V / 1500 mAh rechargeable Li-ion Power Adapter 100–240 V, 50/60 Hz Dimensions 130 × 70 × 55 mm Weight 161 g What's in the Box UT338A formaldehyde meter USB charging cable English user manual Certifications: CE, RoHS | HS Code: 9027.89

Product details

The A2212/13T 1000KV Brushless DC Motor is designed for RC drones, quadcopters, RC airplanes, and DIY aircraft projects. It features a brushless outrunner design that provides efficient motor operation for hobby and educational applications. This motor is suitable for drone-building projects and remote-controlled aircraft. It works with a compatible Electronic Speed Controller (ESC), battery, and propeller.

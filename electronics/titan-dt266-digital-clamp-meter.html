Product details

The TITAN DT266 is the classic red clamp meter electricians carry for checking load current on a live wire without cutting it. Close the jaw around one conductor and read AC current up to 1000A, then switch to the test leads for DC and AC voltage, diode and continuity checks. It runs on a single 9V battery and has a 3½-digit display with data hold, so you can take a reading in a tight DB box and read it after you pull the meter out. Key Features ⚡ AC current up to 1000A through the clamp jaw, no need to break the circuit 🔧 DC voltage to 1000V and AC voltage to 750V with the test leads ✅ Continuity buzzer and diode test for quick wiring checks 📏 Data hold freezes the reading on the 1999-count LCD 🔋 Low-battery and overload indication Technical Specifications Model DT266 Brand TITAN AC Current up to 1000A, ±2.5% DC Voltage up to 1000V, ±0.5% AC Voltage up to 750V, ±1.2% Other functions Diode test, continuity buzzer, data hold Display 3½ digit LCD, max reading 1999 Power 9V battery Size 230 × 70 × 37 mm Optional accessory 500V insulation test adapter (not included) What's in the Box 1 × TITAN DT266 digital clamp meter

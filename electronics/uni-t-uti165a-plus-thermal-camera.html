Product details

The UNI-T UTi165A+ (also known as UTi165A) is a rugged, reliable thermal imaging camera for accurate temperature measurement and industrial diagnostics. Its 160 × 120 infrared detector captures clear thermal images for electrical inspections, mechanical diagnostics and building maintenance, with automatic hot/cold-spot tracking and temperature alarms. Built to an IP65-rated, drop-resistant design for demanding environments. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by Digilog.pk.

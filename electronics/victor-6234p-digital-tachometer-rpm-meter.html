Product details

The VICTOR 6234P is a non-contact digital tachometer for measuring the speed of motors, fans, pulleys, conveyor rollers and engines. Stick a piece of the supplied reflective tape on the shaft, aim the meter and read the RPM, with no need to touch the moving part. It reads from 2.5 to 99,999 RPM with 0.1 RPM resolution on a 5-digit LCD, and holds the reading so you can note it after stepping away from the machine. Key Features ⚙️ 2.5 to 99,999 RPM measuring range 🎯 0.1 RPM resolution , accuracy ±(0.05% + 1 digit) 🛡️ Non-contact : measure safely from a distance 📌 Data hold , auto power-off, low-battery indicator 🏷️ Reflective tape included Technical Specifications Model 6234P Brand VICTOR Measuring range 2.5 to 99,999 RPM Resolution 0.1 RPM Accuracy ±(0.05% + 1 digit) Sampling rate 4 times per second Display 5-digit LCD Power 4 × 1.5V AA batteries Size 157 × 64 × 31 mm Weight 156 g What's in the Box 1 × VICTOR 6234P tachometer Reflective tape

Product details

UNI-T UTG1042X — The UNI-T UTG1042X is a 40 MHz dual-channel arbitrary waveform generator built around a 16-bit DAC and 200 MSa/s sample rate. It is the bandwidth-extended member of the UTG1000X Essential-Series — identical chassis, sample rate, modulation set, burst modes, and DAC resolution as the UTG1022X, with double the top-end sine output frequency. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by Digilog.pk with warranty. Key Features 40 MHz sine / 20 MHz square & pulse — dual-channel output with 1 µHz frequency resolution. Double the bandwidth of the UTG1022X in the same chassis. 16-bit vertical resolution — 65,536 amplitude levels. 4× the resolution of typical 14-bit Essential-tier generators. 200 MSa/s sample rate — supports accurate waveform reconstruction across the 40 MHz output range. 200 built-in arbitrary waveforms — 4 kpts arbitrary waveform length for custom shapes; max arb output frequency 10 MHz. 7 modulation types — AM, FM, PM, ASK, FSK, PSK, PWM — internal and external source. FM frequency deviation extends to 20 MHz (vs 10 MHz on UTG1022X). Pulse burst function — three modes (N-cycle, gated, infinite) with internal or external trigger. Built-in 7-bit frequency counter — 100 mHz to 200 MHz input range.

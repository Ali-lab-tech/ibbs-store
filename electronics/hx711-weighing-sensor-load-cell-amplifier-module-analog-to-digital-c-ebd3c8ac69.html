Product details

Key Features: Dimensions : 33mm x 21mm Hx711 Chip : The core of the module is the Hx711 chip, a 24-bit precision analog-to-digital converter (ADC) designed specifically for load cell applications. Load Cell Interface : The module connects to strain gauge load cells, which convert physical pressure into electrical signals. It measures very small voltage changes from the load cell. High Precision : The Hx711 offers a high-resolution reading with a precision of up to 24 bits, ensuring accurate weight measurement. Low Power Consumption : Ideal for battery-operated devices. Selectable Gain : The module allows selectable gains of 32, 64, or 128 to adapt to different load cell outputs. Analog Input : It amplifies low-amplitude analog signals from the load cell and converts them into digital form for microcontroller interfaces like Arduino or Raspberry Pi. Simple Wiring : It uses only 2 data lines (clock and data) to communicate with the microcontroller, simplifying the design. Applications : Commonly used in digital weighing scales, industrial process control, and precision force sensors. Pin Description: VCC : Power supply (typically 2.6V to 5.5V) GND : Ground DT (Data) : Data output from the Hx711 to the microcontroller SCK (Clock) : Clock signal for synchronous data transfer This sensor module is widely used in electronic scales and pressure measurement systems due to its high accuracy and ease of use.

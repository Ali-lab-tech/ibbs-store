SKU: FNB41560-IPM

Product details

The FNB41560 is an advanced Motion SPM® 45 module providing a fully-featured, high-performance inverter output stage for AC Induction, BLDC, and PMSM motors. These modules integrate optimized gate drive of the built-in IGBTs to minimize EMI and losses, while also providing multiple on-module protection features including under-voltage lockouts and thermal monitoring. Designed for compact and efficient motor drive applications, the FNB41560 combines high-speed, low-loss IGBTs with comprehensive protection logic. It is ideal for low-power industrial applications such as washing machines, air conditioners, and industrial fans. Key Features: High Performance: 600V / 15A 3-phase IGBT inverter bridge including control ICs for gate driving and protection. Low Loss: Uses advanced NPT (Non-Punch Through) IGBT technology. Protection: Built-in Under-Voltage Lock-Out (UVLO) and Over-Current Protection. Thermal Monitoring: Built-in temperature sensing unit for precise thermal monitoring. Easy Integration: 3 separate open-emitter pins for Low-Side IGBTs for three-leg current sensing. Isolation: Isolation rating of 2000 Vrms / 1 min. Applications: Inverter motor drives for Washing Machines and Dryers. Air Conditioner compressors and fans. Industrial sewing machines. General purpose 3-phase motor controls (AC, BLDC).

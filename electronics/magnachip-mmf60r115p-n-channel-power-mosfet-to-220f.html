Here's an expanded version with a fuller description and a "Package Includes" section: Product Name: MagnaChip MMF60R115P N-Channel Power MOSFET (TO-220F) Description: The MagnaChip MMF60R115P is a high-voltage N-Channel power MOSFET built using MagnaChip's advanced Super Junction technology. This design allows the device to achieve a very low on-resistance (RDS(on)) and reduced gate charge compared to conventional MOSFETs of similar ratings, resulting in higher switching efficiency and lower power loss during operation. Rated for 600V and capable of handling up to 33A of continuous drain current, this MOSFET is well suited for demanding power electronics applications such as switch-mode power supplies (SMPS), motor drives, and DC-DC converters. It also features low EMI (electromagnetic interference) and low switching loss, along with excellent ESD (electrostatic discharge) protection — making it a reliable choice for industrial and consumer power designs. The device comes in a TO-220F package , which is the fully-insulated (molded) version of the standard TO-220. Unlike the standard TO-220 where the metal tab is electrically connected to the drain, the TO-220F has a plastic-molded body covering the tab, allowing it to be mounted directly onto a heatsink without needing a separate insulating pad — simplifying assembly and improving safety in tightly packed circuits. Key Specifications: Manufacturer: MagnaChip Semiconductor Part Number: MMF60R115P Type: N-Channel MOSFET (Super Junction technology) Drain-Source Voltage (VDSS): 600V Drain Current (ID): 33A RDS(on): 0.115Ω (max) Gate-Source Voltage (VGSS): ±30V Power Dissipation (PD): 35W Package Type: TO-220F (Isolated/Fully Molded, 3-Pin) Max Junction Temperature: 150°C Applications: Switch-mode power supplies (SMPS), motor drives, DC-DC converters, industrial power conversion, and general-purpose high-voltage switching circuits. Package Includes: 1 x MagnaChip MMF60R115P N-Channel Power MOSFET (TO-220F)

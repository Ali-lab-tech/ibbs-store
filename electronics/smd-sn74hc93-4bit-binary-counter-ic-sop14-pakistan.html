Product details

The SMD SN74HC93 is a 4-bit binary ripple counter IC in SOP-14 (SOIC-14) surface-mount package, designed for counting and frequency division applications in Pakistan. This CMOS logic integrated circuit features independent clock inputs and can be configured as a divide-by-2 and divide-by-8 counter, providing reliable counting operations for digital clocks, timers, frequency dividers, and control systems. The SMD package enables compact PCB layouts and automated assembly for professional electronics manufacturing and prototyping.

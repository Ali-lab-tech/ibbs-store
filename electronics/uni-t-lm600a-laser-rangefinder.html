Product details

The UNI-T LM600A (LM600) is a 6× monocular laser rangefinder that measures distance from 3 m up to 600 m with ±1 m accuracy — built for golf, hunting, shooting, archery and outdoor surveying. Look through the 6× optics, press once, and read the exact distance to your target on the in-view display. It goes well beyond plain distance: flagpole lock grabs the pin in front of background clutter, golf slope / trajectory compensation gives play-as distance, and it also measures speed, height, angle, area and volume . A rechargeable lithium battery (USB) powers it, and it fits in a pocket at just 171 g. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty and English manual.

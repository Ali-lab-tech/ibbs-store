Product details

20A 8S LiFePO4 BMS for 24V-Class Battery Packs The 6334-8S battery protection board is labelled for eight 3.2V LiFePO4 cells connected in series. It is intended for a 24V-class battery pack , with a calculated nominal voltage of 25.6V (8 × 3.2V). The manufacturer's label specifies a common charge/discharge port and a current rating of up to 20A . Key Features 8S configuration for eight series-connected LiFePO4 cells. 3.2V nominal cell chemistry, as stated on the label. 24V-class / 25.6V nominal battery-pack application. Common-port design for charging and discharging. Metal cover, plug-in cell-sense wiring harness and attached black probe visible in the product photo. Clearly labelled B− and P− connection points. Specifications Model 6334-8S Product type Battery management / protection board (BMS) Battery chemistry LiFePO4 / lithium iron phosphate Series count 8S Nominal cell voltage 3.2V Pack voltage 24V class; 25.6V nominal calculated from cell count Labelled current ≤20A, common port B− terminal Battery-pack negative P− terminal Shared charging and load negative Suitable Applications Use in compatible 8S LiFePO4 battery-pack builds or repairs where the load and charging requirements match this board. Check current requirements and starting surges before selecting a BMS. Installation and Compatibility This version is for 3.2V LiFePO4 cells . Do not substitute it for a BMS designed for 3.7V lithium-ion cells. Have a qualified battery technician verify the cell-sense connector sequence and all terminal connections before applying power. Do not determine connections from wire colour alone. Use a charger suitable for the completed LiFePO4 pack. Specification note: Balancing capability, protection thresholds, peak current, charge-current limits, temperature thresholds, dimensions and probe operation have not been verified for this exact model. No Bluetooth or app connectivity is specified. Package Includes 1 × 6334-8S battery protection board with the attached harness and probe shown. Battery cells and charger are not included.

Product details

The UNI-T UT261A is a professional 3-phase sequence indicator that instantly shows phase rotation (L1-L2-L3) and missing-phase faults on a large LCD — essential before connecting motors, pumps, compressors and any three-phase equipment where wrong rotation can cause damage. Connect the three colour-coded alligator clips to your supply and read the rotation direction and phase status directly, with a clear R (clockwise) / L (anti-clockwise) indication. It draws its power from the line under test, so no battery is required . Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty.

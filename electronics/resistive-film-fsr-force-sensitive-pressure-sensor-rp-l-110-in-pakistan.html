RP-L-110 FSR force-sensitive resistor pressure sensor resistive film pressure sensor flexible force-sensitive resistor Features: The film pressure sensor is flexible and slim, characterized by low energy consumption and fast response. The flexible pressure sensor can sense weak dynamic or static forces. The top layer is a flexible film and a pressure-sensitive layer folded on the film. The pressure detection range is 20g to 10 kg or more. Precise detection, fast response, long life. Specifications: Detection range: 20g ~ 10kg or more Trigger force: 20g, trigger when resistance <200kO default Length: 10cm overall length 11.5cm Thickness: 0.35mm Shape: sheet, flexible No trigger resistance: > 10MO Activation time: <0.01 s Hysteresis: + 10%, (RF + -RF -) / FR +, force 1000g Response time: <10 ms EMI electromagnetic interference: not generated ESD electrostatic discharge: not sensitive to ESD Drift: <5%, load 2.5 kg, 24 H Package Includes: 1 x RPL110 FSR sensor

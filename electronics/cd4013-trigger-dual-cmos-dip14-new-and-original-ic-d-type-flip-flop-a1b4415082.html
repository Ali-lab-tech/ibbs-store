Product details

CD4013 consists of two identical, independent data-type flip-flops. Each flip-flop of CD4013 has independent data, set, reset, and clock inputs and Q and Not Q outputs. These devices can be used for shift register applications, and, by connecting Q output to the data input, for counter and toggle applications.

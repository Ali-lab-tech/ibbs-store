Product details

Description: The AIFEN-A9 Pro smart soldering station is compatible with C115/C210/C245 handles. The soldering station kit can be used for BGA phone and PCB repair. Features: - The handle with buttons to control temperature and can be used to turn on/off - Larger plug-in box design adapts to C245/C210/C115 tips - Sleep bracket with adjustable angle - The temperature control of the program chip is different from the traditional AC voltage temperature control, and the temperature rises quickly and accurately - Automatic sleep: the handle is placed on the stand and it will immediately switch to sleep state. And it will heat up immediately when raised - The temperature compensation function automatically compensates the temperature of the soldering iron tip during soldering, so that the soldering iron tip is always kept at the set temperature - Mechanical short-circuit protection: when the machine is short-circuited, it will automatically stop operating to protect the machine - There are 4 sets of standby temperature (0°C, 150°C, 180°C & 200°C), and the standby temperature can be set according to your needs - Key lock, °C and °F conversion, buzzer and other functions Specifications: - Power: 120W maximum - Input voltage: Universal AC 220V/ AC 110V - Frequency: 50-60Hz - Temperature range: 100°C-450°C/212°F-842°F - Product size: 24 x 21 x 17.7cm/9.4 x 8.3 x 7" - G.W.: 1.55KG/3.4LB Package Included: - 1 x Set of AIFEN-A9 Pro Soldering Station

Product details

The SMD SN74HC125 is a quad 3-state bus buffer IC in SOP-14 (SOIC-14) surface-mount package, designed for bus interfacing and signal buffering applications in Pakistan. This CMOS logic integrated circuit contains four independent non-inverting buffers with 3-state outputs, providing reliable signal isolation and bus control for digital systems, microcontroller interfacing, and data communication. The SMD package enables compact PCB layouts and automated assembly for professional electronics manufacturing and prototyping.

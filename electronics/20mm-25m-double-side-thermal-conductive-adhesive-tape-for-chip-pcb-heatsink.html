Description: Ensure optimal heat transfer and secure mounting with our premium 20mm 25M Double-Sided Thermally Conductive Adhesive Tape . Specially designed for high-temperature electronics, this high-performance tape offers an efficient, mess-free alternative to thermal grease, paste, or mechanical fasteners when attaching heatsinks to chips, GPUs, CPUs, and PCBs . Featuring strong double-sided stickiness and excellent thermal conductivity, it creates a durable bond while effectively dissipating heat to protect sensitive electronic components from overheating . Key Features: High Thermal Conductivity: Efficiently transfers heat away from hot electronic components to heatsinks or cooling plates, preventing thermal throttling . Strong Double-Sided Adhesion: Holds heatsinks firmly in place without requiring mechanical screws, clips, or messy liquid paste . Generous 25-Meter Roll: Provides ample material (20mm wide × 25M long) for multiple DIY electronic repairs, production runs, or custom builds . High Temperature & Shock Resistance: Maintains strong bonding power and electrical insulation under elevated temperatures and physical vibrations. Easy Application: Simple to cut to custom lengths and shapes; peel-and-stick design ensures quick, clean installation . Key Applications: Component Cooling: Ideal for attaching aluminum or copper heatsinks to IC chips, CPUs, GPUs, RAM, MOSFETs, and microcontrollers . LED Lighting: Perfect for mounting high-power LED light strips, aluminum PCB boards, and backlight modules . Consumer Electronics: Used in 3D printers, SSD heat spreaders, power supplies, automotive electronics, and TV repair.

50V–90V to 24V 3A DC-DC Buck Converter Module – 72W Product Description This DC-DC Buck Converter Module is designed to step down high DC input voltages ranging from 50V to 90V to a stable 24V DC output. It supports an output current of up to 3A, delivering a maximum output power of 72W. Featuring a compact PCB design and an aluminum heatsink, this module is suitable for various power conversion applications, including electric vehicles, solar power systems, LED lighting, and industrial electronics. Do not exceed the specified input voltage range. Ensure that the connected load is compatible with the 24V output and does not exceed the module's maximum rated current and power.

Product details

The IP5328P Power Bank Module is a complete two-way fast-charging circuit board that turns ordinary 3.7V lithium cells (18650 or Li-Po) into a full-featured fast-charging power bank. Built around the IP5328P power management chip, it supports up to 18W bidirectional fast charging — it can fast-charge your phone and be fast-recharged itself through the same board. Just connect one or more 3.7V lithium batteries in parallel, and you get USB-A, USB-C, and Micro-USB ports with automatic 5V / 9V / 12V output, plus LED indicators showing the remaining battery level. It's an ideal ready-made core for DIY power bank builds and portable power projects. Key Features IP5328P power management chip with up to 18W two-way fast charging 4 ports: 2× USB-A outputs, 1× USB-C (input/output, PD), 1× Micro-USB input Supports QC2.0 / QC3.0, USB PD, FCP, AFC, and MTK fast-charge protocols Output voltage: 5V / 9V / 12V (auto-negotiated with the device) Works with 3.7V lithium batteries — 18650 or Li-Po, single cell or parallel pack 4 LED indicators for battery level and charge/discharge status Conversion efficiency up to 95% Built-in protection: overcharge, over-discharge, overcurrent, short-circuit, and over-temperature Compact board: approx. 65mm × 32.5mm Ideal For DIY power bank builds using 18650 or Li-Po cells Portable / backup power projects Fast-charge upgrades for custom battery packs Electronics hobbyists, students, and repair labs Note The module does not charge the battery and power a phone at the same time — the output port turns off while the battery itself is being charged. Batteries are not included. Package Includes 1 x IP5328P 18W Fast Charging Power Bank Module

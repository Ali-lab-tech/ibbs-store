Product details

The UNI-T UT383S is a pocket light (lux) meter that measures illuminance from 0 up to 199,900 Lux — handy for checking lighting levels in offices, classrooms, workshops, photography and horticulture without carrying a full-size meter. It reads in Lux or foot-candles with ±(4% + 8) accuracy, offers MAX/MIN and data hold, updates twice a second, and fits in a shirt pocket. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty and English manual.

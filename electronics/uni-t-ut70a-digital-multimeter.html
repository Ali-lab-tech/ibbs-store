Product details

UNI-T UT70A — a 2000-count multi-function hand-held digital multimeter that reaches well beyond a standard DMM: alongside AC/DC voltage and current it measures inductance to 20 H , capacitance to 100 µF, frequency to 10 MHz , K-type temperature to 1000°C, transistor hFE and TTL logic levels . With data hold, maximum-value hold, backlight and auto power-off, it is a capable single-instrument tool for electronics development and servicing. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by Digilog.pk with warranty.

Product details

INA226 CJMCU-226 Current Shunt & Power Monitor Module The INA226 is a precision current shunt and power monitor with an I²C/SMBus-compatible interface. It monitors both shunt voltage drop and bus supply voltage, enabling direct readouts of current in amperes and power in watts — making it ideal for embedded power management, battery monitoring, and test equipment applications. Key Features Senses bus voltages from 0 V to 36 V (high-side or low-side sensing) Reports current, voltage, and power directly High accuracy: 0.1% gain error (max) , 10 μV offset (max) Configurable averaging options for noise reduction 16 programmable I²C addresses — connect multiple modules on one bus Operates from a single 2.7 V to 5.5 V supply (~330 μA typical) Wide operating temperature: –40°C to +125°C Compact 10-pin VSSOP (DGS) package on breakout board Pin Description IN+ / IN– : Non-inverting and inverting analog inputs (connect across shunt resistor) VBUS : Bus voltage input SDA / SCL : I²C data and clock lines ALERT : Multi-function programmable alarm output A0 / A1 : I²C address selection pins GND / VCC : Power supply pins Applications Power management & battery chargers Servers & telecom equipment Computing & embedded systems Power supply monitoring & test equipment

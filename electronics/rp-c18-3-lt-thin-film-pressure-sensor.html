Operating Temperature Range: Suitable for various environments, often from -20°C to +85°C. Response Time: The sensor typically has a quick response time, allowing it to detect and measure pressure changes in real time. Power Requirements: Low power consumption, making it suitable for battery-powered or energy-efficient devices.

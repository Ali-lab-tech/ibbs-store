Product details

74245 Octal Bus Transceiver IC – DIP-20 Package The 74245 octal bus transceiver IC provides bidirectional bus communication in a compact 20-pin DIP package. Its 3-state outputs drive bus lines directly, while PNP inputs reduce DC loading on the bus. Hysteresis at the bus inputs improves noise margins. For a digital circuit that needs bidirectional bus interfacing, this DIP-20 bus transceiver brings together direct bus-line drive and a typical port-to-port propagation delay of 8 ns. Key Features Bidirectional bus transceiver in a high-density 20-pin package. 3-state outputs drive bus lines directly. PNP inputs reduce DC loading on bus lines. Hysteresis at bus inputs improves noise margins. Typical port-to-port propagation delay: 8 ns. Typical enable/disable times: 17 ns. IOL (sink current): 24 mA. IOH (source current): −15 mA. Specifications Package: DIP-20. Supply Voltage (VCC): 7 V. Input Voltage (VI): 7 V. Operating free-air temperature range: 0°C to +70°C. Storage temperature range: –65°C to +150°C.

Product details

The UNI-T UT351 is a professional sound level (decibel) meter for measuring noise from 30 dB to 130 dB with ±1.5 dB accuracy — for workplace safety, environmental noise surveys, HVAC, machinery and quality control. It offers both A and C frequency weighting , Fast and Slow response, a 31.5 Hz–8 kHz frequency range, plus AC and DC analogue outputs for connecting to a recorder or analyser. Data hold and auto power-off make field use easy. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty and English manual.

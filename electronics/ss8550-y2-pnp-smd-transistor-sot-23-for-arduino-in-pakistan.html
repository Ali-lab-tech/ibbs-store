FAQ Q: Kya yeh Arduino ke 5V GPIO se directly drive ho sakta hai? A: Yes, but always use a base resistor (4.7kΩ–10kΩ) between the GPIO pin and the base to limit current and protect your microcontroller. Q: NPN aur PNP mein kya farq hai — SS8550 kab use karein? A: SS8550 is PNP — it switches ON when the base is pulled LOW. Use it for high-side switching (load between VCC and collector). For low-side switching, use an NPN like SS8050. Q: Kya yeh 1A se zyada current handle kar sakta hai? A: The absolute max is 1.5A, but for reliable operation keep it under 1A and ensure adequate PCB copper area for heat dissipation.

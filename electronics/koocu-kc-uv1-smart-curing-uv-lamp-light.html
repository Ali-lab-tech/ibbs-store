Portable Design : Compact and lightweight, perfect for both industrial and personal use. Frequently Asked Questions What is a UV curing lamp used for? UV curing lamps are used to harden or "cure" materials that are sensitive to ultraviolet light. Common applications include: * Nail Salons: Curing UV gel polish on nails. * 3D Printing: Curing resin-based 3D printed objects. * Jewelry Making: Curing UV resin used in jewelry making. * Industrial Applications: Curing adhesives, coatings, and inks. How long does it take to cure UV gel with this lamp? The curing time can vary depending on the specific UV gel used, the thickness of the gel layer, and the lamp's intensity. The Koocu KC-UV1 is designed for fast curing, typically within 60-90 seconds . However, it's always best to follow the instructions on the UV gel product packaging for the recommended curing time. Is this lamp safe to use? Yes, the Koocu KC-UV1 is generally safe to use when used as directed. Avoid direct eye exposure to the UV light. Wear protective eyewear if you are working close to the lamp for extended periods. Keep the lamp away from children. Can I use this lamp for curing resin jewelry? Yes, the Koocu KC-UV1 is excellent for curing resin jewelry. Ensure the resin is placed within the curing range of the lamp. Rotate or flip the jewelry piece during curing to ensure even hardening. Follow the instructions on your resin kit for the recommended curing time and any specific precautions. Package Include 1x KC-UV1 Smart Curing UV Lamp Light

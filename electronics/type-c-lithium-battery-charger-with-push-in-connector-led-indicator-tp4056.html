TYPE-C Lithium Battery Charger with Push-in Female Connector and LED Indicator This TYPE-C Lithium Battery Charger is designed to safely and efficiently charge lithium-ion batteries using the popular TP4056 IC. With a compact form factor and a push-in female Type-C connector, this module eliminates the need for cutting or modifying enclosures. Featuring a built-in LED indicator, it displays charging status—red while charging and green when fully charged. Ideal for DIY electronics, battery-powered projects, and compact device integration. Key Features Charging IC: TP4056 for reliable lithium battery charging Output Voltage: 4.2V Maximum Charging Current: 1A Connector: Type-C push-in female (no enclosure cutting required) Charging Indicator: Red LED when charging, green when fully charged Weight: 10 grams Benefits Compact and easy-to-install in most enclosures Modern Type-C interface for universal compatibility Automatic charge termination for safe battery use Great for portable electronics, robotics, and embedded systems Applications DIY rechargeable battery packs Power banks and portable devices Embedded lithium-powered systems Battery charging modules for electronics projects FAQs Q: Is this charger safe for 3.7V lithium-ion batteries? Yes, it is designed to charge 3.7V lithium-ion batteries to a full charge of 4.2V. Q: Can it be used with any USB Type-C cable? Yes, any standard Type-C cable can be used for charging. Q: Does it support overcharge protection? Yes, the TP4056 IC includes overcharge protection and automatic charge cut-off.

Product details

Description Marked "MUR1060F" on the package, this is a super-fast/ultrafast rectifier diode housed in the isolated two-lead ITO-220AC package, with the diode schematic symbol printed directly on the body confirming polarity. This series is designed to meet general commercial application requirements by providing low reverse recovery time (Trr), high-temperature tolerance, low leakage, and low forward voltage drop. The isolated package construction means it can typically be mounted directly to a heatsink without a separate insulating mica pad, simplifying thermal design in dense boards. Key Specifications Type: Ultrafast Recovery Rectifier Diode Package Marking: MUR1060F (multiple manufacturers produce this generic part — GOOD-ARK, Diode Semiconductor Korea, DIYI, and others all list functionally equivalent MUR1060F datasheets) Package: ITO-220AC, isolated, 2-lead through-hole Reverse Voltage: 600V Forward Current: 10A Forward Voltage Drop (VF): 1.7V Peak Forward Surge Current: 150A Reverse Leakage Current (IR): 1µA Reverse Recovery Time (trr): 60ns Junction Temperature Range: -55°C to +150°C Common Applications Output rectifiers in switch-mode power supplies (SMPS) and DC-DC converters Free-wheeling or boost diode in converters and motor control circuits Anti-parallel diode for high-frequency switching devices such as IGBTs Uninterruptible power supplies (UPS) Inductive heating, melting, ultrasonic cleaning, and welding equipment Package Includes 1 x MUR1060F Ultrafast Rectifier Diode (ITO-220AC)

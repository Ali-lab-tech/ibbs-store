Product details

Expand your Raspberry Pi 5 with blazing-fast NVMe storage and PCIe accessories using the Official Raspberry Pi M.2 HAT+ (M Key) . Designed specifically for Raspberry Pi 5, this HAT+ adapter bridges the Pi's PCIe interface to a standard M.2 M key edge connector — perfect for engineers, makers, and hobbyists who demand high-speed storage performance. Key Features ⚡ Single-lane PCIe 2.0 interface with up to 500 MB/s peak transfer rate 🔌 Supports M.2 M key edge connector devices 📐 Compatible with 2230 and 2242 form factors (NVMe SSDs & PCIe accessories) 🔋 Supplies up to 3A of power to connected M.2 devices 💡 Onboard power and activity LEDs for easy monitoring 🤖 HAT+ spec compliant — Raspberry Pi OS auto-detects the board and connected devices ❄️ Threaded spacers included for compatibility with the Raspberry Pi Active Cooler Package Includes 1× Raspberry Pi Official M.2 HAT+ (M Key) Threaded spacers and mounting hardware Compatibility Note: Compatible with Raspberry Pi 5 only. Fits inside the official Raspberry Pi 5 Case with lid and fan removed.

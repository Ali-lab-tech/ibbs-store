SKU: FNIRSI-CTG-20

Product details

The FNIRSI CTG-20 measures paint and coating thickness on steel and non-ferrous metal surfaces from 0 to 1400µm. Used cars are its most common job: uneven readings across panels show repainting or body filler. It reads in µm or mil with 0.1µm resolution, supports zero and multi-point calibration, and charges over USB-C. Readings on a curved panel need a convex radius of at least 5mm and an area of at least 20mm.

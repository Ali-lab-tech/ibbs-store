Product details

Description: This module turns any standard computer ATX power supply into a bench-top power supply, splitting the standard 24-pin ATX cable into four different power outputs, each with a separate ground and output fuse. Simply plug in your ATX PSU's 24-pin connector to gain instant access to multiple regulated voltage rails — no rewiring or soldering required. Specifications: Parameter Value Model No. XH-M229 Output Voltages +3.3V, +5V, +12V, and -12V Connector Standard 24-pin ATX port (compatible with ATX Silver Box SMPS), also works with 20-pin PSUs Switch Yes — on-board power switch Fusing Each output channel individually protected with a replaceable 5A fuse Power Indicator On-board LED power indicator Dimensions 128 x 48 x 30mm Weight 60g Terminal Layout: 8x binding post terminals (color-coded red = positive, black = ground/negative) providing quick-access screw connections for each voltage rail Native 24-pin ATX female header for direct PSU connection Key Features: Converts any ATX/BTX PC power supply into a multi-rail bench power supply Individual fuse protection per output channel — prevents overload damage to the board and connected devices Toggle switch for instant power ON/OFF without unplugging the PSU Compact PCB design, ideal for electronics prototyping, testing, and DIY power supply builds Ideal For: Electronics hobbyists, PC hardware testing, DIY bench power supply projects, and repurposing old/spare ATX power supplies for lab use. Package Includes: 1x XH-M229 ATX Power Supply Breakout Board

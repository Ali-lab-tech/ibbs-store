MM75N65 IGBT - High-Efficiency Power Transistor for Industrial Applications The MM75N65 IGBT is a high-efficiency insulated-gate bipolar transistor designed for industrial applications. It offers a voltage rating of 650V and a continuous current capacity of 75A, ensuring fast switching with minimal power loss. Ideal for use in motor drives, inverters, and energy systems. Benefits of MM75N65 IGBT High Voltage Capacity: Operates up to 650V for industrial applications. Fast Switching Speed: Reduces power loss, enhancing system efficiency. High Current Handling: Supports continuous operation at 75A. Thermal Stability: Reliable performance across extreme temperature ranges. Compact TO-247 Package: Easy to integrate into circuit designs.

Product details

A DC (Direct Current) electric gear motor is a type of electric motor that is designed to convert electrical energy into mechanical energy, providing rotational motion. These motors are widely used in a variety of applications, including robotics, automotive systems, industrial machinery, and more. Here's a description of its features and specifications: Features: Direction of Rotation Control: DC gear motors allow precise control over the direction of rotation by simply reversing the polarity of the applied voltage. Variable Speed: They can operate at different speeds, making them suitable for various applications where speed control is essential. High Torque: DC gear motors are known for their high torque output, which makes them capable of handling heavy loads and providing sufficient power for tasks like lifting and moving objects. Compact Size: These motors come in compact sizes, making them suitable for applications with limited space. Efficiency: DC gear motors are generally efficient, meaning they convert a high percentage of electrical energy into mechanical work. Reliability: They are known for their reliability and long lifespan, especially when properly maintained. Specifications: Voltage Rating: DC gear motors typically operate at specific voltage ratings, such as 12V, 24V, or 48V, depending on the application's requirements. Speed: The speed of a DC gear motor is measured in revolutions per minute (RPM). It can vary widely based on the motor's design and intended application. Some gear motors have adjustable speed settings. Torque: Torque is the rotational force generated by the motor and is measured in Newton-meters (Nm) or ounce-inches. DC gear motors are selected based on their torque output to match the specific load requirements of the application. Gear Ratio: The gear ratio determines how much the motor's output shaft rotates for each rotation of the motor's input shaft. A higher gear ratio provides more torque but reduces speed. Gear ratios are typically specified in the form of X:1, where X represents the number of input shaft rotations required for one output shaft rotation. Motor Type: There are various types of DC gear motors, including brushed and brush less.

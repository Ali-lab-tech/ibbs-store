YIHUA 995D+ 2 in 1 Hot Air Rework and Soldering Iron Station The YIHUA 995D+ puts a hot air rework gun and a temperature-controlled soldering iron into one ESD-safe unit, so you can lift an SMD chip and solder the replacement back without moving to another bench. Both tools run from the same station, and both temperatures stay on the backlit LCD together with a live wind-speed bar for the air gun. What separates the 995D+ from the older 995D is the three-channel memory. CH1, CH2 and CH3 each hold a complete profile - iron temperature, air temperature, airflow, auto or manual mode, sleep time, calibration offset and the Celsius/Fahrenheit setting - so your preheat profile and your final profile are one button apart instead of a menu dive. A PID algorithm holds the set point to within plus or minus 1 degree C. Key Features 🔥 2 in 1 rework station - hot air gun plus soldering iron in a single ESD-safe unit for SMD removal and rework. 🎛️ Three user channels - CH1, CH2 and CH3 each store temperature, airflow, scale, mode, sleep time and calibration. 📏 PID temperature control - temperature fluctuation held to within plus or minus 1 degree C for repeatable joints. 🎯 Digital calibration - minus 50 to plus 50 degree C compensation lets you trim the reading against a reference thermometer. 🖥️ Backlit LCD with wind speed bar - soldering and hot air temperatures shown at the same time, with a dynamic airflow display. 💤 Sleep mode, 0 to 99 minutes - the iron cools down when idle, which protects the tip and saves power. ❄️ Cool air function - press and hold the rotary knob to blow unheated air and bring a board back to room temperature quickly. 🔁 Manual and automatic hot air modes - switchable on the fly, along with Celsius and Fahrenheit display. 🛡️ Protection and alarms - high temperature auto protection with alarm, and the air gun self-detects faults and alerts you. ⚙️ Rotary encoder control - one digital encoder sets airflow and opens the menu when pressed, so the panel stays uncluttered. ⚡ ESD safe - safe around MOSFETs, microcontrollers and other static-sensitive components.

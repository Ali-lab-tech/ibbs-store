Product details

The Obstacle Detector sensor module is perfectly adaptable to the environment light, IR Object Avoidance Sensor has a pair of infrared transmitting and receiving tubes; transmitting tube emits infrared in a certain frequency, when detecting direction meets with obstacles(reflecting surface), reflected infrared will be received by the receiving tube; after the comparator circuit processing, the green indicator will light up, at the same time signal output interface to output digital signal(1 low-level signal). Users can adjust the detection distance through the potentiometer knob, effective distance range 2~30cm and working voltage is 3.3V to 5V. The Obstacle Avoidance module LM393 can be widely used in robot obstacle avoidance, obstacle avoidance car, line count, black and white line tracking, and so on many occasions. Features Of IR Object Detector Sensor Module: Easy to assemble and use Onboard detection indication The effective distance range of 2cm to 20cm A preset knob to fine-tune distance range There is an obstacle, the green indicator light on the circuit board.

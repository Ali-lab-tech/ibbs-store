Product details

This is DC to DC converter IC. It is normally used to design step-down, step-up, or inverter circuits. This IC can be found in automobile phone chargers to regulate the flow of current. It is very efficient than linear circuits. The input voltage for this IC is 4V to 40V. And the output voltage is 2V to 40V. So if you are looking for a DC-DC converter IC, then this might be the right choice for you. Note:- Read the specifications carefully. Proper knowledge is mandatory.

BQ25570 Energy Harvester Module Step-Up Boost Charger & Buck Converter The BQ25570 Energy Harvester Module offers a highly integrated nanopower management solution for ultra-low-power applications. This module efficiently manages power from sources like solar panels, thermoelectric generators (TEG) , and piezoelectric modules , making it ideal for wireless sensor networks (WSNs) . The module integrates a boost charger to extract power from inputs as low as 100mV , ensuring continuous energy harvesting. It also features a buck converter to support a variety of storage components such as rechargeable batteries and capacitors .

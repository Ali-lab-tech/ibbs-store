Product details

The Raspberry Pi Zero Official Case has been designed to fit both the Pi Zero V1.3 and the Pi Zero W. The case consists of two parts. It has a standard base featuring a cut-out to allow access to the GPIO and a choice of three lids: a plain lid, a GPIO lid (allowing access to the GPIO from above), and a camera lid (which, when used with the short camera cable supplied, allows the Raspberry Pi Camera or Camera Noir to be fitted neatly inside it).

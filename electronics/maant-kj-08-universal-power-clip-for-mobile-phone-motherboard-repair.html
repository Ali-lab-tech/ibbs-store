Product details

MAANT KJ-08 Universal Power Clip for Phone Repair The MAANT KJ-08 is a compact power-connection tool for mobile-phone motherboard startup testing and fault diagnosis. Its spring-clamp design lets a technician contact suitable motherboard power points without soldering a dedicated battery connector lead. Key Features Spring-loaded clamp with power contact pin and separate ground clip. Physical button for switching the connected power on and off. Red and black banana-plug leads for a suitable external DC bench power supply. Supplier-advertised voltage indicators, automatic mA/A current indication and input reverse-polarity protection. Designed for repair benches working with compatible phone motherboards. Specifications Brand MAANT / MaAnt Model KJ-08 Type Universal power clip / motherboard power adapter Approximate product size 91 × 25 × 20 mm, supplier stated Approximate net weight 40g, supplier image Supplier-listed gross weight 60g Use and Compatibility Check motherboard pinout, polarity, voltage and current settings before connection. Universal design does not guarantee compatibility with every phone. An external regulated DC supply is required and is not included. This tool is not a standalone power supply. Package Includes 1 × MAANT KJ-08 universal power clip assembly with leads. Phone and bench supply shown in demonstration images are not included.

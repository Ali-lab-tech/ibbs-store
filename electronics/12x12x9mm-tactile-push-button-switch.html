12x12x9mm Tactile Push Button Switch is used as general-purpose PCB tactile pushbutton. Tactile Push Button Switch colour may differ from the image. This is a standard 12mm square momentary button. What we really like is the large button head and good tactile feel (it ‘clicks’ really well). This button is great for user input on a PCB or a good, big reset button on a breadboard. Breadboard friendly! Features of 12x12x9mm Tactile Push Button Switch: NO (Normally Open) contacts. Good snap action feels with an audible click. 4-terminal, these switches are 4-terminal normally open pushbuttons. Pins are formed solder type on 5.08mm (0.2″) centres on one side x 12.7mm (0.5″) and are breadboard friendly. Breadboard friendly. The 4-pin layout works well in solderless breadboards or perf boards.

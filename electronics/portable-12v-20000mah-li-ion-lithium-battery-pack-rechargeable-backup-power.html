Product details

This is portable high-capacity 12V 20,000mAh Li-ion rechargeable battery pack, designed to provide reliable backup power for your 12V DC devices. Ideal for portable use , CCTV cameras, fishing lamps, and other compatible equipment, this battery pack ensures extended usage with its substantial capacity. Key Features: High Capacity: With a true 20,000mAh capacity, it offers prolonged power supply for your devices. Built-in ON/OFF Switch: Conserve energy with the integrated power switch. Overcharge/Discharge Protection: Equipped with safety features to prevent overcharging and over-discharging, ensuring longevity and safety. Charging Indicator: Includes a working indicator to monitor charging status. Specifications: Weight: Approximately 630g Color: White Capacity: 20,000mAh Discharge Current: 5A (customizable up to 10A upon request) Battery Type: Rechargeable Li-ion Output Voltage: 7.5V to 12.6V DC Charging Current: 3,000mAh Charging Voltage: 12.6V Lifespan: Over 1,000 charge cycles Applications: Suitable for a wide range of 12V devices, including: Wireless cameras Surveillance systems Portable DVDs Personal data assistants (PDAs) Walkie-talkies Digital cameras Bluetooth devices LED lighting Charging Instructions: Initiate Charging: Connect the charger; a red light indicates charging has commenced. Monitor Progress: Charging typically takes over 8 hours. Completion: Once the red light turns green, the battery is fully charged. Package Includes: 1 x 12V 20,000mAh Li-ion Battery Pack 1 x High-Quality 12.6V 3A Charger (EU or US plug)

Product details

The UNI-T UT3248+ is a 48-channel multi-channel temperature recorder (paperless temperature data logger) for simultaneous multi-point thermocouple measurement and logging. It supports eight thermocouple types across a −200 to 1800°C range with 0.01°C resolution, a 4.3-inch true-colour display (numeric / curve / histogram views) and USB, RS-232 and LAN interfaces with SCPI and MODBUS RTU.

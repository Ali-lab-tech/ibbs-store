Product details

The MC1458P is a dual operational amplifier integrated circuit (IC) commonly used in electronic circuits. It features two independent op-amps within a single package, making it convenient for designing circuits that require multiple amplification stages or signal processing. The IC is designed to operate with a wide range of power supplies and offers a bandwidth of 1 MHz, making it suitable for various audio and low-frequency signal applications. Users can easily incorporate the MC1458P into their projects on a breadboard or PCB (Printed Circuit Board) to amplify, filter, or process analog signals with precision. It is a versatile and widely available component in the field of electronics, making it a popular choice for both hobbyists and professionals. Features: Short-Circuit Protection Wide Common-Mode and Differential Voltage Ranges No Frequency Compensation Required Low Power Consumption No Latch-Up Designed to Be Interchangeable with Motorola MC1458 Specification: The MC1458 is a dual operational amplifier, with each half electrically similar to the uA741, except that offset null capability is not provided. The high-common-mode input voltage range and he absence of latch-up make these amplifiers ideal for voltage-follower applications.

Product details

Description This is a compact 5V 1A USB Type-C lithium battery charging module , designed for charging and powering single-cell 18650 (or similar) Li-ion/LiPo batteries in small DIY electronics and handheld device builds. It combines a battery charging circuit with a one-button power switch and motor output terminals, making it a convenient all-in-one board for battery-powered projects. The module accepts a standard USB Type-C 5V input and charges the connected battery at up to 1A , with dedicated BAT+ / BAT- pads for simple battery wiring. A built-in tactile push button (K1) serves as the power on/off switch, controlling output to the onboard M+ / M- terminals — ideal for directly driving a small DC motor or load from the battery with one-touch control. Its compact size and integrated charge-and-switch functionality make it a practical choice for mini fans, handheld gadgets, flashlights, and other small motorized or battery-powered DIY builds where you want charging and power control combined on a single small board.

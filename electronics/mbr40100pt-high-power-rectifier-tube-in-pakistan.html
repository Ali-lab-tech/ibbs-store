Product details

The MBR40100PT High Power Rectifier 40.0 AMPS Schottky Barrier Rectifiers are designed with plastic material that meets Underwriters Laboratory Classifications 94V-0, ensuring safety and reliability. These rectifiers feature metal silicon junction with majority carrier conduction, resulting in low power loss and high efficiency. With high current capability, low forward voltage drop, and high surge capability, they are ideal for use in low voltage, high frequency inverters, free wheeling, and polarity protection applications. The guardring provides overvoltage protection, high temperature soldering up to 260 º C/10 seconds is guaranteed.

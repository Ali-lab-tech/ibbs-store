SKU: FNIRSI-WD-02

Product details

The FNIRSI WD-02 wall detector finds rebar, metal pipes, wood studs and live AC wiring behind walls, floors and ceilings before you drill. It is the upgraded version of the WD-01, with an HD TFT screen, new interface and three sensitivity levels. The screen shows the target's depth and material, and a centre-locating hole lets you mark the exact drilling point. It recharges over Type-C. Key Features 🧱 Ferrous metal to 120mm copper to 100mm ⚡ Live AC wire to 50mm 🪵 Wood detection to 38mm in depth mode 🎚️ 3-level sensitivity and auto calibration 🎯 Centre-locating hole for marking drill points Technical Specifications Ferrous metal 120mm max Non-ferrous (copper) 100mm max AC wire 50mm max Single-strand copper wire 40mm max Wood 20mm (precision) / 38mm (depth mode) Display HD TFT Alarm Buzzer and indicator light Auto off 5 min Battery 3.7V 300mAh, Type-C Size 138 × 68 × 22 mm Weight 122 g What's in the Box 1 × FNIRSI WD-02 wall scanner USB cable as supplied by FNIRSI

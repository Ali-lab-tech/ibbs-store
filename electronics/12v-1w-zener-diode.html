A Zener diode is a special kind of diode which allows current to flow in the forward direction like a regular diode, but also allows it to flow in the reverse direction when the voltage is above a certain value. Hence this is known as the ‘breakdown voltage’. That means you apply voltage across them, and when a present voltage is reach. They start conducting. A common analogy is a ‘pressure relief valve’. They have lots of uses, especially in safety circuits where you want to make sure a voltage signal doesn’t exceed a limit that might damage the circuit. This 1N4742A Zener diode is set at 12V, making it ideal for protecting the inputs of a 12V circuit. Zener diodes are often use to stabilize and clip circuits.

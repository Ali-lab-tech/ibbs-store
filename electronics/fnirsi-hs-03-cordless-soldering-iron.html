Product details

The FNIRSI HS-03 is a true cordless soldering iron with a replaceable 2600mAh 18650 battery, so you can solder on site or away from a socket. Plug in USB-C power and it keeps working from the cable. It heats in about 7 seconds, holds temperature with a PID controller from 100 to 450°C, and uses F210 precision tips. A 0.96-inch IPS display, 5 presets and auto sleep make it practical for daily repair work. Key Features 🔋 2600mAh replaceable battery plus USB-C powered mode ⚡ Melts solder in about 7 seconds 🌡️ 100 – 450°C PID control, 5 presets ✏️ F210 tips K, I and IS tips included 💤 Auto sleep and standby left/right-hand modes Technical Specifications Temperature range 100 – 450°C (212 – 842°F) Power 10W max Working voltage 5V, Type-C Battery 2600mAh 18650, replaceable Tip type F210 Display 0.96" IPS Weight ≈134 g What's in the Box 1 × HS-03 iron with 2600mAh battery and K tip 1 × pen cap 1 × I tip and 1 × IS tip 1 × screwdriver 1 × USB cable 1 × storage box

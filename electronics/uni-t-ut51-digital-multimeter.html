Product details

UNI-T UT51 — a 2000-count hand-held digital multimeter from the UT50 series, built around a dual-integral A/D converter with full-range overload protection. The UT51 is the high-current-resolution model of the series: it is the only UT50 that offers 20 µA and 200 µA current ranges alongside a 10 A range, making it well suited to electronics bench work, education and appliance servicing. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by Digilog.pk with warranty.

Product details

This DC voltmeter and ammeter display panel provides simultaneous monitoring of voltage and current levels. It is designed for integration into electrical systems, power supplies, or test benches to track DC performance in real-time. The unit includes a shunt, which is required for accurate current sensing and measurement. This panel is suitable for technicians and hobbyists who need a clear, digital readout for DC circuits operating up to 100V and 50A.

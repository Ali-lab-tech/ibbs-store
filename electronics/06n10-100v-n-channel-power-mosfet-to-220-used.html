Product details

Description The 06N10 is an N-channel power MOSFET housed in the industry-standard TO-220 through-hole package with a G-D-S pinout. Based on its part number, which follows the common "N10" naming convention used across the industry (indicating a 100V N-channel device), this device is rated for approximately 100V drain-source voltage and around 6A continuous drain current — consistent with similar parts in this family, such as the UTC UTT6N10 and other 6N10-series MOSFETs. Its TO-220 package makes it easy to mount to a heatsink for improved thermal performance in higher-power applications, while its low on-resistance allows it to switch moderate current loads efficiently with minimal conduction losses — useful in power supplies, DC motor control, battery management, and general-purpose switching circuits. Key Specifications Type: N-Channel Power MOSFET Package: TO-220 (3-lead, G-D-S) Drain-Source Voltage (VDS): ~100V Continuous Drain Current (ID): ~6A Suitable for heatsink mounting for higher power dissipation Common Applications DC-DC converters and switch-mode power supplies DC motor drivers and speed control Battery/load switching circuits General-purpose power switching in DIY and industrial electronics Package Includes 1 x 06N10 N-Channel Power MOSFET (TO-220)

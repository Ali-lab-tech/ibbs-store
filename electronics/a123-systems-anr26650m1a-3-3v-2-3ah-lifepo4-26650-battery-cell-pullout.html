Product details

Description The ANR26650M1A is a 26650 LiFePO4 (lithium iron phosphate) rechargeable cell manufactured by A123 Systems, built on their patented Nanophosphate™ technology. It's designed for very high power output, long cycle and calendar life, and excellent abuse tolerance — a hallmark of LiFePO4 chemistry, which offers greater thermal stability and safety margin compared to standard lithium-ion cells. This cell is well suited for demanding high-discharge applications where both power delivery and longevity matter — such as power tools, e-bikes, RC/robotics, backup power systems, and DIY high-drain battery pack builds. Key Specifications Nominal Capacity / Voltage: 2.3Ah, 3.3V Internal Impedance (1kHz AC): 8mΩ typical Internal Resistance (10A, 1s DC): 10mΩ typical Recommended Standard Charge: 3A to 3.6V CC-CV, ~45 min Recommended Fast Charge: 10A to 3.6V CC-CV, ~15 min Maximum Continuous Discharge: 70A Pulse Discharge (10 sec): 120A Cycle Life: 1,000+ cycles at 10C discharge, 100% DOD Recommended Cutoff Voltage: 3.8V (charge) / 1.6V (discharge) Operating Temperature: -30°C to +60°C Storage Temperature: -50°C to +60°C Cell Weight: ~70g Dimensions: 26 x 65mm (26650 format) Common Applications Power tools and high-drain electronics E-bikes, RC vehicles, and robotics Backup/UPS power systems High-current DIY battery pack builds Package Includes 1 x A123 Systems ANR26650M1A LiFePO4 Cell

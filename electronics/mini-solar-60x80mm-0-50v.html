Product details

Mini Solar 60x80mm 0.50v Mini Solar: Indicates that the device is a small solar panel designed to convert sunlight into electrical energy. Solar panels, also known as photovoltaic panels, generate electricity through the photovoltaic effect. 60x80mm: Specifies the physical dimensions of the solar panel. In this case, it is 60 millimeters in width and 80 millimeters in height. The size is given in millimeters. 0.50V: Represents the output voltage of the solar panel. The solar panel produces a voltage of 0.50 volts. This voltage is the potential difference between the positive and negative terminals of the solar panel when exposed to sunlight.

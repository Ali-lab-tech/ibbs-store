This single head Complete Kit of Assembled MK8 Extruder has outstanding quality and the best performance for DIY and professional systems. With a 5.5kg (torque) stepper motor and all metal parts, you will find it easy to solve the problems of uneven extrusion, jamming of hotend, and filament loading/unloading . The 3D Printer MK8 Extruder Kit is the most important component for a 3D printer . The MK8 extruder is responsible for delivering the plastic material in a smooth, continuous way at a certain flow rate. The Key to printing resolution is mostly depending on the Extruder . Based on the MK8 extruder , these are upgraded third generations extruders . It is fully assembled with a stepper motor. super-fine CNC machined nozzle with more clearer nozzle wall of 0.4 mm hole permits more precise filament extruding without jamming. The hot end threaded pipe connects the filament to the injection nozzle . The filament melt in this pipe and change from solid condition to liquid condition. We made these kits available in different Nozzle diameter sizes because some enthusiasts have also used these as quick-swap hotend assemblies. These can help to save a lot of time if you like to switch filaments or nozzles often. And instead of going through the entire nozzle or filament changing process, you can simply pull off one of your hotend assemblies, and then magnetically clip the other assembly straight on. Of course, swapping out the cables is probably the most time-consuming part, but you can also slightly modify these cables with quick-swap connectors to make the entire process of swapping out hotends even easier than leveling the bed!

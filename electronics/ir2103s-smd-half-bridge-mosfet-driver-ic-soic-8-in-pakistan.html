Product details

The IR2103S is a half-bridge MOSFET and IGBT gate driver IC in an 8-pin SMD (SOIC-8) package. It drives two N-channel MOSFETs or IGBTs in a half-bridge: one low-side and one high-side switch whose floating supply can sit up to 600V above ground, powered by a simple bootstrap diode and capacitor. It is the surface-mount version of the IR2103, with the same pinout as the DIP-8 part. It is widely used in inverter, UPS, DC motor driver, H-bridge and SMPS boards. The logic inputs accept 3.3V, 5V or 15V signals, so it connects directly to an Arduino, ESP32, STM32 or PIC microcontroller, or to an SPWM chip. Key Features ⚡ 600V high-side floating channel – drives the top MOSFET of a half-bridge using bootstrap operation, no isolated supply needed. 🔧 10–20V gate drive supply – suits standard MOSFETs and IGBTs. ✅ 3.3V, 5V and 15V logic compatible – works straight from microcontroller pins. 🛡️ Built-in shoot-through protection – cross-conduction prevention logic and an internal dead time of about 520 ns stop both switches turning on together. 🔋 Undervoltage lockout – outputs switch off if VCC falls below about 8.2V, protecting MOSFETs from weak gate drive. 📏 SMD SOIC-8 package – small footprint for compact, machine-assembled boards. 🔁 dV/dt immune and tolerant to negative transients on the VS pin.

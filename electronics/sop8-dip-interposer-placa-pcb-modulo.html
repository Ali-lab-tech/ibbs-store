Product details

The Dual-Sided SMD to DIP Adapter Board is a valuable tool for working with surface mount components (SMD) in your projects. This adapter allows you to easily convert SMD packages to DIP-8, making them compatible with breadboards and other prototyping tools. Key Features: Dual-Sided Design: Accommodates both 0.5mm and 0.95mm pin pitches. Versatile Compatibility: Works with a wide range of SMD packages, including SOP, SOIC, SSOP, TSSOP, and MSOP. Easy-to-Use: Simply plug your SMD device into the adapter and connect to your breadboard or other prototyping tools. Durable Construction: Built to last for long-term use. Streamline your prototyping and development process with the Dual-Sided SMD to DIP Adapter Board.

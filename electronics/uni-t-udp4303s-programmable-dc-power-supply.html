Product details

The UNI-T UDP4303S is a high-precision 4-channel programmable linear DC power supply with built-in USB data logging for precise voltage and current control. With 297 W total power, fine 1 mV / 1 µA resolution and a full complement of interfaces (RS-232, USB, LAN, Digital IO), it is ideal for laboratories, industrial testing, R&D and electronics development. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by Digilog.pk.

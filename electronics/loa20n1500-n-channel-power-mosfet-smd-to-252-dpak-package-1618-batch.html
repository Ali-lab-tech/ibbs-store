Product details

The LOA20N1500 is a high-performance N-Channel Power MOSFET housed in a compact TO-252 (DPAK) surface-mount package. Designed for high-efficiency power management, this component is widely used in modern electronics for switching and amplification purposes. This specific batch carries the marking codes 1618 and M4K7040 , ensuring you get the exact specification required for your repair or prototyping needs. Its SMD design allows for automated assembly and excellent thermal dissipation, making it suitable for compact power supply units and motor driver applications. Key Features: Surface Mount Design: The TO-252 package offers a reduced footprint with reliable heat dissipation capabilities. High Switching Speed: Optimized for fast switching applications to minimize power loss. Low On-Resistance: Ensures high efficiency and reduced heat generation during operation. Robust Design: Built to withstand standard industrial operating temperatures and electrical stress.

Product details

The JEC CT7 Series is a high-performance Safety Standard Certified Ceramic Disc Capacitor designed for AC line filtering and surge suppression. This Class Y1 capacitor is specifically engineered for "Line-to-Ground" applications, providing critical protection by suppressing electromagnetic interference (EMI) and radio frequency interference (RFI) in power supplies and electronic circuits. With a capacitance of 470pF and a rated voltage of 250V AC (often tested to much higher surge voltages), this component ensures reliability and safety in household appliances, industrial equipment, and lighting ballasts. It features a flame-retardant epoxy resin coating and strictly adheres to international safety standards including UL, VDE, and CQC. Features: High Reliability: Designed to withstand high voltage surges and protect sensitive electronics. Safety Certified: Class Y1 insulation for bridging primary circuitry to ground. Low Dissipation: SL temperature characteristic ensures stable performance and low signal loss. Durable Construction: Encapsulated in flame-retardant epoxy resin (UL94V-0). Compact Design: Radial lead type for easy through-hole mounting on PCBs. Specifications: Brand: JEC (Jyh En Electronics) Series: CT7 Type: Safety Ceramic Disc Capacitor Safety Class: Y1 Capacitance: 470pF (Code: 471) Rated Voltage: 250V AC / 400V AC Dielectric Material: Ceramic Temperature Characteristic: SL Termination: Radial Leaded Mounting Type: Through Hole Applications: Switching Power Supplies (SMPS) AC Line Filters Household Appliances (TVs, Monitors, Audio) Surge Protection Devices AC Adapters Package Includes: 1 x JEC CT7 470pF 250VAC Y1 Safety Capacitor

Product details

The UNI-T UT395A laser distance meter measures from 0.05 m up to 50 m and shows the result on a 2.4-inch TFT colour screen — easier to read than the monochrome displays common at this price. A built-in electronic angle sensor works across ±90°, and measurements can be exported to a PC over USB using UNI-T's software. It stores 100 sets of readings, making it a practical tool for interior fit-out, flooring and wall-covering estimates, property surveys and site take-offs where you need to record as you go rather than write everything down. Key Features 0.05–50 m measuring range 📏 — covers rooms, corridors and building frontages. 2.4-inch TFT colour screen ⚡ — clearer and easier to read than a monochrome LCD. Electronic angle sensor 🔧 — multi-directional levelling across -90° to +90°. 100-set memory ✅ — record measurements as you work through a site. USB data export 🔧 — transfer stored results to a PC with UNI-T's software. Low-power laser ✅ — maximum laser output under 1 mW. AAA battery powered 🔋 — runs on 3 × AAA cells, no proprietary pack.

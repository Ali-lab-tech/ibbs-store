Product details

The UNI-T UT325 is a professional dual-input thermocouple thermometer that accepts K, J, T, E, R, S and N thermocouple types across a wide -200 °C to 1372 °C range — for HVAC, industrial, laboratory and R&D temperature work where two simultaneous channels and high accuracy matter. It reads two probes at once (T1, T2 and T1-T2 differential), holds ±(0.2%+0.6) accuracy, and adds MAX/MIN/AVG , 100-point data storage, high/low alarms, a USB interface for logging, and a backlit display. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by Digilog.pk with warranty and English manual.

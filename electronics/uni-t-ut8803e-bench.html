Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty. Key Features 3⅚-digit, 5,999-count display — 0.3% basic DC voltage accuracy at the tier price point. True-RMS AC measurement — accurate readings on non-sinusoidal waveforms (switching supplies, motor drives, PWM, dimmed loads). Auto-ranging — across voltage, current, resistance, and capacitance functions, reducing operator workflow friction. Inductance measurement to 100 H — with D and Q quality-factor readout on capacitors and inductors. Thermocouple temperature measurement — range -40°C to 1000°C. USB-Device PC connection — included software for historical-data viewing and trend monitoring.

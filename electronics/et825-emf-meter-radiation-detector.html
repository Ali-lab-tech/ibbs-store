Product details

The ET825 is a handheld EMF meter that detects both electric field and magnetic field radiation at the same time. Use it to check radiation near mobile phones, computers, TVs, fridges and high-voltage lines, at home, in the office or outdoors. When the reading goes above the safe level it warns you with a sound and light alarm, and the LCD shows the trend as a graph so you can see the level rise and fall. It can also check how well radiation-shielding clothing or films work.

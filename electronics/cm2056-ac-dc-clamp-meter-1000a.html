Product details

The CM2056 is a true RMS clamp meter for both AC and DC current up to 1000A , with a 52 mm jaw that fits round heavy cables in solar, battery and industrial installations. It has a 4000-count backlit display, a built-in torch, NCV live-wire detection, and measures voltage, resistance, capacitance up to 40mF, frequency up to 10MHz, duty cycle and temperature. Auto power-off saves the battery.

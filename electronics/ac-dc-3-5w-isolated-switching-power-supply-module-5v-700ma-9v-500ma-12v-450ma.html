Product details

The AC-DC 3.5W Precision Buck Converter Module is a compact, high-performance isolated switching power supply designed for space-constrained electronics projects. Whether you are powering a microcontroller, an IoT device, or a smart home sensor, this module allows you to step down mains voltage (AC) directly to a stable DC output (5V, 9V, or 12V) without the need for bulky transformers. This module features a bare-board design with high efficiency, low power consumption, and low output ripple. It comes equipped with built-in protection against over-current, overload, and short circuits, ensuring the safety of your connected components. Key Features: Wide Input Range: Supports AC 85V-265V or DC 110V-370V input, making it versatile for various grid standards. Compact Footprint: Ultra-small size allows for easy integration into custom PCB designs or small enclosures. Safety Isolation: Fully isolated input and output for safer operation. High Reliability: Built-in temperature protection, over-current protection, and short-circuit protection. Stable Output: High efficiency with low output ripple and noise (<60mV). Technical Specifications: Feature Specification Input Voltage AC 85 ~ 265V 50/60Hz Output Power 3.5W (Max) Output Voltage Options 5V , 9V , or 12V (Select Variation) Output Current (5V) 700mA Output Current (9V) 500mA Output Current (12V) 450mA Output Voltage Accuracy ±1% Working Temperature -20°C to +60°C Relative Humidity 40-90% RH Efficiency ~80% Applications: Powering Raspberry Pi, Arduino, or ESP8266/ESP32 directly from mains. Smart home automation devices. Industrial control equipment. LED drive power. DIY electronics power supply repair. Package Includes: 1 x AC-DC Step Down Module (Selected Voltage Variant)

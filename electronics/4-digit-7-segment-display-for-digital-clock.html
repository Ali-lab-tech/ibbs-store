Product details

Build your own digital clocks, timers, and counters with this 0.36-inch, 4-digit, red 7-segment LED display with a common cathode connection. Perfect for Arduino, Raspberry Pi, and other microcontroller-based DIY electronics projects, this display offers crisp, high-brightness red digits that stay clearly visible even at low drive currents — making it energy-efficient for battery-powered projects. A seven-segment display is a simple, low-cost alternative to complex dot-matrix displays for showing decimal numerals, and remains a go-to component for digital clocks, timers, counters, and numeric readouts in hobbyist and educational electronics. Overview Four-digit, 0.36" red LED display with common cathode wiring, standard 2.54mm pin pitch for easy breadboard and PCB use.

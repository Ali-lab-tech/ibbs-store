Equipped with a multifunctional digital display , it shows real-time voltage, current, and power values for accurate monitoring. Its fine & coarse adjustment knobs allow precise tuning of output parameters, while the overload, short-circuit, and thermal protection ensures safe and reliable operation. Whether you’re powering microcontrollers, testing circuit boards, charging batteries, or running electronic experiments, the RF4 RF-3005D is a versatile tool for any serious electronics workspace.

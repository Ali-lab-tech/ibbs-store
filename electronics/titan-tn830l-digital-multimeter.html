Product details

The TITAN TN830L is a basic pocket multimeter for students, hobbyists and home electrical checks. It measures DC and AC voltage up to 600V, DC current up to 10A and resistance, with a continuity test for checking wires and fuses. It runs on a 9V battery and shows readings on a 1999-count LCD.

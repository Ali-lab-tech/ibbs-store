Product details

The FNIRSI S1 is a smart multimeter built around a large, easy-to-read display. Its automatic identification picks the right function for you, so checking a socket, battery or wire takes a single probe touch. It reads AC and DC voltage with True RMS, has NCV for finding live wires without contact, a built-in flashlight and data hold. The internal battery recharges from a power bank or computer. Key Features 🔍 Automatic identification and auto measurement 📏 True RMS AC and DC voltage ⚠️ NCV induced-voltage detection with anti-burn protection 🔦 Built-in flashlight and data hold 🔋 Rechargeable battery charge from a power bank or computer Technical Specifications Display Large digital screen Functions AC/DC voltage, True RMS, auto identification, NCV, data hold Extras Flashlight Power Built-in rechargeable battery Body ABS What's in the Box 1 × FNIRSI S1 multimeter Test leads and charging cable as supplied by FNIRSI

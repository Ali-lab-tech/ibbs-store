Product details

A 3-axis gyroscope and a 3-axis accelerometer are combined together on the same silicon die with an onboard Digital Motion Processor. Easy to get it fit into your project! It is very accurate, as GY-521 Mpu6050 Module contains 16-bits analog to digital conversion hardware for each channel. Therefor Three axis Gyrometer captures the x, y, and z channel at the same time. The sensor uses the I2C -bus to interface with the Arduino.

Product details

UNI-T UTG4162A — The UNI-T UTG4162A is a 2-channel arbitrary waveform generator with a maximum sine output of 160 MHz, built on Direct Digital Synthesis with a 500 MSa/s sample rate and 16-bit vertical resolution. An 8 pts to 32 Mpts arbitrary-waveform memory with 7 GB of non-volatile storage supports long-form custom stimulus, and an 8-inch TFT display with a structured key-and-knob interface keeps parameter entry direct. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty. Key Features 160 MHz sine / 50 MHz square & pulse — 2-channel output with 1 µHz frequency resolution. Standard dual channels — independent or channel-coupling mode for phase-locked, dual-tone, and tracking signal pairs. 500 MSa/s sample rate, 16-bit DDS — stable, precise, low-distortion signal generation across all standard waveforms. 32 Mpts arbitrary memory, 7 GB storage — deep custom-waveform depth with a large non-volatile on-instrument library. 12 modulation types — AM, FM, PM, ASK, FSK, PSK, BPSK, QPSK, OSK, PWM, SUM, QAM — analog and digital in one instrument. Linear and logarithmic sweep — with internal, external, and manual trigger sources. Three burst modes — N-cycle, gated, infinite, with configurable cycle count and start phase.

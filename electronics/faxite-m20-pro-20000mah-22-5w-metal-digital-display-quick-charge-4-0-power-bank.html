Product details

The FAXITE M20 Pro Powerbank is a high-capacity 20000mAh portable charger with a digital Display and five ports for super-fast charging. It is equipped with TYPE-C and Micro USB input types and offers quick charge 3.0 technology and a power output 12v. Its multiple-device charging capability is ideal for mobiles, tablets, and other gadgets. The package includes a charging cable for added convenience. Faxite M20Por 20000mAh Power Bank Specifications: High Power & Fast Charging: This product supports fast and standard charging and has an output of up to 22.5 watts. Versatile Charging: It is equipped with PD and Quick Charge 3.0/4.0 systems and includes a 20-watt PD port, ideal for iPhones and other compatible devices. Multiple Device Support: Can charge up to 3 devices simultaneously. Enhanced Durability: Shockproof design with an ultra-lightweight metal body for robust protection. Safety First: Built-in short-circuit protection ensures safe usage. Convenient Features: o Digital Display to monitor battery status. o Leather strap for easy portability. Superior Battery Technology: Lithium polymer battery provides long life, high efficiency, and lightweight performance. Super-Fast Recharging: Compatible with super-fast chargers and turbocharging. Perfect for users seeking reliability, efficiency, and style in a single-power solution.

This best limit switch uses a standard 4 pin 100″ pitch header and accepts a standard, old-style CD-ROM audio connector cable. The mechanical end stop is a simple solution to a simple problem. We want to be able to detect when an X/Y/Z stage has reached its minimum or maximum. Instead of messing with flags or complicated light beam interruption, we use a mechanical switch . If we place the switch in the path of the stage, then the stage itself will simply close the switch module when it moves against it.

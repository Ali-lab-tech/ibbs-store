Product details

The DIGILOG QRR0.01G Heat Aerosol Fire Extinguishing Device is a compact automatic fire suppression device designed to protect enclosed spaces from fire. It works by detecting high temperatures and automatically releasing an aerosol extinguishing agent to stop flames quickly. The device does not require an external power source and leaves very little residue after operation. Its small size and reliable performance make it suitable for electrical panels, battery cabinets, UPS systems, server racks, and other sensitive equipment that require effective fire protection. Features Automatic heat-activated fire suppression system. Operates without any external power supply. Releases a clean aerosol extinguishing agent. Compact and lightweight construction. Fast response to fire at an early stage. Suitable for Class A, B, C, and electrical fires. Wide operating temperature range. Easy to install in confined spaces. Maintenance-free design with long service life. Provides reliable protection for sensitive equipment. Specifications Model: DIGILOG QRR0.01G Type: Heat Aerosol Fire Extinguishing Device Activation Method: Thermal activation Activation Temperature: 175°C ± 15°C Aerosol Agent Weight: 10 g ± 1 g Protection Volume: Up to 0.1 m³ Extinguishing Density: 100 g/m³ Discharge Time: ≤ 20 seconds Operating Temperature Range: -50°C to +90°C Dimensions: 76.5 × 65.5 × 19.5 mm Uses Used for fire protection in electrical control panels. Suitable for battery cabinets and UPS systems. Protects server racks and communication equipment. Used in solar inverter and industrial enclosures. Ideal for small enclosed spaces requiring automatic fire suppression. Package includes: 1x DIGILOG QRR0.01G Heat Aerosol Fire Extinguishing device

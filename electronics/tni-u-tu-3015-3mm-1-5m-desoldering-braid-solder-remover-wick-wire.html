Product details

Description: This is a desoldering braid (also known as solder wick), a fine, flat braided copper wire used to remove unwanted or excess solder from PCBs and electronic components during rework, repair, or component replacement. When heated with a soldering iron while pressed against a solder joint, the braid's tightly woven copper strands draw up the molten solder through capillary action, effectively lifting it off the board — making it an essential tool for desoldering through-hole components, cleaning up solder bridges, and general circuit board rework. The braid is treated with a rosin flux coating, which helps the copper wick more effectively absorb solder by improving wetting and reducing oxidation during the desoldering process. This particular reel offers a 3.0mm width, a versatile middle-ground size suitable for a wide range of common desoldering tasks — wide enough to lift solder efficiently from standard through-hole joints and pads, while still being manageable for more detailed work. Packaged on a compact plastic spool with a clear label, the 1.5-meter length provides ample braid for multiple desoldering sessions before needing replacement, making it a practical addition to any electronics repair toolkit, workbench, or soldering station. Key Specifications: Manufacturer: TNI-U (Hong Kong) Model: TU-3015 Product Type: Desoldering Braid (Solder Wick) Width: 3.0mm Length: 1.5m Material: Braided Copper with Rosin Flux Coating Spool Type: Compact Plastic Reel Applications: PCB rework and repair, desoldering through-hole components, removing solder bridges and excess solder, cleaning solder pads before reuse, and general electronics repair, prototyping, and hobbyist soldering work. Package Includes: 1 x TNI-U TU-3015 Desoldering Braid, 3.0mm x 1.5

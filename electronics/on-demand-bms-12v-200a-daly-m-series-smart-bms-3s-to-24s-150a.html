Product details

The Daly M Series Smart BMS is a powerful and reliable solution for managing lithium-ion and LiFePO4 batteries. This advanced system is engineered to handle high-current applications, making it ideal for electric vehicles, energy storage systems, and other demanding industries. Key Features: Versatile Compatibility: Supports 3S to 24S configurations, accommodating a wide range of battery pack voltages. High Current Capacity: Handles up to 150A or 200A continuous discharge current, ensuring efficient power delivery. Advanced Protection: Safeguards against overcharge, over-discharge, short circuit, and temperature extremes. Smart Communication: Features CAN, RS485, and dual UART communication interfaces for seamless integration. User-Friendly Interface: Comes with a self-developed app and PC software for easy monitoring and control. Cloud Connectivity: Integrates with the DALY Cloud platform for remote monitoring and data analysis. Applications: Electric Vehicles: Aerial work vehicles, ships, ATVs, and low-speed vehicles Energy Storage: RV energy storage and grid-scale energy storage Industrial Equipment: Floor sweepers, golf carts, and forklifts Benefits: Enhanced Battery Life: Protects batteries from harmful conditions, extending their lifespan. Improved Safety: Prevents potential hazards such as fires and explosions. Optimized Performance: Ensures efficient charging and discharging, maximizing battery capacity. Remote Monitoring: Track battery health and usage data from anywhere. Scalability: Easily adapts to different battery configurations and applications. Choose the Daly M Series Smart BMS for reliable, efficient, and safe battery management in high-current applications.

Product details

Upgrade your electronic projects with this high-reliability 2.8 Ohm Wirewound Power Resistor. Designed for applications requiring stability and heat dissipation, this component features a rugged silicone coating that offers excellent flame resistance and insulation. Unlike standard carbon film resistors, this wirewound variant is constructed by winding a metal wire around a ceramic core, making it ideal for high-current applications, audio crossovers, power supplies, and current sensing circuits. The axial lead design ensures easy through-hole mounting on PCBs or breadboards. With a tight tolerance of 2% to 3%, this resistor ensures precise performance for sensitive electronic calibrations. Key Features: High Stability: Excellent stability in high-temperature environments. Rugged Construction: Silicone coated body provides flame-proof protection and insulation. Wirewound Technology: Superior pulse handling capability compared to film resistors. Low Noise: Operates with minimal current noise, making it suitable for audio applications. Easy Mounting: Standard axial leads for reliable soldering.

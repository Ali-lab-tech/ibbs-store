Product details

High-Performance M.2 SSD Cooling Solution The TEUCER M.2-HS02S heatsink is engineered to keep your M.2 NVMe SSD running cool under heavy workloads. Featuring a diversion-type aluminum heat dissipation design with thermally conductive silicone pads, this heatsink effectively reduces SSD temperatures to maintain optimal performance and longevity. Key Features Diversion-Type Heat Dissipation: Advanced finned aluminum design rapidly draws heat away from the SSD controller and NAND chips Dual-Layer Thermal Interface: Includes thermally conductive silicone sheets on both upper and lower surfaces for maximum heat transfer M.2 2280 Form Factor: Specifically designed for 22mm × 80mm M.2 NVMe SSDs (verify your SSD size before purchase) Universal Compatibility: Works with desktop PCs, laptops, and PlayStation 5 M.2 expansion slots Aluminum Construction: Lightweight yet durable aluminum alloy with black anodized finish Easy Installation: Simple clip-on design with included thermal pads Technical Specifications Model: M.2-HS02S Dimensions: 72mm (L) × 23mm (W) × 9mm (H) Compatible SSD Size: M.2 2280 (22mm × 80mm) Material: Aluminum alloy heatsink with thermal silicone pads Color: Black Compatibility Note Important: This heatsink is designed for M.2 2280 form factor SSDs only. Please verify your SSD dimensions before purchasing. Also compatible with M.2 2242, 2260, and 2210 with proper positioning. What's Included 1× TEUCER aluminum heatsink 2× Thermally conductive silicone pads (pre-installed)

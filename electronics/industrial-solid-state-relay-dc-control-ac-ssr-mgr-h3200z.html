Product details

The MGR-H3200Z is a high-performance Industrial Solid State Relay (SSR) designed for demanding applications in various industries. It offers reliable switching of AC loads (30-480VAC) using a low-voltage DC control signal (4-32VDC), making it ideal for automation systems, HVAC equipment, petrochemical applications, and more. Key Features and Benefits: Superior Performance: Handles high currents with minimal heat generation, extending relay life and improving system efficiency. Long-lasting Durability: Robust construction and advanced circuitry ensure reliable operation even in harsh industrial environments. Versatile Control: DC control input simplifies integration into existing control systems and enables precise switching. Compact and Convenient: DIN rail mountable design saves space and facilitates easy installation. Zero Arc Noise: Eliminates noisy arcing common with traditional relays, creating a quieter operating environment. EMI Suppression: Built-in filters minimize electromagnetic interference, ensuring signal integrity and system stability. Safety and Certifications: CE certified for reliable performance and adherence to stringent safety standards.

Product details

The Ai-Thinker NodeMCU-PB-03F-Kit Development Board is a BLE5.0 development board that is based on the PB-03F transceiver. It has an integrated radio frequency transceiver, PA, radio frequency filter, antenna switch and power management. It has excellent communication performance and stability in various wireless environments. It supports up to BLE 5.2 protocol, supports Sig Mesh, supports Tmall Genie/APP voice control, supports Amazon Alexa Echo audio control. The half hole pad can guide the antenna to the main board, and the antenna hole can directly weld the spring antenna on the module.

Product details

The Stackable Arduino Female Header Strip is a versatile accessory designed to enhance your Arduino projects. With its extended male pins, this header allows you to easily stack multiple shields while maintaining the ability to connect jumper wires to your Arduino's pins. Key Features: Stackable Design: Easily connect multiple shields without obstructing the USB-B connector. Compatible with Arduino Boards: Works seamlessly with Arduino Main Board, Arduino Pro, and Arduino Mega. Maintains Jumper Wire Access: Continue to use jumper wires with your Arduino pins. Durable Construction: Made from high-quality materials for long-lasting performance. Expand your Arduino projects with the Stackable Arduino Female Header Strip and enjoy seamless connectivity and versatility.

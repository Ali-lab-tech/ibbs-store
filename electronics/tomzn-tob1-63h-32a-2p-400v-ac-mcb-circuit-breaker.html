Product details

Protect your wiring from overload and short circuits with the TOMZN TOB1-63H miniature circuit breaker. It is rated 32A with a C-curve trip characteristic, which suits general home, shop and office loads such as air conditioners, geysers, sockets and lighting circuits. The 2-pole design switches both live and neutral, and the blue handle makes ON/OFF clear at a glance. Key Features Rated current: 32A 2 pole (2P) design Trip curve: C (suitable for general and motor-type loads) Rated voltage: 400V AC Breaking capacity: 6000A (6kA) Standards: IEC/EN 60898-1, CE marked Screw terminals for secure wiring [Confirm: DIN rail mounting] Suitable For Home distribution boards and main switch panels Shops, offices and small workshops Air conditioners, water heaters and other high-load appliances Important Safety Note This is an AC-only breaker. Do not use it on solar DC circuits, which need a DC-rated breaker. Installation should be done by a qualified electrician, and the breaker rating must match the cable size and the load.

SIM800L EASY GSM Board GSM Breakout Board With Battery SIM800L GSM Shield The item in the image is an Easy GSM Board . It’s a compact and inexpensive GSM breakout board that supports sim800l module. Here are some of its features: The operating voltage of the chip ranges from 3.4V to 4.4V, making it an ideal candidate for direct LiPo battery or li ion battery supply. All the necessary data pins of the SIM800L GSM chip are broken out to a 0.1″ pitch headers, including the pins required for communication with the microcontroller over the UART. This board is typically used in projects where cellular connectivity is required. For example, it can be used in home automation projects, remote sensing applications, or any project where you need to send data remotely. Note: USB CONNECTOR IS NOT USEABLE YET

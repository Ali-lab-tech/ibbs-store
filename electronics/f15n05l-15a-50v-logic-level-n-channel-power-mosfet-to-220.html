Product details

The F15N05L is an N-channel enhancement-mode logic-level power MOSFET, part of the well-established 15N05L family (equivalent to the RFP15N05L), housed in the industry-standard TO-220 through-hole package with a G-D-S pinout. It's designed to be driven directly from standard 5V logic-level gate signals, making it a great fit for microcontroller-driven power switching without needing a separate gate driver circuit. Rated for 15A and 50V, with a low on-resistance of around 0.140Ω, this MOSFET was designed specifically for optimized 5V gate drives, making it well suited for applications such as switching regulators, switching converters, motor drivers, relay drivers, and drivers for high-power bipolar switching transistors. These devices can handle currents up to 15A and voltages up to 50V, feature low gate drive requirements of 5V, fast switching speeds in the nanosecond range, and high input impedance, giving efficient, low-loss switching performance in low-voltage power control circuits. Its TO-220 package makes it easy to mount to a heatsink for improved thermal performance in higher-power applications, while its logic-level gate threshold simplifies integration into Arduino, Raspberry Pi, and other microcontroller-based power switching projects.

Product details

The KADA T210D Thermostatic Soldering Station (75W) is a professional soldering tool designed for home electronics repair, PCB assembly, and precise welding applications. Featuring adjustable temperature control (120°C–450°C) with preset modes (300°C, 350°C, 400°C), this station offers consistent and accurate heat delivery via its intelligent PID control system . With rapid heat-up performance , LED display , and automatic standby & sleep functions , the KADA T210D ensures efficiency, energy savings, and extended tip lifespan.

Product details

Dual Usb Power Bank Module Input Voltage : The module typically accepts an input voltage of 5 volts (5V), which is standard for USB power sources. This allows you to power and charge the module conveniently from USB ports, power adapters, or other 5V power supplies. Output Ports : A key feature of the Dual USB Power Bank Module is the presence of two or more USB output ports. These ports provide a 5V output for connecting and charging USB-compatible devices like smartphones, tablets, portable speakers, or other electronic gadgets. Dual USB ports enable you to charge two devices simultaneously. Output Current : The module is designed to deliver a specific output current to each USB port. Commonly, the combined maximum output current for all ports is around 2 amperes (2A) to 2.4 amperes (2.4A), which is typically sufficient for charging most smartphones and tablets at a reasonable speed. Battery Compatibility : The module is compatible with various rechargeable battery types, such as lithium-ion (Li-ion) or lithium-polymer (LiPo) batteries. You can connect one or more cells, like 18650 batteries, to the module. Charging and Discharging : These modules can manage both battery charging and discharging functions. They charge the connected battery when provided with a power source and can simultaneously discharge stored energy to power external devices via the USB ports. Protection Features : Many Dual USB Power Bank Modules include protection circuitry to ensure safe operation. This protection may include overcharge protection, over-discharge protection, over-current protection, and short-circuit protection to safeguard the battery and connected devices. Indicator LEDs : Some modules come equipped with LED indicator lights to display the charging and discharging status, providing visual feedback on the module's operation. Compact Design : Dual USB Power Bank Modules are generally compact and lightweight, making them suitable for use in DIY projects, custom enclosures, or as standalone portable power banks. Fast-Charging Support : Some modules may support fast-charging protocols such as Quick Charge or Power Delivery, depending on the components and capabilities of the module. Package Included: 1x Dual Usb Power Bank Module

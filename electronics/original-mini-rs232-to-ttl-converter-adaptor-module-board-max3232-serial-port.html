Serial Port Mini RS232 to TTL Converter Adaptor Module Board MAX3232 for the electrical level converter. The RS232 to TTL 5V Signal Converter Module is a professional module for projects requiring PC, Laptop RS232 interface to 5V TTL logic. This RS232 to TTL module converts signals between RS232 voltage levels and TTL voltage levels. The serial port to TTL board is bi-directional, where TTL signals are converted to RS232 levels and RS232 levels are converted to TTL levels. MAX3232 module is ideal for use in pc interfacing, microcontroller-based projects, robotics, or for experimental and educational purposes.

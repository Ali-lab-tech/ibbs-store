Product details

The SS34 is a commonly used Schottky diode designed for a wide range of applications where high-speed switching and low forward voltage drop are essential. It is often used in power rectification circuits and voltage clamping applications. Features: Schottky Barrier Design: The SS34 features a Schottky barrier diode design. Schottky diodes have a lower forward voltage drop compared to standard PN junction diodes, making them more suitable for high-frequency and low-voltage applications. High Current Rating: With a maximum forward current (IF) of 3A, the SS34 can handle relatively high current levels, making it suitable for power rectification tasks. Low Forward Voltage Drop: Schottky diodes have a lower forward voltage drop (VF) compared to regular silicon diodes. This characteristic reduces power dissipation and is advantageous in applications where efficiency is critical. Fast Switching Speed: Schottky diodes have a fast switching speed, making them ideal for applications that require rapid switching between conducting and non-conducting states. Reverse Voltage Protection: The SS34 can handle a maximum reverse voltage (VR) of up to 40V, providing protection against reverse voltage conditions. Specifications : Here are the key electrical specifications for the SS34 Schottky diode: Maximum Forward Voltage Drop (VF): Typically around 0.45V at 3A forward current. Maximum Reverse Current (IR): The reverse current is typically low, usually in the microampere range. Maximum Reverse Recovery Time: Schottky diodes have minimal reverse recovery time, contributing to their fast switching characteristics. Operating Temperature Range: The diode can typically operate within a temperature range of -65°C to +125°C, depending on the manufacturer's specifications. Package Type: The SS34 is available in various package types, including the popular DO-214AC (SMA) surface-mount package. Polarity: It is important to observe the correct polarity when using the SS34 diode. The anode (positive side) is typically marked with a band or line on the diode's body.

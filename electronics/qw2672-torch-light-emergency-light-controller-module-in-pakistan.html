Product details

The QW2672/E is a specialized chip designed for efficient flashlight lighting control. Whether you’re building a compact flashlight or a more complex LED system, this chip offers essential features and protection mechanisms. Here’s what you need to know: High-Precision Battery Management: The QW2672/E integrates single-cell lithium battery management, ensuring optimal performance and longevity. Features include overcharge protection, over-discharge protection, overcurrent protection, battery reverse connection protection, and short circuit protection. User-Friendly Dimming: With just one touch switch, you can achieve stepless dimming to any desired brightness level. Minimal peripheral components are required, simplifying your design process. Powerful LED Output: The QW2672/E supports up to 2A drive capability for WLEDs (white LEDs). You can directly connect a current-limiting resistor to drive a single string of LEDs or use external boost circuits for multiple strings

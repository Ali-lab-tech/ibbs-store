Description: The CM3024 is a PWM (Pulse Width Modulation) solar charge controller designed to regulate the flow of power from a solar panel array to a battery bank, protecting the battery from overcharging while maximizing charging efficiency. It automatically detects whether it's connected to a 12V or 24V system, adjusting its operation accordingly without requiring manual configuration — a convenient feature for a wide range of common off-grid and backup power setups. The controller uses reliable three-stage PWM charge management (bulk, boost/absorption, and float charging), which helps extend battery lifespan by tapering the charging current appropriately as the battery approaches full charge, rather than applying a constant charge rate throughout. It's rated for up to 30A of charging current, making it suitable for small-to-mid-sized solar arrays commonly used in cabins, RVs, boats, and remote equipment installations. A built-in backlit LCD display provides real-time visual feedback, showing system voltage, charging status, and simple graphic icons representing the solar panel, battery charge level, and connected load — making it easy to monitor system health at a glance without needing external tools or a smartphone app. The unit includes a full set of built-in protections, including overcharge protection, short-circuit protection, open-circuit protection, reverse polarity protection, and overload protection, all of which trigger automatically without damaging the controller or connected equipment. Wiring is handled through clearly labeled screw terminals on the front panel, with dedicated positive/negative connections for the solar panel, battery, and load (such as a DC light or small appliance) — plus three front-panel buttons for navigating and adjusting operational settings and parameters.

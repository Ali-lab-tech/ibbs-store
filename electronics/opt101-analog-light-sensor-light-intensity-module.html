Product details

The OPT101 Analog Light Sensor Light Intensity Module Monolithic Photodiode (ER-SEE01079S) is a powerful and versatile tool for measuring light intensity with high precision. It features an integrated photodiode and transimpedance amplifier in a single chip, eliminating common problems like leakage current and noise. Here's what makes the OPT101 stand out: Integrated design: Combining the photodiode and amplifier on one chip minimizes errors and simplifies implementation. Wide operating voltage: Accepts a single supply voltage of 2.7-36V, making it suitable for battery-powered applications. Low quiescent current: Minimal power consumption when not actively measuring light. High responsivity: Accurately detects even subtle changes in light intensity. Linearity and low dark current: Provides accurate readings across a wide range of light levels. Applications: Smoke detectors: Enhance fire detection accuracy and reliability. Currency changers: Precisely identify counterfeit bills. Barcode scanners: Streamline data entry and improve efficiency. Medical instrumentation: Monitor light levels in medical applications. Laboratory instrumentation: Conduct precise scientific experiments. Photographic analyzers: Optimize image quality and color accuracy. Additional features: Single or dual power supply operation Wide working temperature range (0-70°C) Compact size (2.29mm x 2.29mm) The OPT101 Analog Light Sensor is the perfect solution for a wide range of projects requiring accurate light measurement. Its high performance, ease of use, and versatility make it an essential tool for engineers, hobbyists, and researchers alike. Package included: 1 x OPT101 Analog Light Sensor Light Intensity Module

Product details

The VICTOR 822M is a compact combustible gas leak detector for locating natural gas leaks at pipes, joints, regulators and appliances. A gas-sensitive semiconductor sensor reacts from 50 to 1000ppm (methane), with a visible and audible alarm . It flashes the power indicator when the battery is low and switches itself off to save power.

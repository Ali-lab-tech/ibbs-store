Product details

The Daly M Series Smart BMS is a cutting-edge battery management system tailored to the unique demands of golf carts and sightseeing vehicles. This advanced solution is designed to provide reliable and efficient performance, ensuring optimal battery health and longevity. Key Features: Versatile Compatibility: Supports 3S to 24S configurations, accommodating a wide range of battery pack voltages. High Current Capacity: Handles up to 150A or 200A continuous discharge current, ideal for demanding applications. Advanced Protection: Safeguards against overcharge, over-discharge, short circuit, and temperature extremes. Rugged Construction: Features patented waterproofing and lower temperature rise for enhanced durability. Compact Design: Offers a smaller size for easy integration into various vehicle designs. Smart Communication: Features CAN, RS485, and dual UART communication interfaces for seamless integration. User-Friendly Interface: Comes with a self-developed app and PC software for easy monitoring and control. Cloud Connectivity: Integrates with the DALY Cloud platform for remote monitoring and data analysis. Benefits: Enhanced Battery Life: Protects batteries from harmful conditions, extending their lifespan. Improved Safety: Prevents potential hazards such as fires and explosions. Optimized Performance: Ensures efficient charging and discharging, maximizing battery capacity. Reliable Operation: Built to withstand the rigors of outdoor use and harsh environments. Cost-Effective: Helps reduce maintenance costs and downtime. Choose the Daly M Series Smart BMS for reliable, efficient, and safe battery management in your golf cart or sightseeing vehicle.

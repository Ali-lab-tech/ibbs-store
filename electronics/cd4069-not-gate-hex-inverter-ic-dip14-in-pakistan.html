Product details

The CD4069 consists of six inverter circuits and is manufactured using complementary MOS (CMOS) to achieve wide power supply operating range, low power consumption, high noise immunity, and the symmetric controlled rise and fall times. This device is intended for all general-purpose inverter applications inputs are protected from damage due to static discharge by diode clamps to VDD and VSS. Features: Wide supply voltage range: 3.0V to 15V High noise immunity: 0.45 VDD typ. Low power TTL compatibility: Fan out of 2 driving 74L or 1 driving 74LS Equivalent to MM74C04 Specification: Logic Type : INVERTER / NOR / NAND Output Current: 6.8mA Supply voltage range : 3V to 18V Package Type : DIP Pin number: 14 Operating temperature range : -55 ° C to +125 ° C Surface Mount Devices : through-hole mounting Datasheet CD4069-dip-14 Package Include: 1x CD4069 Not Gate Hex Inverter IC

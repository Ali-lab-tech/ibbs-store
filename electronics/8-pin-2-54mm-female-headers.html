Product details

8 pin 2.54mm Female Headers These high-quality female pin headers are essential electronic components for breadboard prototyping, PCB projects, and module connections. With a standard 2.54mm pitch and a single row of 8 pins, they are compatible with Arduino, Raspberry Pi, ESP32, and many other development boards and shields. Each header features a through-hole DIP design, making installation easy on printed circuit boards or solderless breadboards. Made from durable copper for reliable conductivity and long-term use, these female headers are ideal for DIY electronics, repairs, and custom circuit builds.

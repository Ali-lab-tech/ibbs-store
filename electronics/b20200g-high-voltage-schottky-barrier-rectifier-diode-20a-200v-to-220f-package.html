Product details

The B20200G is a high-performance Dual Schottky Barrier Rectifier designed for high-frequency switch-mode power supplies. Featuring a TO-220F full-plastic isolated package, this component offers excellent heat dissipation and electrical isolation With a high blocking voltage of 200V and a continuous current capacity of 20A (10A per leg), the B20200G is engineered to minimize power loss and improve efficiency in low-voltage, high-frequency inverters, free-wheeling, and polarity protection applications. Its proprietary barrier technology allows for reliable operation up to junction temperatures of 150°C. Key Features: High Power Capacity: 20 Amps total continuous forward current. High Voltage: 200 Volts maximum repetitive peak reverse voltage. Efficiency: Low forward voltage drop means less wasted energy and cooler operation. Fast Switching: High switching speed ideal for modern power converters. Protection: High surge capability and guard ring for over-voltage protection. Package: TO-220F (Fully Isolated Plastic Package) for easy mounting. Applications: Switch Mode Power Supplies (SMPS) DC to DC Converters Battery Chargers Solar Inverters LED Power Drivers Freewheeling Diodes Technical Specifications: Specification Value Model B20200G Type Dual Common Cathode Schottky Rectifier Max Repetitive Reverse Voltage (Vrrm) 200V Average Rectified Forward Current (Io) 20A (Total) Forward Voltage Drop (Vf) ~0.85V (Typical) Operating Temperature -55°C to +150°C Mounting Style Through Hole Package / Case TO-220F (Isolated) Package Includes: 1 x B20200G Schottky Diode (20A 200V)

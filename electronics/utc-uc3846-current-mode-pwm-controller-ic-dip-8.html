Product details

UTC UC3846 L ow Cost Power-Saving Mode PWM Controller for Flyback Converters The UTC UC3846 is a high-performance current mode PWM controller ideally suited for low standby power and cost-effective off-line flyback converters. Its low VDD startup current ensures reliable startup design, and a large-value resistor can be used in the startup circuit to minimize standby power. At no-load condition, the IC operates in burst mode for lower standby power and in decreasing frequency for higher conversion efficiency at light load. The PWM switching frequency is programmable externally during normal operation. Protection Features The UTC UC3846 includes comprehensive automatic self-recovery protection: OCP – Cycle-by-cycle current limiting OTP – Over temperature protection UVLO – VDD over voltage clamp and under voltage lockout Gate-drive output clamped at 15V max to protect the power MOSFET Advanced Performance Internal slope compensation improves system stability at high PWM duty cycle. Leading-edge blanking on the current sense (CS) input removes signal glitches, minimizing external component requirements and lowering system cost. Excellent EMI performance is achieved via UTC's proprietary frequency hopping technique combined with soft driver control. Audio noise is eliminated as the switching frequency exceeds 20kHz during operation.

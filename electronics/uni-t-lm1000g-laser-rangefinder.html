Product details

The UNI-T LM1000G is a 6× monocular laser rangefinder that reaches from 3 m to 1000 m with ±1 m accuracy — extra reach for hunting, shooting, surveying and long-course golf, in a compact pocketable body. Sight through the 6× optics and read the distance in the eyepiece, then use flagpole lock to grab the pin, golf slope / trajectory compensation for play-as yardage, and speed, height and angle modes with a wide ±60° range. A USB-rechargeable lithium battery powers it. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty and English manual.

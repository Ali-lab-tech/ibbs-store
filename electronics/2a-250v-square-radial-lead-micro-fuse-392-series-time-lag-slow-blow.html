Product details

Protect your valuable electronics with this high-quality 2A 250V Sub-miniature Square Fuse . Designed for compact circuit protection, this radial lead fuse belongs to the widely used 392 series. It is commonly found in LCD TVs, power adapters, battery chargers, and various consumer electronic power supplies. Unlike standard glass fuses, this square plastic fuse is designed for durability and space-saving on Printed Circuit Boards (PCBs). The "T" rating indicates a Time-Lag (Slow Blow) characteristic, allowing it to withstand momentary current surges without blowing unnecessarily, making it ideal for power input circuits. Key Features: Compact Design: Rectangular, space-saving plastic body ideal for crowded PCBs. High Reliability: Vibration resistant with high breaking capacity. Radial Leads: Designed for easy through-hole soldering. Safety Standards: Flame retardant casing. Applications: Monitors, Adapters, LED Drivers, and general electronic circuit protection.

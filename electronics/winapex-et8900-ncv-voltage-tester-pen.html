Product details

The WinAPEX ET8900 is a pen-type non-contact voltage tester. Hold the tip near a wire, socket or switch and it shows whether the line is live, red for live and green for neutral , without touching any bare conductor. Sensitivity can be switched between 12-1000V and 48-1000V AC. As the signal gets stronger the beep speeds up and the bargraph rises, which helps trace a hidden live wire inside a wall. It has an LED torch and switches off after 3 minutes. Key Features ⚡ 12V to 1000V AC (high sensitivity) or 48V to 1000V (low) 🔴🟢 Red for live, green for neutral 🔊 Sound and light alarm with signal-strength bargraph 🔦 LED torch for dark DB boxes 🔋 2 × AAA, auto off after 3 minutes, low-battery indication Technical Specifications Model ET8900 Brand WinAPEX AC voltage range 12V to 1000V (high sensitivity), 48V to 1000V (low sensitivity) Frequency 50/60Hz Indication Sound and light; bargraph; red = live, green = neutral Safety rating CAT III 1000V Auto power-off 3 minutes Power 2 × 1.5V AAA (not included) Size approx. 156 × 20 × 20 mm What's in the Box 1 × WinAPEX ET8900 voltage tester pen

Product details

The Thin Film Pressure Sensor RP-C7.6-LT-LF2 is a versatile thin film pressure sensor designed for projects requiring high sensitivity and dynamic pressure detection. Its compact size, flexible design, and wide pressure range make it ideal for various applications. Key Features : Ultra-Thin and Flexible: Easily integrates into various projects. Highly Sensitive: Detects even the slightest pressure changes (30g trigger force). Wide Pressure Range: Measures static and dynamic pressure up to 1.5kg. Fast Response Time: Reacts to pressure changes in less than 10ms. Durable: Withstands over 1 million presses and operates in a wide temperature range (-40°C to +85°C). Easy to Use: Simple integration and reliable operation. Applications: Pressure Switch: Monitor and trigger actions based on pressure changes. Bed Monitoring Systems: Detect pressure distribution and improve sleep quality analysis. Smart Wearables: Enhance functionality in intelligent sneakers and other wearables. Medical Devices: Monitor pressure in various medical applications. Robotics & Automation: Integrate pressure sensing for precise control.

Product details

The BENETECH GM8800B is a handheld combustible gas leak detector for finding leaks in gas pipes, fittings, cylinders, heaters and stoves . Its 40 cm flexible neck reaches behind appliances and along pipe runs, and the sensitive sensor picks up small leaks (50ppm methane). It warms up in about 60 seconds, responds in 2 seconds and alerts with an alarm LED. Sensitivity is adjustable to home in on the exact leak point, and it runs for about 8 hours on four AA batteries.

Product details

MikroDuino Kiwi board has been designed for a student and hobbyist to learn and explore AVR microcontrollers. The board contains a 40 pin AVR microcontroller, ATMega32A that has 32 general purpose IO lines. Eight of these lines can be configured to act as analog to digital converters. The board also has Eight LED in-dicators that can be connected to the IO lines of your choice. Also board has two push switches and an inter face for character LCD.

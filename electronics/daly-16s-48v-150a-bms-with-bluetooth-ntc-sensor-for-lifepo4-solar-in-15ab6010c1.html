DALY 16S 48V 150A BMS with Bluetooth & NTC Sensor for LifePO4 Solar Inverter UPS Battery Pack The DALY 16S 48V 150A BMS is a high-performance Battery Management System (BMS) designed for LifePO4 battery packs used in solar inverters, UPS systems, and other energy solutions. Equipped with Bluetooth capability, temperature monitoring, and advanced protection functions, this BMS ensures reliable performance and a longer battery lifecycle. With built-in features like overcharge, over-discharge, and short-circuit protection, it offers comprehensive safety and control over your battery pack. Key Features Model Number: DL16S Bluetooth Version: Monitor battery health remotely through Bluetooth. Battery Compatibility: For 16S LifePO4 battery packs. Overcharge Protection: Prevents battery damage due to excessive charging. Over-discharge Voltage Protection: Safeguards against battery depletion. Short-Circuit Protection: Ensures safety by cutting off the circuit during faults. Temperature Sensor: Built-in NTC sensor for temperature monitoring and safety. Waterproof Design: Durable, water-resistant construction. Balancing Function: Ensures each cell charges and discharges evenly. Benefits of the DALY 16S 48V 150A BMS Enhanced Safety: Offers multiple layers of protection, including overcurrent and temperature monitoring, to prevent damage or thermal runaway. Remote Monitoring: Bluetooth functionality enables easy monitoring and control of battery health through your smartphone. Long Battery Life: Balancing each battery cell ensures longer cycles and efficient energy use. High Reliability: Built with premium MOSFETs and IC components for consistent and reliable performance. Energy Efficiency: Consumes extremely low power, with a total consumption of less than 50µA.

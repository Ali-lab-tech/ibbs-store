Product details

A 1µF (microfarad) SMD (Surface Mount Device) capacitor with a 50V voltage rating and an 0805 package is a small and versatile electronic component used in various circuits. Here’s a detailed description: Capacitance : 1µF (microfarad), indicating the amount of electrical charge the capacitor can store. Voltage Rating : 50V, the maximum voltage that the capacitor can handle without risk of damage or breakdown. Package Type : 0805, a standard SMD package size, with dimensions of approximately 0.08 inches (2.0 mm) in length and 0.05 inches (1.25 mm) in width. Construction : These capacitors are typically ceramic, which provides stability, reliability, and a wide range of temperature tolerance. Applications : Commonly used in decoupling, filtering, and bypassing applications in power supply circuits, signal processing, and general electronic circuits where space is a concern.

Product details

The UNI-T LM120C is a premium camera laser distance meter that measures up to 120 m with ±2 mm accuracy. Unlike ordinary rangefinders, it has a built-in digital camera shown on a 2.4-inch IPS colour screen — so you can see exactly where the laser dot lands, even in bright daylight or at long range where the red dot would normally be invisible to the eye. A crosshair with 2× zoom makes far-off targets easy to hit, while the rotatable screen keeps readings upright at any angle. It calculates area, volume, Pythagorean (indirect) height and triangle area , adds and subtracts, stores 100 readings, and includes electronic levelling with auto horizontal/vertical modes. Recharges over USB. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty and English manual.

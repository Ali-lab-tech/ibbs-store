Product details

The STM32F030C8T6 Development System Board is a budget-friendly platform for exploring the STM32F0 series of microcontrollers. With its powerful ARM Cortex-M3 core, extensive peripherals, and versatile programming options, this board is ideal for a wide range of embedded applications. Key Features: Affordable and Powerful: Enjoy the benefits of a high-performance microcontroller at an affordable price. Versatile Programming: Program using both USB/Serial converter or ST-Link USB Dongle. Multiple Power Options: Power the board using USB, 5V external supply, or 3.3V directly. Wide Range of Applications: Suitable for rapid prototyping, robotics, consumer products, and more. Upgrade your embedded development projects with the STM32F030C8T6 Development System Board and experience the power and versatility.

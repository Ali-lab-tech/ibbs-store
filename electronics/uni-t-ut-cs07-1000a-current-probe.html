Product details

The UNI-T UT-CS07 is a 1000 A AC/DC current probe (clamp-on sensor) that extends the current-measuring range of your digital multimeter or clamp meter. Its clamp-on jaw reads current without breaking the circuit, making high-current work on panels, motors and cabling fast and safe. Rated CAT III 1500 V / CAT IV 600 V for industrial and solar DC work, it pairs with UNI-T multimeters and clamp meters. Genuine UNI-T supplied by with warranty.

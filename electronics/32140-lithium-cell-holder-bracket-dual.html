Product details

Build cleaner, safer lithium battery packs with this Dual Cell Holder Bracket — compatible with 32140 sized cylindrical lithium cells (32mm × 140mm). Whether you're assembling a solar storage bank, EV pack, or off-grid power system, this snap-fit spacer keeps your cells perfectly aligned and properly spaced. Key Features Cell Compatibility: Fits 32140 (32mm × 140mm) lithium cells. Dual Cell Design: Holds 2 cells side by side for modular, scalable battery pack assembly. Durable ABS Plastic: Lightweight, flame-retardant construction built for demanding energy storage environments. Improves Safety: Maintains consistent cell spacing for airflow and heat dissipation, reducing thermal runaway risk.

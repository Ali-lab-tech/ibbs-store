Product details

The UNI-T UT658A is a USB tester that plugs inline on a Type-A port to read voltage, current, capacity and energy — perfect for checking chargers, power banks, cables and fast-charging (QC 2.0/3.0). It measures 4–24 V and 0.05–3 A , logs mAh capacity and Wh energy , tests cable equivalent resistance (1–480 Ω) and D+/D- data-line voltage, stores 10 record sets and times sessions up to 99 hours.

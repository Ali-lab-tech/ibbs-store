Product details

The FNIRSI FDM01 is a handheld decibel meter for checking noise in workshops, factories, classrooms, generators and audio setups. It measures 30 – 130dB with ±1.5dB accuracy, A-weighting and fast or slow response. It meets IEC651 Type 2 and ANSI S1.4 Type 2, adds temperature and humidity readings, a light alarm and MAX hold, and runs on a rechargeable 600mAh battery. Key Features 🔊 30 – 130dB range ±1.5dB accuracy at 94dB/1kHz 📏 A-weighting, fast and slow response 📈 MAX hold and over/under indication 🌡️ Temperature and humidity readout 🔋 Rechargeable 600mAh battery with 15-minute auto power-off Technical Specifications Measuring range 30 – 130dB Accuracy ±1.5dB (94dB @ 1kHz) Frequency range 31.5Hz – 8.5kHz Weighting A Response Fast / Slow Resolution 0.1dB, 2 readings/s Microphone 6 mm condenser Standards IEC651 Type 2, ANSI S1.4 Type 2 Battery 3.7V 600mAh lithium Operating conditions 0 – 40°C, 10 – 80% RH What's in the Box 1 × FNIRSI FDM01 sound level meter 1 × charging cable 1 × product manual

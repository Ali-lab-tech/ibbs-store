Product details

The FNIRSI TDM-120P combines a 120 × 90 thermal imaging camera with a 19,999-count True RMS multimeter. Spot a hot breaker, loose terminal or overloaded cable on the thermal image, then confirm it with a voltage or resistance reading from the same tool. The vanadium-oxide sensor has <60mK sensitivity and reads -20 to 400°C with ±2°C or ±2% accuracy, with hot and cold spot tracking. A 2.8-inch touchscreen, 7 colour palettes and SD card storage make reporting easy.

Product details

The FNIRSI SFD-02 monitors formaldehyde (HCHO) and TVOC levels indoors, which is useful after painting, new furniture or renovation. A 2.4-inch tempered-glass touchscreen shows HCHO, TVOC, humidity and time together. It grades formaldehyde from excellent to severe, records history, averages readings across a room and lets you set your own alarm thresholds. The electrochemical HCHO sensor warms up in 5 seconds. Key Features 🧪 Electrochemical formaldehyde sensor 5-second warm-up 🌫️ TVOC 0 – 10mg/m³ semiconductor sensor 🖐️ 2.4" glass touchscreen with HCHO, TVOC, humidity and time 📈 History, spatial averaging and alarms 🔋 1000mAh battery Type-C 5V 2A Technical Specifications Formaldehyde Electrochemical, 5 s warm-up TVOC 0 – 10mg/m³, semiconductor, 120 s warm-up HCHO grading Excellent <0.08 / Good <0.1 / Mild <0.2 / Moderate <0.3 / Severe ≥0.3 mg/m³ Display 2.4" tempered-glass touch, colour Battery 1000mAh, Type-C 5V 2A Size ≈75 × 45 × 95 mm Weight ≈193 g What's in the Box 1 × SFD-02 monitor 1 × data cable 1 × user manual

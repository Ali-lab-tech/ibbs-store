Product details

Differential Bus Transceiver The SN75176A differential bus transceiver is a monolithic integrated circuit designed for bidirectional data communication on multipoint bus transmission lines. It is designed for balanced transmission lines and meets ANSI Standard EIA/TIA-422-B and ITU Recommendation V.11. Features: * Bidirectional Transceiver * Meets or Exceeds the Requirements of ANSI Standards EIA/TIA-422-B and ITU Recommendation V.11 * Designed for Multipoint Transmission on Long Bus Lines in Noisy Environments * 3-State Driver and Receiver Outputs * Individual Driver and Receiver Enables * Wide Positive and Negative Input/Output Bus Voltage Ranges * Driver Output Capability . . . +/-60mA Max * Thermal-Shutdown Protection * Driver Positive- and Negative-Current Limiting * Receiver Input Impedance . . . 12kOhm Min * Receiver Input Sensitivity . . . +/-200mV * Receiver Input Hysteresis . . . 50mV Typ * Operates From Single 5V Supply * Low Power Requirements

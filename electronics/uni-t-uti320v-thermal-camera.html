Product details

The UNI-T UTi320V is an industrial thermal imaging camera that converts invisible infrared radiation into a visible thermal image for fault-finding in the dark and non-contact temperature analysis. It features a 320 × 240 infrared detector, a −20 to 350°C measurement range, high thermal sensitivity (≤60 mK) and a wide 42.5° × 32.5° field of view. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by Digilog.pk.

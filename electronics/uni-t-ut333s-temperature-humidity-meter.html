Product details

The UNI-T UT333S is a pocket-sized temperature and humidity meter (thermo-hygrometer) that reads ambient temperature from -10 °C to 60 °C and humidity across the full 0–100% RH range — ideal for offices, warehouses, greenhouses, labs, server rooms and home comfort monitoring. It delivers ±1 °C temperature and ±5% RH humidity accuracy with 0.1 resolution, updates once per second, and adds MAX/MIN , data hold, °C/°F selection and a backlit display in a light handheld body. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty and English manual.

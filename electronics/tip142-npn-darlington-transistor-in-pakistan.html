Product details

The TIP142 NPN Darlington Transistor is a high-performance component designed for demanding applications requiring high current and voltage handling capabilities. With its exceptional power output and reliability, the TIP142 is an ideal choice for various electronic projects. Key Features: High Current Handling: Capable of handling up to 10A of continuous current. High Voltage Capability: Handles voltages up to 100V. Darlington Configuration: Provides high current gain and low saturation voltage. Versatile Applications: Suitable for power supplies, audio amplifiers, motor control, and general-purpose switching.

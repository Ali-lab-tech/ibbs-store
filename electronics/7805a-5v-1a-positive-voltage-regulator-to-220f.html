Product details

The 7805A is a monolithic 3-terminal positive voltage regulator. It is designed to provide a fixed output voltage of 5V, making it an essential component for a wide variety of electronic applications. Unlike standard regulators, the KIA7805A series features internal current limiting, thermal shutdown, and safe operating area protection, making it virtually immune to blowouts under normal operating conditions. With adequate heat sinking, this device can deliver over 1A of output current. The unit shown is the TO-220F (Full Pack/Isolated) version. This means the mounting tab is encased in plastic, eliminating the need for insulating washers when mounting to a heatsink, reducing assembly time and short-circuit risks. Key Features: Output Current: Up to 1A (1.5A Peak with proper heatsink). Fixed Output Voltage: 5V. Protection: Internal thermal overload protection and short circuit current limiting. Package Type: TO-220F (Isolated Case) – No mica insulator required. Reliability: High ripple rejection and precise output voltage. Applications: Arduino and Microcontroller Power Supplies. Logic Systems. Battery Chargers. Solar Power Regulation. General purpose 5V DC supply. Technical Specifications: Specification Value Manufacturer KEC (Korea Electronics) Part Number KIA7805A Output Voltage +5V DC Max Input Voltage 35V DC Recommended Input 7V to 25V Output Current 1A (Max) Dropout Voltage 2V (Typ) Operating Temperature -40°C to +85°C Pin Configuration Pin 1: Input, Pin 2: Ground, Pin 3: Output How to Connect (Quick Guide): Pin 1 (Left): Connect to positive input voltage (e.g., 9V or 12V battery). Pin 2 (Center): Connect to Ground (GND). Pin 3 (Right): Output +5V (Connect to your load/circuit). Recommendation: Add a 0.33µF capacitor on the input and a 0.1µF capacitor on the output for stability.

Product details

The BENETECH GT5306A is a digital insulation resistance tester with 250V, 500V and 1000V test outputs, measuring up to 2000MΩ for motor, cable and wiring insulation checks. It also tests continuity and low resistance (buzzer below 50Ω), and measures DC voltage to 1000V and AC voltage to 750V. A large LCD, data hold, over-limit and high-voltage alarms, and an input for an external 9V supply.

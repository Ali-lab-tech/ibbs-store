Product details

Fill With Bubble Solution Once you've got a Battle Bubbles toy in your possession, you'll be able to fill its inner chamber with the included bubble solution or your favorite homemade or store-bought non-toxic bubble solution. The filling process is similar to that of filling a water gun, except you'll be pouring the solution in from a bottle instead of using water from a tap or hose. Activate Bubble Emissions Next, after filling and securely closing your toy's inner chamber, aim the end of the toy with the spigots away from you and place the other end closer to your body. On the end that's closest to you, you'll find a small round button, and once you press it and hold it with one finger, your toy will begin to emit thousands of bubbles. They should be coming out of all eight spigot holes on the front of the toy. To change the direction the bubbles are flowing in, simply aim the spigots in a different direction. Stop Spraying When you want to stop spraying bubbles, release your finger from your toy's small round button. Otherwise, the toy will keep spraying thousands of bubbles per minute until all the solution inside it is gone. At that point, you can refill the toy, or if you're done playing, store it somewhere safe until you want to have another bubble-spraying session.

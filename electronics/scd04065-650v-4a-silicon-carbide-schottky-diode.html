Description Marked "SCD04065" on the package with AUK Semiconductor's logo, this is AUK's SCD04065 , a 650V, 4A Silicon Carbide (SiC) Schottky Barrier Diode. Built with advanced SiC technology, it features near-zero reverse recovery current, temperature-independent switching behavior, and extremely fast switching performance. Housed in an isolated 2-pin TO-220F package, it provides internal electrical isolation for easy heat-sink mounting while dramatically reducing switching losses and electromagnetic interference (EMI) in high-frequency power systems.

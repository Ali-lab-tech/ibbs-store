Product details

The FNIRSI DPOX180H is a handheld two-channel digital phosphor oscilloscope with 180MHz analog bandwidth and 500MSa/s real-time sampling. A refresh rate of up to 50,000 waveforms per second helps it catch rare glitches that slower pocket scopes miss. It adds a 14-waveform DDS signal generator, 120Kpts memory, FFT, X-Y mode and a ZOOM time base for looking at serial data. The IPX6-rated body runs about 3.5 hours on its 3000mAh battery and charges over USB at 5V 2A.

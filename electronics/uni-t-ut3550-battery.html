Product details

UNI-T UT3550 — The UT3550 measures battery internal resistance and voltage together using a 4-wire Kelvin connection and 1 kHz AC injection — the method that reveals a weak cell before it fails under load. It resolves internal resistance from 0.001 mΩ to 30 Ω at ±0.5% and voltage to 100 V at ±0.05%, covering everything from small consumer cells to large industrial battery banks. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty.

Product details

The TB6560 Single Axis 3A Stepper Motor Driver Board is a high-performance solution for controlling stepper motors in various CNC router applications. This easy-to-use driver offers exceptional features and robust functionalities for precise and efficient motor control. Key Features: Wide Voltage Range: Operates from 10V to 35V DC, compatible with different power supplies (24V recommended). Powerful Output: Delivers a rated output of ±3A with a peak of 3.5A, suitable for a wide range of stepper motors. Micro-Stepping Support: Provides various micro-stepping modes (synchronizing, half-step, 1/8 step, 1/16 step) for smoother and quieter operation. Advanced Protection Circuits: Features low-voltage shutdown, over-heating protection, and over-current protection for safe and reliable operation. High-Speed Optocoupler Isolation: Minimizes signal noise and ensures reliable performance. Adjustable Current: Customize the current output to match your specific motor requirements. Large Heat Sink: Efficiently dissipates heat for long-lasting operation. Applications: CNC Routers: Control stepper motors for precise and accurate cutting and engraving. 3D Printers: Drive stepper motors for smooth and reliable filament extrusion. Industrial Automation: Automate tasks requiring precise motor control. Upgrade your CNC router or other stepper motor projects with the TB6560 Single Axis 3A Stepper Motor Driver and experience powerful, reliable, and user-friendly control.

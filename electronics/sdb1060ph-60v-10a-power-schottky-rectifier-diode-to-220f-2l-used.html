Product details

Description The SDB1060PH is a 60V, 10A Power Schottky Barrier Rectifier , manufactured by KODENSHI/AUK Corp, housed in the isolated 2-pin TO-220F-2L through-hole package. As a Schottky rectifier, it offers a low forward voltage drop and fast switching characteristics compared to standard silicon diodes, delivering low power loss and high efficiency in power conversion circuits. Key electrical characteristics include a forward voltage (VFM) of just 0.55V at 125°C, and a high surge current capacity of 150A (IFSM), giving it strong resilience against current spikes. It also features low leakage current, and is built as a fully lead-free (Pb-free) and RoHS-compliant device. The SDB1060PH is well suited for switch-mode power supplies (SMPS) and high-frequency DC-DC converters, and is especially intended for use in low-voltage, high-frequency inverters, freewheeling, and polarity protection applications — common roles for Schottky rectifiers in efficient power electronics design.

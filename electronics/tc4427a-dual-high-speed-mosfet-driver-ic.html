TC4427A Dual High-Speed MOSFET Driver IC The TC4427A is a high-performance dual MOSFET driver designed for high-speed switching applications. This versatile IC is ideal for driving power MOSFETs in motor control, power supplies, and other high-frequency circuits. Key Features: Dual high-speed MOSFET driver High peak output current: 1.5A Wide operating voltage range: 4.5V to 18V Fast rise and fall times Low propagation delay Non-inverting outputs Low supply current 8-pin DIP and SOIC packages available Technical Specifications: Supply Voltage (VDD): 4.5V to 18V Peak Output Current: 1.5A Rise Time: 25ns (typical) Fall Time: 25ns (typical) Propagation Delay: 30ns (typical) Operating Temperature: -40°C to +125°C Package Type: 8-pin DIP/SOIC Applications: Motor control circuits Switch-mode power supplies DC-DC converters PWM controllers High-frequency switching applications Power MOSFET gate drivers

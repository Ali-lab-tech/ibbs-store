Product details

The UNI-T UT-P43 is a high-bandwidth AC/DC current probe for oscilloscopes reaching DC to 25 MHz at up to 20 A , 100 mV/A — for fast switching-current waveforms in SMPS, inverters and RF power. The clamp jaw measures current non-intrusively and outputs to a scope over BNC. Genuine UNI-T accessory supplied by with warranty.

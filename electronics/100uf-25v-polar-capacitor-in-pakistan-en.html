Product details

Aluminium 100uF 25V Electrolytic Polar Capacitor is general- purpose and filter capacitor. Aluminium 100uF 25V Electrolytic Polar Capacitor, whose anode electrode (+) is made of a pure aluminium foil with an etched surface. The aluminium forms a very thin insulating layer of aluminium oxide by anodization that acts as the dielectric of the capacitor. A non-solid electrolyte covers the rough surface of the oxide layer, serving in principle as the second electrode (cathode) (-) of the capacitor. A second aluminium foil called “cathode foil” contacts the electrolyte and serves as the electrical connection to the negative terminal of the capacitor. Features: 105°C General purpose radial electrolytic capacitors. Ideal for use in power supply smoothing. High-Quality and Longlife Electrolytic Capacitors. Low Leakage Current. Wide Operating Temperature Range. Great transient/surge suppressor capacitors. Attach one between the power and ground of your project to ensure smooth power delivery.

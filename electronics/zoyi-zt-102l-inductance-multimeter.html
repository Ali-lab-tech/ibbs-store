Product details

The ZOYI ZT-102L is a compact true RMS multimeter that adds something most multimeters lack: inductance measurement up to 60H (resolution 0.001mH), with automatic range. It is handy for checking inverter chokes, transformer windings and filter coils without a separate LCR meter. It also covers voltage, current up to 10A, resistance, capacitance and frequency, with NCV live-wire detection, on a 6000-count display. It weighs 114 g.

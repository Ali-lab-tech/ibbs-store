P75V65 IGBT - High-Performance Transistor for Industrial Applications The P75V65 IGBT is a high-efficiency insulated-gate bipolar transistor designed for demanding power applications. Its 650V voltage rating and 75A continuous current handling capability provide fast switching with minimal power losses, making it ideal for industrial motor drives, inverters, and renewable energy systems. Benefits of P75V65 IGBT High Voltage Capacity: Operates efficiently up to 650V, ensuring reliable performance in industrial-grade systems. Fast Switching: Reduces energy losses and boosts system efficiency. High Current Handling: Capable of continuous operation at 75A without degradation. Thermal Stability: Performs reliably across wide temperature ranges. Compact TO-247 Package: Easy integration into circuit designs.

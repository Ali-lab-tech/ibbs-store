📝 Product Description: The 8-Position DIP Switch (Slide Type) is a manual electrical switch designed for use on printed circuit boards (PCBs) to control device settings, select modes, or set addresses. Each of the 8 switches operates independently, allowing for binary input configurations and quick customization without the need for jumpers. Commonly used in microcontroller projects, communication devices, industrial controls, and configuration settings , this DIP switch offers easy on/off switching with no parts to lose.

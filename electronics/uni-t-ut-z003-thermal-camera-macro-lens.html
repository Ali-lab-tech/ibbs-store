The UNI-T UT-Z003 macro lens is a snap-on close-up adapter for UNI-T thermal cameras UTi730E, UTi720E, UTi730V and UTi720V . It magnifies fine detail so the imager can resolve tiny electronic components and reveal the exact fault point during circuit-load and temperature analysis. It clips around the camera in seconds and removes just as easily for normal use. Genuine UNI-T accessory supplied by Digilog.pk.

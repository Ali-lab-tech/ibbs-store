Product details

The SMD SN74HC76 is a dual JK flip-flop IC in SOP-16 (SOIC-16) surface-mount package, designed for sequential logic and data storage applications in Pakistan. This CMOS logic integrated circuit contains two independent negative edge-triggered JK flip-flops with preset and clear inputs, providing reliable state storage for counters, registers, and digital control systems. The SMD package enables compact PCB layouts and automated assembly for professional electronics manufacturing and prototyping.

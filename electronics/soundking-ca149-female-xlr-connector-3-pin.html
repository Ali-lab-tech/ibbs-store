Soundking CA149 Female XLR Connector (3-Pin) The Soundking CA149 is a durable 3-pin female XLR connector used for professional audio connections. It is commonly used to connect microphones, mixers, speakers, and other sound equipment. 🎤 Key Features 3-Pin Female XLR Design: Standard 3-pin configuration for balanced audio signals Strong Metal Body: Made from high-quality metal for durability and long life Secure Locking Mechanism: Built-in latch ensures a tight and reliable connection Noise-Free Signal: Designed for balanced audio transmission , reducing noise and interference Easy Cable Installation: Screw-type assembly makes it simple to connect and repair cables 🔌 Common Uses Microphone cables Audio mixers and amplifiers Professional sound systems Studio and live performance equipment ⚙️ How It Works 👉 This is a female connector , meaning it connects to a male XLR plug 👉 The 3 pins carry audio signals: Pin 1 → Ground Pin 2 → Positive signal Pin 3 → Negative signal Package Includes: 1x Soundking CA149 Female XLR Connector 3 Pin

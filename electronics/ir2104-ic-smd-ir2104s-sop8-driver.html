IR2104/IR2104S: Power Your High-Voltage Projects The IR2104 and IR2104S are high-voltage, high-speed power MOSFET and IGBT drivers with independent high-side and low-side referenced output channels. These integrated circuits (ICs) are designed for bootstrap operation and are fully operational up to +600V. Key Features : Dual Channel: Drive both high-side and low-side MOSFETs/IGBTs with a single IC. High Voltage Operation: Handles voltages up to 600V, making it suitable for a wide range of applications. Bootstrap Operation: No need for an isolated power supply for the high-side gate driver. Undervoltage Lockout: Protects your MOSFETs/IGBTs from damage due to low supply voltage. Deadtime Control: Prevents shoot-through currents in half-bridge configurations. Applications : The IR2104/IR2104S are ideal for: Motor Drives: Control brushless DC (BLDC) motors, AC induction motors, and stepper motors. Inverters: Build power inverters for solar systems, UPS, and other applications. Switch Mode Power Supplies (SMPS): Drive high-voltage switches in SMPS designs.

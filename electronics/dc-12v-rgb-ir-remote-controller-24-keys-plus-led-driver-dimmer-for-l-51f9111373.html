Product details

This is a remote rgb led controller with it remote to drive SMD 2835/3528/5050/5730/5630/3014 LED's. This IR controller with remote offers a convenient way to control color changing RGB LED strip lights. Adjust brightness, turn lights on or off, choose from 16 solid colors, or choose a dynamic mode for added effect. This is an IR remote which requires a line of sight between the remote and the controller. Installation is simple, just connect it between the driver and the strip.

Product details

UNI-T UPO2204 — The UPO2204 is a 200 MHz mixed signal oscilloscope with 4 analog channels. Digital channels available via optional UT-M15 logic probe pod. It samples at 2.0 GSa/s with 56 Mpts memory depth and captures waveforms at up to 1,000,000 wfms/s — one of the fastest capture rates in its class — on an 8-inch capacitive touchscreen. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by Digilog.pk with warranty. Key Features 200 MHz Bandwidth, 4 Analog Channels — Four-channel capture at up to 200 MHz. 2.0 GSa/s Sample Rate — Captures fast edges and high-frequency content with accurate timing. 56 Mpts Memory Depth — Extended capture without reducing sample rate. 1,000,000 wfms/s Capture Rate — High acquisition speed reveals rare glitches, runts, and timing violations. 16 Digital Channels (Optional) — Add mixed-signal capability with the UT-M15 logic probe pod. 36 Automated Measurements — Time, voltage, frequency, and statistical measurements with real-time tracking. Area Trigger — Draw a region on screen to trigger on waveform anomalies.

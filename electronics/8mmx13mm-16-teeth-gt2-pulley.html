Product details

The 8mm x 13mm 16 teeth GT2 pulley is a small mechanical component designed for use in timing belt systems, particularly in precision motion control applications such as 3D printers, CNC machines, and robotics. Here's a breakdown of its key features: Bore Diameter : 4mm (the central hole where the motor shaft or other rotating part fits) Outer Diameter : 13mm (the diameter of the pulley itself) Number of Teeth : 16 teeth (the grooves that engage with the GT2 timing belt) Pitch : 2mm (the distance between two consecutive teeth, as specified by the GT2 standard) Material : Often made from aluminum or steel for strength and durability, with some models having a smooth or anodized finish. Flanges : Typically, this pulley includes flanges on the sides to help keep the belt aligned. Use Case : Designed to work with a GT2 timing belt (with a 2mm pitch) for accurate movement and low backlash in linear motion systems. Package Included: 1x 8mmx13mm 16 teeth gt2 pulley

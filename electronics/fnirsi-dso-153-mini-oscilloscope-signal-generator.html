Product details

The FNIRSI DSO-153 is a pocket oscilloscope for students, hobbyists and routine repair work. It samples at 5MSa/s with 1MHz bandwidth and measures up to ±400V with a 10X probe. A 14-waveform signal generator up to 10kHz is built in, so you can test amplifiers and filters on the bench. It has a 2.8-inch 320 × 240 screen and a 1000mAh battery that lasts about 4 hours.

K40T120 IGBT: A Powerful and Versatile Solution The K40T120 IGBT (Insulated Gate Bipolar Transistor) is a high-performance power semiconductor designed to meet the demands of modern power electronics applications. With its fast switching speed, high current capacity, and low on-state voltage, the K40T120 is an ideal choice for a wide range of applications, including: Motor control Power supplies Inverters Solar power systems Uninterruptible power supplies (UPS) Key Benefits of the K40T120 IGBT Fast switching speed for efficient power conversion High current capacity to handle demanding loads Low on-state voltage for reduced power losses Rugged construction for reliability and durability Wide operating temperature range for versatile applications Technical Specifications Parameter Value Collector-Emitter Breakdown Voltage (V CE(sus) ) 1200 V Collector Current (I C ) 40 A Collector-Emitter Saturation Voltage (V CE(sat) ) 1.8 V (typ) Input-Output Capacitance (C iss ) 1500 pF Junction Temperature (T j ) -40°C to +150°C Package Includes 1 x K40T120 IGBT

Product details

The Hwy-800 15ML flux is a chemical agent designed for use in soldering applications to improve the quality of electrical connections. It helps to clean surfaces and prevent oxidation during the heating process, facilitating better solder flow and wetting on printed circuit boards and electronic components. Volume: 15ML Model: Hwy-800 Application: Soldering and rework

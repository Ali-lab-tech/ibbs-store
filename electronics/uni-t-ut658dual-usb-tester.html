Product details

The UNI-T UT658DUAL is a second-generation USB tester with both Type-A and Type-C input/output ports, so you can measure standard and USB-C fast charging on one instrument. It reads voltage, current, power, accumulated capacity, energy, circuit equivalent resistance and even the D+/D- data-line voltage on a clear backlit LCD. With QC3.0 (Type-A) and QC4.0 (Type-C) fast-charge protocol support, it is built for checking modern chargers, PD power banks and cables. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty. Key Features Dual Type-A + Type-C input and output ports on one tester Wide 4–24 VDC range; current up to 3 A (Type-A) / 5 A (Type-C) Measures capacity 0–99999 mAh , energy 0–1000 Wh and circuit resistance 1–480 Ω Reads USB data-line voltage (D+, D-) and stores 10 data sets Supports QC3.0 (Type-A) and QC4.0 (Type-C) fast-charge protocols Backlit LCD; recording up to 99 h 59 min 59 s Technical Specifications Input / output ports USB Type-A & Type-C Voltage range 4–24 VDC (resolution 0.01 V) Current range Type-A 0.05–3 A; Type-C 0.05–5 A (0.01 A) Capacity 0–99999 mAh (1 mAh) Energy 0–1000 Wh Circuit equivalent resistance 1–480 Ω USB data cable voltage (D+, D-) 0–3.3 V Fast-charge protocol QC3.0 (Type-A) / QC4.0 (Type-C) Data storage 10 sets Max recording time 99 h 59 min 59 s (1 s) Net weight 65.8 g Dimensions 93 × 55 × 13 mm Body colour White What's in the Box UT658DUAL USB tester Gift box English user manual Downloads UNI-T UT658 Series Product Page Certifications: CE, RoHS | HS Code: 9030.39

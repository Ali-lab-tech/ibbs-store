Product details

A 5 point 9V universal battery charger is a device used to recharge batteries that have a 9V power rating. It typically has five charging points, allowing multiple batteries to be charged simultaneously. This type of charger is designed to be universal, meaning it can charge various types of batteries, including rechargeable nickel-cadmium (NiCd) and nickel-metal hydride (NiMH) batteries. Universal battery chargers are useful for powering devices such as remote controls, smoke detectors, and portable radios. They are also commonly used in professional settings, such as in the music industry for guitar and bass effects pedals that require 9V batteries. Overall, a 5 point 9V universal battery charger can be a convenient and cost-effective solution for recharging batteries with a 9V power rating. Package Include: 1 x 9v Universal Battery Charger

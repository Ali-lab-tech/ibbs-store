Product details

An IDC (Insulation Displacement Connector) 34-pin male connector, arranged in a 2x17 configuration, is commonly used to connect 34-wire flat ribbon cables to various electronic devices. Each row contains 17 pins, totaling 34 pins, with a standard pin pitch of 2.54mm (0.1 inches). These connectors are often utilized in computer hardware, such as floppy disk drive interfaces and other peripheral connections. Key Features: Pin Configuration: 2 rows of 17 pins each, totaling 34 pins. Pin Pitch: 2.54mm (0.1 inches) between adjacent pins. Connector Type: Male header, designed to mate with female IDC connectors or sockets. Applications: Ideal for connecting flat ribbon cables in computer hardware and other electronic devices.

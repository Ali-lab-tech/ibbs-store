Product details

Material: These toothpicks are made from high-quality, natural wood, ensuring they are sturdy yet flexible. The wood is smooth and free from splinters, providing a safe and comfortable user experience. Design: The toothpicks feature a flat, thin design. This flat shape allows for a gentle and precise cleaning experience, making it easier to navigate between teeth compared to round toothpicks. The edges are slightly tapered to facilitate easy insertion between tight spaces without causing damage to gums. Softness: Despite being made of wood, these toothpicks are designed to be softer than traditional wood toothpicks. The soft texture ensures they are gentle on gums, reducing the risk of irritation or injury during use. Dimensions: The typical length of each toothpick is around 2 inches , providing a comfortable length for handling. The flat design is usually about 0.1 inches (0.25 cm) wide and thin enough to ensure flexibility. Usage: These toothpicks are ideal for dental hygiene, helping to remove food particles and plaque from between teeth. They are also suitable for other uses, such as serving small appetizers, crafting, or various household tasks.

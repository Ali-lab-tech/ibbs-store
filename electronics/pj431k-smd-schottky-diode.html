PJ431K SMD Schottky Diode – Precision Adjustable Voltage Reference The PJ431K SMD Schottky Diode is a three-terminal adjustable precision voltage reference IC, designed in a compact SMD package . It is often called a Schottky-like diode , making it perfect for regulation circuits. This component is ideal for various applications, including switching power supplies , voltage regulation, and battery management. Additionally, its efficient design ensures reliable performance across a range of electronic systems . It operates similarly to a programmable Zener diode and offers excellent temperature stability, fast response, and tight voltage tolerance . Key Features of PJ431K SMD Schottky Diode First, it provides an adjustable precision voltage reference, ranging from 2.5V to 36V. Additionally, it ensures excellent thermal stability , guaranteeing reliable performance. Furthermore, it comes in a compact SOT-23-3 SMD package , making it ideal for space-saving applications. As a result, it is perfect for use in switching regulators and power supplies . Moreover, it features a programmable shunt regulator function, offering added flexibility.

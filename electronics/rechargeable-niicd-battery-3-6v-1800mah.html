The nickel-cadmium battery (Ni-Cd battery or NiCd battery) is a type of rechargeable battery using nickel oxide hydroxide and metallic cadmium as electrodes. The abbreviation Ni-Cd is derived from the chemical symbols of nickel (Ni) and cadmium (Cd). Nicad Batteries Typically Have 20% - 40% More Capacity Than Other Regularly Available Batteries of the Same Size and Can Support up to 500 Charge/ Discharge Cycles or Five Years Standby Power Under Normal Conditions. Nickel Cadmium Batteries Are Commonly Found in Emergency Lighting, Alarm/ Security Systems, Backup Power Devices, and Other Electrical Devices.

Product details

The 10D390K is a high-quality metal oxide varistor (MOV) designed for surge protection and voltage clamping in electronic circuits. This 10mm disc varistor provides reliable overvoltage protection for sensitive components. Specifications: Model: 10D390K Varistor Voltage: 390V (at 1mA DC) Maximum Clamping Voltage: ~650V (at 10A) Disc Diameter: 10mm Lead Spacing: 5mm Maximum Energy: 25J Operating Temperature: -40°C to +85°C Blue epoxy coating for insulation Applications: Ideal for surge suppression in AC mains power supplies, motor controls, relay circuits, and general overvoltage protection in consumer and industrial electronics operating at higher voltages. Features: Fast response time to voltage transients High surge current capability Bidirectional protection RoHS compliant Reliable performance in harsh environments

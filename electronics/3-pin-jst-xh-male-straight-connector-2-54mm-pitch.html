Product details

JST-XH 3 Pin Connector Polarized connectors and headers for your perf board/PCB projects. Use these connectors for power connections or any sensor/general connection to prevent users from connecting the peripheral incorrectly or reversed. The connector comes with a compatible male connector that mates with it in only one orientation. Featured by RoboticsBD. The male connector has a spacing of 0.1′(2.54 mm) and can be easily soldered onto a standard prototype board.

Product details

The UNI-T UT381A is a high-range illuminance (lux) meter measuring from a fraction of a lux up to 400,000 lx with ±3% accuracy — for lighting design, workplaces, photography, horticulture, laboratories and quality inspection. It auto-ranges across the full scale, switches between lux and foot-candles , and adds MAX/MIN/AVG , a colour-correction coefficient (CCF) for accurate readings under different light sources, a backlit display and Bluetooth app with 99-set data logging. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty and English manual.

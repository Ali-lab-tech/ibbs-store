Product details

The FNIRSI 2D15P is a compact benchtop instrument that combines a two-channel phosphor oscilloscope (100MHz single channel, 50MHz dual channel, 500MSa/s), a 19,999-count True RMS multimeter and a 10MHz DDS signal generator. It pairs physical buttons with a 4.3-inch capacitive touchscreen and offers memory depth up to 1M points, math functions, XY mode, infinite persistence and 13 automatic measurements. A 5000mAh battery makes it usable away from the mains.

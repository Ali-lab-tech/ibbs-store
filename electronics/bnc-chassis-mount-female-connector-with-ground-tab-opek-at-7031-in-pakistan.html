BNC Female chassis mount connector. Solder connection with ground tab. The BNC connector is a miniature quick connect/disconnect radio frequency connector used for coaxial cable.This TE BNC connector is a front mount bulkhead socket connector with a 50 Ω. With a body plating made from nickel and the male contact made from brass, these BNC connectors are made for brilliant quality. They will terminate to popular RG coax cables using a 4GHz frequency. This BNC connector is a female gender.

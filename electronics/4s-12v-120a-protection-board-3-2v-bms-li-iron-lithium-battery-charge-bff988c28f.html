Product details

The 4S 12V 120A BMS (Battery Management System) is a protection board designed for 4-cell (4S) lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO4) batteries . It ensures your battery pack works safely by preventing damage from overcharging, over-discharging, or excessive current. ⚡ Key Features For 4S (12V) LiFePO4 Batteries: Designed for 4 × 3.2V cells connected in series High Current Support: Continuous current: up to 120A Peak current: up to 300A Multiple Protection Functions: Overcharge protection Over-discharge protection Overcurrent protection Short circuit protection Cell Balancing Function: Keeps all battery cells at equal voltage to extend battery life Efficient Heat Dissipation: Built with aluminum plate/heatsink for safer operation 🔌 Technical Specifications Battery Type: LiFePO4 (Lithium Iron Phosphate) Nominal Voltage: 12V (4S) Charging Voltage: 14.8V – 16V Charging Current: Up to 50A Overcharge Cutoff: ~3.75V per cell Over-discharge Cutoff: ~2.1V per cell Max Load Power: Up to ~1500W 🔧 How It Works 👉 Connect it to your 4 battery cells (in series) 👉 The BMS monitors voltage, current, and balance 👉 It automatically cuts off power if unsafe conditions occur 🧰 Applications Electric bikes & scooters Solar battery storage systems DIY battery packs Power tools & backup systems ⚠️ Important Notes Only for LiFePO4 batteries (NOT Li-ion or LiPo) Correct wiring is very important (follow diagram carefully) Use proper insulation and cooling for high-current use Package Includes: 1 x Protection Board 1 x connector

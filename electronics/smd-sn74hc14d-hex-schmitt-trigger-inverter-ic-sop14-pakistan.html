The SMD SN74HC14D is a hex Schmitt-trigger inverter IC in SOP-14 (SOIC-14) surface-mount package, designed for digital logic and signal conditioning applications in Pakistan. This CMOS logic integrated circuit contains six independent Schmitt-trigger inverters with hysteresis, providing noise immunity and clean signal transitions for digital circuits, oscillators, and waveform shaping. The SMD package enables compact PCB layouts and automated assembly for professional electronics manufacturing and prototyping.

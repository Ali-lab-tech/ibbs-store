Product details

40 Pin Female IDC Connector The 40 Pin Female IDC (Insulation Displacement Connector) is a reliable and widely used connector for flat ribbon cables. Designed for easy termination without stripping wires, it is ideal for data transmission in embedded systems, development boards, and DIY electronics projects. Key Features 40-pin dual-row female IDC connector Compatible with standard 2.54mm (0.1") pitch flat ribbon cables Insulation displacement contact — no wire stripping required Secure locking mechanism for reliable connection Durable black thermoplastic housing Suitable for high-density PCB and cable applications Specifications Number of Pins 40 (2 rows × 20 pins) Pitch 2.54mm (0.1 inch) Connector Type Female IDC (Insulation Displacement) Cable Compatibility 40-wire flat ribbon cable Housing Material Black Thermoplastic (PBT/Nylon) Contact Material Phosphor Bronze / Tin-plated Current Rating 1A per contact Voltage Rating 250V AC Operating Temperature -25°C to +85°C Mounting Type Cable Mount Common Applications IDE hard drive and optical drive interfaces Microcontroller and development board connections Parallel data bus wiring DIY electronics and prototyping projects Embedded systems and industrial control panels

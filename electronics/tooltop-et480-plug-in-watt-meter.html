Product details

The TOOLTOP ET480 is a plug-in watt meter: plug it into the wall socket, plug your appliance into it, and it shows how much voltage, current and power the appliance is using. It is a simple way to check the real load of an AC, fridge, motor or UPS, or to size a solar system. It works on 85 to 265V supplies and measures current from 0.002A up to 20A. Key Features 🔌 Plug-in design : no wiring needed ⚡ Voltage range 85 to 265V 📏 Current range 0.002 to 20A 📊 Shows voltage, current and power of the connected load ✅ Accuracy class 3% Technical Specifications Model ET480 Brand TOOLTOP Voltage range 85 to 265V Current range 0.002 to 20A Accuracy class 3% Operating temperature −10 to 40℃ What's in the Box 1 × TOOLTOP ET480 watt meter

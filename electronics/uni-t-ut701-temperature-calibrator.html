Product details

UNI-T UT701 — a single-function temperature calibrator that sources DC millivolts, resistance and temperature to check and calibrate thermocouple and RTD instrumentation. It supports thermocouple types J, K, T, E, R, S, B, N, Wre325 and Wre526 and RTDs Pt10, Pt100, Cu50, Cu100 , with slow/fast/step ramp and 25%/100% step functions. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by Digilog.pk with warranty.

Product details

The FNIRSI IPS3608 is a mains-powered digital bench power supply with 0 – 36V and 0 – 8A output, up to 285W. Voltage sets in 10mV steps and current in 1mA steps, with readback accurate to ±(0.3% + 3 digits) for voltage. Eight protections cover over-voltage, over-current, over-power, over-temperature, short circuit and reverse connection. USB-C PD3.0 and USB-A QC/FCP/SCP/AFC ports also let you charge or test phones and laptops. Key Features ⚡ 0 – 36V, 0 – 8A, 285W from 100 – 240V AC 🎯 10mV / 1mA setting resolution 🛡️ 8 protection circuits OVP, OCP, OPP, OTP, short circuit and more 🔌 USB-C PD3.0 and USB-A fast charge 🖥️ 2.8" colour screen adjustable backlight Technical Specifications Input 100 – 240V AC, 50/60Hz Output 0 – 36V, 0 – 8A, 0 – 285W Setting resolution 0.01V / 0.001A Voltage readback ±(0.3% + 3 digits) Current readback ±(0.15% + 5 digits) Load regulation ±(0.2% + 2 digits) Fast charge USB-C PD3.0; USB-A FCP, SCP, AFC, QC2.0, QC3.0 Display 2.8" colour Size ≈138 × 214 × 115 mm Weight ≈1.54 kg What's in the Box 1 × IPS3608 power supply 1 × power cable 2 × alligator clip leads (red, black) 1 × data cable 1 × user manual

Product details

The FNIRSI HRM-10H measures a battery's internal resistance and voltage together using a true 4-wire, 1kHz AC method. It is the quickest way to match 18650 and LiFePO4 cells for a pack or to find a tired cell. Sorting mode flags good and bad cells against your thresholds, and results are stored and exportable over USB. This is the HRM-10H kit; contact us to confirm the included probes and clips. Key Features 🔋 Internal resistance 0 – 200Ω from 20mΩ range, ±0.5% ⚡ Voltage 0 – ±100V DC measured at the same time ✅ Sorting mode pass/fail against your limits 💾 History and data export over USB 🔌 Type-C rechargeable 1000mAh battery Technical Specifications Measurement True 4-wire, AC 1kHz Voltage 0 – ±100V DC (auto, 1V, 10V, 100V) Internal resistance 0 – 200Ω (auto, 20mΩ – 200Ω ranges) Accuracy ±0.5% Functions Sorting mode, history, data export, user calibration Battery 1000mAh, Type-C 5V 1A Size 158.7 × 80.5 × 28.4 mm Weight 225 g What's in the Box 1 × FNIRSI HRM-10H tester Test accessories as supplied in the HRM-10H kit (contact us to confirm)

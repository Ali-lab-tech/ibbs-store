FSR Single Zone Sensor RP-C7.6LT-LF2 | Thin Film Pressure Sensor The FSR Single Zone Sensor RP-C7.6LT-LF2 by Legact is an ultra-thin, flexible force-sensitive resistor designed to detect varying pressure levels. With a trigger force of just 2g and a thickness of only 0.2mm, this sensor provides highly sensitive and accurate pressure readings across a range of 2g to 1.5Kg. It is ideal for applications in electronics, robotics, medical devices, and consumer products where precise force measurements are essential.

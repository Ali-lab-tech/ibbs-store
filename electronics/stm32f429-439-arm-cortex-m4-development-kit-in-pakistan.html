Product details

The STM32F429/ 439 Discovery Board is a powerful and versatile platform for exploring the STM32F4 series of microcontrollers. With its high-performance Cortex-M4 core, rich set of peripherals, and integrated development tools, this board is ideal for a wide range of embedded applications. Key Features: Powerful Cortex-M4 Core: Experience high-performance processing capabilities for demanding applications. Integrated ST-Link/V2 Debugger: Easily program and debug your microcontroller projects. Touchscreen LCD: Create intuitive and engaging user interfaces. 3-Axis Accelerometer and Gyroscope: Measure motion and orientation for various applications. SDRAM: Expand memory for high-resolution graphics and data storage. USB Connectivity: Connect to external devices and peripherals. Versatile I/O: Access a wide range of I/O pins for customization. Upgrade your embedded development projects with the STM32F429/ 439 Discovery Board and unlock the full potential of the STM32F4 series.

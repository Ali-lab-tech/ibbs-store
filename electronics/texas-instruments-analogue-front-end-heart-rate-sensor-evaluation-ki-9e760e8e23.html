Product details

The AFE4403EVM is a comprehensive evaluation kit designed to prototype and test the AFE4403 analog front-end (AFE) for heart rate monitoring and pulse oximetry applications. This versatile kit provides a user-friendly platform for developers and engineers to explore the capabilities of the AFE4403. Key Features: Integrated AFE4403: Includes the AFE4403 analog front-end for efficient heart rate and pulse oximeter measurements. DB9 Pulse Oximeter Sensor Cable Support: Easily connect your pulse oximeter sensor for seamless integration. High-Speed Data Acquisition: Acquire data at up to 3000 Hz in evaluation mode for precise measurements. USB Connectivity: Connect to your PC for easy configuration and data analysis. User-Friendly GUI: Access all AFE4403 registers through an intuitive graphical interface. Firmware Upgradeable: Update the device's firmware via USB for enhanced functionality. Applications: Medical Devices: Develop heart rate monitors, pulse oximeters, and other wearable health devices. Research and Development: Conduct research and development in the field of physiological monitoring. Healthcare: Improve patient monitoring and diagnosis. Upgrade your projects with the AFE4403EVM and unleash the power of the AFE4403 analog front-end. Package Include: - AFE4403EVM Demonstration Kit - USB to micro USB cable - DB9 to 8-pin header sensor cable - NJRC NJL5310R sensor board - Osram SFH7050 sensor board

Product details

Dual USB 5V 2A power Bank Module Dual USB Ports: This power bank module is equipped with two USB output ports, allowing you to charge two devices simultaneously. This feature is useful when you need to charge multiple devices on the go. Output Voltage: The module provides an output voltage of 5V, which is the standard voltage for USB-powered devices. This ensures compatibility with a wide range of electronics. Output Current: Each USB port delivers a maximum current of 2A (Amps). This current rating indicates the speed at which devices can be charged. A 2A output is considered a fast charging rate and can quickly charge most smartphones and tablets. Input Voltage: The module can be charged itself with a 5V input voltage. This is typically achieved through a micro USB or USB-C input port. You can connect it to a compatible charger or a USB-enabled power source for recharging. Battery Capacity: The module may come with a built-in rechargeable lithium-ion or lithium-polymer battery. The capacity of the battery determines how many times you can charge your devices before needing to recharge the power bank module. LED Indicator: Many power bank modules have LED indicators to display the remaining battery capacity. This helps users gauge when the power bank needs to be recharged. Protection Features: A good power bank module should have built-in protection mechanisms, such as overcharge, over-discharge, over-current, and short-circuit protection. These features ensure the safety of your devices and the power bank itself. Compact and Portable: Power bank modules are designed to be compact and portable, making them easy to carry in a bag, pocket, or purse. They are ideal for travel, outdoor activities, or situations where you may not have access to a power outlet. Versatility: These modules can charge a variety of devices, including smartphones, tablets, Bluetooth headphones, portable speakers, and more, as long as the devices are compatible with USB charging. DIY and Integration: Some power bank modules are designed for DIY enthusiasts and electronics hobbyists. They can be integrated into custom projects, making them suitable for building custom power banks or embedded power solutions. Package Included: 1x Dual USB 5V 2A power Bank Module

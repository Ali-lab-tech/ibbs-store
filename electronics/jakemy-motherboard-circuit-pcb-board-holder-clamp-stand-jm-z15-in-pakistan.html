Product details

Jakemy JM-Z15 is an adjustable metal holder used for mounting and soldering small PCBs. The holder is equipped with three removable card mouths, a screw, and a spring that moves along the guide, which provides the maximum PCBs' fixation. The tool is made of anti-static material, which protects electronic components from being damaged by static electricity. Features: Adjustable card mouth, easy to adjust to the best width and intensity, leaving plastic antiskid granules Screw fixing hole and bottom, to prevent the occurrence of displacement clamp in operation Equipped with a variety of tools to repair the phone to use, more than one type of single screw Available for various maintenance of mobile phones and digital products Instructions: Use the 3 removable objects to choose the best position on the PCB. Adjust the two objects which can move up and down to achieve the best width and strength. Then use the automatic screws to fix it on the workbench. Hold one side of the PCB, push back, but the other side into the mobile area, and finally fix the repairing unit with screws. Specifications: Manufacturer: JAKEMY Country of Origin: China Item model number: JM-Z01 Material: Steel alloy Dimensions: 80 mm × 125 mm Weight: 200 g Package Includes: 1 x Adjustable Metal PCB Holder Jakemy JM-Z15

Shelled N20 Gear Motor – Waterproof, High Torque, Compact The Shelled N20 Gear Motor (5V 90RPM) is a high-torque, slow-speed micro DC reduction motor completely enclosed in a protective outer casing. Combining a miniature N20 core motor with an all-metal gearbox, this module delivers immense twisting power at a steady 90 RPM. Its unique waterproof shelled enclosure shields the delicate inner gears and electrical terminals from moisture and dust, making it the premier choice for demanding outdoor robotics and manual DIY hardware projects.

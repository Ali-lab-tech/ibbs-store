The Hall Effect Sensor Module 3144 is a compact and cost-effective device used to detect the presence of a magnetic field. It operates on the principle of the Hall Effect, where a voltage difference (Hall voltage) is produced across an electrical conductor, transverse to an electric current in the conductor and a magnetic field perpendicular to 1 both. This module is widely used in various applications, including: Position sensing: Detecting the position of a moving object. Speed measurement: Measuring the rotational speed of a motor or wheel. Current sensing: Measuring the current flowing through a conductor. Proximity detection: Detecting the presence of a nearby object without physical contact.

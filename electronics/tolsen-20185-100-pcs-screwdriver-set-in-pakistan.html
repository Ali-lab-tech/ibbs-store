Tolsen 20185 100 Pcs Screwdriver Set with Ratchet and VDE Drivers Most screwdriver sets force a compromise: either full-size drivers for cabinet and panel work, or tiny precision drivers for electronics. The Tolsen 20185 gives you both in one tray, plus a 72-tooth ratchet handle, 60 quarter-inch bits, magnetic nut sockets and a mains voltage tester - 100 pieces that cover almost everything a workshop, a service van or a home toolbox runs into. Every blade is chrome vanadium (Cr-V) steel with a two-component grip, so the handles stay comfortable through a long job and the tips resist rounding. The full-size and precision drivers are magnetic, which matters when you are fishing a screw out of a recessed housing. Two VDE insulated drivers are included for work near live circuits, and the set arrives on a plastic display stand so you can see at a glance which size is missing.

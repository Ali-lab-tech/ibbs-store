Product details

A 1W LED with a heat sink is a compact, high-efficiency light source designed for various applications, such as in flashlights, indicators, and small lighting fixtures. Here’s a breakdown of its components: 1W LED: Power : The LED operates at 1 watt of power, offering a bright output relative to its size. Light Output : It typically produces around 80-120 lumens, depending on the specific model and design. Forward Voltage : The forward voltage is generally between 3V and 3.6V, and the current required is around 300-350 mA. Color Options : It can come in various colors (white, red, green, blue, etc.) or be part of a multicolor RGB setup. Size : The LED chip is small, usually a few millimeters across. Heat Sink: Material : The heat sink is usually made of aluminum, which has high thermal conductivity to dissipate heat efficiently. Shape : It can be designed with fins or grooves to increase the surface area for better heat dissipation. The size of the heat sink depends on the application but is generally compact to match the size of the LED. Purpose : As LEDs generate heat during operation, the heat sink is essential to maintain the LED's lifespan and efficiency. Without it, the LED could overheat, leading to reduced performance or failure. Integration: Mounting : The LED is often mounted directly onto a metal core printed circuit board (MCPCB) for heat transfer. The heat sink is either integrated into the package or attached externally. Usage : These LEDs are ideal for applications requiring energy efficiency, long life, and high light output, such as in spotlights, automotive lighting, and displays.

Product details

The UNI-T A12T is a desktop/wall temperature and humidity meter (thermo-hygrometer) with a large 4.5-inch LCD , an external temperature probe for indoor/outdoor readings, and a built-in comfort indicator and clock — ideal for homes, offices, greenhouses and nurseries. It reads -10 to 50 °C at ±1 °C and 20–95% RH at ±5% RH, shows a wet/dry/comfort rating, and includes an alarm clock with 12/24-hour display. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by Digilog.pk with warranty and English manual.

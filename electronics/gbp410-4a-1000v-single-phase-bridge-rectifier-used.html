Description The GBP410 is a single-phase glass passivated bridge rectifier , rated for 4A output current and 1000V peak reverse voltage (VRRM) , housed in the compact 4-pin GBP through-hole package. It integrates four rectifier diodes in a bridge configuration on a single glass-passivated chip, converting AC input into full-wave rectified DC output — a fundamental building block for power supply circuits. Clearly marked terminals ( +, ~, ~, − ) make wiring straightforward: the two "~" pins accept the AC input, while the "+" and "−" pins provide the rectified DC output. With a forward voltage drop of around 1.1V and a high surge current capability of up to 135A (IFSM), this rectifier is built to handle inrush current spikes reliably, making it a durable choice for general-purpose AC-DC conversion in a wide range of electronic devices. Its compact, PCB-friendly package and robust glass-passivated construction make the GBP410 a popular choice for power supplies, battery chargers, motor control circuits, and LED drivers where reliable full-wave rectification is needed. Key Specifications Type: Single-phase bridge rectifier Package: GBP (4-pin, through-hole) Peak Reverse Voltage (VRRM): 1000V Output Current (Io): 4.0A Forward Voltage (VF): ~1.1V @ IF = 2.0A Surge Current (IFSM): 135A Glass passivated chip construction High surge forward current capability Common Applications AC-DC power supplies and converters Battery chargers (including automotive and UPS systems) Motor control circuits in industrial automation and appliances LED driver circuits General-purpose full-wave AC rectification Package Includes 1 x GBP410 Bridge Rectifier

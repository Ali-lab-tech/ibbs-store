Product details

Description The VICTOR 5150 is a stainless steel electronic digital caliper designed for precise linear, internal, external, step, and depth measurements. It features a large LCD display with mm/inch selection and a zero-reset button, allowing measurements to be taken relative to any starting point rather than only from absolute zero. The smooth-sliding jaw assembly and thumb-roller wheel allow fine, controlled positioning for accurate readings, while the stainless steel body offers durability for regular workshop, engineering, or hobbyist use. Like other calipers in Victor's 5000-series lineup, it's built around a linear electronic encoder that converts jaw movement into a digital readout, offering fast response and consistent repeatability across the measuring range — well suited for machining, metalworking, woodworking, and general precision measurement tasks. Key Specifications Type: Electronic Digital Vernier Caliper Manufacturer: Victor (Shenzhen-based test/measurement tool manufacturer) Measurement Range: 150mm / 6" Resolution: 0.01mm / 0.0005" Accuracy: ±0.01mm (typical for this class; varies slightly by batch/calibration) Repeatability: 0.01mm / 0.0005" Maximum Measuring Speed: 3 m/s (per comparable model in the Victor 5000-series lineup) Display: LCD with mm/inch conversion and zero-set button Power: SR44 (silver oxide) 1.5V button-cell battery Operating Temperature: 0°C to 40°C Body Material: Stainless steel Common Applications Machining and metalworking measurement Mechanical and engineering inspection Woodworking and DIY precision projects General workshop and quality-control measurement tasks Package Includes 1 x VICTOR 5150 Digital Vernier Caliper

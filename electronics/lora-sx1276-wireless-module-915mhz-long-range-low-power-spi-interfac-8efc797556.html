Product details

The SX1276 LoRa Module (915MHz) is a high-performance long-range wireless transceiver module designed for Internet of Things (IoT) and low-power wireless communication . Based on Semtech’s SX1276 chipset , it supports LoRa™ spread spectrum modulation , offering extended range , interference immunity , and minimal power consumption — ideal for industrial, agricultural, and smart home applications. With support for SPI interface , this LoRa module works seamlessly with popular development platforms like Arduino , ESP32 , STM32 , and Raspberry Pi , making it a preferred solution for building LPWAN (Low Power Wide Area Network) systems. 🔧 Key Features 📶 Frequency : 915MHz (ISM band) 🔋 Ultra-Low Power Consumption 🚀 Long Range : Up to 10 km (Line of Sight) 📡 Modulation : LoRa™ / FSK / OOK 🔧 Interface : SPI – Easy to integrate with MCUs 💻 Compatible With : Arduino, STM32, ESP8266, ESP32 🏭 Ideal For : IoT nodes, telemetry, agriculture, smart grids, wireless sensor networks 📋 Technical Specifications: Attribute Details Chipset Semtech SX1276 Frequency 915 MHz (ISM band) Modulation LoRa™, FSK, OOK Operating Voltage 1.8V – 3.7V (Typically 3.3V) Interface SPI Communication Range Up to 10 km (LOS, depending on antenna) Data Rate 300 bps to 37.5 kbps Antenna Interface IPEX / SMA (depends on variant) Operating Temperature -40°C to +85°C Current (TX/RX) 28mA TX / 10.3mA RX (typ.) 🌍 Applications 📡 Long-range remote control 📊 Wireless sensor networks 🚜 Smart agriculture & irrigation 🏙️ Smart city monitoring 🔐 Industrial telemetry & metering 🔗 Arduino & ESP-based IoT Projects 📦 Package Includes 1 × LoRa SX1276 915MHz Module

Product details

The UNI-T MSO3054HD is a lab-grade 500 MHz, 4-channel mixed signal oscilloscope built on a native 12-bit ADC architecture, with 2.5 GSa/s sample rate, 500 Mpts memory depth and a 10.1-inch capacitive multi-touch display. Nine instruments in one — oscilloscope, 16-channel logic analyzer (optional pod), spectrum analyzer, DVM, frequency counter and more — for high-resolution signal-integrity work and mixed-signal debug.

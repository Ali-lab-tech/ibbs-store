Protect your home or office appliances from power surges and fluctuations with the Tomzn WiFi Multi-Function Protector TOMPD-63LW. This intelligent device offers comprehensive protection and remote control capabilities, ensuring your devices remain safe and operational. Key Features: Remote Control: Easily control power on/off from anywhere using your smartphone. Real-Time Monitoring: Track current, voltage, leakage current, and total kWh consumption. Under/Over Voltage Protection: Safeguard your devices from voltage fluctuations. Overcurrent Protection: Prevent damage caused by excessive current draw. Reconnect Timer: Automatically restores power after a power outage. Calibration: Fine-tune voltage, current, and kW readings for accuracy.

Product details

Unlock high-efficiency power management for your electronic projects with the YJD50N06A , a robust N-Channel Enhancement Mode Power MOSFET. Designed to handle demanding loads, this component features a breakdown voltage of 60V and a continuous drain current of 50A . Housed in a compact TO-252 (DPAK) surface-mount package, this MOSFET is engineered for low on-resistance and fast switching speeds, making it an excellent choice for miniaturized power supply designs and motor control circuits. Whether you are repairing an inverter or building a DIY drone controller, the YJD50N06A offers the thermal stability and reliability required for modern electronics. Key Features: High Performance: Capable of handling 60V and 50A, suitable for medium to high-power applications. Low RDS(on): Low static drain-source on-resistance ensures minimal power loss and high efficiency. Fast Switching: Optimized for high-frequency switching applications. Compact Design: TO-252 (DPAK) SMD package saves valuable PCB space while offering good heat dissipation. Reliability: High avalanche ruggedness for durable circuit designs. Applications: DC-DC Converters Motor Drivers (Brushless and Brushed DC) Solenoid and Relay Drivers Power Supply Switching Battery Management Systems (BMS) Automotive Electronics Technical Specifications: Part Number: YJD50N06A Type: N-Channel MOSFET Drain-Source Voltage ( $V_{DSS}$ ): 60V Continuous Drain Current ( $I_D$ ): 50A Package Type: TO-252 (DPAK/SMD) Mounting Type: Surface Mount Package Includes: 1 x YJD50N06A N-Channel MOSFET

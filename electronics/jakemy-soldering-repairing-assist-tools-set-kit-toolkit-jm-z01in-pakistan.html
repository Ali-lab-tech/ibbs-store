Product details

Jakemy JM-Z01 is a set of six tools that are used to solder and repair mobile phones, tablets, computer boards and other electronic devices. Features: This set of tools is dedicated to the integrated circuit assembly and disassembly, precision electronic components, welding, grinding, cleaning, repair, etc. Including knives, copper brush, spatula, fork and needles, pressure to meet the welding, cutting, scraping, hook, brush, etc. A number of different ends to help you with a variety of tasks including desoldering different kinds of components, surface mount components, removing chips from sockets, repairing electronics, desoldering parts of PCB, assembling miniatures, etc. A must-have for working with SMD. Handy to have around to hold wires to solder and to strap the board for cleaning with these tools.

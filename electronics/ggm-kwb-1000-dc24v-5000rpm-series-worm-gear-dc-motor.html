Product details

The GGM KWB-1000 is a DC worm gear (right-angle) geared motor , combining a 24V DC brushed motor with a compact worm-drive gearbox to deliver high torque output through a space-saving right-angle shaft configuration. It's built for applications that need reliable, low-speed, high-torque motion in a compact footprint. Running on a 24V DC supply with a base motor speed of 5000 RPM , the worm gear reduction inside the KWB-1000 steps this down to a much lower, more usable output speed while multiplying torque — a typical trait of worm gear drives, which also offer natural self-locking characteristics that resist back-driving from the output side. This makes the motor well suited for positioning, holding, and load-bearing applications where the mechanism needs to stay put without power applied. Housed in a robust metal gearbox casing with multiple mounting holes, the KWB-1000 is easy to integrate into machinery, automation equipment, and other mechanical assemblies requiring a compact, geared DC drive solution. Key Specifications Series: GGM KWB-1000 Voltage: DC 24V Motor Speed (pre-reduction): 5000 RPM Gear Type: Worm gear, right-angle output Housing: Metal gearbox with mounting holes Output Shaft: Perpendicular (right-angle) to motor axis Common Applications Automated machinery and conveyor systems Valve actuators and positioning mechanisms Robotics and low-speed, high-torque motion control Vending machines, packaging equipment, and industrial automation Applications requiring self-locking gear output (no back-drive under load) Package Includes 1 x GGM KWB-1000 DC24V Worm Gear Motor

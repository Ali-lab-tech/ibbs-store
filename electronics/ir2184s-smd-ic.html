Product details

Features of the IR2184S include: High and Low-Side Drivers: Capable of driving both the high-side and low-side power transistors, making it ideal for half-bridge or full-bridge configurations. Floating Channel: The high-side driver can operate with a floating channel up to 600V, allowing it to drive high-voltage power transistors. Bootstrap Circuit: The IC supports bootstrap operation, which helps in driving the high-side MOSFET or IGBT with the appropriate gate voltage. Undervoltage Lockout (UVLO): Ensures reliable operation by preventing the IC from operating if the supply voltage is too low. Logic Input: The IC can be controlled by standard logic signals, making it easy to interface with microcontrollers or other control circuitry. Matched Propagation Delays: Ensures synchronized switching between the high-side and low-side transistors, which is crucial for efficient power conversion and reduced power losses.

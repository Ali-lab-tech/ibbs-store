Product details

Description: This is a Sunshine-brand SS-C210-I replacement soldering tip cartridge, designed as a compatible/aftermarket alternative for the JBC T210-style soldering handpiece system, widely used in precision micro-soldering, mobile phone repair, and PCB rework applications. Unlike traditional soldering iron tips that are separate from the heating element, this cartridge integrates the heating element directly into the tip itself — a design used by professional micro-soldering stations to achieve extremely fast heat-up times and precise temperature control at the very point of contact. Because the heater is built directly into the replaceable tip, swapping tips is quick and doesn't require cooling down and reassembling a separate heater/tip combination — simply plug the new cartridge into the T210 handpiece. This cartridge features a fine, pointed tip geometry, well suited for delicate, high-precision soldering work such as reworking small SMD components, connector pins, and other fine-pitch tasks common in phone and small electronics repair. Key Specifications: Model: SS-C210-I Type: Integrated Heater Soldering Tip Cartridge Compatible Handpiece: T210-style (JBC T210-A / T210-NA compatible) Total Cartridge Length: 89mm Tip Assembly Length: 80mm Cartridge Diameter: 13mm Tip Point Length: ~6mm Tip Shape: Fine pointed tip Heater Type: Integrated cartridge heater (typical resistance ~2Ω, commonly rated ~12V/32W for this tip series) Important Usage Notes This is the "-I" variant with a fine pointed tip shape — other suffix codes in the SS-C210 series (e.g., -K, -IS) denote different tip geometries for different soldering tasks. Designed as a drop-in replacement for genuine C210-series cartridges; always confirm handpiece compatibility (T210-style) before ordering. As an integrated heater tip, do not attempt to disassemble or separate the heating element from the tip — replace the entire cartridge as a unit when worn or damaged. Applications: Mobile phone and small electronics repair, fine-pitch SMD rework, precision micro-soldering, connector pin soldering, and other detailed soldering tasks requiring a fast-heating, fine-point tip. Package Includes: 1 x Sunshine SS-C210-I Replacement Soldering Tip Cartridge

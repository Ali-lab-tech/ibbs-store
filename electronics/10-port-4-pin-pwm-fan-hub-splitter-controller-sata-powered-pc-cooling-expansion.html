Product details

Expand your PC’s cooling capabilities with this high-quality 10-Port PWM Fan Hub . Designed for gaming rigs and high-performance workstations, this hub allows you to connect up to 10 case fans to a single motherboard header, ensuring your system stays cool under load. It features a dedicated SATA power connection to draw power directly from your Power Supply Unit (PSU), preventing current overload on your motherboard. With full PWM (Pulse Width Modulation) support, the fan speeds can be automatically adjusted by your motherboard's BIOS based on system temperature. The PCB is equipped with high-quality solid capacitors to ensure stable voltage delivery to all connected fans. Key Features: Massive Expansion: Convert 1 motherboard fan header into 10 ports , perfect for cases requiring multiple fans (radiators, intake/exhaust). SATA Powered: Powered directly by the PSU via a SATA connector, ensuring stable power delivery without risking damage to the motherboard. PWM Smart Control: Supports 4-pin PWM fans. The motherboard can adjust the speed of all connected fans simultaneously for a balance between silence and performance. Dedicated RPM Port (RED): The Red interface is the dedicated CPU fan interface. It reports the fan speed (RPM) to the motherboard. The other 9 black interfaces follow the same PWM speed signal but do not report RPM to avoid signal conflict. Stable Design: Built with independent capacitors for each channel/rail to ensure smooth and safe operation. Compact & Clean: Helps with cable management by centralizing fan connections at the back of the case. ⚠️ Important Notes: Speed Control: All connected fans will run at the same PWM duty cycle (speed percentage) . Individual speed control for each fan is not possible; they all follow the "Master" signal. 3-Pin Fans: You can plug 3-pin fans into this hub, but they will run at full speed continuously because they do not support PWM control. Red Port Mandatory: Please ensure at least one fan (preferably the CPU cooler or main exhaust) is connected to the RED port so the motherboard can detect an RPM signal.

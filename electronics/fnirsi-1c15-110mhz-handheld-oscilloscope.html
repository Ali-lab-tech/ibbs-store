Product details

The FNIRSI 1C15 is a palm-sized single-channel oscilloscope with 110MHz bandwidth and 500MSa/s sampling, a lot of speed for a meter-sized tool. A joystick and one-key AUTO make it quick to get a stable waveform on screen. A silicone sleeve protects it from drops, and the 3000mAh battery runs for more than 10 hours. Persistence and roll modes help when you are hunting intermittent faults during maintenance or repair.

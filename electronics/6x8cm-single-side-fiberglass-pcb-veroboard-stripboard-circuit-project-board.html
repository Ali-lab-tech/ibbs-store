Product details

6x8cm Single Side Fiberglass PCB Veroboard / Stripboard This single-sided fiberglass veroboard is a versatile prototyping board for building and testing electronic circuits. Measuring 6cm x 8cm (60mm x 80mm) , it provides plenty of space for DIP ICs, discrete components, headers, and modules, making it ideal for students, hobbyists, and professionals who need a quick, reliable way to prototype. Made from durable FR4 fiberglass with copper strip tracks, this board offers good mechanical strength, stable electrical insulation, and easy soldering. The standard 2.54mm (0.1 inch) hole pitch matches most through-hole components, including resistors, capacitors, transistors, ICs, pin headers, and Arduino/Raspberry Pi shields. The parallel copper strips run the length of the board and can be easily cut with a spot-face cutter or knife to isolate sections, allowing you to build custom circuit layouts without designing a custom PCB. The board is single-sided, so all soldering and component placement is done on one side, keeping assembly straightforward.

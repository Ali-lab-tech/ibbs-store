A3150 DIP Coupler - Reliable Hall-Effect Sensor for Non-Contact Sensing Applications The A3150 DIP Coupler is a versatile Hall-effect sensor designed for accurate non-contact position sensing. It features chopper-stabilized technology to ensure stable performance under varying environmental conditions. With a robust DIP package, it can be easily integrated into multiple electronic designs, making it ideal for industrial, automotive, and consumer electronics applications. Benefits of the A3150 DIP Coupler High Precision Sensing: Offers reliable non-contact position detection. Chopper-Stabilized Design: Reduces signal drift for improved accuracy. Compact DIP Package: Facilitates easy integration into electronic circuits. Energy Efficient: Low power consumption makes it suitable for battery-powered devices. Wide Operating Temperature Range: Functions reliably from -40°C to +150°C.

SRE100N65 IGBT - High-Performance Transistor for Power Electronics The SRE100N65 IGBT is a high-efficiency, insulated-gate bipolar transistor designed for power electronics. With a voltage rating of 650V and 100A current capability, it provides fast switching with minimal power losses, making it ideal for industrial inverters, motor drives, and renewable energy systems. Benefits of SRE100N65 IGBT High Voltage Capacity: Operates up to 650V for industrial-grade applications. High Current Handling: Supports continuous operation at 100A. Fast Switching Speed: Reduces energy losses and maximizes system efficiency. Thermal Stability: Performs reliably in extreme temperatures. Compact TO-247 Package: Easy to integrate into circuit designs.

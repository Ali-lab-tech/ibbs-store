Product details

The FNIRSI DSO-TC4 combines a 10MHz, 48MSa/s oscilloscope, an automatic component tester and a 13-waveform signal generator in one handheld unit, a practical kit for repair benches and electronics students. The component tester identifies transistors, diodes, MOSFETs, IGBTs, SCRs, capacitors, resistors and inductors and shows their pinout and key parameters. It also reads DS18B20 and DHT11 sensors. A 1500mAh battery gives about 4 hours of use. Key Features ⚡ 10MHz, 48MSa/s oscilloscope ±400V with a 10X probe 🔬 Automatic component tester for BJT, FET, IGBT, SCR, diode, zener, R, L and C 🎛️ Signal generator with 13 waveforms, 0 – 50kHz, 0.1 – 3.0V 📏 Auto measurements Max, Min, Avg, RMS, Vpp, frequency, period, duty cycle 🔋 1500mAh battery about 4 hours, Type-C charging Technical Specifications Sampling rate 48MSa/s Bandwidth 10MHz Input impedance 1MΩ Test voltage 1:1 probe 80Vpp (±40V); 10:1 probe 800Vpp (±400V) Vertical sensitivity 10mV – 10V/div Time base 50ns – 20s Capacitor test 25pF – 100mF Resistor test 0.01Ω – 50MΩ Inductor test 10µH – 1000µH Zener test 0.01 – 32V Signal generator 13 waveforms, 0 – 50kHz, 0.1 – 3.0V Display 2.8" TFT colour Battery 3.7V 1500mAh Size ≈90 × 142 × 27.5 mm Weight ≈186 g What's in the Box 1 × FNIRSI DSO-TC4 Probes and test leads as supplied by FNIRSI

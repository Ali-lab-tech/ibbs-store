Product details

600W 25A Adjustable DC-DC Buck Converter Module This high-power DC-DC buck converter steps a higher DC input voltage down to a lower adjustable output voltage. It is suitable for solar, battery, workshop, LED, and electronics projects where a regulated DC supply is required. The module includes a heatsink and cooling fan to support higher-power operation. Key Features High-power DC-DC step-down (buck) converter Wide input voltage range: 12V to 75V DC Adjustable output voltage: 2.5V to 50V DC Maximum output current: up to 25A Rated power: up to 600W High conversion efficiency: up to 95% Built-in cooling fan, large heatsink, and screw terminals Adjustable controls for setting the required output Specifications Converter type Non-isolated DC-DC buck (step-down) converter Input voltage 12V to 75V DC Output voltage 2.5V to 50V DC, adjustable Maximum output current Up to 25A Maximum power Up to 600W Efficiency Up to 95% Cooling Heatsink and cooling fan SKU B261, KRT98 Suitable Applications Solar-panel and battery voltage regulation DC motor, LED, and electronics projects Workshop adjustable DC power systems Battery charging systems where a suitable charging controller is used Inverter, automotive, and industrial DC power projects Important Usage Notes This is a buck converter: the output voltage must always be set lower than the input voltage. Set and verify the output voltage with a multimeter before connecting your load. For high current or high power operation, use short, thick cables and ensure proper airflow around the heatsink and fan. Actual continuous power and current capability depend on input voltage, output voltage, ambient temperature, airflow, and installation conditions. Observe correct input/output polarity. Reverse connection may damage the module or connected equipment. Package Includes 1 × 600W 25A Adjustable DC-DC Buck Converter Module

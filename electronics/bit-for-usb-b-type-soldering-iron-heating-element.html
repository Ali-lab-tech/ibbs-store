A USB-B soldering iron heating element is the heating core used in USB-powered soldering irons. It generates controlled heat to melt solder wire for joining electronic components. How It Works The heating element converts electrical energy from a 5V USB supply into heat. Temperature is adjusted through a three-speed switch to match different soldering requirements.

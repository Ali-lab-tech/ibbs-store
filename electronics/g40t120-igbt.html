Product details

G40T120 IGBT - High-Efficiency Power Transistor for Industrial Applications The G40T120 IGBT is a high-performance insulated-gate bipolar transistor designed for heavy-duty industrial applications. It features a voltage rating of 1200V, making it ideal for use in motor drives, power inverters, and renewable energy systems. With its fast switching speed and low conduction losses, the G40T120 offers superior performance in power electronics. Benefits of G40T120 IGBT High Voltage Capacity: Supports up to 1200V, perfect for industrial applications. Fast Switching Speed: Minimizes power loss and maximizes energy efficiency. High Current Handling: Capable of continuous operation with 40A current. Thermally Robust: Operates reliably across a wide temperature range. Compact TO-247 Package: Allows easy integration into circuit designs.

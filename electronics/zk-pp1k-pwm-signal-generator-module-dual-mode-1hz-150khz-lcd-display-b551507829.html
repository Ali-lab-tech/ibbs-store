📝 Product Description The ZK-PP1K PWM Signal Generator is a versatile signal controller designed for high-precision frequency and duty cycle modulation . It supports dual output modes — PWM mode and PULSE mode — allowing you to control a wide range of devices such as LEDs, motors, solenoids , and other load-driven equipment with configurable signal parameters. Equipped with a high-resolution LCD display , push-button control, and a power-down memory function, this module is an ideal tool for signal testing, motor speed control, LED dimming , and automation timing tasks.

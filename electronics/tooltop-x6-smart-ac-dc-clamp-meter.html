Product details

The TOOLTOP X6 is a smart clamp meter with a dual colour screen and both automatic and manual test modes. It measures AC and DC current in 40A and 400A ranges , so it suits both small appliance currents and DC solar strings. It also measures AC/DC voltage up to 600V, resistance to 60MΩ, capacitance to 60mF, frequency and temperature, on a 6000-count display with auto power-off.

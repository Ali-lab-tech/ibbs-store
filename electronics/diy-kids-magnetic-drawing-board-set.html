Product Description Unlock your child's creativity and fine motor skills with our Interactive Magnetic Board Set ! Key Features: Sensory & Skill Development: Boosts hand-eye coordination, fine motor skills, problem-solving, and creative thinking. Mess-Free & Self-Contained: High-quality casing holds all steel beads securely inside—no tiny pieces to lose or clean up! Complete Activity Kit: Includes 1 magnetic stylus pen and 10 double-sided activity cards with fun design templates. Easy to Reset: Simply push the beads back down with the side of the stylus or a finger to clear the board and start a new design. Safe & Durable: Made with child-safe, non-toxic materials featuring smooth edges, designed for ages 3 and up. Specifications: Included in Box: 1 Magnetic Board, 1 Magnetic Stylus Pen, 10 Activity Pattern Card

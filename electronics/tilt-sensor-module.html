Product details

The SW-520D Tilt Sensor Module is a versatile and cost-effective solution for detecting changes in device orientation. With its simple interface, low power consumption, and built-in LED indicator, this sensor is ideal for various applications. Key Features: Accurate Orientation Detection: Accurately detects horizontal and vertical orientation changes. TTL Level Output: Easily integrates with microcontrollers and other digital devices. Low Power Consumption: Suitable for battery-powered applications. Built-in LED Indicator: Visually indicates changes in orientation. Adjustable Sensitivity: Fine-tune the sensitivity using the built-in potentiometer. Versatile Applications: Ideal for security alarms, robotic arms, digital cameras, and more. Upgrade your projects with the SW-520D Tilt Sensor Module and enhance their functionality.

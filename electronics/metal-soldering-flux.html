Product details

This 25ml bottle of metal soldering flux is designed to facilitate soldering on steel, iron, nickel, brass, and copper surfaces. Please note that this flux is acidic in nature and is not compatible with aluminum surfaces. To ensure the longevity of your components and prevent corrosion, it is essential to rinse the surface thoroughly with IPA or CTC after the soldering process is complete. Volume: 25ml Compatible surfaces: Steel, iron, nickel, brass, and copper Incompatible surfaces: Aluminum Chemical nature: Acidic

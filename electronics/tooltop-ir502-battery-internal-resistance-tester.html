Product details

The TOOLTOP IR502 measures a battery's internal resistance and voltage together, the quickest way to grade lithium 18650 and LiFePO4 cells, check lead-acid and NiCd batteries, and match cells before building a pack. It reads internal resistance from 0.000mΩ to 500Ω using a 1kHz AC signal and four-wire Kelvin probes, and voltage up to ±120V DC (non-polarised). Six resistance ranges switch automatically, the 3.2-inch screen shows both readings at once, and it charges from a built-in 1200mAh lithium battery.

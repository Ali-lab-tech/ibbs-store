Product details

UNI-T UT8805E — The UNI-T UT8805E Performance-Series Bench Digital Multimeter is a 5½-digit auto-ranging True-RMS meter with 0.015% basic DC voltage accuracy, a 5,000 rdgs/s maximum reading rate, and a 199,999-count display on a 4.3in 480×272 TFT-LCD. SCPI over LAN, RS-232, and USB makes the UT8805E a clean drop-in for automated test setups. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by Digilog.pk with warranty. Key Features 5½-digit, 199,999-count display — 0.015% basic DC voltage accuracy at the tier price point. True-RMS AC measurement — accurate readings on non-sinusoidal waveforms (switching supplies, motor drives, PWM, dimmed loads). Auto-ranging — across voltage, current, resistance, and capacitance functions, reducing operator workflow friction. 5,000 rdgs/s maximum reading rate — fast acquisition for high-throughput automated test and noise-reduction averaging. Thermocouple and RTD temperature measurement — range -270°C to 1820°C, with built-in cold-end compensation. Four-wire (Kelvin) resistance measurement — eliminates lead and contact resistance from low-resistance readings. Dual display — simultaneous viewing of two parameters (e.g., voltage + frequency) without menu toggling.

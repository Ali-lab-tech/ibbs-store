Product details

Description: This is a retractable razor blade scraper designed for quickly and safely removing stubborn residue from flat surfaces such as glass, ceramic, tile, glass-top stoves, and windows. The tool features a durable stainless steel blade mounted at the head, ideal for scraping away dried paint, adhesive residue, stickers, labels, sealant, grime, and other hard-to-remove buildup without scratching most hard, non-textured surfaces when used correctly. The blade is housed in a retractable design, controlled by a sliding thumb switch on the handle, allowing the user to extend the blade for use and safely retract it back into the protective housing when not in use — reducing the risk of accidental cuts and making the tool safer to store, carry, or pack away. The ergonomic handle is built with a soft-touch, contoured grip design, combining a rigid yellow plastic core with gray textured rubber grip zones along the sides, providing a comfortable, secure hold and reducing hand fatigue during extended use. A hole at the base of the handle allows for convenient hanging storage on a hook or pegboard, keeping the tool easily accessible in a kitchen, garage, or workshop setting. Compact and lightweight, this scraper is a practical, everyday tool for household cleaning, automotive detailing, and light-duty maintenance tasks. Key Features: Retractable stainless steel razor blade Sliding thumb-lock mechanism for safe blade extension/retraction Ergonomic, non-slip rubberized grip handle Hanging hole for convenient storage Compact, lightweight design for easy handheld use Applications: Removing paint splatters, adhesive residue, stickers, decals, sealant, and grime from glass, tile, ceramic, glass stovetops, windows, and other smooth, hard surfaces. Useful for household cleaning, automotive care, and light workshop tasks. Package Includes: 1 x Retractable Razor Blade Scraper

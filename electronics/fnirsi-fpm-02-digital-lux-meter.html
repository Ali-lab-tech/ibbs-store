SKU: FNIRSI-FPM-02

Product details

The FNIRSI FPM-02 measures light level from 0 to 99,990 lux, along with temperature and humidity, on a 2-inch colour display. The 180° rotating sensor head lets you aim at the light source while still reading the screen. It reads in lux or foot-candles, auto-ranges, logs a curve and can alarm on thresholds. Use it to check lighting in offices, classrooms, factories, greenhouses and photo studios. Key Features 💡 0 – 99,990 lux auto range, lux or foot-candle 🔄 180° rotating sensor head 🌡️ Temperature and humidity on the same display 📈 Curve logging, threshold alarm and HOLD 🔋 Rechargeable battery with sleep mode Technical Specifications Illuminance range 0 – 99,990 lux Accuracy ±(3% + 5 lux) Units Lux / foot-candle Sensor Silicon photodiode with optical filter Other readings Temperature, humidity Display 2" colour Power Rechargeable battery What's in the Box 1 × FNIRSI FPM-02 lux meter Charging cable as supplied by FNIRSI

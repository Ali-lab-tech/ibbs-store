Product Description The 3296W series is a widely used square-body multiturn trimmer potentiometer, valued for its precision adjustment capability in analog circuits. This 2M Ohm version uses a 25-turn cermet element, allowing fine, gradual resistance adjustment via a top-mounted adjustment screw — ideal for applications where accurate calibration is critical. Housed in a compact blue plastic body with side or top pin configurations, the 3296W is a standard component in circuit prototyping, calibration circuits, and control panels.

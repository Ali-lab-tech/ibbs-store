Product details

The SN75174N is a quadruple differential line driver integrated circuit housed in a 16-pin DIP package. This component is designed to provide high-speed differential signal transmission in digital communication systems. Each unit contains four independent line drivers suitable for balanced transmission lines. This package includes 5 pieces of the SN75174N IC.

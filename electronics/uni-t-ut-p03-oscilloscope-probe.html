Product details

The UNI-T UT-P03 is a 60 MHz passive oscilloscope probe with switchable 10:1 / 1:1 attenuation, rated 600 Vpp (CAT II 300 V) — a reliable replacement or spare probe for most bench oscilloscopes. It uses a standard BNC connector on a 1.3 m cable and includes trimming for compensation. Genuine UNI-T accessory supplied by Digilog.pk.

LM311 COMPARATOR IC LM311 is a single high-speed voltage comparator IC. The IC basically offers a low-cost high-performance solution to conventional design issues & also possesses direct interfacing capability with almost every PMOS, CMOS, and NMOS device. The IC also offers flexibility in terms of optimization preferences in high optimization areas, allowing it to be the first choice for most electronic design applications.

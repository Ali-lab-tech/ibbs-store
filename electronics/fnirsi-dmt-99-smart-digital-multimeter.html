Product details

The FNIRSI DMT-99 is a 10,000-count True RMS multimeter with a 2.4-inch full-colour screen. It measures AC/DC voltage to 1000V, current to 10A, resistance, capacitance up to 99.99mF, frequency, duty cycle and temperature. NCV and live-wire detection, anti-burn input protection and a recording mode make it practical for electricians, automotive work and appliance repair. The 1500mAh battery recharges instead of eating 9V cells.

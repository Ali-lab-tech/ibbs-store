Product details

Solder Paste for PCB Assembly is a crucial component in the manufacturing of printed circuit boards (PCBs). It acts as an adhesive, holding surface mount components in place during the soldering process. When heated, the paste melts, forming a strong electrical connection between the components and the PCB traces. Key Features: Adhesive Properties: Holds components securely during assembly. Conductive Properties: Creates reliable electrical connections. Easy Application: Applied to PCBs using stencils or other methods. Essential for PCB Manufacturing: A vital component in the PCB assembly process.

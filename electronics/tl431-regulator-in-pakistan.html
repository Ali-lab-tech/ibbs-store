Product details

The TL431 Regulator is a highly versatile and programmable shunt regulator diode, offering precise voltage reference control for various electronic applications. With its low output noise, temperature compensation, and wide output voltage range, the TL431 is an essential component for a variety of circuits. Key Features: Programmable Output Voltage: Set your desired output voltage from Vref to 36V using external resistors. Low Output Noise: Ensures precise and stable voltage regulation. Temperature Compensation: Maintains accurate performance over a wide temperature range. Low Output Impedance: Provides a stable voltage source even with varying loads. Versatile Applications: Suitable for digital voltmeters, power supplies, and op-amp circuitry. Upgrade your electronic designs with the reliable and efficient TL431 Regulator.

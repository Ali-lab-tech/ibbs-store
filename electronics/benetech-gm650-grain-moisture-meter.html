Product details

The BENETECH GM650 is a pen-style grain moisture meter with a two-pin metal probe . Select one of 14 grain types , push the probe into the grain and read moisture with 0.1% resolution, quick checks during buying, storage and processing. You can set a moisture limit and it gives a voice alert when the grain is over it. It also has deviation adjustment, a backlit LCD, low-battery warning and auto power-off.

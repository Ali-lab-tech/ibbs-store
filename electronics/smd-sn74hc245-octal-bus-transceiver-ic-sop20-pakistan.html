Product details

The SMD SN74HC245 is an octal bus transceiver IC in SOP-20 (SOIC-20) surface-mount package, designed for bidirectional bus interfacing and level shifting applications in Pakistan. This CMOS logic integrated circuit contains eight bidirectional buffers with 3-state outputs, providing reliable data transfer between buses operating at different voltage levels for microcontroller interfacing, data communication, and digital systems. The SMD package enables compact PCB layouts and automated assembly for professional electronics manufacturing and prototyping.

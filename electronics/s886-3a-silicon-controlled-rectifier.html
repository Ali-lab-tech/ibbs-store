Product details

What It Is The S886-3A is a discrete semiconductor device manufactured by HARRIS, packaged in the standard TO-220 case. Based on its part-numbering (S8xx series) and package style, this is a Silicon Controlled Rectifier (SCR/Thyristor) — a 3-terminal switching device. What an SCR Does An SCR is a solid-state switch used to control power to a load. It's a four-layer (PNPN) thyristor with three terminals: an input control terminal (gate), an output terminal (anode), and a terminal common to both (cathode). Once triggered by a small gate current, it "latches" on and conducts current until the main current drops below a holding threshold — making it ideal for power switching and control circuits.

PR12-4DP INDUCTIVE PROXIMITY SENSOR, PR SERIES, CYLINDRICAL, CABLE, 4MM, PNP, 10 V TO 30 V BR2295 Inductive proximity sensors are used for non-contact detection of metallic objects. Their operating principle is based on a coil and oscillator that creates an electromagnetic field in the close surroundings of the the sensing surface.

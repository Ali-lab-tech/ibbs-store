Product Description: The SN74HC85N is a high-speed CMOS 4-bit magnitude comparator integrated circuit (IC) designed to compare two 4-bit binary numbers. It provides outputs indicating whether one binary value is greater than , less than , or equal to the other. This IC is commonly used in digital logic circuits, arithmetic operations, microprocessor systems, and embedded electronics projects. Key Features: Model: SN74HC85N Type: 4-Bit Magnitude Comparator IC Package: DIP-16 (Dual In-line Package) High-speed CMOS logic Compares two 4-bit binary inputs Outputs for A > B , A < B , and A = B Wide operating voltage: 2V to 6V Low power consumption Suitable for digital electronics, educational projects, and industrial applications.

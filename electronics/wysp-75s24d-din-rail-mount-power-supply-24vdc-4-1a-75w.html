Product details

The WYSP-75S24D is a compact DIN rail mount switching mode power supply (SMPS) designed for industrial automation and control applications. This reliable power supply delivers 24VDC output at 4.1A with 75W total power capacity. Key Features: Input: 100-240V AC, 1.5A, 50/60Hz Output: 24VDC, 4.1A (75W) DIN rail mounting for easy installation Voltage adjustment potentiometer (V-ADJ) ON and OC (Over Current) LED indicators CE and RoHS certified Compact Din Series design by WoonyoungMicro Made in Korea for superior quality Applications: Perfect for PLCs, industrial controllers, sensors, actuators, and automation systems requiring stable 24V DC power.

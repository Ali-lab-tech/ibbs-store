Product details

The T2H 250V Ceramic Fuse is a robust circuit protection component designed for electronic devices requiring time-lag characteristics. Unlike standard fast-acting fuses, this Slow Blow (Time Lag) fuse allows for temporary current surges (such as motor startups or power supply inrush) without blowing, while still protecting against sustained overcurrent faults. Built with a high-quality ceramic body, this fuse offers superior thermal stability and high breaking capacity compared to glass counterparts. It features pre-attached axial leads (pigtails) , making it perfect for direct through-hole soldering onto Printed Circuit Boards (PCBs) where fuse holders are not used. This is an essential component for repair and manufacturing of power supplies, adapters, chargers, and various consumer electronics. Features & Specifications Fuse Type: Ceramic Cartridge Fuse Response Characteristic: Time-Lag / Slow Blow (Type T) Voltage Rating: 250V AC Amperage Rating: 2A (2 Amperes) Breaking Capacity: High (H) Mounting Style: Axial Leaded (Through-Hole for PCB) Body Material: Ceramic Tube Lead Material: Tin-plated Copper Applications: Switch-mode power supplies (SMPS), LED drivers, Circuit protection. Package Includes 1 x T2H 250V Ceramic Axial Fuse

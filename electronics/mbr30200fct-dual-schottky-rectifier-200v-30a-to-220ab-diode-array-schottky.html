Product details

The MBR30200FCT dual Schottky rectifier diode is engineered for high-frequency applications, offering low power loss and high efficiency. Encased in the TO-220AB package, it ensures robust performance with a maximum reverse voltage of 200V and a continuous forward current of 30A. Its low forward voltage drop and high surge current capability make it suitable for power supply designs, converters, and other demanding applications. Key Features: High Efficiency: Low power loss suitable for high-frequency operations. High Surge Capability: Handles high surge currents effectively. Reliable Construction: High-temperature epoxy encapsulation enhances mechanical strength and moisture resistance. Package: ITO-220AB ensures ease of integration into various designs. Applications: Ideal for use in switching power supplies, converters, freewheeling diodes, and reverse battery protection. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) Q1: What is the maximum operating temperature for the MBR30200FCT? A1: The MBR30200FCT can operate at junction temperatures up to 175°C. Q2: Can the MBR30200FCT be used in parallel configurations? A2: Yes, but ensure proper current sharing techniques to prevent thermal runaway. Q3: What is the typical forward voltage drop of the MBR30200FCT? A3: The typical forward voltage drop is approximately 0.90V at 30A. Package Include: 1x MBR30200FCT Dual Schottky Rectifier

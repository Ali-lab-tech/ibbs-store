Product details

The aP89085 OTP Voice IC is a high-performance audio chip designed for various applications requiring voice playback and control. With its advanced features and flexible options, the aP89085 offers exceptional versatility and customization capabilities. Key Features: Embedded EPROM: Stores up to 85 seconds of voice data at 6kHz sampling and 4-bit ADPCM compression. Multiple Voice Groups: Create and manage up to 254 distinct voice groups for diverse audio playback needs. Flexible Trigger Modes: Choose between key trigger, CPU parallel mode, or CPU serial mode for precise control. Customizable Output Options: Select ramp or no-ramp output, long or short debounce time, and utilize three user-programmable outputs for added functionality. Efficient Power Consumption: Operates on a single 2. 2V-3. 6V power supply and consumes minimal standby current. Built-in Oscillator: Controls sampling frequency with an external resistor for precise audio playback. Applications: Electronic Toys and Games: Add engaging sounds and voice prompts. Interactive Devices: Create interactive experiences with voice feedback. Home Automation Systems: Integrate voice control and alerts. Industrial Applications: Provide audible notifications and instructions. Upgrade your projects with the aP89085 OTP Voice IC and unlock its full potential.

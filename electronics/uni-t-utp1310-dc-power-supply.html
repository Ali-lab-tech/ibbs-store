Product details

UNI-T UTP1310 — a 320 W switching bench DC power supply delivering a fully adjustable 0–32 V / 0–10 A single output with 10 mV / 1 mA setting and readback resolution. The 10 A output drives motors, automotive electronics, LED loads and power circuits that a 6 A supply cannot, while keeping the same compact 80 mm-wide chassis. Twin 4-digit LED displays, settable OVP/OCP and three M1–M3 memory presets make repeat testing quick. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by Digilog.pk with manual and warranty.

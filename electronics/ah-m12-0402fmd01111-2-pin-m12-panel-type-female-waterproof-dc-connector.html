Product details

The AH-M12-0402FMD01111 is a 2-pin M12 panel-type female waterproof DC connector, engineered for dependable power connections in various applications. Key Specifications: Voltage Rating: 60V Current Rating: 5A Wire Gauge Compatibility: ≤0.5mm² Cable Outer Diameter (OD): 3.5mm to 5.5mm Operating Temperature Range: -20°C to +80°C Mechanical Life: 500 mating cycles Waterproof Level: IP65 This connector is designed with a 5.5mm outer diameter and offers options for either a 2.1mm or 2.5mm inner diameter, making it versatile for various DC power applications. The housing is constructed from durable PVC and is available in black or white. It complies with CE and RoHS certifications, ensuring adherence to international safety and environmental standards. Common applications include LED lighting systems, outdoor lights, and other scenarios requiring secure and waterproof DC connections. The IP65 rating ensures protection against dust and low-pressure water jets, making it suitable for outdoor and industrial environments.

Product details

HOW TO USE? There are only two buttons "READ" and "WRITE" for easy operation. OPERATIONS: Firstly, remove the battery lid, then install 2 pieces of 1.5V AAA batteries, and then push the lid back. Secondly, push the switch upward to turn on the device, then the red light is on, the green light and yellow light are flashing shortly, meanwhile the device beeps twice, that means the device is ready to work. If the lights aren't on, please re-install the batteries and re-start the device until it can work. Thirdly, hold the handheld ID duplicator towards the original card (by the way, the card must be ID card or low frequency HID card), then press the "READ" button to read, the yellow light flashes, that means it is reading, and then the yellow light is off and green light is on along with one beep or two beeps, means successfully read the card. One beep stands for working on the ID card, twice beep stands for working on the HID card. Fourthly, once success reading, you can change the original card to writable ID card or HID card, then press the "WRITE" button to write, the yellow light flashes, that means it is writing, and then the yellow light is off and green light is on along with one beep or two beeps, means successfully written the card. One beep stands for working on the ID card, twice beep stands for working on the HID card. If more cards need to write, just continue to write one by one.

Product details

UNI-T UPO1202CS — The UPO1202CS is a 200 MHz 2-channel digital storage oscilloscope with 1.0 GSa/s sample rate, 56 Mpts memory depth, and a 500,000 wfms/s waveform capture rate. It features UNI-T's Ultra Phosphor 2.0 display technology for intensity-graded waveform visualization on a 7in TFT LCD display. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by Digilog.pk with warranty. Key Features 200 MHz Bandwidth, 2 Channels — Two-channel capture at up to 200 MHz. 1.0 GSa/s Sample Rate — Captures fast edges and high-frequency content with accurate timing. 56 Mpts Memory Depth — Deep memory for capturing long signal records without reducing sample rate. 500,000 wfms/s Capture Rate — Ultra Phosphor 2.0 display technology reveals rare signal anomalies. 36 Automated Measurements — Time, voltage, frequency, and statistical measurements. FFT Spectrum Analysis — Frequency domain analysis for harmonics, EMI, and spectral characterization. Independent DVM Module — Dedicated digital voltmeter readout alongside oscilloscope display.

Product details

The UNI-T LM600G is a 6× monocular laser rangefinder that measures from 3 m up to 600 m with ±1 m accuracy — a compact, pocketable tool for golf, hunting, shooting and outdoor distance work. Sight through the 6× optics, press once, and the distance appears in the eyepiece. Beyond distance it offers flagpole lock to grab the pin, golf slope / trajectory compensation for true play-as yardage, and speed, height and angle measurement, with a wide ±60° angle range. A USB-rechargeable lithium battery keeps it going, in a light 108 mm body. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by Digilog.pk with warranty and English manual.

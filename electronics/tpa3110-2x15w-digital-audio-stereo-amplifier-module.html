Product details

This powerful and efficient Class D amplifier board is ideal for driving professional stereo audio speakers. With its wide input voltage range and high output power, it's perfect for a variety of applications, including televisions, compact audio systems, and DIY projects. Mounting Holes: Allows for easy integration into your projects. Ideal for powering speakers in televisions, computers, vehicles, and home audio systems. Package includes: 1x TPA3110 30W (15W+15W) Digital Stereo Audio Power Amplifier Board Module

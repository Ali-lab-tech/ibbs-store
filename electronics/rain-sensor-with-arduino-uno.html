Rain Sensor with Arduino Uno – Weather Detection & Automation Project Kit First, this Rain Sensor with Arduino Uno bundle gives you a simple way to build your own rainfall detection system. Next, the sensor detects water drops and quickly sends a signal to the Arduino Uno . Then, the Arduino processes the signal to perform smart actions. Finally, you can easily use it to close windows, turn on alarms, or control watering systems automatically. Key Features First, enjoy rain detection and moisture sensitivity for accurate weather-based input. Next, connect it easily since it’s compatible with Arduino Uno and other boards. Then, use both analog and digital outputs for flexible project needs. After that, just plug and play with jumper wires no hassle at all. Finally, build smart DIY automation projects with ease and fun.

Product details

The ET8201 is a smart clamp meter that recognises what you are measuring: connect it and it switches between voltage, current and resistance by itself, so you don't need to turn the dial. AC current goes up to 600A through the jaw. With true RMS, a 6000-count backlit display, NCV live-wire detection and a flashlight, it suits electricians working in poorly lit panels and solar installations. Capacitance, diode, continuity and temperature can be selected manually.

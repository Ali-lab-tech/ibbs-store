Product details

The UNI-T UT320T is a 2-in-1 thermometer that combines a non-contact infrared sensor with a K-type contact probe — measure surface temperatures at a distance or insert the probe for liquids, food, HVAC and machinery, all in one pocket tool. The infrared mode reads -40 °C to 300 °C with an 8:1 distance-to-spot ratio and adjustable emissivity (0.1–1.0) for accurate readings on different materials, guided by a Class 2 aiming laser. The contact probe covers the same range for direct measurement. A clear backlit FSTN display and ~30 h battery life round it out. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by Digilog.pk with warranty and English manual.

Professional Screen Opening Made Easy The KS 2292 2-in-1 Heat-Free Screen Opener is the essential tool for safely separating phone and tablet screens without the need for heat guns or risky thermal methods. Designed for repair professionals and DIY enthusiasts alike, this innovative tool makes screen replacement faster, safer, and more efficient. Key Features: Heat-Free Technology - Safely open screens without risking heat damage to sensitive components like batteries, LCD panels, or adhesive seals 2-in-1 Design - Dual-purpose tool combines screen separation and prying functions in one compact device Universal Compatibility - Works with smartphones, tablets, and other mobile devices with adhesive-sealed screens Precision Control - Ergonomic design provides better grip and control for delicate screen removal operations Durable Construction - Built with high-quality materials to withstand repeated professional use Safe for Devices - Non-marring edges protect screens and frames from scratches during the opening process Why Choose Heat-Free Screen Opening? The KS 2292 eliminates these risks while providing consistent, reliable results every time.

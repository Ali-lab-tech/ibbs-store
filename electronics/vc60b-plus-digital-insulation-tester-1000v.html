Product details

The VC60B+ is a digital insulation resistance tester (megger) for checking cables, motors, transformers and house wiring. Choose a test voltage of 250V, 500V or 1000V and read insulation resistance up to 2000MΩ, to catch damaged or damp insulation before it causes a fault or shock. It also measures AC voltage up to 750V and DC voltage up to 1000V, so you can confirm a circuit is dead before testing. A backlit 1999-count LCD, data hold and auto power-off.

Product details

The FNIRSI FNB58 measures voltage, current and power on USB-A, Micro-USB and Type-C ports, up to 28V, 7A and 140W. Use it to check chargers, power banks, cables and phones, and to see which fast-charge protocol a charger supports. A 16-bit external ADC and a dedicated PD protocol chip give stable readings and let it trigger PD and other fast-charge voltages. This listing is the Standard version, without Bluetooth. Key Features ⚡ 4 – 28V, 0 – 7A, 140W measurement range 🔌 USB-A, Micro-USB and Type-C in one meter 📲 Fast-charge detection and triggering PD and other protocols 📊 Capacity, energy and cable resistance plus D+/D- voltage 🖥️ 2.0" colour TFT wide viewing angle Technical Specifications Voltage 4 – 28V Current 0 – 7A Power 0 – 140W D+/D- voltage 0 – 3.3V Capacity 0 – 9999.99Ah Energy 0 – 9999.99Wh Cable resistance 0 – 9999.9Ω Recording time up to 99 days ADC External 16-bit Display 2.0" colour TFT Version Standard (no Bluetooth) What's in the Box 1 × FNIRSI FNB58 USB tester Cable as supplied by FNIRSI

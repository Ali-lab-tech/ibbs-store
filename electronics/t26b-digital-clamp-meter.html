Product details

The T26B is a slim clamp multimeter that combines a 400A AC current clamp with a full set of multimeter functions: voltage, resistance, capacitance, diode, continuity and temperature. Its 30 mm jaw fits around household and panel wiring, and auto power-off with a low-battery warning keeps two AAA cells going for a long time. A handy single tool for home, lab and workshop.

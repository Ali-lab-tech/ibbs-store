UNI-T UDP6720 — The UDP6720 is a 100 W programmable DC switching power supply with a 60 V × 5 A wide-range output, RS-232 with full SCPI control, 600-group memory, list mode, and OVP/OCP/OTP protection. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by Digilog.pk with warranty.

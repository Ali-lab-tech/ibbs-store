Product details

The FNIRSI NVS-40 is a binocular-style digital night vision device with a 3-inch IPS screen you view with both eyes. Its 850nm infrared illuminator gives up to 300m of vision in complete darkness, and it works as a colour viewer by day. It records 4K video and photos up to 36MP to a TF card, offers 6x digital zoom and is IPX6 water resistant. Power comes from an internal 18650 cell with Type-C charging.

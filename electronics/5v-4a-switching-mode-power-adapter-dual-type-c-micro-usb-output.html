Description This is a genuine OpenPeak switching mode power supply , rated for 5V DC output at 4A , designed to charge or power devices via its dual-lead cable ending in both USB-C and Micro-USB connectors. Built as a Class 2 wall-plug transformer, it converts standard AC mains power into a clean, regulated 5V DC output suitable for tablets, phones, routers, and other 5V-powered electronics that use one of these two connector types. The adapter accepts a wide 90–132VAC input at 60Hz (1.0A max) , making it suitable for standard North American wall outlets, and features a built-in T2A/250V protection fuse along with a fully enclosed protective case for safe indoor use. It carries recognized safety certifications, giving confidence in its build quality and electrical safety compliance. With its dual-connector output cable, this adapter is convenient for charging or powering two compatible devices from a single wall unit, or for keeping a spare charger on hand that works with both common connector types.

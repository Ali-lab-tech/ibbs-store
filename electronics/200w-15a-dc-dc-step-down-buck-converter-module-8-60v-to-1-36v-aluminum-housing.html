Product details

This heavy-duty 200W Synchronous Buck Converter is designed for high-efficiency voltage reduction in automotive, solar, and industrial applications. Encased in a rugged aluminum shell , this module offers superior heat dissipation and physical protection compared to standard bare-board converters. Using synchronous rectification technology , it delivers ultra-high efficiency (up to 94%) with minimal heat generation. It accepts a wide input voltage range (8V–60V) and steps it down to a user-adjustable output (1V–36V). It is capable of handling 10A continuous current (natural cooling) and up to 15A peak current (with added cooling), making it ideal for driving high-power LEDs, charging batteries, or powering 12V/24V devices from 48V telecom or e-bike batteries. Key Features: High Power Density: Supports up to 200W output power (with active cooling) and 15A peak current. Wide Input Range: Safely steps down voltages from 8V to 60V DC. Synchronous Rectification: Advanced circuit design ensures high efficiency (up to 94%) and low ripple. Aluminum Housing: The integrated metal case acts as a heatsink and protects components from dust and accidental shorts. Protections: Built-in Over-Current Protection and Reverse Polarity Protection (prevents damage if input +/- are swapped). Easy Connections: High-current screw terminals allow for solder-free wiring. Applications: E-Bike/Automotive: Convert 48V/36V battery power to 12V for lights, horns, or audio systems. Solar Systems: Efficiently step down solar panel voltage to charge batteries. LED Driver: Constant voltage source for high-power LED strips. DIY Power Supply: Create a robust adjustable bench power supply.

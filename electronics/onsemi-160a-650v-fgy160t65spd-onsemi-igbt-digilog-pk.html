Product details

Product Description: The FGY160T65SPD-F085 is a high-performance Field-Stop Trench IGBT designed for demanding industrial and automotive applications. Rated at 650 volts and 160 amps , it combines low conduction loss with high-speed switching and excellent thermal efficiency. It also integrates a soft, fast-recovery diode for enhanced performance in half-bridge and inverter circuits. Engineered for reliability and efficiency, this IGBT offers low saturation voltage , fast switching , and rugged short-circuit capability . It is AEC-Q101 qualified, making it a dependable choice for automotive powertrain systems, industrial inverters, EV charging stations, and motor drives. Key Features: Collector-Emitter Voltage: 650V Collector Current: 160A continuous, 240A max at 25°C Pulsed Collector Current: Up to 480A Saturation Voltage (V<sub>CE(sat)</sub>): Typically 1.6V at 160A Gate-Emitter Voltage: ±20V (Threshold ~5V) Maximum Junction Temperature: 175°C Switching Speeds: Turn-on Delay: ~53ns Turn-off Delay: ~98ns Rise/Fall Time: ~197ns / ~141ns Switching Energy: Turn-on Energy: ~12.4mJ Turn-off Energy: ~5.7mJ Power Dissipation: Up to 882W Thermal Resistance (R<sub>θJC</sub>): 0.17°C/W Built-in Diode: Soft and fast recovery Package Type: TO-247-3LD Typical Applications: EV powertrains and electric vehicle inverters Industrial motor drives UPS systems Welding machines High-efficiency DC/AC power conversion Battery charging stations (EV chargers) The package includes: 1 x Onsemi 160A 650V FGY160T65SPD Onsemi IGBT

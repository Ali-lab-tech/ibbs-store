Product details

The Red UL1015 Wire Cable 12 AWG is a standard electrical wire used in various electrical and electronic applications, known for its durability, flexibility, and ability to handle higher current loads. Here's a detailed description: Key Features: Type: UL1015, which is a specific type of wire rated by the Underwriters Laboratories (UL) for its electrical properties and safety standards. Gauge: 12 AWG (American Wire Gauge) , indicating a wire diameter of approximately 2.05 mm (0.081 inches) . This size is capable of carrying more current (typically up to 20-25 amps depending on the insulation and application) compared to thinner wires. Conductor: Made of stranded copper wires, providing excellent electrical conductivity and flexibility. Stranded copper is preferred in applications requiring flexibility as it bends more easily than solid copper wire. Insulation: Covered with PVC (Polyvinyl Chloride) insulation, which is heat-resistant, flame-retardant, and provides protection against abrasion, moisture, and chemicals. Voltage Rating: The UL1015 specification typically allows for a voltage rating of 600V . Temperature Rating: Can operate in temperatures up to 105°C (221°F) , making it suitable for various environments. Color: The wire is red , which is commonly used in electrical wiring to indicate positive voltage, power lines, or other specific functions depending on the application. Applications: Internal wiring in appliances, machinery, and control panels. Automotive and marine applications where durability and flexibility are important. General-purpose electrical wiring for power distribution and other circuits.

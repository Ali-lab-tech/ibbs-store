This product is designed with a professional grip that effortlessly transforms into a tripod, making it an ideal choice for vlogging. It is equipped with an LED light that provides ten levels of brightness, ensuring optimal lighting for your videos. The package also includes a universal boom microphone with a 3.5mm connector, making it compatible with a wide range of devices including smartphones and cameras. The product comes with an expandable smartphone holder, a user manual, and a microphone cable. Despite its compact size of 16x22x4cm and lightweight design of 250 grams, it packs a punch with its 36 LED lights that offer three brightness settings and a flash brightness of 80LM. The LED lamp is energy-efficient with a power consumption of just 5W. The microphone operates at a frequency range of 100-16000HZ, ensuring clear audio capture. The product is crafted from durable DUI material, promising longevity.

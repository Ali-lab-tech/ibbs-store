Product details

What It Is A single-channel isolated gate driver chip used to switch power transistors (MOSFETs, SiC, IGBTs) on and off safely and efficiently. It replaces older opto-coupled (optical) isolators with a more reliable, higher-performance alternative — while keeping the same pinout, so it can be swapped in directly. Key Specifications Feature Spec Output current 5A peak (source & sink) Supply voltage Up to 32V, with UVLO (under-voltage lockout) Isolation Galvanic isolation between input and output CMTI (noise immunity) ±150 kV/µs Propagation delay ~75 ns typical Pulse width distortion 30 ns max Package 8-pin DIP What It's Used For This chip sits between a microcontroller (low-voltage control signal) and a power transistor (high-voltage switching device). It: Converts a weak logic-level signal into a strong enough pulse to switch a power transistor on/off Electrically isolates the control side from the high-voltage power side, protecting circuits and users from voltage spikes or faults Improves reliability, speed, and thermal performance versus traditional opto-isolated drivers Common applications: Motor drives, power inverters, switch-mode power supplies (SMPS), solar inverters, and industrial power electronics. Package Includes 1 × NSi6801 Isolated Gate Driver IC (8-Pin DIP)

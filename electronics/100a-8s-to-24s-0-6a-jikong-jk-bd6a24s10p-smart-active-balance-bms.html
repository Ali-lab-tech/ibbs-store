Product details

Description The JK-BD6A24S10P is a smart active balance Battery Management System from JIKONG (JKBMS), housed in a compact, potted enclosure with pre-attached power leads (P- and B-) for straightforward installation into your battery pack build. Like other boards in JIKONG's active balance lineup, it actively transfers energy between cells rather than simply bleeding off excess charge — helping your pack reach true full balance, extend battery life, and maximize usable capacity. This model supports a flexible 8S to 24S configuration, making it suitable for a wide range of battery pack sizes, and works with Li-ion, LiFePO4, and LTO cell chemistries. It handles a higher 100A continuous charge/discharge rating than JIKONG's lower-power boards, and delivers active cell balancing at 0.6A , keeping cells tightly matched even under heavier loads. Built-in Bluetooth connectivity and display support let you monitor pack voltage, cell balance, temperature, and BMS status in real time via the JKBMS app — simply scan the QR code on the board to download the app and user manual. The board's labeled balance-lead terminals (B1–B24, plus temperature and B-) make wiring to your battery pack straightforward. Key Features Smart Active Balancing — actively transfers charge between cells for true balance, not just passive dissipation Battery Strings: 8S–24S configurable Charge/Discharge Current: up to 100A Balancing Current: 0.6A Compatible Battery Types: Li-ion / LiFePO4 / LTO Connectivity: Bluetooth + Display support Pre-wired P-/B- power leads for easier installation Labeled balance terminal strip (B1–B24 + temperature sensor input) Ideal For Custom battery packs for solar energy storage, e-bikes, EVs, marine/RV battery banks, and other higher-current multi-cell lithium applications where precise balancing and long pack life matter. Package Includes 1 x JIKONG JK-BD6A24S10P BMS Board (with pre-attached P-/B- leads)

Product details

Description The RELIFE Q7 is a multi-port charging station designed for repair shops, tech workstations, and everyday multi-device charging needs. It features a built-in smart digital display that shows real-time voltage, current, and wattage output for each connected port — making it easy to monitor charging performance and diagnose device charging issues, which is especially useful for mobile repair technicians. With 6 charging ports including USB-C PD, USB-A, and Qi wireless charging on top, the Q7 can charge multiple devices simultaneously — phones, tablets, earbuds, and other USB-powered devices — all from one compact station. Key Features 6-Port Charging Station – Multiple USB-A, USB-C PD, and wireless charging outputs in one unit Smart Digital Display – Live readout of voltage (V), current (A), and wattage (W) per port Qi Wireless Charging Pad – Built-in top surface for wireless phone charging (up to 40.2W shown) Fast Charging Support – PD/QC fast charging protocols for supported devices Compact, Sturdy Design – Space-saving build ideal for repair benches, offices, or home use Multi-Device Compatibility – Works with phones, tablets, earbuds, and other USB-powered gadgets Who It's For Mobile phone and electronics repair technicians (diagnostic charging monitoring) Tech workstations and repair shops needing multiple charging outputs Everyday users who want to charge several devices at once with real-time data Package Includes 1 x RELIFE Q7 Smart Display Charging Station

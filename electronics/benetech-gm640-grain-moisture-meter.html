Product details

The BENETECH GM640 is a probe-type grain moisture meter for farmers, millers, traders and godown keepers. Insert the long probe into the heap or bag and read moisture from 5 to 30% and grain temperature together, to decide whether grain is dry enough to store or sell. It has 8 grain settings including paddy, wheat, barley, forage, corn, rapeseed and soybean, and a moisture limit you can set so it warns when grain is too wet. Key Features 🌾 8 grain types : paddy, wheat, barley, forage, corn, rapeseed, soybean and more 💧 Moisture 5% to 30% , resolution 0.5% 🌡️ Grain temperature −10 to 60℃ , ℃/℉ 🚨 Over-limit moisture warning 💡 Backlight, data hold, auto power-off, 3 × AAA Technical Specifications Model GM640 Brand BENETECH Moisture range 5% to 30%, ±(1% + 0.5), resolution 0.5% Temperature range −10 to 60℃, ±2.5℃, resolution 0.1℃ Grain types 8 Operating environment 0 to 40℃, 0 to 85% RH Power 3 × 1.5V AAA Size Host 72 × 35 × 145 mm; probe 415 × 36 × 36 mm Weight Host 190.6 g; probe 208.3 g What's in the Box 1 × BENETECH GM640 meter 1 × grain probe

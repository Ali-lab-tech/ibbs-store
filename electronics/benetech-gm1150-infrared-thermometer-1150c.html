Product details

The BENETECH GM1150 is a high-temperature infrared thermometer for industrial heat: −30 to 1150℃ . Use it on furnaces, kilns, forging, glass, casting moulds and exhausts where a normal IR gun would run out of range. Its 20:1 distance-to-spot ratio lets you measure a small target from further away, and emissivity is adjustable from 0.10 to 1.00 for different surfaces. Key Features 🔥 −30 to 1150℃ for furnaces and kilns 🎯 20:1 distance-to-spot ratio ✅ ±1.5℃ or ±1.5% accuracy above 0℃ ⚙️ Emissivity 0.10 to 1.00 adjustable, 0.1℃ resolution ⏱️ 500 ms response, 9V battery Technical Specifications Model GM1150 Brand BENETECH Temperature range −30 to 1150℃ (−22 to 2102℉) Distance to spot 20:1 Accuracy 0 to 1150℃: ±1.5℃ or ±1.5%; −30 to 0℃: ±3℃ (whichever is greater) Resolution 0.1℃ / 0.1℉ Repeatability 1% of reading or 1℃ Response time 500 ms, 95% response Spectral response 8 to 14 µm Emissivity 0.10 to 1.00 adjustable (0.95 preset) Power 9V alkaline or NiCd battery Battery life 22 h non-laser, 12 h laser (alkaline) Weight 270 g Size 141 × 60 × 200 mm What's in the Box 1 × BENETECH GM1150 infrared thermometer

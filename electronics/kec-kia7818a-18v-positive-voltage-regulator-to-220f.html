Product details

Description: The KIA7818A is a three-terminal positive fixed voltage regulator manufactured by KEC (Korea Electronics Corporation), designed to output a stable, regulated 18V from an unregulated DC input. Part of KEC's 78xx-series regulator family, it's built for straightforward, drop-in voltage regulation in a wide range of analog and digital circuits, including C-MOS, TTL, DTL, and HTL power supply applications. The device includes internal thermal overload protection and internal short-circuit current limiting, making it robust against overheating and output faults during operation. It can supply an output current in excess of 1A, and requires only minimal external components (typically a couple of capacitors) to function reliably in a circuit. This particular unit is housed in a TO-220F (isolated) package — a fully-insulated variant of the standard TO-220 outline, allowing direct mounting to a heatsink without a separate insulating pad, which simplifies board assembly while keeping the mounting tab electrically isolated from the circuit. Key Specifications: Manufacturer: KEC (Korea Electronics) Part Number: KIA7818A Type: Fixed Positive Voltage Regulator Output Voltage: 18V Output Current: Up to 1A (1.5A on some variants) Package Type: TO-220F (3-Pin, Isolated, Through Hole) Protection Features: Internal thermal overload protection, internal short-circuit current limiting Equivalent To: NTE1974 Applications: General-purpose 18V DC power regulation, on-board voltage regulation for logic families (C-MOS, TTL, DTL, HTL), power supplies, and embedded electronic systems requiring a stable 18V rail. Package Includes: 1 x KEC KIA7818A Voltage Regulator (TO-220F)

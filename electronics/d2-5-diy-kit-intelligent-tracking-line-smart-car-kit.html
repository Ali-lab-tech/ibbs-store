Product Description The D2-5 DIY Intelligent Tracking Line Smart Car Kit is an educational electronic kit designed for students, hobbyists, and robotics enthusiasts. This DIY robot car follows black tracking lines using infrared sensors, helping users learn the fundamentals of electronics, robotics, motor control, and circuit assembly through hands-on experience. The kit includes all the essential electronic components, dual DC motors, tracking sensors, PCB board, wheels, battery holder, and assembly accessories required to build a functional line-following robot. It is an excellent STEM learning project for schools, science fairs, robotics clubs, and beginners interested in DIY electronics.

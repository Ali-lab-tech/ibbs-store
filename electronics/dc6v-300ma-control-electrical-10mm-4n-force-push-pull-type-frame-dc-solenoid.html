Product details

A DC 6V 300mA Control Electrical 10mm 4N Force Push-Pull Type Frame DC Solenoid is an electromechanical device that converts electrical energy into mechanical motion, specifically designed for pushing or pulling actions. It consists of a coil of wire that creates a magnetic field when current passes through it, moving a plunger or armature within the solenoid's frame. Key Features: Operating Voltage: 6V DC (Direct Current). Current: Draws around 300mA when energized. Stroke (Movement Distance): The plunger or armature has a movement range of 10mm , indicating how far it travels when activated. Force: Capable of exerting a pushing or pulling force of 4N (Newtons), which is roughly equivalent to 0.4kgf (kilogram-force). Type: This solenoid is a push-pull type , meaning it can be used for either pushing or pulling depending on how it is mounted and connected. Frame: Encased in a sturdy metal frame for structural support, ensuring durability during repeated operation. Application: Commonly used in automation, robotics, door locks, vending machines, and other electromechanical systems that require controlled linear motion. The solenoid provides controlled mechanical movement, ideal for tasks requiring reliable, small-scale force in limited spaces. Package Included: 1x 6v 300ma Solenoid

Product details

The UNI-T UT361 vane anemometer measures wind speed, air volume and temperature with a separate wearproof vane head on a lead, so you can hold the meter comfortably while placing the vane squarely in the airflow. Wind speed reads from 2 to 30 m/s and air volume in CFM or CMM once you enter the duct area. It stores 2044 readings with a settable auto-record interval, switches between seven units, and shows MAX/MIN and real-time modes — a practical instrument for HVAC commissioning, duct balancing, ventilation surveys and environmental testing. Key Features Wind speed 2~30 m/s 🔧 — ±(3%+0.5) from 2–10 m/s and ±(3%+0.8) from 10–30 m/s. Air volume in CFM and CMM ⚡ — 0.001~9999 ×100 with sample-area entry for duct flow. Separate vane probe 📏 — wearproof fan shaft on a lead for accuracy and easy positioning. Temperature 0~40°C 🔧 — ±3°C, with °C/°F selectable. 2044-set data storage ✅ — auto-record with a 0.5~255 s interval for unattended logging. Seven selectable units ⚡ — m/s, km/h, ft/min, MPH, KNOTS, CFM and CMM. MAX/MIN and averaging modes ✅ — wind speed MAX/MIN/real-time; air volume MAX, 2/3 MAX, MIN, AVG. Backlit LCD with data hold 🔋 — 10000-count display and cancelable auto power-off.

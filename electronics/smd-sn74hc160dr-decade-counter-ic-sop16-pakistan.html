Product details

The SMD SN74HC160DR is a synchronous decade (BCD) counter IC in SOP-16 (SOIC-16) surface-mount package, designed for counting and timing applications in Pakistan. This CMOS logic integrated circuit features synchronous counting operation with parallel load capability, providing reliable counting from 0 to 9 for digital clocks, frequency dividers, and control systems. The SMD package enables compact PCB layouts and automated assembly for professional electronics manufacturing and prototyping.

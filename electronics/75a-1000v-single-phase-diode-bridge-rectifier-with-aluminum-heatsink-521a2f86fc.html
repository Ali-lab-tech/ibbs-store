Product details

The FSB7510A is a high-performance Single Phase Bridge Rectifier designed for heavy-duty power conversion applications. Encased in a durable aluminum casing with integrated cooling fins, this component is engineered to efficiently manage heat dissipation while converting Alternating Current (AC) into Direct Current (DC). With a robust current capacity of 75 Amperes and a peak reverse voltage of 1000 Volts , this rectifier is perfect for industrial automation, power supplies, and battery charging circuits. The internal chip is electrically insulated from the base, ensuring safety and allowing for direct mounting onto additional chassis or heatsinks. Key Features High Power Handling: Capable of handling continuous forward current up to 75A. Superior Thermal Management: Features an aluminum alloy base with heat-sink fins to maximize heat dissipation and improve reliability. Durable Construction: The internal diode chip is protected by high-quality vacuum epoxy resin potting, making it resistant to moisture and vibration. Low Forward Voltage Drop: Ensures high efficiency during power conversion. Easy Installation: Standard 4-terminal spade/blade design with a central mounting hole for secure attachment.

Product details

The Atmel ATmega328P-AU is a powerful and versatile 8-bit microcontroller ideal for a wide range of embedded applications. With its 32KB of flash memory, 2KB of SRAM, and 1KB of EEPROM, this microcontroller offers ample storage for your programs and data. Key Features: High Performance: Operates at up to 20MHz for fast execution. Low Power Consumption: Ensures efficient operation and extended battery life. Versatile I/O: Features 23 general-purpose I/O lines for connecting external devices. Powerful Peripherals: Includes 6 PWM channels, 8-channel 10-bit ADC, USART, SPI, I2C, and more. In-Circuit Programming: Allows for easy programming and debugging. Ideal for projects like Arduino, robotics, home automation, and industrial control.

Product details

The TOOLTOP ET50 is a 150 mm stainless steel digital vernier caliper with 0.01 mm resolution. It reads outside, inside and depth measurements on an easy LCD, so you don't need to read a vernier scale. A button switches between millimetres, inches and fractions, and the zero button lets you take comparative measurements from any point. It turns on and off by itself and comes with its LR44 battery fitted.

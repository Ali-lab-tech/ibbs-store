Product details

Description: The Hawk M15 is a simple, no-frills wired USB optical mouse designed for everyday business and office computing tasks. Built with a classic three-button layout (left click, right click, and a middle scroll wheel), it offers reliable, straightforward navigation and scrolling for common office work such as document editing, spreadsheets, web browsing, and general desktop use. The mouse features a comfortable, ergonomic curved shape designed to sit naturally under the palm during extended use, helping reduce hand fatigue during a full workday. Its textured black finish provides a secure, non-slip grip, while the glossy black accent strip across the front adds a clean, professional look suitable for office and business environments. Connectivity is handled via a standard USB Type-A wired connection, offering true plug-and-play functionality with no drivers or software setup required — simply plug it into a USB port and it's ready to use with virtually any Windows, macOS, or Linux computer. The mouse operates on a low input power draw (5.0V / 100mA), making it energy-efficient and broadly compatible with standard USB ports. Key Specifications: Brand: Hawk Model: M15 Product Weight: 73g ± 5g Product Dimensions: 118mm x 62mm x 37mm Connection Type: Wired USB (USB-A) Input Power: 5.0V, 100mA Buttons: Left Click, Right Click, Scroll Wheel (Middle Click) Compliance/Certifications: FCC, CE Execution Standard: GB/T 14081-2010 Applications: Everyday office and business computing, document and spreadsheet work, general web browsing, and as a reliable, affordable wired mouse for desktop or laptop setups in home or workplace environments.

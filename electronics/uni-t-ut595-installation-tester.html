Product details

The UNI-T UT595 multifunction installation tester brings a full electrical-installation test suite into one 9999-count instrument: insulation resistance at 250V, 500V and 1000V up to 1000MΩ, 200mA low-resistance continuity, line and loop impedance from 0.01Ω to 2000Ω with prospective fault current to 26kA, RCD trip-time and ramp testing, phase sequence and AC voltage to 440V. The UT595 is the upper model of the UT590 series, extending RCD trip-time coverage with ×2 I△n test ranges. With connection checks displayed in real time, one-key storage of RCD results and a 0°/180° phase-angle option to catch the fastest trigger time, it is built for electrical contractors and inspectors certifying installations. Loop and line impedance ✅ — 0.01Ω~2000Ω with 20A test current and PFC/PSC to 26kA. Non-trip loop impedance 🔧 — 1.00Ω~2000Ω at 15mA so you can test without tripping the RCD. Full RCD trip-time testing ⚡ — 10/30/100/300/500mA at ×1/2, ×1, ×2 and ×5 I△n, plus ramp testing. 0°/180° phase angle option ✅ — detect the fastest trigger time; half-wave and 30 s count-down modes. Phase sequence and missing-phase test 🔧 — 100V~440V, forward and reverse rotation display. 9999-count LCD with analogue bar 🔋 — white backlight, data storage and auto power-off.

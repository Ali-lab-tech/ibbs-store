Product details

Description: The RELIFE RL-309H is a multi-functional charging hub designed to power all your devices from a single station. It features multiple universal AC sockets for plugging in tools, lamps, or chargers, along with a built-in 15W wireless charging pad and phone stand that lets you charge your smartphone hands-free while keeping it visible and accessible. With 72W combined QC (Quick Charge) and PD (Power Delivery) output across USB-A and USB-C ports, it rapidly charges phones, tablets, and other USB devices at full speed. The compact, sturdy design with a non-slip base makes it ideal for repair workbenches, offices, or home desks where multiple devices need charging at once.

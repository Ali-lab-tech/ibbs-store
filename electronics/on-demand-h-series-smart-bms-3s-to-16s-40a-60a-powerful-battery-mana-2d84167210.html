Product details

The H-Series Smart BMS is a cutting-edge solution for managing lithium-ion and LiFePO4 batteries. This compact yet powerful device offers comprehensive protection and optimization, ensuring your batteries operate at their peak performance. Key Features: Versatile Compatibility: Supports 3S to 16S configurations, accommodating a wide range of battery pack voltages. High Current Capacity: Handles up to 40A or 60A continuous discharge current, ideal for demanding applications. Advanced Protection: Safeguards against overcharge, over-discharge, short circuit, and temperature extremes. Smart Communication: Features CAN, RS485, and dual UART communication interfaces for seamless integration. User-Friendly Interface: Comes with a self-developed app and PC software for easy monitoring and control. Cloud Connectivity: Integrates with the DALY Cloud platform for remote monitoring and data analysis. Applications: Electric Vehicles: Electric bikes, scooters, tricycles, and wheelchairs Industrial Equipment: AGVs, robots, and automated systems Portable Power Supplies: Backup power and off-grid solutions Lead-Acid to Lithium Conversions: Seamlessly transitioning from lead-acid to lithium batteries Battery Rental and Swap Stations: Efficient management of rental battery packs Benefits: Enhanced Battery Life: Protects batteries from harmful conditions, extending their lifespan. Improved Safety: Prevents potential hazards such as fires and explosions. Optimized Performance: Ensures efficient charging and discharging, maximizing battery capacity. Remote Monitoring: Track battery health and usage data from anywhere. Scalability: Easily adapts to different battery configurations and applications. Choose the H-Series Smart BMS for reliable, efficient, and safe battery management.

Product details

The SMD SN74LS155 is a dual 2-to-4 line decoder/demultiplexer IC in SOP-16 (SOIC-16) surface-mount package, designed for address decoding and data distribution applications in Pakistan. This TTL logic integrated circuit contains two independent 2-to-4 decoders with active-low outputs, providing reliable decoding for memory systems, data routing, and digital control logic. The SMD package enables compact PCB layouts and automated assembly for professional electronics manufacturing and prototyping.

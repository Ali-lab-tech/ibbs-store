Product details

The FNIRSI SG-002 is a low-cost current and voltage signal source for testing PLC analog inputs, valve actuators, VFD speed inputs and panel meters. It outputs 0 – 24mA in 0.01mA steps, covering both 0 – 20mA and 4 – 20mA standards, in active or passive loop configuration. Switch to voltage mode for 0 – 10V style signals. It runs from its internal lithium battery, from USB 5V, or from a 15 – 30V external supply such as a PLC's 24V rail, and connects through detachable screw terminals. Key Features 🔌 0 – 24mA current output 0.01mA steps, active or passive ⚡ Voltage output mode for 0 – 10V, 0 – 5V and similar signals 🔧 2-, 3- and 4-wire connection via detachable terminals 🔋 Internal 3.7V 1100mAh battery plus USB 5V or 15 – 30V external power 🎚️ Coarse and fine adjustment with a 4-digit display Technical Specifications Current output 0 – 24mA, 0.01mA steps (0 – 20mA / 4 – 20mA) Loop modes Active / passive Voltage output Selectable ranges including 0 – 10V Display 4-digit LED Power 3.7V 1100mAh Li-ion, micro USB 5V, or DC 15 – 30V external Size 89 × 69 × 28 mm What's in the Box 1 × FNIRSI SG-002 signal generator (battery version)

Product details

The SBD20C150F is a high-efficiency Dual Schottky Barrier Diode (SBD) designed for high-voltage and high-current applications. Encased in a fully isolated TO-220F (Full Pack) plastic package, this component offers excellent thermal resistance and electrical isolation, making it ideal for use in compact power supply units without requiring additional insulation hardware. With a 20A total forward current rating (10A per leg) and a 150V repetitive peak reverse voltage, this rectifier is engineered to minimize power loss with its low forward voltage drop. It is widely used in high-frequency inverters, DC/DC converters, and as a freewheeling diode in varied electronic circuits. Key Features: High Junction Temperature: Capable of operating reliably under high-temperature conditions. Low Forward Voltage Drop: Increases efficiency by reducing power dissipation. Fast Switching: High switching speed makes it perfect for high-frequency conversion. Dual Configuration: Common Cathode structure (Center pin is Cathode, outer pins are Anodes). Isolated Package: TO-220F plastic package prevents shorting against heatsinks. Technical Specifications: Part Number: SBD20C150F Type: Schottky Barrier Rectifier (Dual) Maximum Repetitive Peak Reverse Voltage ( $V_{RRM}$ ): 150V Average Rectified Forward Current ( $I_{O}$ ): 20A (Total) / 10A (Per Leg) Configuration: Common Cathode Package Type: TO-220F (Insulated) Mounting Type: Through Hole Applications: Switch Mode Power Supplies (SMPS) High-frequency DC to DC converters LED Power Drivers Freewheeling Diodes Battery Chargers Package Includes: 1 x SBD20C150F Schottky Barrier Diode

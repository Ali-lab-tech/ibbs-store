NTC (Negative Temperature Coefficient) thermistors like this one decrease in resistance as temperature rises — making them ideal for temperature sensing and inrush current limiting. PTC thermistors do the opposite and are typically used as resettable fuses for overcurrent protection. NTC vs RTD — Which Should You Choose? Compared to Resistance Temperature Detectors (RTDs), NTC thermistors offer higher sensitivity within a focused temperature range, making them the preferred choice for precision sensing in embedded systems and DIY electronics. RTDs cover wider ranges but at higher cost and complexity.

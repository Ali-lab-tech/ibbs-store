Product details

The HoldPeak HP-4070L is a handheld LCR meter for measuring inductance, capacitance and resistance , plus a socket for testing transistor gain (hFE). It is a practical bench tool for checking coils, chokes and capacitors during electronics repair. It has a backlit 3½-digit LCD, manual range selection and protective rubber corner guards.

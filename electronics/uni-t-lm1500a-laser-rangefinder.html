Product details

The UNI-T LM1500A (LM1500) is a long-range 6× monocular laser rangefinder reaching from 3 m to a full 1500 m with ±1 m accuracy — the top of the range, for long-distance hunting, shooting, surveying and golf where reach matters most. Its 6× optics and clear in-view display make target acquisition easy, while flagpole lock , golf slope / trajectory compensation and speed, height, angle, area and volume modes cover every measurement task. Powered by a USB-rechargeable lithium battery in a pocket-sized 171 g body. Genuine UNI-T product supplied by with warranty and English manual.

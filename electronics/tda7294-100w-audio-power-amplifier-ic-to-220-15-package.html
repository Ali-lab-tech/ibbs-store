TDA7294 High-Performance Audio Power Amplifier IC Professional-grade monophonic audio power amplifier integrated circuit from STMicroelectronics. The TDA7294 delivers exceptional audio quality with high output power, low distortion, and comprehensive protection features, making it ideal for high-fidelity audio applications and DIY amplifier projects. Key Features: High output power capability up to 100W RMS Low total harmonic distortion (THD) Wide supply voltage range Built-in mute and standby functions Comprehensive protection circuitry (thermal, short-circuit, overvoltage) TO-220-15 package with excellent thermal performance Minimal external components required Technical Specifications: Output Power: 100W RMS @ 8Ω, 70W RMS @ 4Ω (±35V supply) Supply Voltage: ±10V to ±40V DC Total Harmonic Distortion: 0.005% typical @ 1kHz Signal-to-Noise Ratio: 100dB typical Voltage Gain: 30dB typical Input Impedance: 100kΩ Frequency Response: 20Hz to 20kHz Package: TO-220-15 (Multiwatt-15) Operating Temperature: -40°C to +150°C (junction) Protection Features: Thermal shutdown protection Short-circuit protection on output Overvoltage and undervoltage protection Load dump protection Applications: Perfect for high-quality audio amplifier designs, home theater systems, active speaker systems, subwoofer amplifiers, professional audio equipment, and DIY hi-fi projects. Note: Requires adequate heatsinking for optimal performance and thermal management. Refer to datasheet for recommended PCB layout and component values.

Product details

The Textool 28 Pin Mini 228-3341 ZIF Socket is a high-quality component designed for easy and secure insertion and removal of 28-pin DIP ICs. Its compact size and durable construction make it ideal for various applications in electronics development and testing. Key Features: Zero Insertion Force (ZIF): Prevents damage to IC pins during insertion and removal. 28-Pin Configuration: Compatible with a wide range of 28-pin DIP ICs. Compact Design: Ideal for space-constrained applications. High Durability: Ensures reliable electrical connections and long-lasting performance. Applications: IC Testing and Prototyping: Quickly and easily swap ICs for testing and development. Programming and Debugging: Program and test DIP ICs without damaging the pins. Development Boards: Use in IC tester boards and other development platforms. Upgrade your electronics projects with the Textool 28 Pin Mini 228-3341 ZIF Socket.

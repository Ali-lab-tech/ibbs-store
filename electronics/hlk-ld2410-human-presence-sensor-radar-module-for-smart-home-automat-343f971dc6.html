Versatile Applications: Ideal for smart home automation, security systems, lighting control, and more. Easy Integration: Simple to integrate into various smart home and IoT projects. Benefits: Enhanced Security: Detects intruders and triggers alarms or alerts for proactive security measures. Smart Home Automation: Automate lights, appliances, and other devices based on human presence. Energy Efficiency: Save energy by automatically turning off lights and devices when no one is present.

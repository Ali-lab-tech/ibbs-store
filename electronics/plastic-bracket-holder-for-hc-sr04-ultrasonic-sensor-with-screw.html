Product details

This plastic bracket holder provides a secure mounting solution for the HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor. It is specifically designed to station the sensor firmly in place for your robotics or distance-sensing projects. The HC-SR04 is a widely used ultrasonic distance sensor that utilizes sonar to determine the distance to an object. This mounting bracket ensures consistent alignment and stability for the sensor during operation. Designed specifically for HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensors Includes necessary screws and nuts for installation Durable plastic construction

Product details

The WinAPEX ET8138 is an ultra-thin smart multimeter with a touch panel instead of a rotary dial. It identifies the measurement and range automatically, and shows the reading on a colour display with an analog bar. It covers DC voltage to 1000V, AC voltage to 750V, resistance and capacitance, with true RMS, NCV live-wire detection, backlight, flashlight and room-temperature display. At 12 mm thick it fits in a pocket.

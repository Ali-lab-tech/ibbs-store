Product details

The 100W Super Fast Charging Cable 3 in 1 Braided Data Cable is designed for high-speed charging and data transfer with multiple devices. Here are its key features: Power Output : Supports up to 100W power delivery, ensuring super-fast charging for devices like laptops, tablets, and smartphones that are compatible with fast charging technology. 3-in-1 Design : Comes with three connectors: USB-C, IPHONE, and Micro USB, allowing compatibility with a wide range of devices, including Android phones, iPhones, and older devices with Micro USB ports. Braided Exterior : The cable is covered with a durable nylon braided material, which enhances durability and resists wear and tear from daily use. The braided exterior also helps prevent tangling. Data Transfer : Capable of high-speed data transfer for syncing devices like smartphones, tablets, and laptops with your computer or other devices. Durability : With a reinforced structure, it offers strong resistance to bending, stretching, and general physical strain. The connectors are also robust, designed to withstand repeated plugging and unplugging. Length : Available in various lengths, usually between 1m. providing flexibility for different usage scenarios like at home, office, or on the go. Universal Compatibility : Compatible with a wide range of devices, including iPhone, iPad, Samsung Galaxy, Huawei, laptops with USB-C ports, and various other gadgets that require USB-C, Micro USB, or Lightning connections. Safe Charging : Includes multiple safety features like over-current, over-voltage, and over-temperature protection to safeguard both the cable and the connected devices during charging. This cable is ideal for users who want a convenient, durable, and fast-charging solution for multiple devices. Package Included: 1x 100W Super Fast Charging Cable 3 in 1 Braided Data Cable

AT350 SMD Coupler - High-Performance Surface Mount Device for Signal Isolation The AT350 SMD Coupler is a high-performance surface-mount device designed for signal isolation and switching applications. It offers excellent electrical isolation between input and output circuits, making it ideal for use in high-speed digital interfaces, power electronics, and industrial systems. Benefits of the AT350 SMD Coupler Compact SMD Package: Saves space on circuit boards, allowing for more compact designs. High Electrical Isolation: Provides safe and efficient signal isolation, protecting sensitive circuits. Fast Switching: Ideal for high-speed digital signals, ensuring accurate performance. Reliable Performance: Operates efficiently over a wide temperature range. Energy Efficient: Low power consumption, making it suitable for battery-powered applications.

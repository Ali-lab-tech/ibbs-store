Industrial Burner Services in Pakistan

IBBS provides burner and boiler engineering support for industrial plants. Work may include burner modifications, troubleshooting discussions, automation support and sourcing of flame safeguard and burner control components, subject to equipment review and confirmed scope.

Burner modifications and flame safeguard parts

Burner systems must be assessed in relation to the boiler, fuel, control arrangement and site operating procedures. IBBS can discuss burner modification requirements and related controls, and help identify suitable replacement components from the details provided by the customer.

For an initial review, share the burner or boiler make and model, fuel type, control panel details, plant city and a clear description of the issue. Photos of the nameplate and control assembly can help identify the equipment. Final compatibility and availability are confirmed before an order or service scope is agreed.
